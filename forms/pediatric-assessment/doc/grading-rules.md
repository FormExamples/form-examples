# Grading rules

The pediatric-assessment form is a developmental and well-child screening
questionnaire. The output is a three-level decision per developmental
domain:

| Outcome | Definition |
| ------- | ---------- |
| Pass | Milestones achieved at the expected age, growth on centile, no parental or clinician concern |
| Concern | One or more amber findings; warrants review at the next routine contact |
| Refer | One or more red findings; warrants prompt onward referral (community paediatrics, health visitor follow-up, speech and language, audiology, etc.) |

These bands are presentational. The underlying milestone and growth
references are drawn from canonical UK sources.

## Underlying references

### Growth

The form uses UK-WHO growth charts. The charts and their guidance documents
are maintained by the Royal College of Paediatrics and Child Health (RCPCH).

- RCPCH UK-WHO growth charts (0–4 years) and UK-WHO 2–18 chart:
  <https://www.rcpch.ac.uk/resources/growth-charts>

Centile flags:

- Weight or length below the 0.4th centile → refer
- Crossing two centile spaces downward → refer
- Head circumference outside 0.4th–99.6th → refer

### Developmental milestones

Milestones are based on the Healthy Child Programme (HCP) and Personal
Child Health Record (PCHR / "red book") progress reviews:

- Department of Health and Social Care, *Healthy Child Programme: pregnancy
  and the first 5 years of life* (2009, refreshed):
  <https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/healthy-child-programme-pregnancy-and-the-first-5-years-of-life>
- UK Personal Child Health Record (the "red book"): published by the
  RCPCH. <https://www.rcpch.ac.uk/resources/personal-child-health-record-pchr>

The form's milestone checks are presented at the routine review ages
specified by the HCP: birth, 6–8 weeks, 9–12 months, 2–2.5 years, and
4–5 years.

For 0–5 years milestone wording, the form follows:

- Ages and Stages Questionnaire 3 (ASQ-3) age bands (where the trust uses
  ASQ-3 as the developmental tool). ASQ-3 is published by Brookes
  Publishing; see Squires J, Bricker D. *Ages & Stages Questionnaires®,
  Third Edition (ASQ-3™): A parent-completed child monitoring system*.
  Baltimore: Paul H Brookes Publishing Co., 2009. Publisher page:
  <https://agesandstages.com/>

For school-age and adolescent assessment, the form uses the WHO
Integrated Management of Childhood Illness (IMCI) framework signs for
common acute presentations, alongside the UK NICE NG143 (*Fever in under
5s*) and NG51 (*Sepsis*) red flags.

- WHO IMCI: <https://www.who.int/teams/maternal-newborn-child-adolescent-health-and-ageing/child-health/integrated-management-of-childhood-illness>
- NICE NG143 — *Fever in under 5s*: <https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/ng143>
- NICE NG51 — *Sepsis: recognition, diagnosis and early management*:
  <https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/ng51>

### NICE "traffic light" sign for under-5s fever

The form references the NICE NG143 traffic-light system (green / amber /
red) for fever in children under 5. Red and amber features feed into the
form's "refer" outcome.

### Immunization status

The form checks UK routine schedule completeness against:

- UK Health Security Agency. *Immunization schedule* (the Green Book
  schedule).
  <https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/the-complete-routine-immunization-schedule>
- *Immunization against infectious disease* ("Green Book"), UKHSA:
  <https://www.gov.uk/government/collections/immunization-against-infectious-disease-the-green-book>

### Adolescent screening (HEEADSSS)

Where the patient is aged 12+ the form invites HEEADSSS-style enquiry
(Home, Education, Eating, Activities, Drugs, Sexuality, Suicide/depression,
Safety). HEEADSSS is described in:

- Goldenring JM, Rosen DS. *Getting into adolescent heads: An essential
  update.* Contemporary Pediatrics. 2004;21(1):64–90. PubMed:
  <https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/15505781/>

## Output

- Per-domain outcome (Pass / Concern / Refer)
- Overall recommendation (continue routine review / discuss at next health
  visitor contact / refer)
- Specific flagged-issues list (e.g. amber NG143 fever feature, missed
  immunizations, safeguarding indicator)
- Structured PDF for the PCHR

## Notes

- The form does not implement a single "developmental quotient" or a
  Bayley-style assessment; both require trained administration.
- The form does not score adolescent mental-health PROMs (use the
  mental-health-assessment form).
- Safeguarding decisions remain with the named professional; the form
  surfaces NICE NG76 prompts.
