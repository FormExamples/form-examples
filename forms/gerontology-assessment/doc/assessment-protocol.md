# Assessment protocol

## Intended use

A structured Comprehensive Geriatric Assessment (CGA)-style questionnaire
that records frailty (CFS), functional ability, cognition, mobility,
nutrition, polypharmacy, mood, and continence. The output supports
multidisciplinary planning and onward referral but does not replace a
face-to-face CGA performed by a geriatrician or geriatric multidisciplinary
team.

## Intended users

- GPs and practice nurses managing older adults
- Hospital frailty teams
- Care of the Elderly (COTE) outpatient teams
- Community matrons and frailty hubs

## Setting

UK general practice, community frailty service, hospital ambulatory frailty
unit, or COTE outpatient clinic.

## Workflow

1. **Demographics** — name, DOB, GP, next of kin, carer, advance care
   planning status (if known).
2. **Functional assessment** — Katz ADL (basic) and Lawton IADL
   (instrumental); identify need for care package, equipment, or environment
   adaptation.
3. **Cognitive screen** — 4AT for delirium, plus MoCA or MMSE for sustained
   cognitive impairment.
4. **Mobility and falls** — number of falls in last 12 months, Timed Up &
   Go (if feasible), use of walking aids, fear of falling.
5. **Nutrition** — MUST score (weight, weight loss, acute disease effect),
   appetite, swallowing, dentition.
6. **Polypharmacy review** — medication list checked against AGS 2023 Beers
   Criteria and STOPP/START v3; anticholinergic burden; opioid burden.
7. **Comorbidities** — multimorbidity index; recent hospital admissions;
   chronic conditions.
8. **Psychosocial** — GDS-15 mood screen; loneliness; carer strain;
   bereavement.
9. **Continence and skin** — urinary and faecal continence; pressure-ulcer
   risk per Waterlow score (separate fields).

## Output

- CFS score (clinician-assigned)
- Per-domain finding (functional, cognitive, mobility, nutrition,
  polypharmacy, mood, continence)
- Flagged-issues list (e.g. CFS ≥ 6, ≥ 2 falls/yr, MUST ≥ 2, anticholinergic
  burden, GDS-15 ≥ 5)
- Recommended CGA actions and onward referrals (OT, physio, dietitian,
  pharmacist medication review, falls clinic, memory clinic, social care)
- Structured PDF for the patient record and the GP

## Safety-net behaviour

- Suspected delirium (4AT ≥ 4) → urgent medical review for reversible cause
- Recent injurious fall → falls clinic referral per NICE CG161
- MUST ≥ 2 → dietetic referral and oral nutritional supplement consideration
- Suicidal ideation on GDS-15 → urgent mental health pathway
- Severe polypharmacy with Beers/STOPP hits → structured medication review
- Capacity concerns → NICE NG108 mental capacity pathway

## Out of scope

- Detailed Waterlow / Braden pressure-ulcer assessment (use specialist
  tissue-viability tool)
- Acute hospital admission triage
- End-of-life prescribing
- Detailed dementia diagnostic assessment (use memory clinic)
- Carer-specific assessment (use Carers Trust resources)
