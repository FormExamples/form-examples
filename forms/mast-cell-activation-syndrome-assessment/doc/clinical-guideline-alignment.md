# Clinical guideline alignment

There is no single national MCAS guideline. The form is aligned with the
internationally recognised consensus statements and expert review papers
listed below.

## Consensus statements

### Valent 2012 / 2019 (Consensus-1)

- Valent P, Akin C, Arock M, et al. *Definitions, criteria and global
  classification of mast cell disorders with special reference to mast cell
  activation syndromes: a consensus proposal*. International Archives of
  Allergy and Immunology 2012;157:215-25. PMID: 22041891
- Valent P, Akin C, Bonadonna P, et al. *Proposed Diagnostic Algorithm for
  Patients with Suspected Mast Cell Activation Syndrome*. J Allergy Clin
  Immunol Pract 2019;7:1125-1133.e1. PMID: 30737190
- All three criteria required for diagnosis:
  1. Typical symptoms in ≥2 organ systems.
  2. Increase in serum tryptase ≥(1.2 × baseline) + 2 ng/mL.
  3. Response to mast-cell-directed therapy.

### Akin 2010 (foundational)

- Akin C, Valent P, Metcalfe DD. *Mast cell activation syndrome: proposed
  diagnostic criteria*. J Allergy Clin Immunol 2010;126:1099-1104.e4.
  PMID: 21035176

### Consensus-2 (Afrin/Molderings 2020)

- Afrin LB, Ackerley MB, Bluestein LS, et al. *Diagnosis of mast cell
  activation syndrome: a global "consensus-2"*. Diagnosis (Berl)
  2020;8:137-152. PMID: 32324159
- Lower laboratory bar; permits diagnosis with any one biochemical marker
  rise. Used for primary care triage but contested by Valent et al.

## WHO mast cell disorder classification

- Khoury JD, Solary E, Abla O, et al. *The 5th edition of the World Health
  Organization Classification of Haematolymphoid Tumours: Myeloid and
  Histiocytic/Dendritic Neoplasms*. Leukemia 2022;36:1703-1719. PMID: 35732831
- Used by the form to distinguish MCAS from systemic mastocytosis: the
  presence of `KIT D816V` somatic mutation or ≥25% atypical mast cells on
  bone marrow biopsy moves the patient to a mastocytosis category and
  out of the MCAS care pathway.

## Hereditary alpha-tryptasemia

- Lyons JJ, Yu X, Hughes JD, et al. *Elevated basal serum tryptase
  identifies a multisystem disorder associated with increased TPSAB1 copy
  number*. Nature Genetics 2016;48:1564-1569. PMID: 27749843
- Lyons JJ. *Hereditary alpha tryptasemia: genotyping and associated
  clinical features*. Immunol Allergy Clin North Am 2018;38:483-495.
  PMID: 30007466

## Related UK / NICE guidance

There is no NICE guideline specifically for MCAS. Related NICE guidance the
form cross-references:

- NICE CG134 *Anaphylaxis: assessment and referral after emergency
  treatment*. <https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/cg134>
- NICE NG196 *Coeliac disease* (overlap symptoms).
- BSACI Anaphylaxis Guideline (Ewan et al. 2021) — for the anaphylaxis
  overlap subset.

## Mastocytosis Society / patient organisations

- The Mastocytosis Society Inc (TMS): <https://tmsforacure.org/>
- UK Mastocytosis Support Group: <https://www.mastocytosis.org.uk/>

These are patient-facing organisations; their educational material is
referenced by the form's patient information leaflet but not its scoring
logic.
