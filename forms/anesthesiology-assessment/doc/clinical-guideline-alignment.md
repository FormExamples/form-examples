# Clinical Guideline Alignment

Cross-walk between this form and the principal UK and international
perioperative anaesthesia guidelines.

## Centre for Perioperative Care (CPOC)

- *Preoperative Assessment and Optimisation for Adult Surgery* (June 2021).
- Index page: https://cpoc.org.uk/guidelines-resources-guidelines

## Royal College of Anaesthetists (RCoA)

- *Guidelines for the Provision of Anaesthesia Services* (GPAS) — annual
  update. Index: https://rcoa.ac.uk/safety-standards-quality/guidance-resources/guidelines-provision-anaesthetic-services
- *Raising the Standard: a compendium of audit recipes* (Audit Recipe Book).
  Index: https://rcoa.ac.uk/research/research-bodies/quality-improvement/audit-recipe-book

GPAS chapters 2 (Preoperative Assessment and Preparation) and 3 (Provision
of Anaesthesia Services for the Perioperative Care of Elective and Urgent
Care Patients) inform the wizard step order.

## Association of Anaesthetists (AAGBI)

- *Pre-operative assessment and patient preparation: the role of the
  anaesthetist 2* (January 2010). Index:
  https://anaesthetists.org/Home/Resources-publications/Guidelines

## NICE NG45 — Routine preoperative tests for elective surgery

- Guideline ID: NG45 (published April 2016).
- URL: https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/ng45

NG45 specifies which routine tests to offer or not to offer based on ASA
grade and surgery grade (minor, intermediate, major or complex). Step 8
(vital signs & investigations) captures only the tests indicated by NG45 for
the recorded ASA × surgery-grade combination.

## NICE NG161 — Suspected sepsis (relevant to emergency anaesthesia)

- Guideline ID: NG51 (published 2016) succeeded by NG51 → updated as NG51 in
  2024 with parallel guidance NG143 for under-18s. Core sepsis guidance index:
  https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/published?ngt=NICE%20guidelines&type=NICE%20guidelines (filter by "sepsis").

The emergency-anaesthesia branch of step 9 references NICE sepsis criteria
where the patient is recorded as septic or potentially septic.

## WHO Surgical Safety Checklist

- WHO *Implementation manual: surgical safety checklist*. Index:
  https://www.who.int/teams/integrated-health-services/patient-safety/research/safe-surgery

The sign-in items (patient ID, site marked, anaesthesia safety check,
pulse oximeter, known allergy, difficult airway, > 500 mL blood loss risk)
correspond to step 7 (airway exam) and step 10 (anaesthetic plan).

## Difficult Airway Society (DAS)

- DAS 2015 guidelines for management of unanticipated difficult intubation
  in adults. URL: https://das.uk.com/guidelines/das_intubation_guidelines

Step 7's difficult-airway predictors (Mallampati, thyromental distance,
inter-incisor gap, neck movement, mandibular protrusion) follow the DAS
pre-operative airway assessment recommendations.
