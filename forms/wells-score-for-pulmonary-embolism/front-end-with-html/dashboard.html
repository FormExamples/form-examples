The Wells PE score is a validated pre-test probability instrument (Wells 2000/2001; NICE NG158). This dashboard is a clinical-review tool and is not a diagnosis. A score of more than 4 means PE is likely and prompts an immediate CTPA; 4 or less means PE is unlikely and prompts a D-dimer test.
