# Clinical guideline alignment — substance use disorders

## NICE CG115 — Alcohol-use disorders: diagnosis, assessment and management of harmful drinking and alcohol dependence

Source: National Institute for Health and Care Excellence.
Guideline ID: **CG115** (published February 2011).
URL: https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/cg115

CG115 covers identification and management of harmful drinking and
alcohol dependence. Relevant alignments:

- **§1.2** — identification and assessment using AUDIT.
- **§1.3** — assisted alcohol withdrawal (community and inpatient).
- **§1.4** — pharmacological interventions (acamprosate, naltrexone,
  disulfiram, nalmefene).
- **§1.5** — psychological interventions (cognitive behavioural therapy,
  behavioural therapies, social network and environment-based
  interventions, behavioural couples therapy).

## NICE CG100 — Alcohol-use disorders: diagnosis and clinical management of alcohol-related physical complications

Source: NICE. Guideline ID: **CG100** (published June 2010).
URL: https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/cg100

CG100 covers acute alcohol withdrawal, Wernicke's encephalopathy, and
the management of alcohol-related liver disease, gastrointestinal
disorders, and pancreatitis.

## NICE PH24 — Alcohol-use disorders: prevention

Source: NICE. Guideline ID: **PH24** (published June 2010).
URL: https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/ph24

Covers screening (AUDIT, AUDIT-C, FAST) and brief intervention at
population level.

## NICE NG58 — Coexisting severe mental illness and substance misuse

Source: NICE. Guideline ID: **NG58** (published November 2016).
URL: https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/ng58

Covers assessment and management of dual diagnosis.

## NICE CG52 — Drug misuse in over 16s: opioid detoxification

Source: NICE. Guideline ID: **CG52** (published July 2007).
URL: https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/cg52

Covers opioid detoxification in community and inpatient settings.

## NICE CG51 — Drug misuse in over 16s: psychosocial interventions

Source: NICE. Guideline ID: **CG51** (published July 2007).
URL: https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/cg51

Covers psychosocial interventions for adults with drug misuse problems.

## Department of Health Drug Misuse and Dependence: UK Guidelines on Clinical Management (2017)

Source: Department of Health and Social Care, Public Health England,
Healthcare Improvement Scotland, Department of Health Northern Ireland,
Welsh Government (2017). *Drug Misuse and Dependence: UK Guidelines on
Clinical Management*. ("The Orange Book").
URL: https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/drug-misuse-and-dependence-uk-guidelines-on-clinical-management

The Orange Book is the principal UK clinical reference for opioid
substitution therapy, drug withdrawal, and harm reduction.

## DSM-5-TR

American Psychiatric Association (2022). DSM-5-TR.

Substance-related and addictive disorders are coded by substance:

- **Alcohol Use Disorder** — 303.90 / F10.20 (moderate); 303.90 / F10.21
  (severe).
- **Opioid Use Disorder** — 304.00 / F11.x.
- **Cannabis Use Disorder** — 305.20 / F12.x.
- **Stimulant Use Disorder** — 305.70 / F15.x (amphetamine), 305.60 /
  F14.x (cocaine).
- **Sedative, Hypnotic, or Anxiolytic Use Disorder** — 304.10 / F13.x.

Severity specifiers:
- Mild: 2–3 criteria.
- Moderate: 4–5 criteria.
- Severe: 6+ criteria.

## ICD-11

World Health Organization. ICD-11 MMS.
URL: https://icd.who.int/browse11/l-m/en

ICD-11 chapter codes:

- **6C40** — Disorders due to use of alcohol.
- **6C41** — Disorders due to use of cannabis.
- **6C42** — Disorders due to use of synthetic cannabinoids.
- **6C43** — Disorders due to use of opioids.
- **6C44** — Disorders due to use of sedatives, hypnotics or anxiolytics.
- **6C45** — Disorders due to use of cocaine.
- **6C46** — Disorders due to use of stimulants including amphetamines,
  methamphetamine or methcathinone.
- **6C47** — Disorders due to use of synthetic cathinones.
- **6C48** — Disorders due to use of caffeine.
- **6C49** — Disorders due to use of hallucinogens.
- **6C4A** — Disorders due to use of nicotine.
- **6C4B** — Disorders due to use of volatile inhalants.
- **6C4C** — Disorders due to use of MDMA or related drugs.

Each substance has sub-codes for harmful pattern of use, dependence,
intoxication, and withdrawal.

## Withdrawal-severity instruments referenced

- **CIWA-Ar** — Clinical Institute Withdrawal Assessment for Alcohol,
  revised (Sullivan et al., 1989). Standard alcohol-withdrawal severity
  scale; total > 8 indicates need for benzodiazepine treatment.
- **SADQ** — Severity of Alcohol Dependence Questionnaire (Stockwell et
  al., 1983). Total > 30 indicates severe dependence.
- **COWS** — Clinical Opiate Withdrawal Scale (Wesson & Ling, 2003).
- **SOWS** — Subjective Opiate Withdrawal Scale.
