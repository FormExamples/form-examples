# Clinical references

Authoritative guidance underpinning the four-axis grading engine.

## Appropriateness (ACR Appropriateness Criteria / RCR–RCPath)

Ordinal 1–9 scale in three bands: usually-appropriate (7–9),
may-be-appropriate (4–6), usually-not-appropriate (1–3). Mapped per
(indication × biopsy site × method). Image-guided / percutaneous needle biopsy
of a suspected mass is generally usually-appropriate once imaging has
characterised the lesion; biopsy without a clear indication or clinical
question is usually-not-appropriate.

## Periprocedural bleeding risk (BSG / ESGE & BSIR)

Diagnostic biopsy (e.g. endoscopic mucosal biopsy) is a **low bleeding-risk**
procedure; aspirin / clopidogrel / warfarin need not be withheld for it. Risk
rises with core-needle and solid-organ (liver, kidney) biopsy, with a known
bleeding disorder, raised INR, or low platelets. Management balances bleeding
risk against thromboembolic risk per the BSG/ESGE algorithm.

| Bleeding-risk band | Typical drivers | Indicative action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Low | diagnostic / superficial biopsy, no antithrombotics, normal INR & platelets | proceed |
| Moderate | antiplatelet on board, core-needle / solid-organ target, borderline platelets | review; consider withholding antiplatelet |
| High | anticoagulant on board, INR raised, platelets low, or bleeding disorder | correct coagulation / withhold-or-bridge per haematology before proceeding |

Indicative coagulation thresholds used by BSIR/CIRSE for percutaneous biopsy:
INR > ~1.5 and platelets < ~50 ×10⁹/L are commonly flagged for correction.

## Urgency / cancer pathway (NICE NG12)

Suspected-malignancy and cancer-staging indications make the request eligible
for the NICE NG12 **suspected-cancer two-week-wait** pathway.

| Tier | Target | Triggers |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Emergency | same day | unstable patient, critical airway / cord compression context |
| Two-week-wait | ≤ 14 days | suspected-malignancy / cancer-staging (NICE NG12) |
| Urgent | 24–72 h | suspected-infection, transplant monitoring, rapid clinical change |
| Routine | standard booking | inflammatory-disease, characterise-lesion, follow-up |
