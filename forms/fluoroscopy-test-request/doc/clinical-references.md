# Clinical references

Authoritative guidance underpinning the four-axis grading engine.

## Appropriateness (ACR Appropriateness Criteria / RCR iRefer)

Ordinal 1–9 scale in three bands: usually-appropriate (7–9),
may-be-appropriate (4–6), usually-not-appropriate (1–3). Mapped per
(indication × study type). For example, ACR Appropriateness Criteria®
*Dysphagia* rates a fluoroscopy biphasic esophagram / modified barium swallow
as usually appropriate for the initial imaging of oropharyngeal dysphagia.
RCR iRefer (*Making the best use of clinical radiology*) provides the
equivalent UK referral guidance.

## Safety and radiation dose

Every fluoroscopic exposure must be **justified** under IR(ME)R (Ionising
Radiation (Medical Exposure) Regulations 2017): the benefit must outweigh the
detriment of the ionising-radiation dose, and the justification is recorded on
the request.

| Driver | Effect on safety band |
| --- | --- |
| Pregnant / possible pregnancy + ionising study | contraindicated (defer / justify, consider alternative) |
| Contrast-media allergy | caution (premedication / alternative agent) |
| Aspiration risk | caution (favour water-soluble contrast) |
| Suspected perforation + barium requested | contraindicated (redirect to water-soluble contrast) |

### Study-type radiation-dose bands

| Study type | Dose band |
| --- | --- |
| Barium swallow / water-soluble contrast swallow | low |
| Barium meal | moderate |
| Barium follow-through | moderate |
| Barium enema | high |
| Defecating proctogram | moderate |
| Hysterosalpingogram | moderate |
| Micturating cystourethrogram | moderate |
| Arthrogram | low |
| Fluoroscopy-guided procedure | variable (screening-time dependent) |

### Contrast choice for suspected perforation

Free barium is highly irritating to the mediastinum and peritoneum, so barium
is **contraindicated** when perforation is suspected; a **water-soluble**
(iodinated) contrast study is used instead. A negative water-soluble study in a
high-suspicion case may be followed by barium, as water-soluble contrast can
miss a minority of perforations.

## Triage tiers

| Tier | Target | Triggers |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Emergency | same day | suspected perforation, acute obstruction, unstable inpatient |
| Urgent | 24–72 h / 1–2 weeks | progressive dysphagia, suspected malignancy, symptomatic obstruction |
| Routine | standard booking | reflux, chronic constipation, tubal-patency assessment, follow-up |
