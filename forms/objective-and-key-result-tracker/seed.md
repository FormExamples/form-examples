An Objective and Key Result (OKR) tracker is a tool used to implement the goal-setting framework made famous by companies like Google and Intel. It helps teams define Objectives (qualitative, inspirational goals) and track progress through Key Results (quantitative, measurable milestones). [1, 2, 3, 4] 
## Popular Tracking Methods
Depending on your team size and budget, you can use several types of trackers:

* Spreadsheet Templates (Free): Best for individuals or small teams starting out.
* [Google Sheets](https://workspace.google.com/products/sheets/): Offers dynamic dashboards and easy sharing. You can find free templates at Spreadsheet Point or through creators like [Jeff Gothelf](https://jeffgothelf.com/blog/okr-tracking-tool-free-template/).
   * [Microsoft Excel](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365/excel): Ideal for offline use with built-in calculation functions for scoring. [5, 6, 7, 8, 9] 
* Integrated Productivity Tools: Best for teams that want OKRs where they already work.
* [Notion](https://www.notion.com/): Features highly visual [OKR templates](https://www.notion.com/templates/objectives-and-key-results-okrs) that link high-level goals directly to daily tasks.
   * Monday.com: Uses a "two-board" setup to connect company objectives to specific execution tasks.
   * Slack: Provides [collaborative canvases](https://slack.com/templates/objective-key-results) to keep goals central to team conversations. [10, 11, 12, 13, 14] 
* Dedicated OKR Software: Best for large organizations requiring automation and alignment.
* Profit.co: A comprehensive platform focusing on strategic alignment and performance measurement.
   * Quantive Results: Offers real-time progress tracking and advanced performance analytics.
   * 15Five: Combines OKR tracking with continuous performance management and employee feedback. [15, 16, 17, 18, 19] 

## Key Features to Look For
When selecting or building a tracker, ensure it includes:

* Visual Status Indicators: Uses "RAG" (Red, Amber, Green) status to quickly show what is on track or at risk.
* Ownership Fields: Clearly designates a "Directly Responsible Individual" (DRI) for each key result to ensure accountability.
* Scoring Mechanism: Automatically calculates progress (often on a scale of 0.0 to 1.0) based on current vs. target values.
* Check-in History: A space for regular updates to provide context beyond just the numbers. [1, 5, 10, 13, 20, 21, 22]
