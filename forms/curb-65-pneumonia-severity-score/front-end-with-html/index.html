Complete the five CURB-65 criteria below for an adult (16 years or over) with community-acquired pneumonia. Each criterion scores 1 point when present, for a total of 0 to 5. The score assigns a mortality-risk band and a recommended site of care. Where serum urea is unavailable, the four-criterion CRB-65 primary-care variant (0-4) is computed instead. Answers are saved automatically in your browser as you go.

CURB-65 informs, but does not replace, clinical judgement. Use it alongside an assessment of oxygenation, comorbidity, and social circumstances. It is not validated for children, hospital-acquired or ventilator-associated pneumonia, or immunosuppressed patients.

The CURB-65 score is a validated clinical prediction rule (Lim et al., Thorax 2003) endorsed by the British Thoracic Society for stratifying community-acquired pneumonia mortality risk and guiding the site-of-care decision. Mortality estimates are indicative; local outcomes vary. This tool is for information only and is not a diagnosis; treat in line with BTS / NICE NG138 guidance and clinical judgement.
