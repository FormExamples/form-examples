The CURB-65 score is a validated British Thoracic Society instrument for community-acquired pneumonia. This dashboard is a clinical-review tool and is not a diagnosis. A score of 3 or more indicates high mortality risk; hospitalise and manage as severe CAP, and for scores 4-5 assess for intensive-care / HDU admission.
