Each section covers a different part of your pregnancy: demographics, pregnancy details, obstetric history, medical history, current symptoms, vital signs, laboratory results, lifestyle and nutrition, mental health, and birth plan preferences. All answers are saved automatically in your browser as you go.

If you are experiencing severe symptoms right now — heavy bleeding, severe abdominal pain, no fetal movement, sudden severe headache, or vision loss — please contact your maternity unit or emergency services immediately.

This prenatal questionnaire estimates pregnancy risk by summing weighted risk factors (range 0+, with 0–2 = low, 3–5 = moderate, 6–9 = high, 10+ = very high). It is a screening aid for information only and is not a diagnosis; please discuss your results with your midwife or obstetrician.
