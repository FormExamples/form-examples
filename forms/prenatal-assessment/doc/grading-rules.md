# Grading rules

The prenatal-assessment form is a generic antenatal risk-stratification
tool. It is closely related to the obstetrics-assessment form but is
intended for use earlier in pregnancy (booking visit and first trimester)
as a triage instrument to identify pregnancies needing earlier or more
intensive evaluation.

Primary guideline: NICE NG201 — *Antenatal care* (August 2021).
<https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/ng201>

## Three-tier risk band

| Band | Definition |
| ---- | ---------- |
| Low | Uncomplicated pregnancy, no medical or obstetric risk factors, normal screening and vital signs to date |
| Moderate | One or more risk factors that warrant additional monitoring (e.g. BMI 30–39.9, smoking, well-controlled chronic condition, previous PPH or shoulder dystocia) |
| High | Significant pre-existing or current pregnancy pathology (Type 1 or Type 2 diabetes, severe pre-eclampsia history, cardiac disease, BMI ≥ 40, multiple pregnancy, prior stillbirth, severe mental illness) |

These bands are presentational. NICE NG201 does not use "low / moderate /
high" terminology; the underlying risk factors are drawn from NG201 §1.1.

## Trigger lists

### High-risk (consultant-led)

- Pre-existing diabetes (Type 1 or Type 2) — NICE NG3
- Hypertensive disorder in previous pregnancy or chronic hypertension —
  NICE NG133
- Pre-eclampsia, eclampsia, or HELLP in previous pregnancy
- Cardiac, renal, or significant liver disease
- Autoimmune disease (SLE, antiphospholipid syndrome)
- Known thrombophilia or prior VTE (per RCOG GTG 37a)
- Multiple pregnancy (NICE NG137)
- BMI ≥ 40 at booking
- Previous stillbirth or neonatal death
- Recurrent miscarriage
- Severe mental illness; history of postpartum psychosis
- HIV / Hepatitis B / Hepatitis C
- Active substance misuse

### Moderate-risk (joint care)

- BMI 30–39.9 at booking
- Age ≥ 40 at expected date of delivery
- Smoking at booking
- Previous postpartum haemorrhage
- Previous preterm birth at 32–37 weeks
- IVF conception
- Asthma or thyroid disease (well-controlled)
- Vulnerable group (asylum seeker, homeless, age < 18)

## Mental-health screening

The form uses the Whooley two-question depression screener and the GAD-2
anxiety screener, with onward referral to EPDS or PHQ-9 if positive.

- Whooley M, Avins AL, Miranda J, Browner WS. *Case-finding instruments for
  depression: two questions are as good as many.* J Gen Intern Med.
  1997;12(7):439–445. DOI:
  <https://doi.org/10.1046/j.1525-1497.1997.00076.x>
- Kroenke K, Spitzer RL, Williams JBW, Monahan PO, Löwe B. *Anxiety
  disorders in primary care.* Ann Intern Med. 2007;146(5):317–325. DOI:
  <https://doi.org/10.7326/0003-4819-146-5-200703060-00004>

## Vital-signs flags

The form flags the following thresholds:

- BP ≥ 140/90 → repeat measurement; consider proteinuria; refer per NICE
  NG133
- BP ≥ 160/110 → urgent referral for assessment
- Tachycardia > 100 sustained → maternal investigation
- Proteinuria > 1+ on dipstick → quantify and refer per NICE NG133

## Booking-bloods and screening result flags

- Hb < 110 g/L at booking → iron studies and NICE-recommended iron therapy
- Hb < 105 g/L at 28 weeks → ditto
- Positive HBV / HCV / HIV → infectious diseases in pregnancy team referral
- Positive sickle/thalassaemia carrier → genetic counselling pathway
- D-positive father, D-negative mother → anti-D prophylaxis schedule

## Output

- Risk band (Low / Moderate / High)
- Specific trigger list
- Recommended care pathway and next-visit schedule per NICE NG201 §1.5
- Mental-health screening result and onward action
- Patient safety-net text covering reduced fetal movements, pre-eclampsia
  warning signs, and bleeding
- Structured PDF for the maternity record

## Relationship to the obstetrics-assessment form

The prenatal-assessment form is the booking-and-first-trimester instrument.
The obstetrics-assessment form covers ongoing antenatal care across all
trimesters with additional fetal-assessment fields. Most fields are shared;
choose the form that matches the encounter type.
