# Assessment protocol

## Intended use

A booking-visit antenatal questionnaire that captures pregnancy details,
obstetric and medical history, vital signs, laboratory and screening
results, and mental-health screening; applies NICE NG201 risk-stratification
logic; and produces a risk band with a recommended care pathway.

## Intended users

- Community midwives at the booking visit and subsequent antenatal visits
- GP antenatal clinic clinicians
- Triage midwives and obstetric trainees in antenatal day units

## Setting

UK community midwifery service, GP surgery, or hospital antenatal day unit.

## Workflow

1. **Demographics** — name, DOB, address, GP, ethnicity, language, country
   of birth, social support.
2. **Pregnancy details** — LMP, EDD, dating-scan confirmation,
   planned/unplanned, single vs multiple.
3. **Obstetric history** — gravidity, parity, prior outcomes including
   complications (pre-eclampsia, GDM, PPH, shoulder dystocia, perineal
   trauma, stillbirth, neonatal loss, miscarriages, terminations).
4. **Medical history** — cardiac, renal, hypertension, diabetes, thyroid,
   autoimmune, thrombophilia, mental health, HIV/HBV/HCV, surgical and
   anaesthetic history.
5. **Current symptoms** — bleeding, abdominal pain, headache, visual
   disturbance, reduced fetal movements (if applicable), hyperemesis.
6. **Vital signs** — BP, pulse, temperature, BMI from height and weight,
   urinalysis findings.
7. **Laboratory results** — booking blood group/antibodies, FBC, HbA1c,
   ferritin, HBV/HCV/HIV/syphilis, sickle/thalassaemia screen.
8. **Lifestyle and nutrition** — smoking, alcohol, drugs, diet, folic acid,
   vitamin D supplementation.
9. **Mental-health screening** — Whooley + GAD-2; positive screen prompts
   EPDS or PHQ-9 and referral pathway.
10. **Birth-plan preferences** — planned place of birth, pain relief,
    birth partner, cultural or religious considerations.

## Output

- Risk band (Low / Moderate / High)
- Care-pathway recommendation (midwife-led / joint / consultant-led)
- A flagged-issues list (specific triggers identified)
- Mental-health screening result and onward action
- Patient safety-net text covering reduced fetal movements, pre-eclampsia
  warning signs, and bleeding
- Structured PDF for the maternity record

## Safety-net behaviour

- Reduced fetal movements after 28 weeks → immediate same-day assessment
- BP ≥ 140/90 with proteinuria → urgent referral for pre-eclampsia
- Severe headache, visual disturbance, epigastric pain → urgent referral
- Bleeding in any trimester → urgent assessment
- Booking BMI ≥ 40 → consultant-led care plus anaesthetic referral
- Previous severe mental illness → perinatal mental-health team referral

## Out of scope

- Intrapartum care
- Detailed fetal medicine ultrasound reporting
- Cytogenetic interpretation of NIPT or amniocentesis results
- Postnatal care after hospital discharge
