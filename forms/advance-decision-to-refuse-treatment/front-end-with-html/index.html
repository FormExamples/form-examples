An Advance Decision to Refuse Treatment (ADRT) is a legal document under the UK Mental Capacity Act 2005 that lets you record, in advance, treatments you wish to refuse if you later lack the capacity to decide for yourself. All answers are saved automatically in your browser as you go.

Refusal of life-sustaining treatment carries additional legal requirements (written, signed, witnessed, with an explicit "even if life is at risk" statement). This wizard checks for those requirements and flags issues for clinical and legal review.
