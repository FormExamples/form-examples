Clinician referral wizard for an audiology / hearing examination. Record the requesting clinician, the patient, the requested test and laterality, the clinical indication and specific question, relevant history, symptoms and red flags, and the requested urgency. The engine computes a four-axis grade (appropriateness, urgency, request completeness, and clinical priority) plus a set of safety flags.

Appropriateness is anchored on the British Society of Audiology recommended procedures and NICE NG98 — there is no single published 1–9 audiology appropriateness score. This is clinical decision support for the audiology triage / vetting desk; the final triage and booking decision remains the responsibility of the vetting audiologist or ENT clinician.
