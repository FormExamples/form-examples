# Clinical references

Authoritative guidance underpinning the four-axis grading engine.

## Appropriateness (NICE NG136)

NICE NG136 *Hypertension in adults: diagnosis and management* is the primary
anchor for the appropriateness axis (ordinal 1–9, three bands).

| Clinic BP | Recommended action | Appropriateness direction |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ≥140/90 and <180/120 mmHg | Offer ABPM to confirm the diagnosis (HBPM if ABPM unsuitable/not tolerated) | usually-appropriate |
| Suspected white-coat or masked hypertension | ABPM / HBPM to characterize | usually-appropriate |
| Known hypertension, treatment monitoring | ABPM / HBPM as adjunct | may-be-appropriate |
| <140/90 mmHg, no labile / symptomatic indication | ABPM not routinely indicated | usually-not-appropriate |
| ≥180/120 mmHg | Same-day specialist review takes priority | escalate triage (see below) |

ABPM is the most accurate method for confirming a diagnosis of hypertension and
its use reduces unnecessary treatment in people without true hypertension.

## Suitability (oscillometric accuracy — BIHS)

The suitability axis reflects how reliably an automated oscillometric ABPM
device can measure this patient.

| Factor | Effect | Band direction |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Atrial fibrillation | Reduces single-reading oscillometric accuracy; repeated readings mitigate but ABPM averaging is affected | caution / limited |
| Large or non-standard arm size | Cuff-fit error | caution |
| No accuracy-affecting factor | Standard oscillometric ABPM reliable | ok |

BIHS guidance: oscillometric devices are less reliable for a single reading in
atrial fibrillation; auscultatory confirmation and/or repeated measurement is
advised, and AF should be recorded so the report is interpreted with caution.

## Triage tiers (NICE NG136 severe-BP escalation)

| Tier | Target | Triggers |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Emergency | same hour | clinic BP ≥180/120 with signs of accelerated hypertension or end-organ symptoms |
| Urgent | same day | clinic BP ≥180/120 mmHg (severe hypertension) — same-day specialist review |
| Routine | standard booking | clinic BP <180/120 mmHg requiring routine ABPM confirmation or monitoring |
