# Clinical guideline alignment

## WHO

- World Health Organization. *World Report on Hearing*. Geneva: WHO, 2021.
  <https://www.who.int/publications/i/item/9789240020481>
- WHO uses the 2021 grade definitions in its International Classification
  of Functioning, Disability and Health (ICF) and in the *Hearing Loss
  Grades and ICF* paper.
- WHO ICF: <https://www.who.int/standards/classifications/international-classification-of-functioning-disability-and-health>

## NICE

- NICE NG98 *Hearing loss in adults: assessment and management*.
  <https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/ng98>
- NICE TA566 *Cochlear implants for children and adults with severe to
  profound deafness*. <https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/ta566>
- NICE CG176 *Head injury: assessment and early management* (cross-ref for
  acute vestibular symptoms).

## BSA

- British Society of Audiology. *Recommended procedure: pure-tone air-conduction
  and bone-conduction threshold audiometry*. Reading: BSA, 2018.
  <https://www.thebsa.org.uk/resources/>
- British Society of Audiology. *Recommended procedure: tympanometry*. BSA, 2013.
- British Society of Audiology. *Recommended procedure: Dix-Hallpike and
  Epley manoeuvres*. BSA, 2016.

## AAO-HNS

- Chandrasekhar SS, Tsai Do BS, Schwartz SR, et al. *Clinical practice
  guideline: sudden hearing loss (update)*. Otolaryngol Head Neck Surg
  2019;161:S1-S45. PMID: 31369359
- Bhattacharyya N, Gubbels SP, Schwartz SR, et al. *Clinical practice
  guideline: benign paroxysmal positional vertigo (update)*. Otolaryngol
  Head Neck Surg 2017;156:S1-S47. PMID: 28248609
- Schwartz SR, Magit AE, Rosenfeld RM, et al. *Clinical practice guideline
  (update): earwax (cerumen impaction)*. Otolaryngol Head Neck Surg
  2017;156:S1-S29. PMID: 28045591

## Bárány Society / ICVD

- The international classification of vestibular disorders (ICVD) is
  curated by the **Committee for the Classification of Vestibular
  Disorders of the Bárány Society**.
- Bisdorff A, Von Brevern M, Lempert T, Newman-Toker DE. *Classification of
  vestibular symptoms: towards an international classification of
  vestibular disorders*. J Vestib Res 2009;19:1-13. PMID: 19893191
- Diagnostic criteria series in *Journal of Vestibular Research*: Ménière's
  disease (Lopez-Escamez 2015), vestibular migraine (Lempert 2012),
  persistent postural-perceptual dizziness (PPPD; Staab 2017), BPPV
  (von Brevern 2015).

## Stroke pathway

- Kattah JC et al. 2009 — HINTS exam for acute vestibular syndrome. PMID:
  19762709
- NICE NG128 *Stroke and transient ischaemic attack in over 16s*.
  <https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/ng128>
