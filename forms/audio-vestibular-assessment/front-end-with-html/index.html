WHO pure-tone audiometry grades classify hearing loss using the better-ear four-frequency average. The Dizziness Handicap Inventory (DHI) is a 25-item self-report instrument quantifying the impact of dizziness on daily life. This screening tool is for information only and is not a diagnosis; please discuss your results with your clinician.
