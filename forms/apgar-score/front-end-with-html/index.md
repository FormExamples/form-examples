# Apgar Score — HTML front-end (form + dashboard)

Consolidated single-directory HTML front-end: `index.html` is the single-page
Apgar wizard with a repeating-timepoint editor (score the five signs at 1, 5,
and 10+ minutes); `dashboard.html` is the clinician dashboard. Shared `css/`
and `js/` (the Apgar scoring engine in `js/{types,rules,grader,flags}.js`, the
apps in `js/form-app.js` + `js/dashboard-app.js`).
