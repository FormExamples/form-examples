The Apgar score is a description of a newborn's condition and trend, not a diagnosis or a prediction of long-term outcome. A total of 3 or below at any timepoint prompts active resuscitation; a 5-minute total below 7 prompts continued scoring. This dashboard is a clinical-review tool only.
