arc42 is a template for documenting software and system architectures. This single-page wizard walks through its 12 sections: introduction and goals, constraints, context and scope, solution strategy, building block view, runtime view, deployment view, crosscutting concepts, architectural decisions, quality requirements, risks and technical debt, and a summary with sign-off. The engine grades each section empty, partial, or complete, derives a composite maturity band (Draft / Reviewable / Ready / Mature) via a max-grade algorithm, and fires independent flags for architecturally critical omissions.

This is documentation-quality decision support aligned with ISO/IEC/IEEE 26514:2022. The final maturity call remains the responsibility of the reviewing architect, who may override the computed band in the final step.
