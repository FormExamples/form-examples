# Safety Case Notes

This form is **non-clinical**. It is a software architecture documentation
wizard, not a medical device; the EU MDR / IVDR MDCG 2019-11 classification
guidance, the UK Medical Devices Regulations 2002, and the UK MHRA Software as
a Medical Device guidance do not apply. The standard ISO/IEC/IEEE 26514:2022
(Design and development of information for users) is followed for the quality
and clarity of the rendered arc42 document and the user-facing wizard interface.
