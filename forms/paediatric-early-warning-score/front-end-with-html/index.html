Record a single set of paediatric observations below. First select the child's age band — it sets the normal respiratory-rate and heart-rate ranges, because a rate that is normal for a neonate is dangerously abnormal for a teenager. Each of the seven parameters scores 0 to 3 against the age-band normal. The engine aggregates the subscores to a total of 0 to 21, applies the single-parameter override (any parameter scoring 3), records nurse and parent/carer concern triggers, and returns the escalation band with the recommended monitoring frequency and escalation response. Answers are saved automatically in your browser as you go.

PEWS is a screening and monitoring aid, not a diagnosis, and does not replace clinical judgement. A low score does not exclude serious illness — documented nurse or parent/carer concern overrides the number.

The Paediatric Early Warning Score follows the concept of the national Paediatric Early Warning System (PEWS) chart (NHS England & RCPCH, 2023), which is age-banded. This scoring tool is for information only and is not a diagnosis; escalate and treat in line with local policy and clinical judgement. Documented clinician or parent/carer concern overrides the number.
