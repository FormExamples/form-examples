The Paediatric Early Warning Score is an age-banded track-and-trigger instrument following the national PEWS chart concept (NHS England & RCPCH, 2023). This dashboard is a clinical-review tool and is not a diagnosis. Any single parameter scoring 3, an aggregate of 4 or more, or documented nurse / parent concern prompts urgent clinical review.
