# Clinical references

Authoritative guidance underpinning the four-axis grading engine.

## Appropriateness (BTS flexible bronchoscopy)

The British Thoracic Society guideline for diagnostic flexible bronchoscopy in
adults sets out the indications, monitoring, sedation, and safety standards for
the procedure. Appropriateness is scored on an ordinal 1–9 scale in three bands:
usually-appropriate (7–9), may-be-appropriate (4–6), usually-not-appropriate
(1–3), mapped per (indication × procedure).

| Indication | Typical procedure | Band |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Suspected lung cancer / lung mass on imaging | flexible bronchoscopy ± EBUS | usually-appropriate |
| Mediastinal lymphadenopathy | EBUS-TBNA | usually-appropriate |
| Haemoptysis (unexplained, age 40+) | flexible bronchoscopy | usually-appropriate |
| Infection sampling | bronchoalveolar lavage | may-be-appropriate |
| Foreign body / stridor | rigid bronchoscopy | usually-appropriate |
| Persistent cough alone, normal imaging | flexible bronchoscopy | usually-not-appropriate |

## Cancer-pathway urgency (NICE NG12)

NICE NG12 *Suspected cancer: recognition and referral* drives the two-week-wait
suspected-cancer pathway for lung cancer.

- Refer people aged 40+ with **unexplained haemoptysis** urgently (appointment
  within 2 weeks).
- Offer an urgent chest X-ray (within 2 weeks) for people aged 40+ with two or
  more unexplained symptoms, or ever-smokers with one or more; refer on the
  two-week-wait pathway when chest X-ray findings suggest lung cancer.

| Tier | Target | Triggers |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Emergency | same hour / day | massive haemoptysis, haemodynamic instability, critical airway |
| Two-week-wait | within 14 days | suspected lung cancer, lung mass on imaging, unexplained haemoptysis age 40+ |
| Urgent | 24–72 h | symptomatic mass with clinical concern not meeting 2WW |
| Routine | standard booking | infection sampling, follow-up, stable indications |

## Pre-procedure risk (axis D)

Bleeding and hypoxia risk per BTS guidance:

- **Anticoagulant / antiplatelet:** warfarin, DOACs, clopidogrel and aspirin
  raise bleeding risk for biopsy; peri-procedure management (hold / bridge) is
  required.
- **Platelet count:** low platelets contraindicate or defer biopsy.
- **Hypoxia / oxygen dependence:** raises sedation and desaturation risk.
- **ASA grade:** ASA IV+ flags high anaesthetic risk.

Bands: low / moderate / high, with a recommended anticoagulant action.
