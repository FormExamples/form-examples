# Clinical guideline alignment — PTSD

## NICE NG116 — Post-traumatic stress disorder

Source: National Institute for Health and Care Excellence (NICE).
Guideline ID: **NG116** (published December 2018).
URL: https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/ng116

NG116 covers recognition, assessment, prevention and management of PTSD
across adults, children and young people. Relevant alignments:

- **§1.4 Assessment**: discuss the trauma in a way that is informed by
  the person's preference; consider use of validated screening tools.
- **§1.5 Trauma-focused interventions**: trauma-focused cognitive
  behavioural therapy (TF-CBT) and EMDR are first-line recommendations
  for PTSD ≥ 1 month after trauma.
- **§1.6 Pharmacological management**: SSRIs (sertraline, paroxetine) or
  venlafaxine where psychological therapy is declined or unavailable.

The form's PCL-5 step produces the severity input required to match the
patient to NG116's stepped-care interventions.

## DSM-5-TR

American Psychiatric Association (2022) *Diagnostic and Statistical
Manual of Mental Disorders, Fifth Edition, Text Revision (DSM-5-TR)*.
Code **309.81 / F43.10** — Posttraumatic Stress Disorder.

DSM-5-TR criteria for PTSD:

- **Criterion A**: exposure to actual or threatened death, serious
  injury, or sexual violence in one or more of four ways (directly
  experienced; witnessed; learned of happening to a close family member
  or friend; repeated or extreme exposure to aversive details).
- **Criterion B**: ≥ 1 intrusion symptom (recurrent memories, nightmares,
  flashbacks, intense psychological/physiological reactivity to cues).
- **Criterion C**: ≥ 1 avoidance symptom (avoidance of distressing
  memories / external reminders).
- **Criterion D**: ≥ 2 negative alterations in cognition and mood
  (amnesia, persistent negative beliefs, distorted blame, persistent
  negative emotional state, diminished interest, detachment, inability
  to experience positive emotions).
- **Criterion E**: ≥ 2 alterations in arousal and reactivity (irritable
  behaviour, reckless/self-destructive behaviour, hypervigilance,
  exaggerated startle, concentration problems, sleep disturbance).
- **Criterion F**: symptoms ≥ 1 month.
- **Criterion G**: clinically significant distress or impairment.
- **Criterion H**: not attributable to substance or medical condition.

Specifiers: with dissociative symptoms (depersonalization /
derealization); with delayed expression (≥ 6 months after the event).

## ICD-11

World Health Organization. ICD-11 MMS.
URL: https://icd.who.int/browse11/l-m/en

- **6B40** — Post-traumatic stress disorder.
- **6B41** — Complex post-traumatic stress disorder (Complex PTSD,
  CPTSD).

ICD-11 PTSD criteria are narrower than DSM-5 (three clusters: re-
experiencing in the present, avoidance, current persistent sense of
heightened threat). CPTSD adds three "disturbances in self-organization"
symptom clusters (affective dysregulation, negative self-concept,
disturbances in relationships) typically following prolonged or repeated
trauma. PCL-5 does not measure CPTSD; the International Trauma
Questionnaire (ITQ) is the validated ICD-11 measure.

## US Department of Veterans Affairs / Department of Defense

The VA/DoD *Clinical Practice Guideline for the Management of
Posttraumatic Stress Disorder and Acute Stress Disorder* (2023) is the
primary US reference. Available at:
https://www.healthquality.va.gov/guidelines/MH/ptsd/

The PCL-5 was developed and is curated by the National Center for PTSD
(US VA). Materials and scoring guidance:
https://www.ptsd.va.gov/professional/assessment/adult-sr/ptsd-checklist.asp
