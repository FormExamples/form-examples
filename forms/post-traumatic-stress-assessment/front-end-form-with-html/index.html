Below are 20 problems that people sometimes have in response to a very stressful experience. Please read each one carefully, then choose how much you have been bothered by that problem in the past month. All answers are saved automatically in your browser as you go.

If at any point you feel unsafe, are having thoughts of harming yourself, or are in crisis, please call your local emergency number, a trusted clinician, or a 24-hour crisis helpline immediately.

The PCL-5 is a 20-item self-report measure of the DSM-5 symptoms of PTSD. It is provided as an unauthenticated screen for information only and is not a diagnosis; please discuss your results with a qualified clinician. A structured clinical interview such as the CAPS-5 is required to confirm a diagnosis.
