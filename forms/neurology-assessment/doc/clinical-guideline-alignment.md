# Clinical guideline alignment — neurology assessment

## NICE NG128 — Stroke and transient ischaemic attack in over 16s

Source: National Institute for Health and Care Excellence (NICE).
Guideline ID: **NG128** (published May 2019; updated April 2022).
URL: https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/ng128

NG128 is the principal NICE guideline on acute assessment, imaging,
thrombolysis, thrombectomy, and secondary prevention for stroke and TIA.
Relevant alignments:

- **§1.3.1**: use the FAST tool (face, arm, speech, time) in
  pre-hospital settings; use a validated stroke recognition tool such as
  ROSIER in the emergency department.
- **§1.4.1–§1.4.5**: brain imaging immediately for patients eligible for
  thrombolysis or thrombectomy, on anticoagulants, with depressed
  consciousness or progressive symptoms.
- **§1.4.7–§1.4.16**: thrombolysis with alteplase within 4.5 hours; later
  windows with imaging selection.
- **§1.4.17–§1.4.22**: mechanical thrombectomy criteria.

The form's NIHSS step provides the standardized severity input for these
decision pathways.

## NICE NG137 — Epilepsies in children, young people and adults

Source: NICE. Guideline ID: **NG217** (published April 2022,
superseding CG137).
URL: https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/ng217

NG217 covers epilepsy diagnosis and management. The Seizure History step
prompts capture of:

- Seizure type (focal, generalized, unknown).
- Frequency and triggers.
- First-aid measures used.
- Antiseizure medication history.
- Last specialist review.

## NICE NG150 — Headaches

Source: NICE. Guideline ID: **CG150** (published September 2012,
updated December 2021).
URL: https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/cg150

CG150 covers headache diagnosis and management. The Headache Assessment
step prompts the clinician to record the features needed to differentiate:

- Tension-type headache.
- Migraine (with/without aura).
- Cluster headache.
- Medication-overuse headache.
- Red-flag features ("SNOOP4"): Systemic symptoms / secondary risk
  factors, Neurological symptoms, Onset (sudden / thunderclap), Older
  age, Pattern change, Postural, Precipitated by Valsalva, Papilloedema.

## Royal College of Physicians — National Clinical Guideline for Stroke

Source: Royal College of Physicians (2023). *National Clinical Guideline
for Stroke for the UK and Ireland*.
URL: https://www.strokeguideline.org

The 2023 RCP/Stroke Association guideline supersedes the 2016 5th edition
and is the principal UK clinical reference for stroke care. It includes
NIHSS-anchored decision points for thrombolysis and thrombectomy and is
endorsed by NHS England.
