# Clinical Guideline Alignment

This document explains how the WHO Emergency First Aid Form fields and
intervention checkboxes align with current first-aid evidence and
guideline statements.

## Guideline framework

The form is anchored on the WHO *Basic Emergency Care* (BEC) curriculum
(2018) and harmonised with:

- **International Liaison Committee on Resuscitation (ILCOR)** — 2020
  and 2022 Consensus on Science with Treatment Recommendations for First
  Aid.
- **European Resuscitation Council** — ERC Guidelines 2021: First Aid.
- **American Heart Association / American Red Cross** — 2020 Guidelines
  for First Aid (Part 17 of *Circulation* 2020;142 suppl 2).
- **Resuscitation Council UK** — First-aid sections of the 2021 RCUK
  guidelines.

## Field-by-field alignment

### Major bleeding (step 5)

| Intervention | Guideline alignment |
| --- | --- |
| Direct pressure | ERC 2021 Class I; ILCOR CoSTR; AHA Class 1 |
| Deep wound packing | ERC 2021 weak recommendation when commercial haemostatic dressing available; ILCOR weak recommendation |
| Tourniquet (life-threatening only, time noted) | ERC 2021 Class I for arterial life-threatening limb bleed; AHA Class 1; *Stop the Bleed* curriculum |
| Uterine massage (post-partum haemorrhage) | WHO BEC; WHO *Managing complications in pregnancy and childbirth* |

### Airway (step 6)

| Intervention | Guideline alignment |
| --- | --- |
| Neck immobilisation | WHO BEC trauma module; consistent with NEXUS / Canadian C-spine clinical decision rules for confirmed need |
| Head-tilt chin-lift | RCUK / AHA / ERC 2021 BLS standard for non-trauma |
| Jaw thrust | RCUK / AHA / ERC 2021 BLS standard when spinal injury suspected |
| Choking care | ERC 2021 Foreign Body Airway Obstruction algorithm |

### Breathing (step 7)

CFAR scope is intentionally narrow at this level — position of comfort
is the only intervention. Supplemental oxygen, BVM, and ventilation are
delegated to prehospital EMS in the WHO BEC framework.

### Circulation (step 8)

| Intervention | Guideline alignment |
| --- | --- |
| Pelvic binder | WHO BEC trauma module; consistent with NICE NG39 (Major Trauma 2016, updated 2023) |
| Minor bleeding control | ERC 2021 First Aid |
| Fracture care (splint in position found) | ERC 2021 First Aid |
| Oral hydration | ILCOR 2020 recommendation for non-trauma exertional dehydration |
| Left-lateral position | WHO BEC; ERC 2021 (recovery position equivalent) |

### Disability (step 9)

| Intervention | Guideline alignment |
| --- | --- |
| Spinal immobilisation | WHO BEC; NEXUS / Canadian C-spine clinical decision rules apply at clinician level |
| Glucose (oral, for conscious hypoglycaemic patient) | ERC 2021 First Aid: 15-20 g rapid-acting glucose |
| Seizure care (protect from injury; do not restrain) | ERC 2021 First Aid; NICE NG217 *Epilepsies in children, young people and adults*, 2022 |
| High-temperature care (active cooling for heat stroke) | ERC 2021 First Aid; AHA First Aid 2020 |
| Low-temperature care (passive rewarming, shelter) | ERC 2021 First Aid; *Wilderness Medical Society Guidelines* (Zafren K, et al. *Wilderness Environ Med.* 2014;25(4S):S66-85) |

### Exposure / Other (step 10)

| Intervention | Guideline alignment |
| --- | --- |
| Recovery position | RCUK / ERC 2021 / AHA 2020 First Aid |
| Burn care (cool burn 20 min running water) | British Burns Association consensus; ANZBA burns first aid; ILCOR 2020 weak recommendation |
| Wound care | ERC 2021 First Aid |
| Drowning care (rescue breaths first; standard CPR if no breathing) | ILCOR / RCUK / ERC 2021 — drowning is an asphyxial arrest |
| Snakebite care (immobilise limb, pressure bandage for elapid bites; WHO snakebite guidance) | WHO. *Guidelines for the management of snakebites*, 2nd edition. 2016. <https://iris.who.int/handle/10665/249547> |

## Pregnancy-specific items

The form captures pregnancy status explicitly because:

- Tourniquet siting is unchanged.
- Chest thrusts replace abdominal thrusts for choking.
- Left-lateral position relieves IVC compression after 20 weeks
  gestation.
- Uterine massage is an option for post-partum haemorrhage.

Reference: WHO. *Managing complications in pregnancy and childbirth.*
Geneva: WHO; 2017. ISBN 978-92-4-156549-3.
<https://www.who.int/publications/i/item/9789241565493>

## Precaution flags

The five precaution checkboxes (highly infectious disease, spinal
immobilisation, possible fracture, fall risk, altered mental status)
trigger downstream actions at the receiving facility per the WHO IMAI
referral framework.

Reference: WHO. *Integrated Management of Adolescent and Adult Illness
(IMAI).* <https://www.who.int/publications/i/item/9789241544016>
