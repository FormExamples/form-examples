This screening tool supports clinical evaluation but does not by itself determine final eligibility — every answer is reviewed by the donor assessment team and (for living donors in the UK) an Independent Assessor under the Human Tissue Act 2004.

Organ donation requires comprehensive evaluation of donor risk index, organ-specific suitability, and (for living donors) informed consent free of coercion. Absolute contraindications include HIV, active non-CNS malignancy, uncontrolled sepsis, and certain CJD risks. Expanded-criteria donors (older age, hypertension, modest organ dysfunction) may be conditionally suitable. Final eligibility is a clinical decision.
