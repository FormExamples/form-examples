# Cataract diagnostic evaluation

A cataract diagnostic evaluation is a comprehensive series of eye tests used to confirm the presence of a cataract, assess its severity, and plan potential surgical treatment. The process typically takes 1 to 2 hours because it requires pupil dilation and precise structural measurements of the eye. [1, 2, 3, 4, 5]

## Essential Diagnostic Tests

Eye specialists perform several non-invasive tests to evaluate how much the cataract is affecting your vision and daily life: [6, 7, 8]

- Visual Acuity Test: You will read letters from a standard [Snellen chart](https://www.medicalnewstoday.com/articles/cataract-diagnosis) to measure the clarity of your distance vision. [1, 9]
- Slit-Lamp Examination: The doctor uses a specialized microscope with a bright light to view the front structures of your eye. This allows them to examine the lens opacity, size, and type of cataract in detail. [1, 10, 11, 12, 13]
- Dilated Retinal Exam: Medicated eye drops expand your pupils so the ophthalmologist can clearly see the back of the eye. This is crucial to check the health of the retina and optic nerve, ruling out competing conditions like glaucoma or macular degeneration. [14, 15, 16, 17]
- Glare Testing: This measures how much your vision degrades when exposed to bright, direct light, simulating night driving or sunny conditions. [1, 15, 18, 19]
- Tonometry: This test measures your intraocular pressure (IOP) to screen for signs of elevated fluid pressure or glaucoma. [1, 9]

## Pre-Surgical Measurements (Biometry)

If surgery is indicated, advanced imaging tools are deployed to customize your treatment plan: [2, 6, 20]

- Optical Coherence Tomography (OCT): High-resolution cross-sectional scans mapping the layers of the retina.
- Corneal Topography: A non-contact test mapping the exact shape and curvature of your cornea.
- Axial Length Measurement: Ultrasound or optical devices measure the exact length of your eyeball. This metric is vital to determine the correct power for your intraocular lens (IOL) implant. [2, 14, 15, 21, 22]

## Important Practical Details

Because your eyes will be dilated during the appointment, your vision will remain blurry and sensitive to light for several hours. You should arrange for someone else to drive you home after the evaluation. Bring your current prescription glasses and a complete list of your current medications. [2, 15, 23, 24, 25]
