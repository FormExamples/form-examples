Clinician referral wizard for one or more coagulation / haemostasis tests. Record the requested tests, the clinical indication and details, the patient's anticoagulant and bleeding / thrombosis history, pre-analytical specimen handling, and the requested urgency. The engine computes a four-axis grade (appropriateness, pre-analytical specimen safety, request completeness, and triage priority) plus a set of safety flags.

This is clinical decision support for the coagulation-laboratory vetting desk. The final triage and processing decision remains the responsibility of the vetting clinician or haematology team.

Submit the form to see the vetting report.

Grading is aligned with British Society for Haematology (BSH) coagulation and thrombophilia-testing guidance, NICE NG158 venous thromboembolism diagnosis (2-level Wells / D-dimer), and BSH / CLSI pre-analytical guidance on sodium-citrate tube fill, 9:1 ratio, and analysis timing. This screening tool is for clinical decision support; the final triage and processing decision remains the responsibility of the vetting clinician.
