Bedside track-and-trigger score: systolic blood pressure, heart rate, respiratory rate, temperature, and AVPU, each scored 0-3.

Record the five routine physiological observations below for an adult inpatient (16 years or over). Each parameter is allocated a sub-score of 0 to 3 by the Subbe (2001) allocation table, and the sub-scores sum to an aggregate MEWS of 0 to 14. An aggregate of 5 or more, or any single parameter scoring 3, is a trigger for urgent medical review. Answers are saved automatically in your browser as you go.

MEWS is a track-and-trigger prompt, not a diagnosis. A low score does not exclude serious illness: re-score if the patient deteriorates, and use clinical judgement. Where a national standard applies, prefer NEWS2.

The Modified Early Warning Score (MEWS) is drawn from Subbe et al. (QJM 2001) and NICE CG50. This track-and-trigger tool is for information only and is not a diagnosis; escalate, monitor, and treat in line with local deterioration policy and clinical judgement. MEWS predates and is superseded by NEWS2 in the UK.
