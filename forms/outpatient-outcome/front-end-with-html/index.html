Structured outpatient outcome report. Record patient and encounter details, operational efficiency (referral-to-appointment wait time against the service target and the NHS Attendance Outcome code), the clinician-rated clinical outcome, three patient-reported outcome measures (EQ-5D-5L before/after, Global Rating of Change, and PROMIS Global Health v1.2), the Friends & Family Test patient-experience measure, a follow-up plan, and clinician sign-off. The engine grades four domains (Clinical, PROM, PREM, Operational) A–E and takes the overall Outpatient Outcome Composite Grade (OOCG) as the worst of the four, plus a set of flagged issues.

This is clinical decision support. The final clinical judgement and any onward action remain the responsibility of the reporting clinician.
