The Outpatient Outcome Composite Grade (OOCG) is advisory clinical decision support. Each domain (Clinical, PROM, PREM, Operational) is graded A–E by the shared engine and the overall grade is the worst of the four. The final clinical judgement remains the responsibility of the reporting clinician.
