# Medical Waiting List Card 

A practitioner fills in a form for a patient. The purpose is for the practitioner to put the patient on a medical waiting list.

This enables each patient to track their position on a medical waiting list. 

This provides a transparent, easy-to-use breakdown of hospital referrals, waiting times, and upcoming appointment dates.

Provides a description of what the patient is waiting to do, such as a medical procedure or medical specialty referral or upcoming appointment.

Records when the patient joined the list.
