# Clinical references

Authoritative guidance underpinning the four-axis grading engine.

## Appropriateness (ACR Appropriateness Criteria / RCR iRefer)

Ordinal 1–9 scale in three bands: usually-appropriate (7–9),
may-be-appropriate (4–6), usually-not-appropriate (1–3). Mapped per
(indication × scan type). For cardiac radionuclide imaging the
ACCF/ASNC/ACR/AHA/ASE/SCCT/SCMR/SNM Appropriate Use Criteria apply.

## Preparation & radiation safety (ARSAC / IR(ME)R / ICRP / EANM–SNMMI)

- **prep_safety_band:** ok / caution / contraindicated.
- **radiation_dose_band:** low / moderate / high (effective dose of the
  radiopharmaceutical).
- IR(ME)R requires the referrer to supply justification and the operator to
  establish pregnancy and breastfeeding status before exposure. Any woman of
  reproductive age with an overdue or missed period is treated as potentially
  pregnant unless excluded.
- Breastfeeding is interrupted for many radiopharmaceuticals; long-retention
  agents (e.g. I-131, I-125, Ga-67, Tl-201) may require 3 weeks' cessation,
  whereas most Tc-99m agents need only short interruption.
- A recent prior radionuclide study can leave residual activity that interferes
  with imaging and should be flagged.

## Triage tiers

| Tier | Target | Triggers |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Emergency | same day | acute PE on V/Q, acute cardiac event, unstable patient |
| Urgent | 24–72 h | suspected metastatic staging affecting urgent treatment |
| Routine | standard booking | function studies, surveillance, elective staging |
