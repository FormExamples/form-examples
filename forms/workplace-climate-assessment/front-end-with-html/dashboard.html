Likert-scale Workplace Climate Assessment covering nine climate domains (Leadership & Management, Psychological Safety, Inclusion & Belonging, Communication, Collaboration & Teamwork, Recognition & Reward, Wellbeing, Career Development, Overall Climate & Recommendations). Item responses are aggregated into a normalised 0-100 Workplace Climate Index and banded Thriving (85-100), Healthy (70-84), Developing (50-69), Strained (25-49), Critical (0-24). This dashboard supports leadership action planning around organisational culture, psychological safety, and inclusion; it is not a clinical instrument.
