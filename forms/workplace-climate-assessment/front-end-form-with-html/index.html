This assessment measures the climate of your workplace across ten short topics: leadership, psychological safety, inclusion and belonging, communication, collaboration, recognition, wellbeing, career development, and an overall climate view. Most items are 1-5 Likert statements (Strongly disagree through Strongly agree).

Your responses are anonymous. Please do not enter your name, your manager's name, or any details that could identify you or a colleague. Demographic questions in step 1 are deliberately broad (department, tenure band, hours band) so no individual can be singled out from the aggregate dashboard.

Answers are saved automatically in your browser as you go. Nothing leaves your device until you submit.

This assessment is a screening and engagement tool. The composite score and per-domain breakdown are designed to inform leadership, inclusion and wellbeing programmes — they are not a clinical assessment. Aggregate results should be discussed with your HR partner or organisational development team. If you are personally in distress, please speak to your GP or a confidential support line such as Samaritans on 116 123 (UK).
