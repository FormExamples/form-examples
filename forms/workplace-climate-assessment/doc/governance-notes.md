# Governance notes — workplace climate assessment

## Regulatory classification

The Workplace Climate Assessment is an HR / engagement instrument. It
is **not** a medical device.

Some items touch on psychosocial well-being (psychological safety,
voice). These should be treated with the same care as health data even
though they are not formally health data; aggregate-only reporting is
required.

## Equality Act 2010 alignment

Climate assessment is one of the tools used to satisfy the Public
Sector Equality Duty (Equality Act 2010 s.149) for in-scope bodies and,
for private-sector employers, to evidence proactive equality work.

The implementation aligns subscales to the nine protected
characteristics of the Act:

- age;
- disability;
- gender reassignment;
- marriage and civil partnership;
- pregnancy and maternity;
- race;
- religion or belief;
- sex;
- sexual orientation.

Equality Act 2010, s.4.

## Anonymisation strategy

- Opaque submission IDs.
- Cohort minimum 10 enforced at every demographic break-down.
- Free-text reviewed for personally identifying content before
  release.
- Trend reports across cycles re-anonymise (no longitudinal join key
  exists at the individual level).

## Audit and assurance

- Append-only audit log of survey design / distribution / close /
  release events.
- Soft delete only.
- Annual review of scale validity and licensing.

## Scale licensing

- Edmondson 1999 psychological safety: published with the article;
  freely usable for non-commercial research with citation.
- Colquitt 2001 justice scale: published with the article; freely
  usable for non-commercial research with citation.
- LMX-7: published in Graen & Uhl-Bien 1995; freely usable with
  citation.
- Mor Barak / Nishii inclusion items: published with the articles;
  citation required.

Commercial deployments should review licensing with their legal team
for each scale.

## Equality and accessibility

- WCAG 2.2 AA UI conformance.
- Multi-language item bundles.
- Reasonable adjustments (extended timing, alternative formats).

## Out of scope

- Individual performance management.
- Investigative procedures into specific complaints (these belong in
  HR case-management systems).
- Public reporting of identifiable team data.
