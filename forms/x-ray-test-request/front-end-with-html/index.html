Clinician referral wizard for a plain-radiograph (X-ray) examination. Record the requesting clinician and patient, the requested body region and laterality, the clinical indication and specific question, relevant history, radiation-safety details, the patient's mobility, and the requested urgency. The engine computes a four-axis grade (appropriateness, radiation safety, request completeness, and triage priority) plus a set of safety flags.

This is clinical decision support for the imaging vetting desk. The final triage and booking decision remains the responsibility of the vetting radiologist or radiographer.

Submit the form to see the vetting report.

Grading is aligned with the ACR Appropriateness Criteria, the Royal College of Radiologists iRefer guidelines, and the UK Ionising Radiation (Medical Exposure) Regulations 2017 (IR(ME)R). This screening tool is for clinical decision support; the final triage and booking decision remains the responsibility of the vetting clinician.
