Complete the seven report sections below for an ambulatory blood pressure (or home) monitoring study. The four-axis interpretation grade — classification, abnormality severity, report completeness, and follow-up urgency — is computed live as you type and finalised on submission. Answers are saved automatically in your browser as you go.

Thresholds follow NICE NG136 / BIHS / ESH ambulatory averages: daytime >= 135/85 mmHg confirms hypertension, and any average >= 150/95 mmHg (equivalent to clinic >= 180/120) is a severe-hypertension result that auto-escalates the follow-up urgency to a critical alert requiring same-day specialist review.

Submit the form to see the graded report.

Interpretation thresholds are drawn from NICE NG136 (Hypertension in adults: diagnosis and management), the British and Irish Hypertension Society (BIHS) guidance, and the European Society of Hypertension (ESH) ABPM position statements. This tool supports structured report entry and is not a diagnosis; interpret and act in line with local policy and clinical judgement.
