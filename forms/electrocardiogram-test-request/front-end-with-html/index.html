Clinician referral wizard for a cardiac electrocardiogram (ECG) examination. Record the requested ECG type, the clinical indication and specific question, relevant cardiac history and medications, symptoms and red flags, and the requested urgency. The engine computes a four-axis grade (appropriateness, urgency triage, request completeness, and clinical priority) plus a set of safety flags.

This is clinical decision support for the cardiac-physiology vetting desk. The final triage and booking decision remains the responsibility of the vetting physiologist or cardiologist. Suspected ACS or active chest pain implies an emergency, same-hour 12-lead ECG.

Submit the form to see the vetting report.

Grading is aligned with AHA/ACC ECG-use guidance, NICE CG95 (recent-onset chest pain), and the NICE NG185 acute coronary syndrome pathway. This screening tool is for clinical decision support; the final triage and booking decision remains the responsibility of the vetting clinician.
