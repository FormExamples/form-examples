# Clinical protocol

Operational protocol for the nutrition assessment, anchored on NICE CG32
and BAPEN MUST.

## Indications

- All adult hospital admissions (NICE QS24 statement 1).
- All adult care-home admissions (BAPEN guidance).
- Annual review in community / GP setting for adults at increased risk
  (older adults, chronic disease, low income).
- After any major weight change, surgery, or new GI diagnosis.

## Preconditions

- Recent (≤ 1 month) height and weight; or alternative measurements
  (MUAC, ulna length) per BAPEN if these cannot be measured.
- Recent biochemistry: U&E, LFTs, magnesium, phosphate, calcium,
  glucose / HbA1c, full blood count, CRP.
- Documented usual dietary intake and any recent intake changes.

## Step-by-step

1. **Demographics.** Identifiers; ethnicity; living situation.
2. **Anthropometric Measurements.** Height, weight, BMI, MUAC, waist
   circumference; usual weight; weight change over 3–6 months.
3. **Dietary History.** 24-hr recall or 3-day food diary; texture
   modifications; supplements; cultural / religious preferences.
4. **Nutritional Screening (MUST).** Compute the three MUST scores and
   total per BAPEN.
5. **Swallowing & Oral Health.** Dysphagia screen (e.g. 4QT, water
   swallow); dentition; oral hygiene.
6. **Gastrointestinal Function.** Appetite, nausea, vomiting, diarrhoea,
   constipation, abdominal pain, malabsorption symptoms.
7. **Food Allergies & Intolerances.** Confirmed allergies (IgE-mediated),
   intolerances, coeliac status, lactose intolerance.
8. **Nutritional Requirements.** Estimated energy (e.g. Henry / Schofield
   predictive equations), protein, fluid; adjustments for stress,
   activity, pregnancy / lactation.
9. **Current Nutritional Support.** Oral nutritional supplements (ONS),
   enteral tube feeding, parenteral nutrition; route; volume; tolerance.
10. **Care Plan & Monitoring.** Goals; review interval (weekly in
    hospital; monthly in care home; annually in community).

## Categorisation

The engine maps to four severity bands:

- **Low**: MUST 0 with no acute concerns; well-nourished.
- **Moderate**: MUST 1 or dietary inadequacy; at risk.
- **High**: MUST ≥ 2; malnourished or actively losing weight; requires
  dietitian.
- **Critical**: severe malnutrition with complications (e.g. refeeding-
  syndrome risk, severe sarcopenia, dysphagia with aspiration,
  uncontrolled GI losses, complications of bariatric surgery).

## Onward actions

- Dietitian referral: MUST ≥ 2; complex co-morbidity; ONS / EN / PN
  setup; refeeding-syndrome risk.
- Speech and language therapy: dysphagia or aspiration risk.
- Gastroenterology: unexplained weight loss, suspected malabsorption,
  coeliac evaluation.
- Mental-health team: suspected eating disorder.
- Social prescribing: food insecurity, isolation, low income.

## Refeeding syndrome (NICE CG32 §1.4)

- Identify risk using CG32 criteria (BMI, weight loss, intake history,
  pre-feeding electrolytes).
- Start at ≤ 10 kcal/kg/day (≤ 5 kcal/kg/day if extreme risk).
- Pabrinex / thiamine before feeding and for 10 days.
- Replenish potassium, phosphate, magnesium pre-feed and monitor daily for
  the first week.
- Slow build-up to full requirements over 4–7 days.
