# Malnutrition Universal Screening Tool (MUST)

MUST is a five-step screening tool developed by the British Association for
Parenteral and Enteral Nutrition (BAPEN) Malnutrition Advisory Group to
identify adults at risk of malnutrition or obesity in any care setting.

## The five steps

### Step 1 — BMI score

| BMI (kg/m²) | Score |
| --- | --- |
| > 20.0 (or > 30.0 if obese) | 0 |
| 18.5 – 20.0 | 1 |
| < 18.5 | 2 |

### Step 2 — Unplanned weight-loss score

| Weight loss in past 3–6 months | Score |
| --- | --- |
| < 5 % | 0 |
| 5–10 % | 1 |
| > 10 % | 2 |

### Step 3 — Acute disease effect score

| Criterion | Score |
| --- | --- |
| Patient is acutely ill *and* there has been or is likely to be no nutritional intake for > 5 days | 2 |
| Otherwise | 0 |

### Step 4 — Total score and risk category

Sum of steps 1–3:

| Total | Risk |
| --- | --- |
| 0 | Low |
| 1 | Medium |
| ≥ 2 | High |

### Step 5 — Management

- **Low risk**: routine clinical care; repeat screening per setting.
- **Medium risk**: observe; document dietary intake over 3 days; if
  intake adequate, low concern; if inadequate, treat.
- **High risk**: treat; refer to dietitian or nutrition support team;
  improve and increase overall nutritional intake; monitor and review.

Re-screening frequency:

- Hospital: weekly.
- Care homes: monthly.
- Community: annually (or earlier if clinically indicated).

## Alternative measurements when BMI cannot be measured

When height and/or weight cannot be measured, BAPEN provides alternative
measurements: mid-upper arm circumference (MUAC), ulna length for height,
recalled or reported weight, and visual assessment. The MUST Explanatory
Booklet contains the conversion tables.

## Use in this form

Step 4 of the nutrition assessment ("Nutritional Screening (MUST)")
implements the BAPEN-published scoring exactly. The form computes the
BMI score, weight-loss score, and acute-disease effect score and stores
the total and risk category.
