## GLIM diagnosis of malnutrition

GLIM (Global Leadership Initiative on Malnutrition) is the diagnostic
framework that complements MUST screening:

- Phenotypic criteria: weight loss, low BMI, reduced muscle mass.
- Aetiologic criteria: reduced food intake or assimilation, inflammation /
  disease burden.
- Diagnosis: ≥ 1 phenotypic *and* ≥ 1 aetiologic criterion.
- Severity grading by phenotypic criterion magnitude.

The form's "Nutritional Requirements" and "Dietary History" steps capture
the inputs needed for GLIM classification.

## Refeeding syndrome

NICE CG32 §1.4 specifies refeeding-syndrome risk criteria:

- BMI < 16 kg/m², or
- Unintentional weight loss > 15 % in past 3–6 months, or
- Little or no nutritional intake > 10 days, or
- Low potassium, phosphate, or magnesium before feeding;

OR ≥ 2 of:

- BMI < 18.5 kg/m².
- Unintentional weight loss > 10 % in past 3–6 months.
- Little or no nutritional intake > 5 days.
- History of alcohol misuse, drug-treated insulin / chemotherapy / antacid
  / diuretic use.

Patients meeting either set require slow refeeding (start ≤ 10
kcal/kg/day; very low ≤ 5 kcal/kg/day for extreme risk) and biochemical
monitoring.
