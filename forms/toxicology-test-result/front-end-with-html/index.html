A toxic level (paracetamol above the treatment line, or a toxic-level flag) auto-escalates the follow-up urgency to a critical alert with an urgent antidote action (start N-acetylcysteine / NAC for paracetamol). The paracetamol nomogram is only interpretable at 4 hours or more post-ingestion.

This structured toxicology report is aligned with TOXBASE / NPIS toxic-threshold guidance and the MHRA paracetamol treatment nomogram (single 100 mg/L treatment line, interpretable only at ≥ 4 hours post-ingestion), including the lithium (≥ 1.5 mmol/L), carboxyhaemoglobin (≥ 10%), and salicylate (≥ 300 mg/L) toxic thresholds. It is a reporting aid, not a diagnosis; interpret and act in line with local toxicology pathways and clinical judgement.
