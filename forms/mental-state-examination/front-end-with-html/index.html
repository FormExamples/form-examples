Record the mental state examination for an adult or older adolescent undergoing psychiatric assessment. Work through the seven ASEPTIC domains — Appearance and behaviour, Speech, Emotion, Perception, Thought, Insight, Cognition — plus context, identification, and formulation. Answers are saved automatically in your browser as you go.

This is a documentation and completeness instrument, not a numeric score. It grades the record Complete or Partial, computes a completeness percentage, and derives a risk indicator from the safety flags raised. The risk indicator is a documentation prompt to complete a full risk assessment where needed — it is not itself a diagnosis or a risk-management decision.

Submit the form to see the report.

The mental state examination follows the widely taught ASEPTIC mnemonic (Semple & Smyth, Oxford Handbook of Psychiatry; Trzepacz & Baker, The Psychiatric Mental Status Examination). This tool documents findings and prompts escalation; it is not a diagnosis and does not replace a full risk assessment or clinical judgement. Where a safety flag is raised, follow local safety policy and NICE NG225.
