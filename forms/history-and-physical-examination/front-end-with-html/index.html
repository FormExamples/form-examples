Complete the clerking sections below for an adult patient being admitted or clerked. This is a documentation / completeness form, not a scored instrument: it does not compute a diagnostic or risk score. Instead a completeness engine grades how thoroughly the clerking has been documented — Complete, Partial, or Incomplete — against ten required components, reports a completeness percentage, and raises safety flags. Answers are saved automatically in your browser as you go.

Allergy status must be explicitly documented (record "no known drug allergies" if none). Leaving allergy status undocumented, or recording neither an impression nor a plan, raises a blocking flag that forces an incomplete grade. A "Complete" grade means the document is well-formed, not that the clinical reasoning is correct.

Submit the form to see the report.

The H&P clerking template follows conventional clinical clerking structure (Macleod's Clinical Examination; Talley & O'Connor; Geeky Medics OSCE guides) with vital-sign reference ranges from the Royal College of Physicians NEWS2 (2017). This tool grades documentation completeness only and is not a diagnosis; use clinical judgement and escalate in line with local policy.
