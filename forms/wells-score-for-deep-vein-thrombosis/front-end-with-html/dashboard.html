The Wells DVT score is a validated pre-test probability instrument (Wells 1997/2003; NICE NG158). This dashboard is a clinical-review tool and is not a diagnosis. A score of 2 or more means DVT is likely and prompts a proximal leg vein ultrasound; 1 or less means DVT is unlikely and prompts a D-dimer test.
