# Clinical guideline alignment — psychology assessment

## NICE NG222 — Depression in adults

Source: NICE. Guideline ID: **NG222** (published June 2022, superseding
CG90 and CG91).
URL: https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/ng222

NG222 §1.3 — *Identification and assessment* — recommends use of a
validated severity measure. PHQ-9 is named explicitly; DASS-21 is a
valid alternative depression measure (DASS-21 Depression subscale).

## NICE CG113 — Generalized anxiety disorder and panic disorder

Source: NICE. Guideline ID: **CG113** (published January 2011, updated
July 2019).
URL: https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/cg113

CG113 §1.2 recommends GAD-7 for anxiety severity grading. DASS-21
Anxiety subscale measures physiological hyperarousal symptoms (e.g.
trembling, dry mouth, awareness of own heart rate, panic) and is
complementary; in clinical settings primarily concerned with stepped-
care GAD treatment, GAD-7 remains the recommended instrument.

## NICE NG225 — Self-harm

Source: NICE. Guideline ID: **NG225** (published September 2022).
URL: https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/ng225

NG225 informs the risk-screen item escalation logic. Any positive
endorsement of suicidality on the supplementary risk-screen item must be
followed by a clinical risk assessment.

## DSM-5-TR

American Psychiatric Association (2022). *Diagnostic and Statistical
Manual of Mental Disorders, Fifth Edition, Text Revision (DSM-5-TR)*.

DSM-5-TR codes commonly recorded after a DASS-21 screen:

- Major Depressive Disorder — 296.2x/F32.x (single episode); 296.3x/F33.x
  (recurrent).
- Generalized Anxiety Disorder — 300.02/F41.1.
- Panic Disorder — 300.01/F41.0.
- Adjustment Disorder — 309.x/F43.2x.
- Persistent Depressive Disorder (Dysthymia) — 300.4/F34.1.

DASS-21 scores contribute to the dimensional severity description but do
not establish diagnosis.

## ICD-11

World Health Organization. ICD-11 MMS.
URL: https://icd.who.int/browse11/l-m/en

Relevant codes:

- 6A70 — Single episode depressive disorder.
- 6A71 — Recurrent depressive disorder.
- 6B00 — Generalized anxiety disorder.
- 6B01 — Panic disorder.
- 6B43 — Adjustment disorder.

## Public-domain status

DASS-21 is in the public domain. The Psychology Foundation of Australia
provides the official scales, scoring sheets, and translations:
https://www2.psy.unsw.edu.au/dass/

This form's implementation mirrors the published scoring exactly.
