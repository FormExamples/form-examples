## Iron-deficiency anaemia

BSH 2020 guideline (DOI 10.1111/bjh.16221) and NICE CKS *Iron deficiency
anaemia*:

- Confirm with ferritin (< 30 µg/L in adults) or transferrin saturation
  (< 16 %).
- Investigate underlying cause: 2-week-wait endoscopy referral for adults
  ≥ 60 y, men ≥ 50 y, or postmenopausal women (NICE NG12).
- Treat with oral iron (e.g. ferrous sulfate 200 mg once daily; check
  haemoglobin response at 2–4 weeks).
- IV iron when oral intolerance / malabsorption / poor response.

## VTE and anticoagulation

NICE NG158:

- D-dimer thresholds; age-adjusted thresholds in ≥ 50 y.
- DVT / PE diagnostic pathways (Wells score; CTPA / V/Q).
- DOAC first-line in most settings; LMWH for cancer-associated VTE
  initial phase.

## Suspected haematological malignancy

NICE NG12 (suspected cancer) red flags:

- Unexplained lymphadenopathy lasting ≥ 6 weeks.
- Unexplained splenomegaly.
- Unexplained pancytopenia.
- Persistent unexplained eosinophilia > 1.5 × 10⁹/L.
- Blast cells on peripheral film → same-day haematology referral.
- Hyperviscosity symptoms with paraproteinaemia.

## Blood transfusion

NICE NG24:

- Restrictive transfusion threshold (Hb < 70 g/L) in stable adults
  without acute coronary syndrome; Hb < 80 g/L if ACS.
- Pre-transfusion patient consent and identification.
- Indication and consent recorded.
- Adverse reaction monitoring and SHOT reporting.
