# References

Annotated bibliography for the haematology assessment.

## Reference intervals and definitions

- WHO. *Guideline on haemoglobin cutoffs to define anaemia in
  individuals and populations.* WHO; 2024.
  <https://www.who.int/publications/i/item/9789240088542>
- Bain BJ, Bates I, Laffan MA. *Dacie and Lewis Practical Haematology.*
  12th ed. Edinburgh: Elsevier; 2017. ISBN: 9780702066962.

  syndromes and chronic myelomonocytic leukaemia.* Br J Haematol; BSH
  index. <https://b-s-h.org.uk/guidelines/>
