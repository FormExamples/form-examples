Clinician referral wizard for a cystoscopic examination of the lower urinary tract. Record the requesting clinician, patient identification, the requested procedure, the clinical indication and specific question, symptoms and red flags, bleeding-risk factors, and the requested urgency. The engine computes a four-axis grade (appropriateness, cancer-pathway urgency, request completeness, and pre-procedure risk) plus a set of safety flags.

This is clinical decision support for the urology vetting desk. The final triage and booking decision remains the responsibility of the vetting urologist or nurse cystoscopist.
