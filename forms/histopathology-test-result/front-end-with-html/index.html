The report is graded on four axes per RCPath structured-reporting standards. An unexpected malignancy (no linked originating request) or an involved resection margin is a critical finding that auto-escalates the follow-up urgency to a critical alert; confirmed malignancy drives urgent cancer-MDT discussion per the 2-week-wait pathway.

This structured histopathology report is aligned with Royal College of Pathologists (RCPath) cancer-dataset and structured-reporting standards, including UICC/AJCC TNM 8th-edition pathological staging, histological grading, resection-margin status, and SNOMED CT coding. It is a reporting aid, not a diagnosis; interpret and act in line with local cancer pathways, MDT processes, and clinical judgement.
