## Instrument licensing

| Instrument | Licence |
| --- | --- |
| PAR-Q+ | Free to reproduce for use by qualified exercise professionals and the public, unaltered, with attribution to the PAR-Q+ Collaboration. <http://eparmedx.com/> |
| AUDIT-C | World Health Organization AUDIT instrument; freely usable in clinical and research settings with citation to Bush et al. 1998. |

Confirm the licence position with the instrument owner before any commercial
distribution of a derived product.
