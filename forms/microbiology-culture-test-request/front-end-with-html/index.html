Clinician referral wizard for a microbiology specimen culture (MC&S). Record the specimen and site, the requested tests, the clinical indication and details, pre-analytical specimen handling, patient-safety factors, and the requested urgency. The engine computes a four-axis grade (appropriateness, pre-analytical specimen safety, request completeness, and triage priority) plus a set of safety flags.

This is clinical decision support for the microbiology vetting desk, aligned with the UKHSA / RCPath Standards for Microbiology Investigations (SMI) and NICE NG51 (suspected sepsis). The final triage and acceptance decision remains the responsibility of the vetting microbiologist.
