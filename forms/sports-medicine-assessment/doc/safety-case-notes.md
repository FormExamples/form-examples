# Safety Case Notes — Sports Medicine Assessment

## Regulatory framework

- DCB 0129 / DCB 0160 (NHS).
  https://digital.nhs.uk/services/clinical-safety/clinical-risk-management-standards
- MDCG 2019-11 Rev.1.
  https://health.ec.europa.eu/document/download/b45335c5-1679-4c71-a91c-fc7a4d37f12b_en
- UK Medical Devices Regulations 2002.
- UK MHRA *Software and AI as a Medical Device*.
  https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/software-and-artificial-intelligence-ai-as-a-medical-device
- ISO/IEC/IEEE 26514:2022.

## Device classification position

The form supports a clinician's clearance decision; final judgment remains
with the clinician. Under MDCG 2019-11 the device is software for
information used in management of a health condition.

## Hazards and mitigations

| Hazard | Mitigation |
| --- | --- |
| Missed sudden-cardiac-death precursor | Step 6 enforces all 14 AHA items; family-history red flags (premature SCD, HCM, ARVC, Marfan, LQTS) escalate to "Not Cleared Pending Further Evaluation" |
| Premature return after concussion | Step 8 SCAT6 baseline; symptomatic or abnormal post-injury record blocks "Cleared" pathway |
| Under-recognized RED-S in female athletes | Step 5 mandatory for menstrual / energy availability history; abnormal pattern triggers referral prompt |
| Heat-illness risk overlooked | Step 9 includes hot-environment sport-specific risk factor capture |
| Asthma / exercise-induced bronchoconstriction missed | Step 3 captures peak flow, prior exercise-induced symptoms |
| Eating disorder missed | Step 5 captures structured RED-S items; positive screen triggers safeguarding referral |
| Sickle cell trait undocumented for high-altitude / strenuous endurance | Step 3 family/personal history captures trait status |
| Loss of athlete privacy on safeguarding disclosures | Records segregated; access role-restricted |

## Vulnerable group safeguards

- Athletes under 16: parental/guardian consent required for clearance
  decisions; minor-specific safeguarding pathway (UK: NSPCC Child
  Protection in Sport Unit, https://thecpsu.org.uk/).
- Para-athletes: clearance category accommodates impairment-specific
  considerations; this form does not replace classification.

## Anti-doping

The form does not record therapeutic-use exemptions but signposts the
World Anti-Doping Agency (WADA) prohibited list and UK Anti-Doping (UKAD)
TUE process when clearance includes prescribed medication.

- WADA Code and Prohibited List: https://www.wada-ama.org/
- UK Anti-Doping: https://www.ukad.org.uk/

## Information governance

- UK GDPR / DPA 2018.
- Athlete records contain sensitive personal data (mental health, sexual
  health where part of RED-S, family genetic history); access tightly
  controlled.
- Sport-organization data sharing requires explicit consent.

## Audit and traceability

- Clearance decisions are timestamped and attributed.
- Both computed (rule-derived) and clinician-confirmed clearance category
  stored; PDF report shows both.
