# Grading Rules — Sports Medicine Assessment

## Pre-Participation Physical Evaluation (PPE) monograph

The primary instrument is the *PPE — Preparticipation Physical Evaluation*
(5th edition, 2019), jointly published by the AAP, AAFP, ACSM, AMSSM,
AOSSM, and AOASM.

- Publisher index: https://www.aafp.org/family-physician/patient-care/care-resources/sports-medicine/preparticipation-physical-evaluation.html
- AAP publication record:
  https://publications.aap.org/aapbooks/book/520/Preparticipation-Physical-Evaluation
- ACSM resource page:
  https://www.acsm.org/education-resources/books/preparticipation-physical-evaluation

The PPE 5 monograph specifies the four clearance categories used by this
form:

| Category | Definition |
| --- | --- |
| Cleared | No concerns; full participation in chosen sport |
| Cleared with Conditions | Participation permitted with named restrictions or accommodations |
| Not Cleared Pending Further Evaluation | Temporary hold pending investigation (e.g., abnormal cardiac finding, concussion not fully resolved) |
| Not Cleared for Sport | Significant condition contraindicating participation in the chosen sport |

## 14-point American Heart Association cardiovascular screen

The cardiovascular screening section uses the AHA 14-element preparticipation
screening framework.

- Maron BJ, Friedman RA, Kligfield P, et al. *Assessment of the 12-lead
  electrocardiogram as a screening test for detection of cardiovascular
  disease in healthy general populations of young people (12-25 years of
  age): a scientific statement from the AHA and ACC.* Circulation.
  2014;130(15):1303-1334. DOI: 10.1161/CIR.0000000000000025.
- AHA cardiovascular preparticipation screening recommendations index:
  https://professional.heart.org/

The 14 elements: personal history (7 items: exertional chest pain,
unexplained syncope, excessive exertional dyspnoea, prior heart murmur,
elevated systemic blood pressure, prior restriction from sports, prior
testing of the heart); family history (3 items: premature death, disability
from heart disease, specific knowledge of certain cardiac conditions);
physical examination (4 items: heart murmur, femoral pulses, Marfan
stigmata, brachial-artery blood pressure).

## SCAT6 — Sport Concussion Assessment Tool

For neurological / concussion baseline (step 8):

- McCrory P, Meeuwisse W, Dvořák J, et al. *Consensus statement on
  concussion in sport — the 6th International Conference on Concussion
  in Sport.* Br J Sports Med. 2023;57(11):695-711.
  DOI: 10.1136/bjsports-2023-106898.
- SCAT6 tool: https://bjsm.bmj.com/content/57/11/622

SCAT6 is licensed for clinical and research use under the BJSM consensus
publication; the form records its score components rather than embedding
the questionnaire verbatim.

## RED-S (Relative Energy Deficiency in Sport)

For step 5 (menstrual history / RED-S screening, where applicable):

  Sport (REDs).* Br J Sports Med. 2023;57(17):1073-1098.
  DOI: 10.1136/bjsports-2023-106994.

## Sudden cardiac death — UK-specific screening

The UK NHS / Royal College of Physicians position on cardiac screening
for athletes is more selective than the AHA 14-point screen. The form
captures all 14 AHA items but flags family-history red flags consistent
with UK Cardiac Society and Cardiac Risk in the Young (CRY) guidance.

- Cardiac Risk in the Young (CRY) index: https://www.c-r-y.org.uk/
