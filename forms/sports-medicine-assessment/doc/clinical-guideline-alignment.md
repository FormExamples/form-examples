# Clinical Guideline Alignment — Sports Medicine Assessment

## PPE 5th edition

- Publisher: AAP/AAFP/ACSM/AMSSM/AOSSM/AOASM.
- AAP publication record:
  https://publications.aap.org/aapbooks/book/520/Preparticipation-Physical-Evaluation
- AAFP overview:
  https://www.aafp.org/family-physician/patient-care/care-resources/sports-medicine/preparticipation-physical-evaluation.html

The PPE 5 monograph organizes preparticipation evaluation into history,
examination, and clearance. The form's step ordering matches the PPE 5
chapter ordering.

## AHA 14-point preparticipation screen

- AHA preparticipation screening scientific statement:
  https://professional.heart.org/en/science-news/preparticipation-cardiovascular-screening-of-young-competitive-athletes

Step 6 implements the 14-point cardiovascular screen.

## Concussion in Sport Group (CISG) — Amsterdam 2023 consensus

- McCrory P, Meeuwisse W, Dvořák J, et al. *Consensus statement on
  concussion in sport — the 6th International Conference on Concussion
  in Sport.* Br J Sports Med. 2023;57(11):695-711.
  DOI: 10.1136/bjsports-2023-106898.
- BJSM concussion-in-sport index:
  https://bjsm.bmj.com/pages/concussion-in-sport-2023

SCAT6 baseline informs return-to-play after concussion (step 8).

## IOC REDs consensus

- Mountjoy M, Ackerman KE, Bailey DM, et al. *2023 IOC consensus statement
  on Relative Energy Deficiency in Sport (REDs).* Br J Sports Med.
  2023;57(17):1073-1098. DOI: 10.1136/bjsports-2023-106994.
