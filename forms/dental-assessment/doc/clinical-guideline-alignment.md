# Clinical Guideline Alignment

The dental assessment uses DMFT (caries experience), BPE (periodontal
screening), and a standard intraoral and radiographic examination. The
following authoritative sources informed the field set.

## WHO

- World Health Organization *Oral Health Surveys: Basic Methods*, 5th
  edition, 2013.
  https://www.who.int/publications/i/item/9789241548649
- WHO Oral Health Country/Area Profile Programme.
  https://capp.mau.se

## NICE guidance

| Code | Title | URL |

| CG19 | Dental recall: recall interval between routine dental examinations | https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/cg19 |
| NICE QS22 | Dental checks: intervals between oral health reviews | https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/qs22 (informational only) |
| CKS | Clinical Knowledge Summaries — Dental health topics | https://cks.nice.org.uk/specialities/oral-and-dental |

## SDCEP (Scottish Dental Clinical Effectiveness Programme)

SDCEP publishes UK dental practice guidance freely:
https://www.sdcep.org.uk

Examples relevant to this assessment:

- SDCEP *Prevention and Management of Dental Caries in Children.*
- SDCEP *Drug Prescribing for Dentistry.*
- SDCEP *Management of Acute Dental Problems.*

## British Society of Periodontology and Implant Dentistry (BSP)

- BSP BPE 2019 guideline.
  https://www.bsperio.org.uk/professionals/bpe.html
- BSP guideline series on the implementation of the European S3-level
  periodontal treatment guidelines.

## British Dental Association (BDA)

- BDA practice guidance index.
  https://www.bda.org/advice
- BDA radiation protection and IRMER guidance for dental radiography.

## General Dental Council (GDC)

GDC publishes the professional standards expected of UK-registered
dental professionals:

- GDC *Standards for the Dental Team* (2013, with subsequent updates).
  https://www.gdc-uk.org/standards-guidance/standards-and-guidance/standards-for-the-dental-team

Step-level safeguarding and consent fields on this form are aligned to
the GDC nine principles.

## AAP / EFP 2018 classification

- Tonetti MS, Greenwell H, Kornman KS. *Staging and grading of
  periodontitis.* Journal of Periodontology 2018; 89 Suppl 1: S159-S172.
  PMID: 29926952.
- Joint AAP / EFP Workshop classification papers (full series in
  J Periodontol 2018 Suppl 1).

## What this engine does NOT compute

- We do not compute DMFS (surface-level index).
- We do not compute the Plaque Index or Gingival Index numerical scores.
- We do not auto-derive the 2018 stage and grade of periodontitis.
- We do not generate a radiographic report.

## Patient self-report caveats

DMFT and BPE are clinician examination fields. Step 3 (Dental History)
is patient self-report; the printed report makes the data provenance
explicit per field.
