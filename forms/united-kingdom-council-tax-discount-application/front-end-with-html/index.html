Complete the six sections below to apply for a Council Tax discount, exemption or reduction. The engine does not award anything. It checks that the application is complete and consistent with the public GOV.UK rules — for example that the number of counted adults fits the discount, that students and severely mentally impaired people have their evidence, and that a disabled band reduction has a qualifying adaptation — and it classifies the application as likely eligible, incomplete, or likely ineligible, then raises flags. Answers are saved automatically in your browser as you go.

Eligibility is decided by the billing authority, your local council, not by this form. A “likely eligible” result is not a decision or a promise of any discount. You must tell the council within 21 days of a change in your circumstances, and giving false information can lead to a penalty and prosecution.

Council Tax discounts in England are governed by the Local Government Finance Act 1992 and its regulations, and explained on GOV.UK. This tool pre-checks an application and makes no decision: eligibility is decided by the billing authority, which may ask for more evidence and applies its own Council Tax Reduction scheme. Tell the council within 21 days of any change in your circumstances.
