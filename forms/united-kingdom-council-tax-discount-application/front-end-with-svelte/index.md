# United Kingdom Council Tax Discount Application — SvelteKit front-end

SvelteKit front-end for the United Kingdom Council Tax Discount Application: a single-page wizard with a live
eligibility check, a dashboard, and a report with a PDF download. The engine is
ported from the HTML front-end (`../front-end-with-html/js/`). Eligibility is
decided by the billing authority.

See [`AGENTS.md`](AGENTS.md) and [`plan.md`](plan.md).
