Complete the weighted risk-factor checklist below for an adult (16 years or over) surgical or medical inpatient. Each factor present contributes its fixed weight (1, 2, 3, or 5 points) and age contributes through a single band; the total is the Caprini score. The score maps to a risk band (very low 0-1, low 2, moderate 3-4, high 5+) and a recommended prophylaxis strategy. Answers are saved automatically in your browser as you go.

A high score prompts thromboprophylaxis after a bleeding-risk check; it is not a substitute for clinical judgement. Where the bleeding risk is high, mechanical prophylaxis substitutes for pharmacological prophylaxis until the bleeding risk resolves. Re-assess after surgery or any change in clinical status.

The Caprini Risk Assessment Model (Caprini, Disease-a-Month 2005) is endorsed for surgical and hospitalised medical patients and underpins the ACCP (Gould et al., Chest 2012) and NICE NG89 guidance on VTE prophylaxis. This tool is a clinical decision-support aid for information only and is not a diagnosis; prescribe and treat in line with local policy and clinical judgement.
