A pathogenic or likely-pathogenic actionable variant classifies the result as critical, auto-escalates the follow-up urgency to a critical alert, and raises the critical-result-alert and pathogenic-variant-found flags regardless of the other axes. A variant of uncertain significance (VUS) is interpreted as inconclusive; do not act clinically on a VUS alone.

This structured genomic report is aligned with ACMG/AMP and ACGS variant-classification and genomic-reporting guidance, including the five-tier classification (pathogenic, likely-pathogenic, uncertain significance, likely-benign, benign), secondary-findings review, and cascade-testing of at-risk relatives. It is a reporting aid, not a diagnosis; interpret and act in line with local genomics pathways and clinical judgement.
