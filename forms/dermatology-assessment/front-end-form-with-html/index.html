Please answer the questions below about your skin condition. The central scoring section is the validated 10-question Dermatology Life Quality Index (DLQI), which asks about the impact of your skin over the last week. All answers are saved automatically in your browser as you go.

If you have a rapidly spreading rash with fever, blistering of the skin, mucosal involvement (mouth, eyes, genitals), or signs of a severe allergic reaction (swelling of the face/throat, difficulty breathing), seek urgent medical care immediately.

The Dermatology Life Quality Index (DLQI) is a validated 10-item questionnaire for measuring the impact of skin disease on quality of life. This screening tool is for information only and is not a diagnosis; please discuss your results with your clinician.
