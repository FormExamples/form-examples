The Glasgow Coma Scale is a validated instrument for assessing impaired consciousness. This dashboard is a clinical-review tool and is not a diagnosis. A defined total of 8 or less signals coma and airway risk; report the E/V/M breakdown alongside the total, and escalate per local head-injury policy.
