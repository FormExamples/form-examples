Glasgow Coma Scale (GCS)

Structured assessment of consciousness: Eye opening (E), Verbal response (V), and Motor response (M), summed to a total of 3-15.

Rate the best response observed for each of the three components, using the 2014 Glasgow structured approach (check → observe → stimulate → rate). The engine sums Eye + Verbal + Motor into the total GCS (3-15), reports the E/V/M breakdown and a severity band, and derives the secondary GCS-Pupils (GCS-P) score. Answers are saved automatically in your browser as you go.

Always record the three components separately — the breakdown carries more information than the total alone. When a component cannot be tested (for example a swollen-shut eye, an intubated airway, or a paralysed limb), select Not testable (NT): the numeric total is then left undefined rather than substituting an assumed value. The GCS supports triage and monitoring but is not a diagnosis.

The Glasgow Coma Scale (Teasdale & Jennett, Lancet 1974; Teasdale et al., Lancet Neurology 2014) and the GCS-Pupils score (Brennan et al., J Neurosurg 2018) are validated instruments for assessing impaired consciousness. This tool is for information only and is not a diagnosis; escalate and treat in line with local head-injury policy (NICE NG232) and clinical judgement.
