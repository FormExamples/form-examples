# Guideline alignment

How the Heart Health Check form maps to NICE and ESC guidance.

## Primary guidance

- NICE CG181 *Cardiovascular disease: risk assessment and reduction,
  including lipid modification.*
  <https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/cg181>
- NICE NG196 *Atrial fibrillation: diagnosis and management.*
  <https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/ng196>
- NICE NG136 *Hypertension in adults: diagnosis and management.*
  <https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/ng136>
- NICE NG28 *Type 2 diabetes in adults: management.*
  <https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/ng28>
- NICE QS167 *Cardiovascular disease prevention quality standard.*
  <https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/qs167>
- NHS Health Check programme guidance.
  <https://www.gov.uk/government/collections/nhs-health-check-detailed-information>

## Step-to-guidance cross-walk

| Step | Topic | Aligned guidance |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Patient Information | NHS Health Check eligibility (age 40-74) |
| 2 | Demographics & Ethnicity | QRISK3 (Hippisley-Cox 2017); ONS 2021 ethnicity categories |
| 3 | Blood Pressure | NICE NG136 §1.2 (out-of-office confirmation for elevated readings) |
| 4 | Cholesterol | NICE CG181 §1.1 (lipid profile measurement) |
| 5 | Medical Conditions | QRISK3 predictors; NICE NG196 (AF); CG181 (CKD) |
| 6 | Family History | QRISK3 predictor (first-degree relative < 60 y) |
| 7 | Smoking & Alcohol | NICE NG209 *Tobacco*; NICE CG115 *Alcohol use disorders* |
| 8 | Physical Activity & Diet | NICE PH44 (Physical activity brief advice); CMO physical activity guidelines |
| 9 | Body Measurements | NICE CG189 *Obesity: identification, assessment and management* |
| 10 | Review & Calculate | NICE CG181 §1.3 (risk discussion and statin offer) |

## NICE CG181 statin thresholds

- Primary prevention, QRISK3 ≥ 10 % → offer atorvastatin 20 mg after shared
  decision-making (CG181 §1.3.7).
- Secondary prevention (established CVD) → atorvastatin 80 mg (CG181 §1.3.8).
- Familial hypercholesterolaemia → manage per NICE CG71.
- Type 1 diabetes age ≥ 40 y or duration ≥ 10 y → offer atorvastatin 20 mg
  (NICE NG17).
- CKD eGFR < 60 mL/min/1.73 m² → offer atorvastatin 20 mg (CG181 §1.3.9).

## Hypertension thresholds (NICE NG136)

- Clinic BP ≥ 140/90 mmHg with confirmed ABPM / HBPM ≥ 135/85 → Stage 1
  hypertension.
- Clinic BP ≥ 160/100 mmHg with confirmed ABPM / HBPM ≥ 150/95 → Stage 2.
- Clinic SBP ≥ 180 mmHg or DBP ≥ 120 mmHg → same-day review (severe).

The form's FLAG-BP-001 / 002 rules surface these thresholds.

## Atrial fibrillation (NICE NG196)

- New AF detected → CHA2DS2-VASc and ORBIT scoring; anticoagulation offered
  per shared decision (NG196 §1.5).
