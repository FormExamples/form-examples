# References

Annotated bibliography for the Heart Health Check form.

- NHS Health Check programme overview.
  <https://www.healthcheck.nhs.uk/>
- *NHS Health Check programme: best practice guidance and implementation

  <https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/nhs-health-check-implementation-review-and-action-plan>
- NHS Heart Age Test.
  <https://www.nhs.uk/health-assessment-tools/calculate-your-heart-age>

## Risk-equation references

- Hippisley-Cox J, Coupland C, Brindle P. *Development and validation of
  QRISK3 risk prediction algorithms to estimate future risk of
  cardiovascular disease in primary care: prospective cohort study.*
  BMJ 2017;357:j2099. DOI: 10.1136/bmj.j2099.
- QRISK3 algorithm and source: <https://qrisk.org/>
- SCORE2 working group. Eur Heart J 2021;42(25):2439-2454.
  DOI: 10.1093/eurheartj/ehab309.
