The Functional Movement Screen (FMS) evaluates seven fundamental movement patterns. Each pattern is scored 0-3 by the assessing clinician. The composite score (0-21) helps identify movement deficiencies and asymmetries that may increase injury risk.

Scores: 0 = pain during movement; 1 = unable to perform pattern; 2 = performs with compensation; 3 = performs without compensation. For bilateral tests, the lower of the left/right scores is used. A total at or below 14 indicates increased injury risk. All answers are saved automatically in your browser as you go.

The Functional Movement Screen (FMS) is a screening tool used by kinesiologists, physiotherapists, and athletic trainers to identify movement-pattern asymmetries and limitations. It is not a diagnostic tool. Results should be interpreted alongside a full clinical assessment.
