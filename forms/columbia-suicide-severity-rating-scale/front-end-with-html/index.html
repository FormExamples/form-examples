Complete the C-SSRS below for the person being assessed. The scale records the severity of suicidal ideation on a five-point ordinal scale (levels 1-5), the categories of suicidal behaviour with their recency, and the lethality of any actual attempt, then derives a Low / Moderate / High risk tier and a management recommendation. Answers are saved automatically in your browser as you go.

C-SSRS is a status- and severity-classification instrument, not a summed score and not a diagnosis. It is a validated clinical screening tool used by trained staff; it does not replace clinical judgement, direct assessment, or local safeguarding and escalation policy. A Low tier does not exclude risk — re-screen on any change. If the person is in immediate danger, follow your local emergency and crisis protocol now.

The Columbia Suicide Severity Rating Scale (C-SSRS) was developed by Posner and colleagues at Columbia University (Posner et al., American Journal of Psychiatry 2011) and is recognised in NICE NG225 and by the US FDA and SAMHSA. This screening tool stratifies risk and prompts escalation; it is not a diagnosis. Escalate and manage in line with local safeguarding, crisis, and mental-health policy and clinical judgement.
