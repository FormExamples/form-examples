## Migraine diagnostic criteria

The aura screening questions follow ICHD-3:
<https://ichd-3.org/>

## Drug interaction reference

The form's interaction check follows the *FSRH Guideline: Drug Interactions
with Hormonal Contraception*. The BNF interactions database
(<https://bnf.nice.org.uk/interactions/>) is the secondary reference for
clinicians.
