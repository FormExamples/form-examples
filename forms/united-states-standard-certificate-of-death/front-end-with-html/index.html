Complete the eleven sections below to document a US Standard Certificate of Death. The engine does not diagnose and is not legal advice. It checks that the certificate is complete and consistent with the NCHS Physicians' Handbook on Medical Certification of Death: that Part I line (a) states a disease or injury, that no mechanism of dying (such as "cardiac arrest") stands alone, that the chain reads in order, that abbreviations are written out, and that the manner, tobacco, pregnancy and injury items are answered. It classifies the certificate as valid, incomplete, or refer to medical examiner or coroner, derives the underlying cause, and raises prioritized flags, including state filing deadlines and certified-copy fees. The US Standard Certificate of Death is the model certificate of the National Center for Health Statistics (CDC/NCHS, 2003 revision). Each state adopts its own version, filing deadlines and fees under its vital statistics law. This tool documents and validates the certificate; it is documentation support, not legal advice, and the registering state agency and the certifier's licensing law decide.
