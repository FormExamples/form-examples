Clinician referral wizard for a tissue histopathology examination. Record the requesting clinician, the patient, the specimen (type, site, number of pots, fixative), the clinical indication and specific question, relevant details, and the requested urgency. The engine computes a four-axis grade (appropriateness, specimen quality, request completeness, and urgency triage) plus a set of safety flags.

This is clinical decision support for the pathology accessioning and vetting desk. The final triage, accept / reject, and reporting decision remains the responsibility of the reporting pathologist.
