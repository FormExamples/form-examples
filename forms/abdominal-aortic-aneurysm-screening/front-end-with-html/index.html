Complete the scan record below for one abdominal aortic ultrasound under the NHS AAA Screening Programme. The maximum antero-posterior aortic diameter classifies the aorta as normal (< 3.0 cm), a small aneurysm (3.0–4.4 cm), a medium aneurysm (4.5–5.4 cm), or a large aneurysm (≥ 5.5 cm), and sets the corresponding surveillance or referral action. Answers are saved automatically in your browser as you go.

This form documents the scan and derives the result; it does not diagnose and does not decide on repair. An aorta that cannot be adequately measured is recorded as non-visualised and a re-scan is arranged. Use clinical judgement alongside the programme standards.

Submit the form to see the report.

The diameter thresholds (3.0 / 4.5 / 5.5 cm) and recall intervals (annual, three-monthly) are those of the NHS Abdominal Aortic Aneurysm Screening Programme; management follows NICE NG156. This tool is for documentation and result classification only and is not a diagnosis; refer, recall, and treat in line with programme standards and clinical judgement.
