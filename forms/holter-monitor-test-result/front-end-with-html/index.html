This structured ambulatory ECG report is aligned with ACC/AHA ambulatory electrocardiography guidance, the 2017 ISHNE-HRS expert consensus on ambulatory ECG and external cardiac monitoring, NICE NG196 atrial fibrillation guidance, and ESC guidance on bradycardia / cardiac pacing and ventricular arrhythmias, including the >3 s significant-pause threshold and the critical-result auto-escalation invariant. It is a reporting aid, not a diagnosis; interpret and act in line with local cardiology pathways and clinical judgement.
