The Revised ASRM (American Society for Reproductive Medicine) staging system and the EHP-30 (Endometriosis Health Profile) questionnaire are validated tools for assessing endometriosis severity and quality-of-life impact. This screening tool is for information only and is not a diagnosis; please discuss your results with your gynaecologist.
