# Staging rules

This form implements two complementary instruments:

1. The **revised American Society for Reproductive Medicine (rASRM)
   classification of endometriosis** (originally 1985, last revised 1996) —
   a points-based surgical staging system that produces Stage I-IV.
2. The **Endometriosis Health Profile (EHP-30)** — a validated 30-item
   patient-reported outcome measure for quality of life.

## rASRM stages

| Stage | Range (points) | Description |
| ----- | --------------:| ----------- |
| I (Minimal) | 1–5 | Isolated superficial implants, no significant adhesions |
| II (Mild)   | 6–15 | Superficial and deep implants on ovary and/or pelvic peritoneum; mild filmy adhesions |
| III (Moderate) | 16–40 | Multiple implants, both superficial and deep; dense adhesions of tube and/or ovary; small endometriomas |
| IV (Severe) | >40 | Multiple deep implants, large endometriomas (typically >3 cm), dense bowel/tube/ovary adhesions, posterior cul-de-sac obliteration |

The point allocation is laid out in:

- American Society for Reproductive Medicine. *Revised American Society for
  Reproductive Medicine classification of endometriosis: 1996.* Fertility and
  Sterility 1997;67(5):817–821.
  PubMed: <https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/9130884/>

The rASRM score is calculated intra-operatively from direct visualisation;
the questionnaire records the score post-operatively and treats it as
clinician-supplied data.

## EHP-30 domains

The EHP-30 produces five core scale scores and six modular scale scores.
Each is reported on a 0–100 scale where 100 represents the worst
health-related quality of life.

| Core scale | Items |
| ---------- | ----: |
| Pain | 11 |
| Control and powerlessness | 6 |
| Emotional well-being | 6 |
| Social support | 4 |
| Self-image | 3 |

Modular scales (use only if relevant): work, intercourse, relationship with
children, medical profession, treatment, infertility.

Primary reference:

- Jones G, Kennedy S, Barnard A, Wong J, Jenkinson C. *Development of an
  endometriosis quality-of-life instrument: The Endometriosis Health Profile
  (EHP-30).* Obstetrics & Gynecology. 2001;98(2):258–264. DOI:
  <https://doi.org/10.1016/s0029-7844(01)01433-8>
- Jones G, Jenkinson C, Kennedy S. *Evaluating the responsiveness of the
  Endometriosis Health Profile Questionnaire: The EHP-30.* Quality of Life
  Research. 2004;13(3):705–713. DOI:
  <https://doi.org/10.1023/B:QURE.0000021316.79349.af>

## Severity bands used by this form

The form combines rASRM stage and symptom impact to derive an overall
severity band:

- Mild — Stage I-II with manageable symptoms (EHP-30 pain domain < 40)
- Moderate — Stage II-III or significant symptoms impacting daily life
- Severe — Stage III-IV or debilitating symptoms (EHP-30 pain domain ≥ 70)
- Critical — Bowel or urinary obstruction, ureteric involvement, severe
  diaphragmatic disease, or fertility crisis

These severity bands are a presentational aid; they are not part of the
official rASRM classification.

## Notes on rASRM limitations

The rASRM score is widely acknowledged to correlate poorly with
patient-reported symptom severity and with fertility outcomes. The ESHRE
2022 guideline (see clinical-guideline-alignment.md) recommends using
multiple instruments rather than rASRM alone. The form therefore reports
both rASRM and EHP-30 and avoids privileging either.
