This structured allergy report is aligned with BSACI and EAACI skin-test and specific-IgE interpretation guidance, including the ≥3 mm positive-weal threshold, the requirement for a valid positive histamine control, and the distinction between sensitisation and clinically relevant allergy. It is a reporting aid, not a diagnosis; interpret and act in line with local allergy pathways and clinical judgement.
