The WHO densitometric classification is derived from the lowest T-score: normal (T ≥ −1.0), osteopenia (−1.0 > T > −2.5), and osteoporosis (T ≤ −2.5). A T-score of −2.5 or below together with an identified fragility / vertebral fracture establishes severe (established) osteoporosis and auto-escalates the follow-up urgency to a critical alert.

This structured densitometry report is aligned with the WHO densitometric classification and ISCD / NOGG reporting guidance, including the T-score thresholds (normal T ≥ −1.0; osteopenia −1.0 > T > −2.5; osteoporosis T ≤ −2.5), FRAX 10-year fracture-risk probabilities, and the escalation of severe (established) osteoporosis with a fragility fracture. It is a reporting aid, not a diagnosis; interpret and act in line with local osteoporosis pathways and clinical judgement.
