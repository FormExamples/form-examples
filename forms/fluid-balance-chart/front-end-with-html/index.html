Record the patient's fluid intake and output for the charting period. Add one row per timed volume in the Intake and Output steps; the live indicator updates the total intake, total output, net balance, urine output rate (mL/kg/h), and fluid-status classification as you go. Answers are saved automatically in your browser.

This is a monitoring aid with an arithmetic reconciliation engine, not a validated named clinical score or a diagnostic test. The numeric outputs and flags prompt clinical review; they do not prescribe treatment.

The Fluid Balance Chart is a bedside monitoring instrument. The classification and thresholds draw on NICE CG174 (intravenous fluid therapy in adults in hospital), the KDIGO oliguria threshold of 0.5 mL/kg/h, RCP NEWS2, and NCEPOD guidance. This tool is for monitoring and decision support only and is not a diagnosis; fluid-prescription and escalation decisions rest with the clinician.
