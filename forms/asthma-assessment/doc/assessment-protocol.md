# Assessment protocol

## Purpose

To capture a structured ACT-based asthma annual or follow-up review and apply
the NICE/BTS/SIGN NG245 (2024) algorithm to identify step-up or step-down
opportunities, NRAD risk markers, and severe-asthma referral criteria.

## Setting

- Primary care annual asthma review (GP, asthma nurse, pharmacist).
- Hospital outpatient respiratory clinic.
- Post-exacerbation follow-up clinic within 48 hours of discharge (per NG245
  and BTS acute asthma guidance).

## Step-by-step protocol

### Step 1 — Demographics
Capture identifiers, asthma diagnosis date, and atopy status. The diagnosis
should already have been confirmed via the NG245 diagnostic pathway
(eosinophils → FeNO → BDR → PEF → BCT).

### Step 2 — Symptom frequency (ACT)
Administer the five ACT items per Nathan et al. 2004. Optionally also
administer the **RCP three questions** for a rapid clinical impression.

### Step 3 — Lung function
- Peak expiratory flow (best of three blows) and percent of personal best or
  predicted.
- Most recent spirometry: FEV1, FVC, FEV1/FVC, BDR if performed.
- FeNO (parts per billion) and blood eosinophil count if recent.

### Step 4 — Triggers
Document allergen, exercise, occupational, viral, NSAID, beta-blocker, and
psychological triggers. Cross-link to allergic comorbidity (Step 6).

### Step 5 — Current medications
- Reliever: SABA vs. ICS-formoterol (AIR). Flag SABA-only as inappropriate
  per GINA 2024 / NG245.
- Controller: ICS dose, LABA, LAMA, leukotriene receptor antagonist (LTRA),
  biologics (mepolizumab, benralizumab, omalizumab, tezepelumab).
- Adherence (prescription refill record), inhaler technique check date.

### Step 6 — Allergies
Aeroallergen sensitization (HDM, pollen, animal dander, mould). Allergic
rhinitis comorbidity (TNSS).

### Step 7 — Exacerbation history
- Number of oral corticosteroid courses in past 12 months.
- Number of unscheduled GP visits, A&E visits, hospital admissions, ITU
  admissions in past 12 months.
- History of mechanical ventilation (life-threatening asthma marker).

### Step 8 — Comorbidities
GORD, obesity, OSA, anxiety/depression, eosinophilic granulomatosis with
polyangiitis (EGPA).

### Step 9 — Social history
Current and ex-smoker pack-years, occupational exposure, vaping, e-cigarette
use, household exposures, pet ownership.

## Output

- ACT total score and category.
- Treatment-step recommendation per NG245 step ladder.
- NRAD risk-marker count.
- Personalized asthma action plan (PAAP) PDF.

## Quality controls

- Inhaler technique competency check (required per NHS QOF and NRAD).
- Asthma action plan completion check (required per NRAD).
- Smoking cessation referral if current smoker.
