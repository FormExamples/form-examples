# Clinical guideline alignment

## NICE / BTS / SIGN joint asthma guideline

The UK national asthma guideline is now jointly published by **NICE / BTS /
SIGN** as **NG245** (November 2024), replacing the legacy NICE NG80 (2017) and
BTS/SIGN 158 (2019).

- NICE NG245 *Asthma: diagnosis, monitoring and chronic asthma management*.
  Published November 2024. <https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/ng245>
- Predecessor: NICE NG80 (2017, withdrawn). <https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/ng80>
- Predecessor: BTS/SIGN 158 (2019, withdrawn).
  <https://www.brit-thoracic.org.uk/quality-improvement/guidelines/asthma/>

### NG245 key changes the form must reflect

| NG245 change | Form coverage |
| --- | --- |
| MART (maintenance and reliever therapy) with low-dose ICS/formoterol as preferred first-line | Step 5 Current Medications: AIR/MART checkbox |
| Discourage SABA-only treatment | Step 5 + flagged-issue logic on SABA-only |
| Diagnostic algorithm: blood eosinophils → FeNO → BDR → PEF variability → BCT | Step 3 Lung Function fields |
| Annual review with structured questions (RCP three questions, ACT, ACQ) | Step 2 ACT captures this |

## GINA (Global Initiative for Asthma)

- GINA *Global Strategy for Asthma Management and Prevention*, 2024 update.
  <https://ginasthma.org/2024-report/>
- The form aligns with the GINA control-based assessment of risk and
  symptom control. The ACT is one of the patient-completed instruments GINA
  endorses for symptom control.
- GINA strongly recommends against SABA-only treatment in any patient ≥12
  years and recommends Track 1 (ICS-formoterol) as the preferred reliever.

## RCP three questions

- **Royal College of Physicians (London) three questions** for asthma review:
  1. In the last week/month, have you had difficulty sleeping because of your
     asthma symptoms (including cough)?
  2. Have you had your usual asthma symptoms during the day (cough, wheeze,
     chest tightness, shortness of breath)?
  3. Has your asthma interfered with your usual activities?
- Pearson MG, Bucknall CE (eds). *Measuring clinical outcome in asthma*. RCP,
  London, 1999.

These three questions are embedded in Step 2 as a quick filter alongside ACT.

## NHS / SPC

- BNF Asthma management: <https://bnf.nice.org.uk/treatment-summaries/asthma/>
- Adrenaline and emergency-management cross-references (severe acute asthma /
  life-threatening asthma) per BTS/SIGN acute asthma algorithm.

## NRAD

- *Why asthma still kills*. National Review of Asthma Deaths (NRAD), Royal
  College of Physicians, London, 2014.
  <https://www.rcp.ac.uk/projects/outputs/why-asthma-still-kills>
- Form coverage: Step 7 captures NRAD risk markers — ≥2 oral steroid courses
  in the past year, hospital admission, ITU admission, history of intubation,
  poor adherence.
