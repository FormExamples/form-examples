Clinician referral wizard for a clinical genetics / genomic test. Record the requesting clinician, patient identification, the requested test and clinical indication, the clinical details and family history, the consent and pre-test counselling status, and the specimen and triage. The engine computes a four-axis grade (appropriateness, consent & counselling, request completeness, and triage priority) plus a set of safety flags.

This is clinical decision support for the Genomic Laboratory Hub vetting desk. The final triage and test-selection decision remains the responsibility of the vetting clinician or laboratory scientist.

Submit the form to see the vetting report.

Grading is aligned with the NHS National Genomic Test Directory eligibility criteria and ACGS / consent-for-genomic-testing guidance. Consent and pre-test counselling are mandatory for predictive / presymptomatic testing.
