# References — sundowner / BPSD assessment

## Note on "sundowner syndrome"

"Sundowner syndrome" or "sundowning" is a clinical descriptor for
late-afternoon and evening behavioural change in older people, most
commonly with dementia. **It is not a discrete diagnostic entity in
DSM-5-TR or ICD-11.** The authoritative literature treats it as a
temporal pattern of behavioural and psychological symptoms of dementia
(BPSD) and as a presentation requiring delirium exclusion.
