This form evaluates sundowner syndrome (sundowning) in elderly patients, particularly those with dementia. It combines the Cohen-Mansfield Agitation Inventory (CMAI) — 29 items rating agitated behaviour frequency — with the Neuropsychiatric Inventory (NPI), which scores 12 behavioural domains by frequency and severity. All answers are saved automatically in your browser as you go.

If the patient is in immediate danger of self-harm, harming others, or is acutely medically unwell, please contact emergency services immediately rather than completing this form.

The Cohen-Mansfield Agitation Inventory (CMAI) and Neuropsychiatric Inventory (NPI) are validated instruments for assessing agitation and behavioural symptoms in dementia. This screening tool is for information only and is not a diagnosis; please discuss results with the responsible clinician.
