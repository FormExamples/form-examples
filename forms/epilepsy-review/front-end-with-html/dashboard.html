This dashboard is a clinical-review tool. The seizure-control status and review completeness document how well seizures are controlled and whether the annual review is whole; they are not a diagnosis. An uncontrolled seizure-control status or a safety flag (valproate without a pregnancy-prevention programme, status epilepticus, or driving while not DVLA-eligible) prompts prompt clinical action.
