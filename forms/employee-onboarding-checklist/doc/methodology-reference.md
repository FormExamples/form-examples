# Methodology reference — employee onboarding

The Employee Onboarding Checklist captures the steps an organization
takes to integrate a new hire from offer-acceptance through the end of
probation. The structure follows the **CIPD onboarding framework** and
incorporates the SHRM "Four Cs" model.

## CIPD onboarding framework

The **Chartered Institute of Personnel and Development (CIPD)** is the
UK professional body for HR and people development. CIPD groups
onboarding into pre-employment, induction (first week / first month),
socialization, and integration phases.

- CIPD — Induction factsheet.
  <https://www.cipd.org/uk/knowledge/factsheets/induction-factsheet/>
- CIPD — Employee turnover and retention factsheet.
  <https://www.cipd.org/uk/knowledge/factsheets/turnover-retention-factsheet/>

## Bauer "Four Cs" model

Talya Bauer, in her 2010 SHRM Foundation report **"Onboarding New
Employees: Maximizing Success"**, identifies four levels of onboarding:

1. **Compliance** — legal and policy basics (right to work, contract,
   IT acceptable use).
2. **Clarification** — role expectations and performance criteria.
3. **Culture** — organizational norms, formal and informal.
4. **Connection** — interpersonal relationships and networks.

Reference: Bauer, T. N. (2010). *Onboarding New Employees: Maximizing
Success*. SHRM Foundation.
<https://www.shrm.org/foundation/about/Pages/onboarding-new-employees-maximizing-success.aspx>

## UK statutory baseline

The compliance level of any UK onboarding checklist must cover at
minimum:

| Obligation | Authority |
| --- | --- |
| Right to work check before start date | Immigration, Asylum and Nationality Act 2006, s.15-25; Home Office Employer's guide to right to work checks |
| Written statement of particulars of employment on or before day 1 | Employment Rights Act 1996, s.1 (as amended by Good Work Plan 2020) |
| Auto-enrolment into a workplace pension | Pensions Act 2008 |
| Income tax / NI new-starter declaration | PAYE via HMRC |
| Health & Safety induction | Management of Health and Safety at Work Regulations 1999, reg. 10 |
| Data protection / GDPR briefing | UK GDPR Article 5; DPA 2018 |
| Equality & dignity-at-work training | Equality Act 2010 |

## Time horizons

The checklist tracks five horizons:

- **Pre-employment** (offer to start date).
- **Day 1**.
- **First week**.
- **First month**.
- **End of probation** (typically 3 or 6 months).

These mirror the horizons used in CIPD induction guidance.

## Engagement check-ins

Research shows that structured 30-/60-/90-day check-ins materially
improve retention. Bauer (2010) reports new hires with formal onboarding
are 58 % more likely to be with the organization after three years.
This implementation embeds three check-in records at 30, 60, and 90
days.
