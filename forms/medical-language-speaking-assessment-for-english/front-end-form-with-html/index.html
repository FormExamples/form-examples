This examiner rating form mirrors the Occupational English Test (OET) Speaking Sub-test for the Medicine profession. The candidate completes two role-played clinical scenarios; the examiner rates the candidate against four linguistic criteria and five clinical communication indicators, producing a scaled score (0-500) and an overall grade (A, B, C+, C, D, or E).

All ratings are saved automatically in the browser as you go. This is a training and assessment tool only and not a substitute for an accredited OET examination.

Modelled on the Occupational English Test (OET) Speaking Sub-test for Medicine. Linguistic criteria are rated 0-6; clinical communication indicators are rated 0-3. Combined raw scores are scaled to 0-500 and mapped to grades A-E. This tool supports examiner training and self-assessment; an official OET grade can only be issued by Cambridge Boxhill Language Assessment.
