# UK and international regulator acceptance

## United Kingdom

The OET Speaking sub-test is one of two accepted English-language
evidence routes for international healthcare professional registration
in the UK (the other being IELTS Academic).

### General Medical Council (GMC)

- *Evidence of your knowledge of English.*
  <https://www.gmc-uk.org/registration-and-licensing/join-the-register/before-you-apply/evidence-of-english-language-skills>
- Minimum OET Medicine result: B in each sub-test (Speaking, Writing,
  Reading, Listening).
- Validity: 2 years from sitting.

### Nursing and Midwifery Council (NMC)

- *English language requirements.*
  <https://www.nmc.org.uk/registration/joining-the-register/trained-outside-the-eueea/english-language-requirements/>
- Minimum OET Nursing result: B in each sub-test.

### General Pharmaceutical Council (GPhC)

- *English language requirements.*
  <https://www.pharmacyregulation.org/registration/registering-pharmacist>
- The pharmacist *Overseas Pharmacists' Assessment Programme* (OSPAP)
  applies for non-UK trained pharmacists; OET evidence is one route.

### General Dental Council (GDC)

- *English language requirements.*
  <https://www.gdc-uk.org/registration/join-our-registers/english-language-requirements>

### Health and Care Professions Council (HCPC)

- *English language requirements.*
  <https://www.hcpc-uk.org/registration/getting-on-the-register/international-registration/english-language-requirements/>

## Other accepting regulators

OET publishes the canonical list of accepting regulators and
employers; verify currency against the OET site.

- OET. *Who recognizes OET?*
  <https://www.occupationalenglishtest.org/about-oet/recognition/>

Includes:

- **Australia** — Australian Health Practitioner Regulation Agency
  (AHPRA), the Medical Board of Australia, Nursing and Midwifery Board
  of Australia.
- **Ireland** — Medical Council, NMBI.
- **New Zealand** — Medical Council of New Zealand, Nursing Council.
- **Singapore** — Singapore Medical Council, Singapore Nursing Board.
- **UAE / Dubai** — Dubai Health Authority.

## OET and IELTS equivalence

The IELTS Academic test is the principal alternative. The regulator-
published equivalence (current at time of writing) is:

| OET grade | IELTS Academic band |
| --- | --- |
| A | 8.0+ |
| B | 7.5 |
| C+ | 6.5 |
| C | 6.0 |

This equivalence is published by the GMC and is OET's *Cambridge
Boxhill / IELTS comparison study* result. Note: equivalence is a
*concordance*, not an exchange — the regulator may require a specific
test even if the candidate has the equivalent band on the other.

## Validity windows

OET certificates remain valid for **2 years** from the test date for
UK regulator purposes. The form's *Candidate Details* step records the
test date; downstream registration systems must apply the 2-year
expiry rule.
