Clinician referral wizard for a DEXA / DXA bone-densitometry examination. Record the requesting clinician, the patient, the requested scan region and clinical question, the fracture-risk factors (FRAX inputs), any previous DEXA history, and the requested urgency. The engine computes a four-axis grade (appropriateness, radiation safety, request completeness, and triage priority) plus a set of safety flags.

This is clinical decision support for the imaging vetting desk. The final triage and booking decision remains the responsibility of the vetting radiologist or radiographer.

Submit the form to see the vetting report.

Grading is aligned with NICE CG146, the National Osteoporosis Guideline Group (NOGG) intervention thresholds, FRAX, and the ISCD Official Positions. DEXA delivers a very low radiation dose (~1–5 microsievert); a scan should be deferred in known or suspected pregnancy. This screening tool is for clinical decision support; the final triage and booking decision remains the responsibility of the vetting clinician.
