# Clinical references

Authoritative guidance underpinning the four-axis grading engine.

## Appropriateness (NICE CG146 / NOGG / FRAX)

NICE CG146 recommends assessing 10-year fracture risk with **FRAX** (without a
BMD value) or **QFracture** first, then considering **DXA** when the estimated
risk sits near an intervention threshold, and recalculating FRAX with the BMD
value. People under 40 at particularly high risk (several previous fragility
fractures, high-dose oral steroids) warrant a bone scan directly.

Appropriateness is scored on an ordinal 1–9 scale in three bands:
usually-appropriate (7–9), may-be-appropriate (4–6), usually-not-appropriate
(1–3), mapped per (indication × scan region × risk context).

| Indication | Typical band |
| --- | --- |
| Fragility fracture | usually-appropriate |
| High FRAX risk near intervention threshold | usually-appropriate |
| Long-term steroids | usually-appropriate |
| Early menopause | may-be-appropriate |
| Osteoporosis screening (risk-stratified) | may-be-appropriate |
| Monitoring treatment (within LSC interval) | may-be / usually-not |
| Screening with no risk assessment | usually-not-appropriate |

## Radiation safety

DEXA is a **very low dose** examination: effective dose is approximately
1–5 microsievert (~0.005 mSv) per scan — far below a chest radiograph. There are
no dose limits for justified medical exposure, so repeat scans are permissible
when each is justified. A scan should be **deferred in known or suspected
pregnancy**. Dose bands: low (standard hip/spine), moderate, high.

## Completeness

Mandatory-field checklist. The **primary indication** and **specific clinical
question** are weighted highest; FRAX inputs, previous-DEXA result, and
scan region contribute the remainder. Output is 0–100 % plus the missing fields.

## Triage tiers

| Tier | Target | Triggers |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Urgent | days–2 weeks | recent fragility fracture, very high FRAX risk, long-term high-dose steroids |
| Routine | standard booking | screening, monitoring, early menopause |
