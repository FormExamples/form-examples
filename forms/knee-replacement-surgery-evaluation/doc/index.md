## Instrument licensing

| Instrument | Licence |
| --- | --- |
| Oxford Knee Score (OKS) | Published instrument (Dawson et al., *J Bone Joint Surg Br* 1998). Free for non-commercial clinical and research use; reproduce the item wording unaltered and cite the source paper. Commercial use of the OKS requires a licence from Isis Innovation / Oxford University Innovation. |
| Kellgren-Lawrence grading | Published classification (Kellgren & Lawrence, *Ann Rheum Dis* 1957); freely citable. |

Confirm the licence position with the instrument owner before any commercial
distribution of a derived product.
