# Clinical guideline alignment — mental health assessment

## NICE NG222 — Depression in adults: treatment and management

Source: National Institute for Health and Care Excellence (NICE).
Guideline ID: **NG222** (published June 2022).
URL: https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/ng222

NG222 replaces CG90 (Depression in adults) and CG91 (Depression in adults
with a chronic physical health problem). Relevant alignments:

- **§1.3 Identification and assessment**: use of a validated measure of
  depression severity such as PHQ-9. The form's PHQ-9 step encodes this.
- **§1.4 Choice of treatment**: matched to severity band (less severe vs
  more severe), patient preference, and prior response.
- **§1.5 Risk assessment**: review of suicidal thoughts and intent at
  every contact in active depression. Captured in the Risk Assessment
  step and the PHQ-9 item-9 safety flag.

## NICE NG134 — Self-harm in over 8s: short-term management and prevention of recurrence

Source: NICE. Guideline ID: **NG225** (published September 2022), which
supersedes CG16 and CG133.
URL: https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/ng225

NG225 covers psychosocial assessment of people who present after
self-harm. The Risk Assessment step prompts the clinician to record:

- Mental state and current intent.
- Self-harm history and method.
- Suicidal ideation, plans, means.
- Modifiable risk factors (substance use, recent loss, social isolation).
- Protective factors.

The form aligns with NICE's position that risk-stratification scales (e.g.
SAD PERSONS) should **not** be used as standalone decision tools; the
score is one element of the clinical formulation.

## NICE NG191 — Generalized anxiety disorder and panic disorder in adults

Source: NICE. Guideline ID: **CG113** (the current adult anxiety
guideline; published January 2011, updated July 2019).
URL: https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/cg113

CG113 §1.2 recommends GAD-7 (and the 2-item version GAD-2) for
identification and severity grading. The form's GAD-7 step encodes the
full 7-item version.

CG113 step-care model:

| Step | Patient group                                  | Intervention                          |
| ---- | ---------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------- |
| 1    | All known and suspected presentations         | Identification, assessment, education |
| 2    | Diagnosed GAD, no improvement after education | Low-intensity psychological interventions |
| 3    | GAD with marked functional impairment / no response | High-intensity psychological therapy or drug treatment |
| 4    | Complex / treatment-refractory                | Specialist mental health input        |

The PHQ-9 / GAD-7 band determines step-care matching.

## DSM-5-TR

American Psychiatric Association (2022). DSM-5-TR.

- Major Depressive Disorder: code **296.2x / F32.x** (single episode);
  **296.3x / F33.x** (recurrent).
- Generalized Anxiety Disorder: code **300.02 / F41.1**.

DSM-5-TR criteria for MDD require ≥5 of 9 symptoms during the same
2-week period, representing a change from previous functioning, with at
least one being depressed mood or loss of interest. PHQ-9 maps directly
to the 9 symptom criteria.

DSM-5-TR criteria for GAD require excessive anxiety and worry on more
days than not for ≥6 months, plus ≥3 of 6 associated symptoms.
