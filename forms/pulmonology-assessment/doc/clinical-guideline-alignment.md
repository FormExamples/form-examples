# Clinical guideline alignment

## GOLD

- Global Initiative for Chronic Obstructive Lung Disease. *Global Strategy for
  the Diagnosis, Management, and Prevention of Chronic Obstructive Pulmonary
  Disease, 2024 Report*. Fontana, WI: GOLD, 2024.
  <https://goldcopd.org/2024-gold-report/>
- The form implements:
  - GOLD spirometric severity grading (1-4).
  - GOLD A-B-E exacerbation-history grouping (2023+).
  - GOLD pharmacotherapy track recommendations (Track 1 / Track 2).

## NICE NG115

- National Institute for Health and Care Excellence. *Chronic obstructive
  pulmonary disease in over 16s: diagnosis and management*. NICE guideline
  NG115. London: NICE, December 2018 (last updated July 2019).
  <https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/ng115>
- Form coverage:
  - Step 3 captures the spirometry quality criteria (ATS/ERS 2019).
  - Step 5 captures pulmonary rehabilitation eligibility per §1.2.41
    (MRC ≥ 3 or recovery from a hospitalized exacerbation).
  - Step 6 captures the NICE-preferred dual or triple inhaler regimens
    differentiated by asthmatic features / steroid responsiveness.

## NICE-related quality standards

- NICE QS10 *Chronic obstructive pulmonary disease in adults*.
  <https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/qs10>

## ATS/ERS spirometry

- Graham BL, Steenbruggen I, Miller MR, et al. *Standardization of Spirometry
  2019 Update*. Am J Respir Crit Care Med 2019;200:e70-e88. PMID: 31613151
- Step 3 records ATS/ERS acceptability/repeatability flags (≥3 acceptable
  curves, two largest FEV1 within 150 mL, two largest FVC within 150 mL).

## Pulmonary rehabilitation

- British Thoracic Society. *BTS guideline on pulmonary rehabilitation in
  adults*. Thorax 2013;68 Suppl 2:ii1-ii30. PMID: 23880483
- Form coverage: Step 5 records pulmonary rehab eligibility, attendance,
  and 6-minute walk test distance change.

## BODE index (cross-reference)

- Celli BR, Cote CG, Marin JM, et al. *The body-mass index, airflow
  obstruction, dyspnea, and exercise capacity index in chronic obstructive
  pulmonary disease*. N Engl J Med 2004;350:1005-12. PMID: 14999112
- The form does not score BODE; instead it captures the inputs (BMI, FEV1%,
  mMRC, 6MWD) and emits them on the report so a clinician can compute BODE.
