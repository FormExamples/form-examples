# Assessment protocol

## Purpose

To capture a structured pulmonology consultation, assign a GOLD severity
grade and A-B-E group, and produce an inhaler / pulmonary-rehabilitation /
referral recommendation.

## Setting

- Respiratory outpatient clinic.
- Primary care annual COPD review.
- Post-discharge clinic within 2 weeks of an exacerbation hospitalization.

## Step-by-step protocol

### Step 1 — Demographics
Patient identifiers; ethnicity (for ATS/ERS GLI-2012 reference equations);
height and weight (for BMI).

### Step 2 — Chief complaint
Presenting symptom, duration, severity. Standard mMRC dyspnoea score.

### Step 3 — Spirometry
Post-bronchodilator FEV1, FVC, FEV1/FVC, % predicted (GLI-2012). ATS/ERS
acceptability flags. Reversibility test if asthma overlap suspected.

### Step 4 — Symptom assessment
CAT (8 items) and full mMRC. Optional St George's Respiratory Questionnaire
or CRQ for completeness.

### Step 5 — Exacerbation history
Number and severity of exacerbations in past 12 months:
- Moderate (treated with antibiotics ± oral steroids in primary care).
- Severe (A&E or hospital admission).
- ITU admission.

### Step 6 — Current medications
- SABA, SAMA, LABA, LAMA, ICS combinations.
- LTRA, theophylline, roflumilast, azithromycin (frequent exacerbators).
- Long-term oxygen therapy / non-invasive ventilation criteria
  (PaO2 ≤ 7.3 kPa or 7.3-8.0 kPa with peripheral oedema, polycythaemia, or
  pulmonary hypertension).

### Step 7 — Allergies
Drug allergies (β-lactam, sulfonamide), inhaler-component allergies
(propellant, lactose excipient).

### Step 8 — Comorbidities
- Cardiovascular disease (high prevalence in COPD).
- Anxiety / depression (HADS).
- Osteoporosis (especially in patients on inhaled or oral corticosteroids).
- Lung cancer screening eligibility (NHS Targeted Lung Health Check).

### Step 9 — Smoking and exposures
- Pack-years, vaping, e-cigarette use.
- Occupational exposure (coal, silica, asbestos, welding fumes).
- Indoor air pollution (biomass cooking fuel — relevant in LMIC settings).

### Step 10 — Functional status
6-minute walk distance, gait speed, sit-to-stand, activities of daily living.

## Output

- GOLD grade (1-4) and group (A/B/E).
- Recommended pharmacotherapy track per GOLD 2024.
- Pulmonary rehabilitation eligibility.
- Smoking cessation pathway (NHS Stop Smoking Services).

## Quality controls

- Spirometry must be post-bronchodilator and meet ATS/ERS acceptability.
- α1-antitrypsin recorded at least once per patient.
- Vaccination status (influenza, pneumococcal, COVID-19, RSV) recorded.
