## Instrument licensing

| Instrument | Licence |
| --- | --- |
| Oxford Hip Score (OHS) | Owned by Isis Innovation Ltd (Oxford University Innovation) on behalf of the University of Oxford and the OHS authors. Free for individual clinical use; a licence is required for commercial or large-scale digital deployment. Reproduce the 12 items unaltered and cite Dawson et al. 1996. |
| Kellgren and Lawrence grading | Published classification (Ann Rheum Dis 1957), freely citable. |

Confirm the OHS licence position with Oxford University Innovation before any
commercial distribution of a derived product.
