Effectiveness-review wizard for workplace neurodiversity reasonable adjustments. A line manager, HR adviser, or occupational-health lead records the reviewer and originating response, the worker identification, the per-category effectiveness of the adjustments in place, the worker's feedback, satisfaction, wellbeing change and remaining barriers, any changes arising and the next review date, and any escalation. The engine computes a four-axis grade (overall effectiveness, wellbeing risk, review completeness, and next-step urgency) plus a set of review flags.

Any adjustment reported not-working, a dissatisfied worker, declining wellbeing, or an escalation drives the wellbeing-risk axis and the next-step urgency and raises the corresponding flag. This is decision support; the employer remains responsible for the Equality Act 2010 duty to make reasonable adjustments and to review them regularly.
