that the form is designed to operationalise.

## Heart-failure staging cross-walk

The 2022 ACC/AHA Stages of Heart Failure (A–D) are complementary to NYHA
classes and to the ESC 2021 four-stage scheme. The form captures NYHA class
explicitly; stage A–D is recorded under "Cardiac History" via the structural
heart-disease and prior-event fields.

## Antiplatelet and lipid therapy

NICE CG181 recommends atorvastatin 20 mg for primary prevention when 10-year
QRISK is ≥10 %, and atorvastatin 80 mg for secondary prevention. The
medications step records statin agent, dose, and start date for guideline
checking by downstream pharmacy review.
