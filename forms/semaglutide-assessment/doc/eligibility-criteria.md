# Eligibility criteria

Semaglutide is a GLP-1 receptor agonist licensed in the UK and EU for
type 2 diabetes (Ozempic, Rybelsus) and for chronic weight management
(Wegovy). This form encodes the eligibility and safety screening required
before initiation.

## Type 2 diabetes (Ozempic / Rybelsus)

Per the UK Summary of Product Characteristics:

- Ozempic SmPC. <https://www.medicines.org.uk/emc/product/9748>
- Rybelsus SmPC (oral). <https://www.medicines.org.uk/emc/product/11507>

Indications:

- Adults with type 2 diabetes mellitus inadequately controlled, as
  adjunct to diet and exercise:
  - As monotherapy when metformin is unsuitable.
  - In addition to other glucose-lowering medicinal products.

NICE technology appraisal:

- NICE TA875 (semaglutide for managing type 2 diabetes).
  <https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/ta875>

NICE NG28 §1.6 places GLP-1 agonists as fourth-line in the treatment
algorithm; combination with insulin requires specialist input.

## Chronic weight management (Wegovy)

Per the UK SmPC:

- Wegovy SmPC. <https://www.medicines.org.uk/emc/product/13901>

Indications: adjunct to a reduced-calorie diet and increased physical
activity for weight management in adults with an initial BMI of:

- ≥ 30 kg/m² (obesity); or
- ≥ 27 kg/m² to < 30 kg/m² (overweight) with at least one weight-related
  comorbidity (e.g. dysglycaemia / prediabetes, hypertension, dyslipidaemia,
  obstructive sleep apnoea, cardiovascular disease).

NICE technology appraisal:

- NICE TA875 (overlap) and TA875 / TA926 *Semaglutide for managing
  overweight and obesity.*
  <https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/ta875>
  See NICE published guidance index for current TA IDs:
  <https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/published>

UK MHRA semaglutide guidance and Drug Safety Update items track
post-marketing concerns (off-label use, suicidality signals, GI
adverse events). See <https://www.gov.uk/drug-safety-update>.

## Contraindications

Per the Wegovy / Ozempic SmPC:

- Hypersensitivity to semaglutide or any excipient.
- Personal or family history of medullary thyroid carcinoma.
- Multiple endocrine neoplasia syndrome type 2 (MEN 2).
- Pregnancy (women of childbearing potential should use contraception;
  discontinue at least 2 months before planned conception).
- Severe gastrointestinal disease (e.g. gastroparesis, severe inflammatory
  bowel disease).

## Cautions

- History of pancreatitis (discontinue if suspected; do not restart if
  confirmed).
- Diabetic retinopathy in patients with T2D (rapid glycaemic improvement
  has been associated with transient worsening of retinopathy in SUSTAIN-6).
- Severe renal impairment (eGFR < 15 mL/min/1.73 m²) — limited data.
- Severe hepatic impairment — limited data.
- Risk of dehydration with persistent GI side effects, particularly with
  RAAS inhibitors.

## Eligibility categorisation (this form)

| Category | Criteria |
| --- | --- |
| **Eligible** | Indication met, no absolute contraindications, no high-risk relative contraindications. |
| **Conditional** | Indication met but ≥ 1 relative contraindication, recent pancreatitis, severe renal impairment, or mental-health risk requiring specialist review. |
| **Ineligible** | Any absolute contraindication (MTC / MEN 2, pregnancy, hypersensitivity, severe gastroparesis). |

## Mental-health considerations

The MHRA and EMA reviewed (2023–2024) signals of suicidal ideation in
patients on GLP-1 agonists; current conclusion is that a causal link has
not been established but vigilance is warranted. See EMA review
<https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/news/ema-statement-ongoing-review-glp-1-receptor-agonists>
and MHRA *Drug Safety Update* April 2024.
