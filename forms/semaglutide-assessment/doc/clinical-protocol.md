# Clinical protocol

Operational protocol for semaglutide initiation review.

## Indications

- Type 2 diabetes inadequately controlled on existing therapy (Ozempic
  subcutaneous; Rybelsus oral).
- Chronic weight management in adults with BMI ≥ 30 kg/m², or BMI ≥ 27
  with at least one weight-related comorbidity (Wegovy).

## Preconditions

- Recent HbA1c (within 3 months for T2D indication).
- Recent eGFR (within 12 months).
- Documented BMI and weight trajectory.
- Personal and family history including thyroid and pancreatic disease.
- Mental-health history including eating disorders.
- Pregnancy status / contraception in women of childbearing potential.

## Step-by-step

1. **Demographics.** Identifiers; ethnicity; pregnancy status.
2. **Indication & Goals.** T2D vs weight management; target HbA1c or
   target weight loss.
3. **Body Composition.** Height, weight, BMI, waist circumference.
   Ethnicity-adjusted BMI thresholds per NICE NG28 / CG189 / NG246
   (lower thresholds for South Asian, Chinese, African-Caribbean and
   Black African ethnicities).
4. **Metabolic Profile.** HbA1c, fasting glucose, lipid panel, LFTs,
   eGFR, urate.
5. **Cardiovascular Risk.** BP, prior MI / stroke / heart failure, QRISK3
   or SCORE2. (Semaglutide has demonstrated cardiovascular benefit in
   SUSTAIN-6 and SELECT trials.)
6. **Contraindications Screening.** Personal or family history of medullary
   thyroid carcinoma, MEN 2, hypersensitivity, pregnancy, severe
   gastroparesis.
7. **GI History.** Pancreatitis, gallstone disease, severe IBD, prior
   bariatric surgery.
8. **Current Medications.** Insulin (consider dose reduction at
   initiation), sulphonylurea (consider dose reduction), warfarin (risk of
   interaction with food intake change), RAAS inhibitors (dehydration
   risk), oral contraceptives (no interaction with subcutaneous; possible
   reduced absorption with Rybelsus around dose).
9. **Mental Health Screening.** Active eating disorder; depression /
   suicidality (PHQ-9, ASRM where relevant). EMA / MHRA reviewed suicide-
   ideation signal in 2023–2024.
10. **Treatment Plan.** Eligible / Conditional / Ineligible; dose-
    escalation schedule per SmPC; monitoring plan.

## Dose escalation (subcutaneous semaglutide — Ozempic / Wegovy)

Standard escalation per SmPC:

| Week | Dose |
| --- | --- |
| 1–4 | 0.25 mg weekly |
| 5–8 | 0.5 mg weekly |
| 9–12 | 1.0 mg weekly |
| 13–16 | 1.7 mg weekly (Wegovy) / continue (Ozempic) |
| 17+ | 2.4 mg weekly (Wegovy maintenance) / 1.0–2.0 mg (Ozempic maintenance) |

Slow titration reduces gastrointestinal adverse effects.

## Monitoring

- 4–8 weeks after initiation: side-effect review; HbA1c trajectory
  (T2D); weight trajectory (Wegovy).
- 3 months: HbA1c reduction target ≥ 11 mmol/mol or weight reduction ≥
  5 % suggests continued use; NICE TA875 references treatment-success
  criteria for continuation.
- Ongoing: BP, lipids, eGFR; pregnancy status as relevant.

## Stopping criteria

- Suspected pancreatitis → discontinue and investigate; do not restart if
  confirmed.
- Severe GI symptoms unresolved despite titration.
- Pregnancy or planning pregnancy → stop ≥ 2 months before conception.
- Insufficient response at 3–6 months per local commissioning policy.
