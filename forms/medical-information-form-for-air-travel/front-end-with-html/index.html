14-step single-page wizard for fitness-to-fly assessment and airline-medical-desk submission.

Single-page MEDIF wizard. Part 1 is completed by the passenger or an accessible-travel agent; Part 2 by the attending physician. The form captures passenger identification, trip details, the clinical evaluation, and requested in-flight medical accommodations, then computes a fitness-to-fly band and a list of safety flags suitable for the airline medical desk.

This is clinical decision support. The final fitness-to-fly decision is taken by the airline medical desk; the attending physician remains responsible for the clinical declaration in step 14.
