Medical Information Form for Air Travel

A MEDIF is a standardized airline document used to assess a passenger's fitness to fly and determine if special in-flight medical equipment (like oxygen or stretchers) is required. It is typically completed in two parts by the passenger and their attending physician.What It ContainsThe form generally requires the following key details:Passenger Information: Booking details and trip specifications.Medical Condition: Diagnosis, stability of the condition, and recent surgeries or medical episodes.In-Flight Needs: Requirements for extra seating, stretchers, medical escorts, or personal medical devices (e.g., Portable Oxygen Concentrators).How to SubmitTimeline: The completed MEDIF must usually be submitted to the airline's medical desk no later than 72 hours before departure, though some carriers prefer it at least 48 hours prior.Process: The form can be accessed through your airline's special assistance or accessible travel portal (like Emirates Accessible Travel or Air India Medical Assistance). It is highly recommended to check with your specific airline's booking policies before arranging travel.Who Needs It?You typically need a MEDIF if you:Have a communicable disease.Require a stretcher, incubator, or supplementary oxygen in-flight.Have a medical condition that might become unstable or cause distress to other passengers.Require medical equipment that uses battery power (which must be cleared under dangerous goods regulations).

The Medical Information Form (MEDIF) is a standard document required by airlines to determine if a passenger with specific health needs is [fit to fly](https://dutydoctor.com/services/travel-letters/). It ensures the airline can provide necessary [special assistance](https://www.lot.com/is/en/explore/inspirations/blog/health-in-the-clouds/medif-form) or medical equipment during the journey. [1, 2, 3, 4, 5]

## When You Need a MEDIF

You generally must submit a MEDIF if you fall into these categories: [6, 7]

- Medical Requirements: You need a [stretcher, incubator](https://www.airindia.com/in/en/travel-information/health-medical-assistance/medical-information-form.html), or [supplemental oxygen](https://www.ana.co.jp/share/assist_eng/pdf/medif.pdf) during the flight.
- Recent Health Events: You have had a recent [surgery, serious injury](https://www.emirates.com/english/before-you-fly/health/medical-information-forms/), or [hospitalization](https://www.finnair.com/gb-en/frequently-asked-questions/special-assistance-and-health/what-is-a-medical-clearance-and-when-do-i-need-one--1904960).
- Pregnancy: Most airlines require a form for pregnancies [beyond 32 weeks](https://www.lot.com/ba/en/journey/special-services/travel-documents/passengers-travellng-with-medical-certificate) or those with complications.
- Chronic Conditions: You have unstable conditions like [severe anemia](https://www.lot.com/content/dam/lot/lot-com/documents/medif/MEDIF-EN-OCT25.pdf.coredownload.pdf), cardiac failure, or [respiratory issues](https://img.static-kl.com/m/6b384c6649c8dbc3/original/Medical-Form-KLM_EN.pdf). [1, 8, 9, 10, 11, 12, 13, 14, 15]

## How the Form Works

The MEDIF typically consists of two main parts: [16]

1.  Part 1 (Passenger/Agent): You provide booking details, flight information, and the [specific assistance](https://webassets2.starlux-airlines.com/en-TW/Images/Medical-Information-Form_tcm14-4087.pdf) you are requesting.
2.  Part 2 (Attending Physician): Your [treating doctor](https://www.emirates.com/pk/english/before-you-fly/health/medical-information-forms/) provides a detailed diagnosis and [clinical evaluation](https://www.britishairways.com/cms/global/pdfs/health/BA_Medical_Information_Form.pdf) to confirm your fitness for air travel. [17, 18, 19, 20, 21]

## Submission Deadlines

Timelines vary by carrier, but the form usually must be submitted: [22, 23, 24]

- Emirates/Qatar Airways: At least [48 hours before](https://www.qatarairways.com/iw-cc/qatar/MEDIF.jsp) departure.
- LOT Polish Airlines: No later than [72 hours before](https://www.lot.com/ba/en/explore/inspirations/blog/health-in-the-clouds/medif-form) departure.
- Validity: The form must typically be completed within [10 to 14 days](https://www.united.com/en/us/fly/travel/accessibility-and-assistance/medical-conditions.html) of your initial flight date. [8, 25, 26, 27, 28]
