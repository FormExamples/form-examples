This structured urinalysis report is aligned with UK SMI B41 and RCPath urinalysis reporting standards, including significance-of- bacteriuria principles, the recognition of asymptomatic bacteriuria in pregnancy as significant, and the NICE NG12 visible-haematuria pathway. It is a reporting aid, not a diagnosis; interpret and act in line with local pathways and clinical judgement.
