Employer-reply wizard for a workplace neurodiversity reasonable-adjustments request. A line manager, HR adviser, or occupational-health lead records the responding manager and originating request, the worker identification, the overall decision and its rationale (and, where anything is declined, the reasonableness category), the adjustment categories agreed and any alternatives offered, the trial period and review arrangements, the support / resources / responsibilities and point of contact, and any escalation. The engine computes a four-axis grade (outcome classification, legal / discrimination risk, response completeness, and follow-up / review urgency) plus a set of compliance-and-risk flags.

Declining adjustments for a worker likely covered by the Equality Act 2010 without an adequate reasonableness justification or alternatives escalates the legal-risk axis to high-risk and raises the discrimination-risk flag. This is decision support; the employer remains responsible for the reply and for the duty to make reasonable adjustments.
