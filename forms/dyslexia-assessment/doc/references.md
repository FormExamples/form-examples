# References — dyslexia assessment

## Definitions

- Rose, J. (2009). *Identifying and Teaching Children and Young People
  with Dyslexia and Literacy Difficulties* (the "Rose Review"). Department
  for Children, Schools and Families. Available at the UK National
  Archives: https://webarchive.nationalarchives.gov.uk
  (search for "Rose Report dyslexia 2009").

- British Dyslexia Association (current). About dyslexia and the BDA
  definition: https://www.bdadyslexia.org.uk/dyslexia/about-dyslexia

- International Dyslexia Association (current). Definition of dyslexia:
  https://dyslexiaida.org/definition-of-dyslexia/

## Assessment standards and guidance

- SpLD Assessment Standards Committee (SASC). *SASC Guidance on
  Assessment of SpLDs in Higher Education* and *Test Reviews*. Updated
  versions available at: https://www.sasc.org.uk

- Patoss (Professional Association of Teachers of Students with Specific
  Learning Difficulties): https://www.patoss-dyslexia.org

- Joint Council for Qualifications (current annual edition). *Adjustments
  for candidates with disabilities and learning difficulties: Access
  Arrangements and Reasonable Adjustments*: https://www.jcq.org.uk

## Classification systems

- American Psychiatric Association (2022). *Diagnostic and Statistical
  Manual of Mental Disorders, Fifth Edition, Text Revision (DSM-5-TR)*.
  Codes 315.00 / F81.0 (impairment in reading) and 315.2 / F81.81
  (impairment in written expression).

- World Health Organization (2024). *ICD-11 for Mortality and Morbidity
  Statistics*. Code 6A03 — Developmental learning disorder.
  https://icd.who.int/browse11/l-m/en

## Key research papers

- Snowling, M. J. (2013). Early identification and interventions for

  Educational Needs*, 13(1), 7–14.
  DOI: https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1471-3802.2012.01262.x

- Hulme, C., & Snowling, M. J. (2016). Reading disorders and dyslexia.
  *Current Opinion in Pediatrics*, 28(6), 731–735.
  DOI: https://doi.org/10.1097/MOP.0000000000000411
  PubMed ID: 27496059

- Peterson, R. L., & Pennington, B. F. (2015). Developmental dyslexia.
  *Annual Review of Clinical Psychology*, 11, 283–307.
  DOI: https://doi.org/10.1146/annurev-clinpsy-032814-112842
