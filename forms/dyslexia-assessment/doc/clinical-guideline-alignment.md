# Clinical guideline alignment — dyslexia

## SpLD Assessment Standards Committee (SASC)

Source: SpLD Assessment Standards Committee. Guidance for assessors of
specific learning difficulties.
URL: https://www.sasc.org.uk

SASC publishes the *Guidelines for the Assessment of SpLDs* (most recent
substantive update 2019, with subsequent amendments) which set the
standards for diagnostic reports in the UK. This form's structure aligns
with the SASC report template:

- **Background information** — captured in Developmental History and
  Educational Background.
- **Behavioural observations** — captured in clinician notes during
  testing.
- **Test scores** — captured in Reading, Writing & Spelling, Phonological
  Processing, and Working Memory & Processing Speed steps.
- **Conclusions and recommendations** — captured in the Recommendations
  & Support Plan step.

## British Dyslexia Association (BDA)

URL: https://www.bdadyslexia.org.uk

The BDA's definition of dyslexia (Rose, 2009; adopted by the BDA Management
Board) frames the assessment scope:

> "Dyslexia is a learning difficulty that primarily affects the skills
> involved in accurate and fluent word reading and spelling.
> Characteristic features of dyslexia are difficulties in phonological
> awareness, verbal memory and verbal processing speed. Dyslexia occurs
> across the range of intellectual abilities."

The Phonological Processing and Working Memory & Processing Speed steps
directly capture these defining features.

## International Dyslexia Association (IDA)

URL: https://dyslexiaida.org

The IDA's research-grounded definition is broadly compatible with the
BDA / Rose definition and emphasises the phonological deficit and the
neurobiological origin. The IDA *Knowledge and Practice Standards for
Teachers of Reading* informs the recommendations captured in the Support
Plan step.

## DSM-5-TR

American Psychiatric Association (2022) *Diagnostic and Statistical Manual
of Mental Disorders, Fifth Edition, Text Revision (DSM-5-TR)*.
Code **315.00 / F81.0** — Specific Learning Disorder with impairment in
**reading**.
Code **315.2 / F81.81** — Specific Learning Disorder with impairment in
**written expression**.
Code **315.1 / F81.2** — Specific Learning Disorder with impairment in
**mathematics** (not the focus of this form).

DSM-5-TR criteria require:

- Difficulties learning and using academic skills for at least 6 months
  despite intervention.
- Affected academic skills are substantially and quantifiably below those
  expected for the individual's chronological age.
- Difficulties begin during school-age years.
- Difficulties are not better accounted for by intellectual disabilities,
  uncorrected visual or auditory acuity, other mental or neurological
  disorders, psychosocial adversity, lack of proficiency in the language
  of instruction, or inadequate educational instruction.

DSM-5-TR severity specifiers (mild / moderate / severe) map to the form's
severity classification.

## ICD-11

World Health Organization. ICD-11 MMS.
URL: https://icd.who.int/browse11/l-m/en

Code **6A03** — Developmental learning disorder.

Sub-codes:

- **6A03.0** — Developmental learning disorder with impairment in
  reading.
- **6A03.1** — Developmental learning disorder with impairment in written
  expression.
- **6A03.2** — Developmental learning disorder with impairment in
  mathematics.
- **6A03.3** — Developmental learning disorder with other specified
  impairment of learning.
- **6A03.Z** — Developmental learning disorder, unspecified.

## NICE

NICE has not published a dyslexia-specific guideline. Relevant guidance
appears in:

- NICE quality standards for special educational needs (where applicable
  to the education sector).
- See https://www.nice.org.uk for the index.

In educational settings the **SEND Code of Practice** (UK Department for
Education, 2015, with subsequent amendments) governs reasonable
adjustments and Education, Health and Care (EHC) plans.

## Joint Council for Qualifications (JCQ)

For access arrangements in UK public examinations the JCQ *Adjustments
for candidates with disabilities and learning difficulties: Access
Arrangements and Reasonable Adjustments* document is the authoritative
reference. Updated annually.
URL: https://www.jcq.org.uk
