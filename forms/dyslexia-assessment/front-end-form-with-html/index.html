Please complete this questionnaire to support a structured assessment of specific learning difficulties. Standardised scores have a mean of 100 and a standard deviation of 15; the lowest domain score sets the overall severity classification.

All answers are saved automatically in your browser as you go. This screening tool is not a substitute for a full diagnostic assessment by a qualified specialist.

Standardised score categories: 85-115 average (no dyslexia), 70-84 below average (mild), 55-69 well below average (moderate), <55 significantly below average (severe). For information only; please discuss results with a specialist clinician or educational psychologist.
