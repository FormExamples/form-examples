# Clinical references

Authoritative guidance underpinning the four-axis grading engine.

## Cancer-pathway urgency (NICE NG12 / DG56)

### Upper GI — two-week-wait (NICE NG12)

Refer on a suspected-cancer (two-week-wait) pathway for **oesophageal or stomach
cancer** if the patient has:

- **dysphagia** (at any age), **or**
- **age ≥ 55 with weight loss** plus upper abdominal pain, reflux, or dyspepsia.

Consider **non-urgent** direct-access OGD in people aged ≥ 55 with
treatment-resistant dyspepsia, or with reflux plus other features.

### Lower GI — FIT triage (NICE DG56)

Refer adults on a suspected colorectal-cancer pathway if the **FIT result is
≥ 10 µg haemoglobin per gram of faeces**. A FIT < 10 µg/g lowers probability but
NG12 features and clinical judgement still apply; persistent or high-risk
features warrant escalation regardless of FIT.

## Appropriateness (ACR / ASGE Appropriate Use Criteria / EPAGE)

Ordinal 1–9 scale in three bands: usually-appropriate (7–9),
may-be-appropriate (4–6), usually-not-appropriate (1–3). Mapped per
(indication × procedure).

## Pre-procedure risk

### Glasgow-Blatchford score (0–23)

Pre-endoscopy, non-invasive bleeding score from urea, haemoglobin, systolic BP,
pulse, melaena, syncope, hepatic disease, and cardiac failure. A score of **0**
identifies very low-risk patients suitable for outpatient management.

### Pre-endoscopy Rockall score (0–11)

Uses age, shock (haemodynamic status), and comorbidity (pre-endoscopy
component); the full Rockall adds endoscopic diagnosis and stigmata. Higher
scores predict rebleeding and mortality.

### BSG / ESGE 2021 anticoagulant / antiplatelet management

Procedures are stratified **low-risk** (diagnostic ± biopsy, stent placement)
vs **high-risk** (polypectomy, sphincterotomy / ERCP, EMR, ESD, dilation,
variceal therapy, PEG, EUS-guided sampling, RFA).

- **Aspirin:** continue for almost all procedures (exceptions: ESD, large
  colonic EMR > 2 cm, upper-GI EMR, ampullectomy).
- **Clopidogrel / P2Y12:** continue for low-risk; stop 7 days before high-risk
  (consider liaison if recent coronary stent).
- **DOACs:** for high-risk procedures omit the last dose ≥ 48 h before; for
  dabigatran with CrCl/eGFR 30–50 mL/min omit ≥ 72 h before.
- **Warfarin:** continue (with INR check) for low-risk; for high-risk, stop
  ~5 days before and bridge if high thrombotic risk.

## ASA physical-status classification

I (healthy) … V (moribund). ASA IV+ flags a high-acuity case that may need a
consultant-led list and anaesthetic support.

## Triage tiers

| Tier | Target | Triggers |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Emergency | same day (< 24 h) | acute / active GI bleed, haemodynamic instability |
| Two-week-wait | ≤ 14 days | NICE NG12 dysphagia / age ≥ 55 + weight loss; FIT ≥ 10 µg/g (DG56) |
| Urgent | ≤ ~2 weeks | symptomatic anaemia, significant unexplained symptoms |
| Routine | standard booking | surveillance, stable dyspepsia, polyp / IBD follow-up |
