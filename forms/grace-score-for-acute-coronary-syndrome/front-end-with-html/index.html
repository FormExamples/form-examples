Complete the eight GRACE admission variables below for an adult with a suspected or confirmed acute coronary syndrome. Each variable maps through a weighted, banded lookup — this is not a simple tally — and the points are summed into a total that maps to an estimated in-hospital and 6-month mortality band and an overall Low / Intermediate / High risk category. Answers are saved automatically in your browser as you go.

GRACE quantifies prognosis and helps decide the timing of an invasive (coronary angiography) strategy; it does not diagnose acute coronary syndrome. A Low category does not exclude an evolving infarction or high-risk anatomy — use clinical judgement alongside troponin pathways and the ECG.

The GRACE (Global Registry of Acute Coronary Events) risk score is drawn from Granger et al. (Arch Intern Med 2003), Fox et al. (BMJ 2006; BMJ Open 2014, GRACE 2.0), the ESC acute coronary syndrome guidelines (2023), and NICE NG185. This tool uses representative published GRACE point tables and is for information only; it is not a diagnosis. Escalate and treat in line with local policy and clinical judgement.
