The GRACE score is a validated acute coronary syndrome risk-stratification instrument. This dashboard is a clinical-review tool and is not a diagnosis. A High category (GRACE > 140 in-hospital, or > 118 at six months) prompts an early invasive strategy — coronary angiography within 24 hours with senior cardiology review.
