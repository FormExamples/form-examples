The Waterlow score is the most widely used pressure-ulcer risk tool in United Kingdom nursing. This dashboard is a clinical-review tool and is not a diagnosis. A higher total means higher risk; escalate the pressure-relieving support surface, repositioning, and skin-care plan in line with the band and local policy.
