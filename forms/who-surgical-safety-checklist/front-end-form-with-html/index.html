Single-page implementation of the WHO Surgical Safety Checklist (WHO/IER/PSP/2008.05, Safe Surgery Saves Lives). The checklist is completed by the operating team — typically the circulating nurse, anaesthetist, and surgeon — for every surgical case across three phases: before induction of anaesthesia (Sign In), before skin incision (Time Out), and before the patient leaves the operating room (Sign Out).

This tool is a process-adherence record (Class I medical device software under MDCG 2019-11). It does not provide clinical decision support; the operating team retains full clinical responsibility.
