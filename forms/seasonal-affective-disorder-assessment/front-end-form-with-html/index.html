Please answer the questions below about your mood, sleep, energy, appetite, and seasonal patterns over the past two weeks (PHQ-9) and across the year (SPAQ). This combined screening tool is designed to identify Seasonal Affective Disorder (SAD) and co-occurring depression. All answers are saved automatically in your browser as you go.

If you are having thoughts of harming yourself or feel unsafe right now, please contact the Samaritans free on 116 123 (UK / ROI), or call 999 or go to your nearest A&E if you are in immediate danger.

The SPAQ (Seasonal Pattern Assessment Questionnaire) Global Seasonality Score and the PHQ-9 (Patient Health Questionnaire — 9 item) are validated screening instruments. This tool is for information only and is not a diagnosis; please discuss your results with your clinician.
