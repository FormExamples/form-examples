# Documentation — US HIPAA Authorization Form

Reference documents for the HIPAA-authorization validity engine and the
sensitive-category overlays that supplement it.

## Files

- [`hipaa-privacy-rule.md`](hipaa-privacy-rule.md) — 45 CFR § 164.508
  core elements and required statements with quoted regulatory text.
- [`sensitive-categories.md`](sensitive-categories.md) — 42 CFR Part 2,
  HIV/AIDS, mental health, psychotherapy notes, and genetic information
  with state-specific notes.
- [`state-templates.md`](state-templates.md) — cross-walk to the
  Tennessee HS-2557, Pennsylvania DHS, and HHS-OCR sample HIPAA
  authorization forms.
- [`validation-rules.md`](validation-rules.md) — rule-by-rule table
  with predicate, citation, priority, and remediation guidance.

## Primary regulatory sources

- 45 CFR Part 160 — General administrative requirements.
- 45 CFR Part 164, Subpart E — Privacy of individually identifiable
  health information.
- 42 CFR Part 2 — Confidentiality of substance-use disorder records.
- 38 U.S.C. § 7332 — Veterans' Affairs sensitive records.
- HHS Office for Civil Rights guidance and sample forms.
- Public Law 104-191 (HIPAA, 1996) and Public Law 111-5 (HITECH, 2009).
