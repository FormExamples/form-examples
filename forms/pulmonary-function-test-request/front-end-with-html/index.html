Clinician referral wizard for a lung-function / spirometry examination. Record the requesting clinician, patient identification, the requested test, the clinical indication and specific question, respiratory symptoms, smoking and inhaler background, a focused safety / contraindication screen, and the requested urgency. The engine computes a four-axis grade (appropriateness, safety / contraindication, request completeness, and triage priority) plus a set of safety-critical flags.

This is clinical decision support for the lung-function vetting desk. The final triage and booking decision remains the responsibility of the vetting respiratory physiologist or physician.
