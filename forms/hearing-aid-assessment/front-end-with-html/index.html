The HHIE-S (Hearing Handicap Inventory for the Elderly - Screening) is a 10-item validated screening questionnaire. Total scores 0-8 = no handicap, 10-22 = mild to moderate handicap, 24-40 = significant handicap. This screening tool is for information only and is not a diagnosis; please discuss your results with your audiologist or clinician.
