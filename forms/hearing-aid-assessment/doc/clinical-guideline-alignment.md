# Clinical guideline alignment

## NICE

- NICE NG98 *Hearing loss in adults: assessment and management*. London:
  NICE, 2018. <https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/ng98>
- NICE TA566 *Cochlear implants for children and adults with severe to
  profound deafness*. <https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/ta566>
- NICE QS185 *Hearing loss in adults*.
  <https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/qs185>
- NICE NG98 §1.6 recommends bilateral fitting unless contraindicated.

## ASHA

- American Speech-Language-Hearing Association. *Hearing aids for adults*.
  <https://www.asha.org/policy/>
- ASHA Practice Portal on adult hearing aids:
  <https://www.asha.org/practice-portal/professional-issues/hearing-aids-for-adults/>

## BSA / BAA

- British Society of Audiology / British Academy of Audiology. *Practice
  guidance: aural rehabilitation for adults*.
  <https://www.thebsa.org.uk/resources/>
- BSA *Recommended procedure: real ear measurements*. BSA, 2018.

## NHS England

- NHS England. *Commissioning Services for People with Hearing Loss: A
  Framework for Clinical Commissioning Groups*. NHS England, 2016.

## ENT-UK / British Society of Otology

- ENT-UK. *Adult hearing aid services*.
- ENT-UK *Guidance on asymmetric hearing loss*.
  <https://www.entuk.org/_userfiles/pages/files/guidelines/asymmetric_hearing_loss_guidance.pdf>

## AAO-HNS / Cochlear implant candidacy

- AAO-HNS *Position statement: cochlear implants*.
  <https://www.entnet.org/resource/position-statement-cochlear-implants/>
- UK BCIG (British Cochlear Implant Group). *Quality standards*.
  <https://www.bcig.org.uk/>

## OTC hearing aid regulation

- US FDA. *Over-the-counter (OTC) hearing aid final rule*. 21 CFR Part 800
  (effective 17 October 2022). <https://www.fda.gov/medical-devices/hearing-aids/otc-hearing-aids-what-you-should-know>
