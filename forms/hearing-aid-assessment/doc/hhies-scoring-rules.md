# HHIE-S scoring rules

## Instrument

The **Hearing Handicap Inventory for the Elderly — Screening (HHIE-S)** is a
10-item screening version of the original 25-item HHIE. It quantifies
self-perceived emotional and social handicap from hearing loss in adults ≥65
years and is widely used as a hearing-aid-candidacy triage tool.

- Ventry IM, Weinstein BE. *The Hearing Handicap Inventory for the
  Elderly: a new tool*. Ear Hear 1982;3:128-34. PMID: 7095321
- Lichtenstein MJ, Bess FH, Logan SA. *Validation of screening tools for
  identifying hearing-impaired elderly in primary care*. JAMA 1988;259:
  2875-2878. PMID: 3367454
- Weinstein BE. *Validity of a screening protocol for identifying elderly
  people with hearing problems*. ASHA 1986;28:41-5. PMID: 3717593

## Items

10 items: 5 emotional + 5 social/situational. Each item scored:

- **Yes = 4**
- **Sometimes = 2**
- **No = 0**

Total range **0-40**.

## Cut-points

| Score | Probability of hearing handicap |
| --- | --- |
| 0-8 | No self-perceived hearing handicap (13% chance) |
| 10-24 | Mild-to-moderate hearing handicap (50% chance) |
| 26-40 | Significant self-perceived hearing handicap (84% chance) |

(Lichtenstein 1988 derivation; sensitivity 72%, specificity 77% against
audiometric pure-tone threshold ≥40 dB HL.)

## Use in hearing-aid candidacy

Per NICE NG98 and ASHA guidance, the HHIE-S is one of the recommended
self-report instruments to confirm hearing-aid candidacy where audiometric
thresholds alone do not predict perceived disability. The form aligns the
HHIE-S band with the audiogram results:

| HHIE-S band | Audiogram PTA | Action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0-8 | Any | Reassurance; recheck in 12 months |
| 10-24 | ≤ 25 dB | Counsel; consider PSAP / situational use |
| 10-24 | > 25 dB | Hearing aid candidacy trial |
| 26-40 | Any | Hearing aid trial; bilateral fitting preferred |

## Real-Ear Measurement (REM)

The form records real-ear-measurement (REM) verification per NHS England /
British Society of Audiology recommended procedure, mandatory in NHS adult
hearing-aid fitting since 2013:

- Target gain at 0.5/1/2/4 kHz within ±5 dB.
- Tolerance for high-frequency emphasis per NAL-NL2 prescription.

## Aided benefit outcome measures

- **APHAB** (Abbreviated Profile of Hearing Aid Benefit) — 24 items.
  Cox RM, Alexander GC. *Ear Hear* 1995;16:176-86. PMID: 7789669
- **COSI** (Client Oriented Scale of Improvement) — Dillon H et al. 1997.
- **IOI-HA** (International Outcome Inventory for Hearing Aids) — Cox RM
  et al. *Int J Audiol* 2002;41:30-5. PMID: 12467367

## Flagged-issue triggers

- HHIE-S ≥ 10 with audiogram PTA ≤ 25 dB → hidden hearing loss /
  auditory processing referral consideration.
- HHIE-S ≥ 26 with no current hearing aid → hearing-aid fitting referral.
- HHIE-S ≥ 26 with binaural symmetric loss → bilateral fitting flag (NICE
  NG98 §1.6).
- HHIE-S ≥ 26 with asymmetric loss → MRI IAM screen first.
