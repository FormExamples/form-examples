# Regulatory framework — workplace safety assessment

The Workplace Safety Assessment supports the statutory duty on UK
employers to ensure, so far as is reasonably practicable, the health,
safety, and welfare at work of all employees.

## Primary statute

- **Health and Safety at Work etc. Act 1974** (c. 37) — the foundation
  Act. Section 2(1) imposes the general duty on employers; s.3 covers
  duties to non-employees; s.4 covers occupiers' duties.
  <https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1974/37/contents>

## Principal regulations

- **Management of Health and Safety at Work Regulations 1999**
  (SI 1999/3242) — requires every employer to make a suitable and
  sufficient assessment of the risks to the health and safety of
  employees (regulation 3).
  <https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1999/3242/contents>
- **Workplace (Health, Safety and Welfare) Regulations 1992**
  (SI 1992/3004) — workplace condition standards (ventilation,
  temperature, lighting, room dimensions, sanitary conveniences).
  <https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1992/3004/contents>
- **Provision and Use of Work Equipment Regulations 1998 (PUWER)**
  (SI 1998/2306). <https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1998/2306/contents>
- **Personal Protective Equipment at Work Regulations 1992**
  (SI 1992/2966; amended SI 2022/8 to include "limb (b) workers").
  <https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1992/2966/contents>
- **Manual Handling Operations Regulations 1992** (SI 1992/2793).
  <https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1992/2793/contents>
- **Health and Safety (Display Screen Equipment) Regulations 1992**
  (SI 1992/2792). <https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1992/2792/contents>
- **Control of Substances Hazardous to Health Regulations 2002 (COSHH)**
  (SI 2002/2677). <https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2002/2677/contents>
- **Reporting of Injuries, Diseases and Dangerous Occurrences
  Regulations 2013 (RIDDOR)** (SI 2013/1471).
  <https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2013/1471/contents>
- **Regulatory Reform (Fire Safety) Order 2005** (SI 2005/1541).
  <https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2005/1541/contents>

  <https://www.hse.gov.uk/simple-health-safety/risk/index.htm>

  and Safety at Work Regulations 1999 — Approved Code of Practice and

  <https://www.hse.gov.uk/pubns/books/l21.htm>
- HSE — Workplace health, safety and welfare: Workplace (Health,

  <https://www.hse.gov.uk/pubns/books/l24.htm>

## Five steps to risk assessment

HSE's published risk-assessment methodology is:

1. **Identify the hazards.**
2. **Decide who might be harmed and how.**
3. **Evaluate the risks and decide on controls.**
4. **Record significant findings.**
5. **Review the assessment** and update where necessary.

Reference: HSE risk-management web pages (above) and HSG65 *Managing
for health and safety*.
<https://www.hse.gov.uk/pubns/books/hsg65.htm>

This implementation operationalises the five steps as the assessment
record's section structure.

## RIDDOR thresholds (selection)

RIDDOR reporting is required for:

- Work-related deaths.
- Specified injuries (e.g. fractures other than fingers/thumbs/toes;
  amputations; loss of sight; crush injuries to head or torso).
- Over-7-day incapacitation injuries.
- Occupational diseases (carpal tunnel syndrome, severe cramp,
  occupational dermatitis, hand-arm vibration syndrome, occupational
  asthma, tendonitis, certain biological agents, cancers from
  occupational exposure).
- Dangerous occurrences (specified events with high potential).

The implementation includes a RIDDOR-reportability checkbox per
incident and links to the online F2508 reporting service:
<https://www.hse.gov.uk/riddor/>.

## Penalties

Breach of HSWA 1974 or the regulations is a criminal offence. Since
the Health and Safety (Offences) Act 2008 most offences can be tried
either way (magistrates' court or Crown Court) with significant
maximum penalties. Sentencing follows the Sentencing Council *Health
and Safety Offences, Corporate Manslaughter and Food Safety and
Hygiene Offences Definitive Guideline* (effective 1 February 2016).

<https://www.sentencingcouncil.org.uk/overview/item/health-and-safety-offences-corporate-manslaughter-and-food-safety-and-hygiene-offences/>
