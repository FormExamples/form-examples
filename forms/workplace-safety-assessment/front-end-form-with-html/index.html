This auditor checklist is aligned with UK Health and Safety Executive (HSE) standards. Walk the area being audited and answer each item with Yes, No, or N/A. Use the observations field to capture evidence, photographs filed, or narrative detail. Findings are graded on submission.

Outcome categories: Compliant (all controls in place); Minor Findings (low-risk gaps, action within 90 days); Major Findings (moderate-risk gaps, action within 30 days); Critical Findings (imminent risk, immediate corrective action). Answers are saved automatically in your browser.

Aligned with the UK Health and Safety Executive (HSE) workplace safety audit framework. Findings should be transcribed into the local risk register and action plan; critical findings require immediate corrective action and may be RIDDOR-reportable.
