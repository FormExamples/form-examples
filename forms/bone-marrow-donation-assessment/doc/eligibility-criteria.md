# Eligibility criteria

The bone-marrow-donation-assessment form supports donor evaluation for
haematopoietic stem cell donation. It captures HLA typing, medical
history, anaesthetic fitness, infectious disease screening, and
psychological readiness, and supports a decision on suitability and on
collection method (peripheral blood stem cells (PBSC) via apheresis vs
bone marrow harvest under general anaesthesia).

## Donor registries and standards

UK adult unrelated donors are recruited and managed by:

- **Anthony Nolan**: <https://www.anthonynolan.org/>
- **NHS Blood and Transplant — British Bone Marrow Registry (BBMR)**:
  <https://www.nhsbt.nhs.uk/bone-marrow/>
- **DKMS UK**: <https://www.dkms.org.uk/>
- **Welsh Bone Marrow Donor Registry** (WBMDR), Welsh Blood Service:
  <https://www.welsh-blood.org.uk/donating-blood/welsh-bone-marrow-donor-registry/>

The form mirrors the published standards of the World Marrow Donor
Association (WMDA) which co-ordinates international donor registries.

- WMDA standards: <https://share.wmda.info/display/WMDAS/WMDA+Standards>
- WMDA donor medical suitability documents: <https://wmda.info/>

## Decision categories

| Eligibility | Definition |
| ----------- | ---------- |
| Suitable | Healthy donor, no contraindications; ideal HLA match |
| Conditionally suitable | Minor health issues, partial match; further evaluation required |
| Unsuitable | Contraindicated by medical history, infection risk, or HLA mismatch |

| Risk level | Definition |
| ---------- | ---------- |
| Low | Ideal HLA match, healthy donor, no comorbidities |
| Moderate | Minor manageable conditions |
| High | Borderline fitness, significant comorbidities |
| Critical | Contraindicated, serious donor-safety concerns |

## HLA matching

HLA matching is performed at HLA-A, -B, -C, -DRB1, -DQB1 (the "10/10
match" standard) — extended to -DPB1 (12/12 match) in some protocols.

- Petersdorf EW. *HLA matching in allogeneic stem cell transplantation.*
  Curr Opin Hematol. 2004;11(6):386–391. DOI:
  <https://doi.org/10.1097/01.moh.0000142784.99000.78>
- BSHI / BSBMT joint guidelines on HLA matching:
  <https://bsbmtct.org/guidelines/>
- British Society for Histocompatibility and Immunogenetics (BSHI):
  <https://www.bshi.org.uk/>

## Donor health criteria (per WMDA / NHSBT / Anthony Nolan policy)

The form's permanent and temporary deferral lists follow the published
donor-suitability documents:

- Anthony Nolan donor health criteria:
  <https://www.anthonynolan.org/get-involved/donate-stem-cells/are-you-eligible-donate>
- DKMS donor health checklist: <https://www.dkms.org.uk/become-donor>
- WMDA medical suitability: <https://wmda.info/>

Common permanent contraindications include:

- HIV, HTLV-I/II, chronic active hepatitis B or C
- Active malignancy
- Significant cardiovascular disease (ischaemic heart disease, recent MI,
  uncontrolled arrhythmia)
- Severe autoimmune disease on immunosuppression
- Severe psychiatric illness compromising consent
- Pregnancy (temporary — defer through pregnancy and lactation)
- Significant inherited bleeding or clotting disorder
- Significant anaesthetic risk for the harvest procedure

Common temporary deferrals include:

- Recent infection
- Recent vaccination (vaccine-specific window)
- Recent body modification (tattoo, piercing)
- Recent travel to high-risk area for transmissible disease
- Pregnancy and lactation

## Collection method assessment

The form supports a decision between:

- **PBSC** — donor receives G-CSF for 4–5 days then donation via apheresis.
- **Bone marrow harvest** — donor receives general anaesthesia; marrow
  aspirated from posterior iliac crests in theatre.

Patient-recipient and donor preference are recorded; medical contraindications
to either method (e.g. anaesthetic risk for marrow harvest, sickle trait
considerations, splenomegaly history for G-CSF) are flagged.

## Anaesthetic assessment

ASA grade assessment per ASA classification:

- American Society of Anesthesiologists. *ASA Physical Status Classification
  System*: <https://www.asahq.org/standards-and-practice-parameters/statement-on-asa-physical-status-classification-system>

Donors are typically ASA 1 or 2; ASA 3 or higher prompts further
evaluation or alternative method.

## Output

- Eligibility (Suitable / Conditionally suitable / Unsuitable)
- Risk level (Low / Moderate / High / Critical)
- Recommended collection method
- Flagged issues
- Structured PDF for the donor registry record

## Notes

- The form is an assessment instrument; final donor clearance is performed
  by the donor's specialist clinic and the requesting transplant centre.
- HLA matching is performed in accredited laboratories; the form records
  the result rather than computing it.
- The form is not a recipient-suitability assessment tool — recipient
  selection follows separate BSBMT / EBMT criteria.
