# Assessment protocol

## Intended use

A donor-evaluation questionnaire that captures medical history, HLA typing,
infectious disease screening, anaesthetic fitness, and psychological
readiness; applies WMDA-aligned donor-suitability rules; and supports a
decision on suitability and collection method (PBSC vs bone marrow
harvest).

## Intended users

- Anthony Nolan donor evaluation team
- NHSBT BBMR / Welsh Bone Marrow Donor Registry / DKMS UK clinical teams
- Apheresis unit clinical staff
- Anaesthetists assessing donor for marrow harvest

## Setting

UK donor evaluation clinic (registry-based) or hospital pre-operative
assessment clinic (for marrow harvest donors).

## Workflow

1. **Demographics** — name, DOB, donor registry, donor number, contact
   details.
2. **Donor registration and HLA typing** — registry, HLA-A, -B, -C, -DRB1,
   -DQB1 (and -DPB1 if available); match grade (10/10 etc.); CMV serostatus;
   ABO and Rh type.
3. **Medical history** — chronic conditions, prior surgery, medications,
   allergies, family history of inherited bleeding/clotting or genetic
   disease; psychiatric history.
4. **Physical examination** — vital signs, BMI, cardiovascular and
   respiratory examination, abdominal examination (splenomegaly is a
   contraindication to G-CSF), iliac crest assessment for marrow harvest.
5. **Haematological assessment** — FBC including platelet count, reticulocyte
   count, ferritin, coagulation, HbS for sickle screen, G6PD if indicated.
6. **Infectious disease screening** — HIV-1/2, HTLV-I/II, HBV (HBsAg,
   anti-HBc, anti-HBs), HCV, syphilis serology, CMV, EBV, toxoplasma;
   additional regional pathogens per WMDA matrix.
7. **Anaesthetic assessment** — ASA grade, prior anaesthetic history,
   airway assessment, anticipated anaesthetic risk; required if marrow
   harvest under GA planned.
8. **Collection method assessment** — donor preference, contraindications
   to PBSC (splenomegaly, sickle trait considerations, autoimmune disease
   on G-CSF), contraindications to marrow harvest (anaesthetic risk).
9. **Psychological readiness** — donor understanding of process,
   willingness, social support, prior donation experience, freely-given
   informed consent.
10. **Consent and eligibility decision** — written informed consent for
    the chosen procedure; eligibility decision; final clinician sign-off.

## Output

- Eligibility (Suitable / Conditionally suitable / Unsuitable)
- Risk level (Low / Moderate / High / Critical)
- Recommended collection method (PBSC vs marrow harvest)
- Flagged-issues list
- Structured PDF for the donor registry record

## Safety-net behaviour

- Borderline FBC, ferritin, or coagulation → defer with retest schedule
- BMI > 40, severe sleep apnoea, OSA → urgent anaesthetic review for
  marrow harvest
- Sickle trait positive → use marrow harvest if PBSC contraindicated,
  per registry policy
- Splenomegaly → contraindication to G-CSF
- Prior severe vasovagal or psychiatric instability → registry medical
  director review
- Positive infectious disease marker → registry medical director review
  and donor counselling

## Out of scope

- Recipient transplant eligibility
- Conditioning regimens
- Post-donation follow-up beyond first cycle
- Cord blood donor selection (separate cord-blood registry pathway)
