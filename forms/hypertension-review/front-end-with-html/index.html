This is a control-classification and documentation-completeness instrument, not a numeric score. It classifies blood-pressure control against the patient's age- and comorbidity-specific target, assigns a hypertension stage, grades the review Complete / Partial / Incomplete, and raises flags — most importantly a severe-hypertension flag (clinic BP ≥ 180/120 mmHg). It does not diagnose or prescribe.

Submit the form to see the report.

The annual review follows NICE NG136 (Hypertension in adults: diagnosis and management), NICE CG181 (cardiovascular risk), and NICE NG203 (chronic kidney disease). This tool documents findings, classifies blood-pressure control, and prompts escalation; it is not a diagnosis and does not replace clinical judgement. Where a severe- hypertension flag is raised (clinic BP ≥ 180/120 mmHg), arrange same-day clinical assessment.
