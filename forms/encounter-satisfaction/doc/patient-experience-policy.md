# Patient experience policy framework

The Encounter Satisfaction form is intended to support routine
collection of patient experience data alongside national programmes
already in place in the UK and US.

## United Kingdom

- **NHS England — Patient experience improvement framework.** Trust-
  level framework setting out how providers should systematically
  collect, review, and act on patient experience data.
  <https://www.england.nhs.uk/patient-experience/>
- **NHS England — Friends and Family Test (FFT).** Mandated single-
  question feedback tool used across NHS-funded services in England.
  Open Government Licence v3.0.
  <https://www.england.nhs.uk/fft/>
- **CQC Adult Inpatient Survey** and **CQC Outpatient Survey** — the
  Care Quality Commission's national patient-experience surveys
  administered by the Picker Institute on behalf of CQC.
  <https://www.cqc.org.uk/publications/surveys>
- **NHS Long Term Plan** (2019) — chapter 6 commits the NHS to
  improving patient experience as a core quality dimension alongside
  effectiveness and safety. <https://www.longtermplan.nhs.uk/>

## United States

- **HCAHPS programme rules.** Public reporting of inpatient experience
  scores is tied to Medicare hospital payment under the Hospital Value-
  Based Purchasing programme.
  <https://www.cms.gov/medicare/quality/initiatives/hospital-quality-initiative/hospital-value-based-purchasing-program>
- **CG-CAHPS — Clinician & Group survey.** Used in MIPS and accountable-
  care reporting. <https://www.ahrq.gov/cahps/surveys-guidance/cg/index.html>

## International

- **WHO — Framework on integrated, people-centred health services.**
  World Health Assembly resolution WHA69.24 (2016) sets people-centred
  care, including measured patient experience, as one of five strategic
  directions.
  <https://www.who.int/publications/i/item/WHO-HIS-SDS-2015.20>
- **OECD — Patient-Reported Indicator Surveys (PaRIS).** International
  survey of patient experience and outcomes in primary care across OECD
  member states. <https://www.oecd.org/health/paris/>

## Operational guidance for this form

- ESS is a *practice-level improvement* tool, not a regulatory
  submission. Do not substitute ESS scores for FFT, HCAHPS, CG-CAHPS,
  or CQC survey responses where those are mandated.
- Free-text comments may contain safeguarding disclosures; route low
  satisfaction (mean ≤ 2.4) with a free-text comment to the practice
  manager for review within one working day.
- Demographic data collected on Step 1 is *protected characteristics*
  under the Equality Act 2010 and *special category* under UK GDPR
  Article 9 — handle accordingly.
