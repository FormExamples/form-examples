# Safety case notes

The Encounter Satisfaction form is a low-risk, post-encounter feedback
instrument. It does not drive clinical decisions and its outputs are
not used to triage, diagnose, or prescribe. The notes below capture
the limited risk pathways the form must handle.

## Free-text disclosure pathway

Free-text comments collected in Step 8 may include disclosures that
require escalation:

- **Safeguarding concerns** (mention of abuse, neglect, coercion) —
  route to the practice or trust safeguarding lead per local policy.
  See NHS England *Safeguarding adults and children* policy index.
  <https://www.england.nhs.uk/safeguarding/>
- **Active suicidal ideation or self-harm** — surface immediately to
  the duty clinician; reference NICE NG225 *Self-harm: assessment,
  management and preventing recurrence* (2022).
  <https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/ng225>
- **Patient safety incidents disclosed** — record under the *Learn
  from patient safety events (LFPSE)* national service that replaced
  the National Reporting and Learning System (NRLS) in 2023.
  <https://www.england.nhs.uk/patient-safety/learn-from-patient-safety-events-service/>
- **Complaint** — escalate per the local NHS complaints procedure
  under the *Local Authority Social Services and National Health
  Service Complaints (England) Regulations 2009*.
  <https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2009/309/contents/made>

These pathways are operational policy; the form itself does not
auto-classify free text. The recommendation is human triage by the
practice manager on the same working day for any submission with a
composite mean ≤ 2.4 or a 1-rated communication item.

## Selection and response bias

Voluntary post-encounter surveys exhibit known selection bias toward
both extremes. The form should not be used to compare clinicians or
services without:

- denominator correction (responses ÷ eligible encounters),
- minimum sample threshold (HCAHPS public reporting requires n ≥ 100
  per quarter for stability — *HCAHPS Quality Assurance Guidelines*,
  CMS, current version),
- and demographic adjustment where mix differs materially between
  units.

See AHRQ *CAHPS Analysis Program* documentation for case-mix
adjustment methodology.
<https://www.ahrq.gov/cahps/surveys-guidance/helpful-resources/analysis/index.html>

## Privacy and data protection

- Lawful basis under UK GDPR: Article 6(1)(e) — public task — for NHS
  providers, with Article 9(2)(h) — provision of health or social care
  — for any health-data items.
- Information Commissioner's Office *Health and social care data*
  guidance. <https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/sector-specific-guidance/health/>
- Retention: align with the *NHS Records Management Code of Practice*
  current edition (NHSX / NHS England).
  <https://www.nhsx.nhs.uk/information-governance/guidance/records-management-code/>

## Out of scope

- Clinical outcome measurement — use a validated PROM (e.g. EQ-5D-5L,
  PROMIS) rather than ESS.
- Regulator reporting — ESS does not replace CQC, FFT, or HCAHPS
  submissions.
