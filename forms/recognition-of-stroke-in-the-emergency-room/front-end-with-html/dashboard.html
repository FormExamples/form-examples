The ROSIER score is a validated stroke-recognition instrument (Nor et al., Lancet Neurology 2005). This dashboard is a clinical-review tool and is not a diagnosis. A total greater than 0 is a positive screen that activates the acute stroke pathway; a total of 0 or below makes stroke unlikely but does not exclude it.
