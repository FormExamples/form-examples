Clinician referral wizard for a cardiac stress / exercise test. Record the requesting clinician, patient identification, the requested test modality, the clinical indication and specific question, presenting symptoms and exercise capacity, the cardiac safety screen (known CAD, recent acute coronary syndrome, aortic stenosis, uncontrolled hypertension, beta-blocker), and the requested urgency. The engine computes a four-axis grade (appropriateness, safety / contraindication, request completeness, and triage priority) plus a set of safety flags.

This is clinical decision support for the cardiac-investigations vetting desk. The final triage and booking decision remains the responsibility of the vetting cardiologist or cardiac physiologist.

Submit the form to see the vetting report.

Grading is aligned with the ACC/AHA Appropriate Use Criteria and exercise-testing contraindications, the ACC/AHA/ASE multimodality AUC, and ESC chronic coronary syndromes and valvular heart disease guidance.
