This questionnaire supports the annual health check for a person with a learning disability. It can be completed by the person, a family member, a paid carer, or a clinician working alongside them. Answers are saved automatically in your browser as you go.

If the person you are completing this for needs urgent medical attention, call your local emergency number now. This form is not a substitute for clinical care.

Severity categories are aligned with DSM-5-TR and ICD-11. This tool is for information only and does not constitute a diagnosis; clinical judgement is required to confirm severity, capacity, and any reasonable adjustments.
