# Clinical Guideline Alignment

The urology assessment uses IPSS as its primary scored instrument and
captures structured urinary, renal, sexual-health, and medical history
fields. The following authoritative sources informed the field set and
rule catalogue.

## Sexual-health screening

Step 7 (Sexual Health) uses simple structured items aligned to the
IIEF-5 / SHIM (Sexual Health Inventory for Men) — Rosen et al. 1999. The
engine does not compute the IIEF-5 score; it records the individual
items so a clinician may compute or interpret as appropriate.

- Rosen RC, Cappelleri JC, Smith MD, Lipsky J, Pena BM. *Development and
  evaluation of an abridged 5-item version of the IIEF (IIEF-5) as a
  diagnostic tool for erectile dysfunction.* International Journal of
  Impotence Research 1999; 11(6): 319-326. PMID: 10637462.

## What this engine does NOT score

- We do not compute the IIEF-5 / SHIM total.
- We do not compute the ICIQ (International Consultation on Incontinence
  Questionnaire) suite of instruments.
- We do not stage prostate or bladder cancer.
- We do not recommend specific medical or surgical therapy.

## Patient self-report caveats

IPSS is validated as a patient-completed questionnaire. eGFR and PSA on
Steps 6 and 8 must be transcribed from laboratory reports and are
clinician-verified fields.
