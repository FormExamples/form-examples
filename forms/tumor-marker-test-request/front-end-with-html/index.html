Clinician referral wizard for one or more serum tumour markers. Record the requesting clinician, the patient, the requested markers, the clinical indication and details, any known cancer site and prior marker value, and the requested urgency. The engine computes a four-axis grade (appropriateness, interpretation safety, request completeness, and triage priority) plus a set of safety flags.

Tumour markers are poor screening tests in unselected populations. This is clinical decision support for the laboratory vetting desk; the final decision to accept, query, redirect, or reject remains the responsibility of the vetting clinical scientist.

Submit the form to see the vetting report.
