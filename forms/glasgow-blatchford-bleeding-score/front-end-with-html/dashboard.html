The Glasgow-Blatchford Bleeding Score is a validated pre-endoscopy risk-stratification instrument for acute upper GI bleeding. This dashboard is a clinical-review tool and is not a diagnosis. A high score (>= 6) signals a high likelihood of needing intervention or transfusion and should prompt admission and urgent endoscopy.
