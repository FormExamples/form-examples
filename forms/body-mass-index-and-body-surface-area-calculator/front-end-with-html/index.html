Enter the measured height (cm) and weight (kg) for an adult. The calculator computes the body mass index (BMI) and bands it into the World Health Organization adult weight-status categories, and computes the body surface area (BSA) by the Mosteller formula (bedside default) and the Du Bois formula (historical reference). Answers are saved automatically in your browser as you go.

BMI is a screening prompt, not a diagnosis of malnutrition or obesity, and does not distinguish muscle from fat. BSA is used to normalise physiological parameters and, most importantly, to calculate weight-and-size-appropriate drug doses; physiologically extreme heights or weights are flagged for re-measurement before dosing.

The BMI categories are the WHO adult weight-status bands (WHO TRS 894, 2000); the Asian lower action points follow the WHO Expert Consultation (Lancet 2004). BSA uses the Mosteller (1987) and Du Bois (1916) formulae. This calculator is a screening and dosing aid for information only and is not a diagnosis; interpret in the clinical context and use age-and-sex centile charts for paediatric patients.
