UK HSE First Aid at Work (FAW) is a workplace competency assessment used by approved training providers to verify scene assessment, primary survey (DRABC), CPR/AED, choking, bleeding, burns, fractures, medical emergencies, and handover skills. This dashboard is a training-administration tool and is not a clinical record. Standard certification validity is three years.
