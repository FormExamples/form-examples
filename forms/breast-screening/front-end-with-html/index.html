Record a mammography breast-screening encounter end to end: eligibility and consent, the mammogram views taken, the double-read reporting outcome, and — where the woman is recalled — the five-point breast imaging classification. The engine derives the screening outcome and the correct onward action, and raises safety flags. Answers are saved automatically in your browser as you go.

This is a documentation and result-classification record, not a scored screen and not a diagnosis. A woman with a breast symptom is not a screening candidate and must be referred via the symptomatic breast pathway. A normal screening result does not exclude interval cancer.

Submit the form to see the report.

The five-point breast imaging classification aligns with the Royal College of Radiologists breast imaging classification and the BI-RADS family, within the NHS Breast Screening Programme (NHSBSP) consolidated standards. This tool is for documentation and result classification only and is not a diagnosis; act in line with local screening policy and clinical judgement.
