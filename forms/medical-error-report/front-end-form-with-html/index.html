Use this form to document a medical error or near-miss. Sections cover the reporter, incident details, the patient (if any), error classification, contributing factors, immediate actions, patient outcome, root cause analysis, corrective actions, and follow-up reporting. Answers are saved automatically in your browser as you go.

This is an internal incident-reporting tool. If a patient requires urgent clinical attention right now, escalate via your local clinical emergency pathway before completing this form.

Grading uses the WHO patient-safety severity scale and the NCC MERP harm-categorisation framework. This form is for internal incident reporting and quality improvement; submit external regulatory reports through Datix, NRLS, CQC, HSIB, or coroner pathways as required.
