# Reference material — agent instructions

Free-form markdown reference material for this form. Place each topic in its
own descriptively named file (e.g. `ihr-2005-annex-6.md`,
`yellow-fever-contraindications.md`, `cdc-icvp-guidance.md`).

Source-of-truth documents:

- WHO. *International Health Regulations (2005), Third Edition*, Annex 6.
- WHO. *Model International Certificate of Vaccination or Prophylaxis*, 2007.
- CDC. *ICVP* page (<https://wwwnc.cdc.gov/travel/page/icvp>).
- WHO. *Vaccines and vaccination against yellow fever: WHO Position Paper*,
  June 2013.
- WHO. *Yellow fever vaccination booster not needed* (2014) — basis for the
  2016 IHR amendment.

Do not duplicate the form-level `index.md` content here; this directory is for
deeper reference material that supports clinician training and regulatory
audit.
