# International Certificate of Vaccination or Prophylaxis — reference material

Free-form documentation directory. Place reference material — IHR Annex 6
extracts, WHO model layout, CDC operational guidance, yellow fever vaccination
contraindications, and any other clinical or regulatory context not captured
in the parent `../index.md` — here as markdown files.

The contents are read by humans and agents alike; keep filenames descriptive
(`ihr-2005-annex-6.md`, `yellow-fever-contraindications.md`, etc.).
