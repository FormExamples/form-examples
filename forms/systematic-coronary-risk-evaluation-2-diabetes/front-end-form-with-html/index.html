10-year CVD risk assessment for adults with type 2 diabetes (ESC 2023).

This 10-step questionnaire collects the data needed to estimate 10-year cardiovascular disease risk in adults aged 40-69 with type 2 diabetes and no prior CVD, using the ESC SCORE2-Diabetes model. Sections include demographics, diabetes history, cardiovascular history, blood pressure, lipid profile, renal function, lifestyle, current medications, complications screening, and a clinician summary. All answers are saved automatically in your browser as you go.

For clinical use only. This screening tool is not a diagnosis; always interpret in context with the full medical record.

SCORE2-Diabetes is the ESC 2023 risk model for predicting 10-year fatal and non-fatal cardiovascular disease in adults aged 40-69 with type 2 diabetes and no prior CVD. Reference: MDCalc — SCORE2-Diabetes. For clinical use only.
