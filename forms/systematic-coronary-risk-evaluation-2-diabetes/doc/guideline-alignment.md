# Guideline alignment

How SCORE2-Diabetes fits ESC, ADA, and NICE guidance for adults with type 2
diabetes.

## ESC (Europe)

- 2021 ESC Guidelines on Cardiovascular Disease Prevention in Clinical
  Practice. Eur Heart J 2021;42(34):3227-3337.
  DOI: 10.1093/eurheartj/ehab484. SCORE2 is the default tool for primary
  prevention in adults without diabetes; SCORE2-Diabetes is recommended
  for type 2 diabetes (added in the 2023 update).
- 2023 ESC Guidelines for the management of cardiovascular disease in
  patients with diabetes. Eur Heart J 2023;44(39):4043-4140.
  DOI: 10.1093/eurheartj/ehad192. Adopts SCORE2-Diabetes as the primary
  risk tool, and codifies "very high risk" categorical assignment for
  patients with established CVD or target-organ damage.

## ADA (US)

- American Diabetes Association. *Standards of Care in Diabetes — 2025.*
  Diabetes Care 2025;48(Suppl 1).
  <https://diabetesjournals.org/care/issue/48/Supplement_1>
  Section 10 (Cardiovascular Disease and Risk Management) recommends
  10-year ASCVD risk estimation (e.g. AHA PREVENT or ACC/AHA risk
  estimator); ADA does not adopt SCORE2-Diabetes but provides parallel
  threshold-based guidance.

## NICE (UK)

- NICE NG28 *Type 2 diabetes in adults: management.*
  <https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/ng28>
- NICE CG181 *Cardiovascular disease: risk assessment and reduction.*
  <https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/cg181>
  NICE in the UK recommends QRISK3 for CVD risk estimation in people with
  type 2 diabetes and recommends atorvastatin 20 mg for primary prevention
  when QRISK3 is ≥ 10 % (CG181 §1.3) or as standard offer in type 2
  diabetes (NG28 §1.6).
- NICE NG136 *Hypertension in adults: diagnosis and management.*
  <https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/ng136>
- NICE NG203 *Chronic kidney disease: assessment and management.*
  <https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/ng203>

## Cross-walk: form steps → SCORE2-Diabetes predictors

| Form step | SCORE2-Diabetes predictor |
| --- | --- |
| 1 Patient Demographics | age, sex, country (risk region) |
| 2 Diabetes History | age at diagnosis of T2D, HbA1c |
| 3 Cardiovascular History | exclusion check (prior CVD → very high risk by category) |
| 4 Blood Pressure | systolic BP |
| 5 Lipid Profile | non-HDL cholesterol (total − HDL) |
| 6 Renal Function | eGFR (CKD-EPI 2021) |
| 7 Lifestyle Factors | current smoking |
| 8 Current Medications | (contextual) |
| 9 Complications Screening | target-organ damage → very high risk by category |
| 10 Risk Assessment Summary | computed SCORE2-Diabetes 10-year risk |

## Treatment thresholds (ESC 2023 diabetes guidelines)

- LDL-C goals based on risk category: moderate < 2.6 mmol/L; high < 1.8
  mmol/L (and ≥ 50 % reduction); very high < 1.4 mmol/L (and ≥ 50 %
  reduction).
- BP target < 130/80 mmHg (lower limit 120/70 mmHg).
- SGLT2 inhibitor recommended in T2D with established ASCVD, HF, or CKD.
- GLP-1 receptor agonist recommended in T2D with established ASCVD or
  multiple risk factors.
