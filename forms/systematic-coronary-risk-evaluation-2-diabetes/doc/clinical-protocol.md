# Clinical protocol

Operational protocol for SCORE2-Diabetes-based primary prevention
assessment.

## Indications

- Adults aged 40–69 years with type 2 diabetes.
- No prior atherosclerotic cardiovascular disease (myocardial infarction,
  stroke, transient ischaemic attack, peripheral arterial disease,
  coronary or peripheral revascularisation).
- No target-organ damage at the threshold that automatically classifies
  the patient as very high risk (eGFR < 45 mL/min/1.73 m²,
  microalbuminuria, advanced retinopathy, neuropathy).

For patients ≥ 70 y use **SCORE2-OP** (not SCORE2-Diabetes).
For patients with type 1 diabetes use the Steno T1 Risk Engine.

## Required measurements

Per Hageman 2023 §Methods:

- Age (40–69 y).
- Sex.
- Country / region (selects recalibration table — low / moderate / high /
  very-high).
- Age at diagnosis of type 2 diabetes (years).
- Current smoking (yes/no).
- Systolic blood pressure.
- Total cholesterol and HDL-C → non-HDL = total − HDL.
- HbA1c (mmol/mol).
- eGFR via CKD-EPI 2021 creatinine equation.

## Step-by-step

1. **Patient Demographics.** Confirm age 40–69 y; select country for
   region recalibration.
2. **Diabetes History.** Age at diagnosis of T2D; current HbA1c.
3. **Cardiovascular History.** Confirm absence of prior CVD (otherwise
   the patient is automatically very high risk per ESC 2023).
4. **Blood Pressure.** Mean of two seated readings after 5 min rest.
5. **Lipid Profile.** Total cholesterol, HDL-C; compute non-HDL.
6. **Renal Function.** Serum creatinine → eGFR via CKD-EPI 2021. Screen
   for microalbuminuria via urine ACR.
7. **Lifestyle Factors.** Current smoking, alcohol, physical activity.
8. **Current Medications.** Statin, antihypertensive, SGLT2i, GLP-1RA,
   metformin, insulin.
9. **Complications Screening.** Retinopathy, neuropathy, foot status,
   nephropathy.
10. **Risk Assessment Summary.** Compute SCORE2-Diabetes 10-year risk;
    apply age-modified thresholds.

## Risk-band actions (ESC 2023)

- **Low / moderate risk** (< 2.5 % / < 5 % depending on age band):
  lifestyle, statin per shared decision.
- **High risk** (2.5–7.5 % / 5–10 %): statin to LDL-C goal < 1.8 mmol/L.
- **Very high risk** (≥ 7.5 % / ≥ 10 %): high-intensity statin to LDL-C
  goal < 1.4 mmol/L and ≥ 50 % reduction; add ezetimibe / PCSK9i if not at
  target; consider SGLT2 inhibitor and/or GLP-1RA for cardiovascular and
  renal benefit.

## Onward actions in any band

- BP target < 130/80 mmHg (ESC 2023).
- HbA1c individualised target (NICE NG28 § 1.6 recommends 48 mmol/mol on
  diet/metformin; 53 mmol/mol if hypoglycaemia risk).
- Annual retinal screening (NHS Diabetic Eye Screening Programme in the
  UK).
- Annual foot review (NICE NG19 *Diabetic foot problems*).
- Annual ACR and eGFR (NICE NG203 *CKD*).
