Clinician referral wizard for a cytology specimen examination. Record the requesting clinician, the patient, the requested specimen type and site, the clinical indication and specific question, the cervical-screening / cytology context, the specimen-collection (pre-analytical) details, and the requested urgency. The engine computes a four-axis grade (appropriateness, pre-analytical specimen adequacy, request completeness, and triage priority) plus a set of safety flags.

This is clinical decision support for the cytology / pathology vetting desk. The final triage and specimen-acceptance decision remains the responsibility of the reporting cytologist or pathologist.

Submit the form to see the vetting report.

Grading is aligned with the NHS Cervical Screening Programme (HPV primary screening), RCPath cytopathology guidance, and NICE NG12 suspected-cancer recognition and referral.
