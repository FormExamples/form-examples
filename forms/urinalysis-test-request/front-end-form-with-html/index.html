Clinician order wizard for one or more urine pathology investigations. Record the requesting clinician, the patient, the requested test panel, the clinical indication and details, symptoms and red flags, the specimen, and the requested urgency. The engine computes a four-axis grade (appropriateness, preanalytical specimen suitability, request completeness, and triage priority) plus a set of safety flags.

This is clinical decision support for the pathology / laboratory triage desk. The final acceptance and triage decision remains the responsibility of the vetting laboratory clinician.

Submit the form to see the vetting report.

Grading is aligned with NICE NG109 (lower UTI), NICE NG12 (suspected cancer / haematuria), NICE NG203 (chronic kidney disease), and UK Standards for Microbiology Investigations (UK SMI) B41 urine investigation guidance.
