## Appropriateness (1–9, anchored)

No single published 1–9 appropriateness score exists for urinalysis. The engine
anchors the ordinal 1–9 on **indication-to-test match** plus **guideline
appropriateness**, in three bands: usually-appropriate (7–9),
may-be-appropriate (4–6), usually-not-appropriate (1–3). Examples:

- Dipstick alone for suspected uncomplicated UTI in a non-pregnant woman →
  usually-appropriate.
- MC&S (MSU to culture) for suspected UTI in pregnancy or in a man → strongly
  usually-appropriate (NICE NG109).
- Dipstick alone for suspected UTI in pregnancy / men / catheter → may-be /
  usually-not-appropriate (culture indicated).
- Cytology as a stand-alone test to exclude bladder cancer → usually-not-
  appropriate (does not replace cystoscopy; see NICE NG12).

## Preanalytical (UK SMI B41)

| Band | Trigger |
| --- | --- |
| ok | Appropriate specimen type for the test, collected, within handling window |
| caution | Specimen type sub-optimal for indication, or timing/transport at risk (>4 h without refrigeration / boric acid) |
| reject-risk | Specimen not collected, wrong type, or contamination/delay likely to invalidate culture |

Key UK SMI B41 facts: transport and process within 4 hours where possible;
beyond that, refrigerate or collect into a boric acid container (holds the
bacterial population for ~48 hours); midstream specimens require peri-urethral
cleaning to be of diagnostic value.

## Triage tiers

| Tier | Target | Triggers |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Stat | same hour / same day | fever + loin pain (suspected pyelonephritis / urosepsis), unstable |
| Urgent | 24–72 h | symptomatic complicated UTI, visible haematuria work-up |
| Routine | standard lab turnaround | monitoring (ACR/PCR), screening, asymptomatic |

A red flag (visible haematuria; fever + loin pain) auto-escalates the tier.
