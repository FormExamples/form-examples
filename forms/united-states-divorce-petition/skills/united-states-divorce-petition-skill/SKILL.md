# United States Divorce Petition

Petition for dissolution of marriage, the divorce decree and the vital-records divorce certificate for United States courts. A single continuous wizard collects the filing state and county, spouses and residency, children and child support, property division (community or equitable), service of process, and the decree; the engine checks the filing state's residency, separation and waiting-period rules and classifies the petition as **ready to file**, **incomplete**, or **not yet eligible**, with prioritized flags.

This skill is the end-user-facing guide to this specific form; for cross-form concepts and terminology shared across the monorepo, use `form-examples-skill`. For implementation work on this form's code, use `united-states-divorce-petition-maintainer-skill` instead.
