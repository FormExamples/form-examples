The Research and Planning Privacy Notice is a read-and-acknowledge instrument recording each patient's opt-in / opt-out preferences for use of their confidential information in research and service planning. This dashboard supports information-governance review under UK GDPR, the Data Protection Act 2018, the Common Law Duty of Confidentiality, and the NHS National Data Opt-Out (Type 1 and National Opt-Out); it does not replace the organisation's IG, Caldicott, or DPO workflows.
