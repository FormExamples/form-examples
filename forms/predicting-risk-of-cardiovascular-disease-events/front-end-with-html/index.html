This questionnaire collects the cardiovascular risk factors needed for the AHA PREVENT (Predicting Risk of Cardiovascular Disease Events) calculator. PREVENT estimates the 10-year and 30-year risk of total cardiovascular disease (and its subtypes ASCVD and heart failure) in adults aged 30 to 79 without known CVD.
