# Guideline alignment

How PREVENT 2023 fits in current cardiovascular prevention guidelines.

## United States

- 2019 ACC/AHA Primary Prevention Guideline.
  Circulation 2019;140(11):e596-e646.
  DOI: 10.1161/CIR.0000000000000678. Sets ASCVD risk thresholds at < 5 %,
  5–7.5 %, 7.5–20 %, ≥ 20 % using the 2013 PCE.
- 2018 AHA/ACC/Multisociety Cholesterol Guideline.
  Circulation 2019;139(25):e1082-e1143.
  DOI: 10.1161/CIR.0000000000000625. Statin intensity by 10-year ASCVD risk.
- AHA PREVENT 2023/24 scientific statement and equations supersede PCE for
  most US primary-prevention assessments going forward. Many specialty
  societies are issuing supplementary guidance during the transition; the
  ACC/AHA 2024 *Joint Statement on the use of PREVENT in clinical practice*
  is at
  <https://professional.heart.org/en/guidelines-and-statements/prevent-calculator>.
- 2022 AHA/ACC/HFSA Heart Failure Guideline.
  Circulation 2022;145(18):e895-e1032.
  DOI: 10.1161/CIR.0000000000001063. Stages of HF (A–D) referenced for the
  HF outcome in PREVENT.

## Risk-band recommendations (translated for clinicians)

Khan 2024 and the 2023 AHA scientific statement classify 10-year total CVD
risk as:

- < 5 % → emphasize lifestyle; reassess in 4–6 years.
- 5 – < 7.5 % → lifestyle + shared decision about statin; consider risk
  enhancers (family history of premature CVD, chronic inflammatory
  disease, etc.).
- 7.5 – < 20 % → intermediate; offer moderate-intensity statin per 2018
  Cholesterol Guideline.
- ≥ 20 % → high; offer high-intensity statin.

PREVENT's 30-year risk is intended primarily as a communication device for
younger adults whose 10-year risk is low but whose lifetime exposure to
risk factors is substantial.

## United Kingdom and Europe

PREVENT is **not** the recommended UK or European tool. NICE CG181
recommends QRISK3 and ESC 2021 recommends SCORE2 / SCORE2-OP. PREVENT is
included in this repository because the form is positioned for US
primary-prevention contexts and for comparison studies.

## Cross-walk: form steps → PREVENT predictors

| Form step | PREVENT predictor |
| --- | --- |
| 1 Demographics | age, sex |
| 2 Blood Pressure | SBP, antihypertensive use |
| 3 Cholesterol & Lipids | total cholesterol, HDL-C |
| 4 Metabolic Health | BMI; HbA1c (optional add-on) |
| 5 Renal Function | eGFR (CKD-EPI 2021); UACR (optional add-on) |
| 6 Smoking History | current smoking |
| 7 Medical History | diabetes, prior CVD (exclusion check) |
| 8 Current Medications | antihypertensive, statin (for shared decision) |
| 9 Review & Calculate | invokes PREVENT equation |

## CKD-EPI 2021

eGFR for PREVENT uses the 2021 race-free CKD-EPI creatinine equation
(Inker LA et al. *New Creatinine- and Cystatin C–Based Equations to
Estimate GFR without Race.* N Engl J Med 2021;385:1737-1749.
DOI: 10.1056/NEJMoa2102953). The form's renal-function step captures
serum creatinine, age, sex; eGFR is then computed.
