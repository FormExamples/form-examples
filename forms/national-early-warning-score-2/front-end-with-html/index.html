Record a single set of adult observations below. Each of the six physiological parameters scores 0 to 3; supplemental oxygen adds 2. The engine aggregates the subscores to a total of 0 to 20 or more, applies the red-score rule (any single parameter scoring 3), and returns the clinical-risk band with the Royal College of Physicians' recommended monitoring frequency and escalation response. Answers are saved automatically in your browser as you go.

NEWS2 is a decision-support aid, not a diagnosis, and does not replace clinical judgement. It is validated for acutely ill adults (16 years or over); it must not be used for children, pregnant women, or patients with spinal-cord injury.

The National Early Warning Score 2 (NEWS2) is published by the Royal College of Physicians (RCP, 2017) and adopted across NHS acute and ambulance trusts. This scoring tool is for information only and is not a diagnosis; escalate and treat in line with local policy and clinical judgement.
