# NEWS2 — HTML front-end (form + dashboard)

Consolidated single-directory HTML front-end: `index.html` is the single-page
NEWS2 bedside wizard; `dashboard.html` is the clinician dashboard. Shared `css/`
and `js/` (the NEWS2 scoring engine in `js/{types,rules,grader,flags}.js`, the
apps in `js/form-app.js` + `js/dashboard-app.js`).
