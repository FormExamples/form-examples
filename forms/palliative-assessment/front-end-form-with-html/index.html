This questionnaire helps your palliative-care team understand the symptoms you are experiencing right now and the support you and your family need. The symptom-scoring section uses the validated Edmonton Symptom Assessment System — revised (ESAS-r): for each of ten symptoms, you choose a number between 0 (no symptom) and 10 (worst possible symptom).

All answers are saved automatically in your browser as you go. If a question does not apply to you, leave it blank. If you are in severe distress or have a sudden change in symptoms, please contact your palliative-care team or local emergency service.

ESAS-r (Edmonton Symptom Assessment System — revised) is a validated ten-item symptom-intensity instrument for palliative-care patients. This screening tool is for information only and is not a substitute for clinical judgement; please discuss your results with your palliative-care team.
