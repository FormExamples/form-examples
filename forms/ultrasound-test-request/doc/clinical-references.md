# Clinical references

Authoritative guidance underpinning the four-axis grading engine for general
(non-obstetric) diagnostic ultrasound.

## Appropriateness (ACR Appropriateness Criteria)

Ordinal 1–9 scale in three bands: usually-appropriate (7–9),
may-be-appropriate (4–6), usually-not-appropriate (1–3). Mapped per
(indication × body region).

Worked examples grounding the rating bands:

- **Right upper quadrant pain / suspected gallstones.** Ultrasound is the
  initial imaging modality of choice for suspected acute cholecystitis and is
  highly accurate (~96 %) for detecting or excluding gallstones — usually
  appropriate (7–9).
- **Suspected abdominal aortic aneurysm (AAA).** Abdominal aortic ultrasound is
  the primary modality for AAA screening — usually appropriate (7–9).
- **Suspected DVT of the leg.** Compression / Doppler ultrasound is first-line —
  usually appropriate (7–9) and time-critical.

## Preparation / technical suitability

| Body region | Preparation |
| --- | --- |
| Abdomen / liver-biliary | Fasting 6 h (reduces bowel gas, distends gallbladder) |
| Renal-tract / pelvis | Full bladder (acoustic window for lower tract / pelvic organs) |
| Thyroid-neck, scrotum-testes, breast, soft-tissue, msk-joint | None |
| Vascular-doppler, dvt-leg, carotid | None |

Suitability band: `ok` when prep matches the body region; `caution` when prep
is requested but not confirmed or body habitus may limit the study; `limited`
when required prep is not met or technical factors materially degrade the study.

## Triage tiers

| Tier | Target | Triggers |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Emergency | same hour / same day | suspected ruptured / symptomatic AAA, suspected testicular torsion |
| Urgent | 24–72 h | suspected DVT, acute severe pain, suspected malignancy fast-track |
| Routine | standard booking | stable indications, follow-up, surveillance |

A red flag (suspected DVT, suspected testicular torsion, suspected AAA)
auto-escalates the triage tier regardless of the other axes.
