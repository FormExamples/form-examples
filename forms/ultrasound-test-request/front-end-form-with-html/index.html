Clinician referral wizard for a general diagnostic ultrasound examination — abdominal, pelvic, renal-tract, hepatobiliary, small-parts (thyroid, scrotum, breast), soft-tissue, vascular (Doppler, DVT, carotid), or musculoskeletal. Record the requesting clinician, the patient, the requested examination, the clinical indication and specific question, preparation requirements, and the requested urgency. The engine computes a four-axis grade (appropriateness, preparation / technical suitability, request completeness, and triage priority) plus a set of safety flags.

This is clinical decision support for the imaging vetting desk. The final triage and booking decision remains the responsibility of the vetting sonographer or radiologist. This form is explicitly not for obstetric scanning.

Submit the form to see the vetting report.

Grading is aligned with the ACR Appropriateness Criteria, the Royal College of Radiologists' iRefer referral guidelines, and BMUS / AIUM practice parameters for abdominal, pelvic, scrotal, thyroid, and vascular ultrasound.
