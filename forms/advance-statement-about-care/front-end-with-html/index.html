An advance statement is a written record of your wishes, values and preferences for future care. It is used if you lose the capacity to make or communicate decisions for yourself.

Under the Mental Capacity Act 2005, an advance statement records your preferences but is not legally binding (unlike an Advance Decision to Refuse Treatment, which is). Your care team must take it into account when making best-interest decisions on your behalf.

  • 9 sections covering personal details, values, care preferences and wishes
  • Takes approximately 20–30 minutes to complete thoughtfully
  • You can revise any answer at any time — answers save automatically in your browser

This document should be completed when you have mental capacity. You may wish to discuss it with your family, carers or healthcare team.
