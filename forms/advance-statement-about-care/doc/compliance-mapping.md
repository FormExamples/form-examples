## Form fields ↔ NHS Confidentiality

The NHS *Confidentiality: NHS Code of Practice* (2003) provides the
"protect-inform-provide choice-improve" model. The advance statement
record satisfies the model by:

- **Protect** — storing the statement within the GP / hospital record under
  the same access controls as other clinical entries.
- **Inform** — the form itself is the patient's expression of preferences;
  no further patient-facing privacy notice is required.
- **Provide choice** — the patient may amend or withdraw the statement at
  any time while they retain capacity.
- **Improve** — anonymised aggregates may inform service planning under
  the separate Research and Planning Privacy Notice.

Code: archived at the National Archives:
<https://webarchive.nationalarchives.gov.uk/ukgwa/+/http://www.dh.gov.uk/en/Publicationsandstatistics/Publications/PublicationsPolicyAndGuidance/DH_4069253>

## Software classification

- MDCG 2019-11 Rev.1 — the form is a documentation aid, not a clinical
  decision-support module, so falls outside the MDR Annex VIII Rule 11
  software classification regime.
- ISO/IEC/IEEE 26514:2022 governs the information design and user
  documentation.
