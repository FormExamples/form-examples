# References

Annotated bibliography for the Framingham Hard CHD risk score.

## Primary equation

- Wilson PWF, D'Agostino RB, Levy D, Belanger AM, Silbershatz H, Kannel WB.
  *Prediction of coronary heart disease using risk factor categories.*
  Circulation 1998;97(18):1837-1847.
  DOI: 10.1161/01.CIR.97.18.1837. PMID: 9603539.
