# Guideline alignment

Where the Framingham Hard CHD score sits in contemporary cardiovascular
prevention guidelines.

## US

- ATP III 2002 — *Detection, Evaluation, and Treatment of High Blood
  Cholesterol in Adults (Adult Treatment Panel III).* NCEP, NHLBI.
  <https://www.nhlbi.nih.gov/files/docs/resources/heart/atp-3-cholesterol-full-report.pdf>
  ATP III adopts the Wilson Hard CHD equation and defines treatment
  intensities by 10-year risk band (< 10 %, 10–20 %, > 20 %).
- 2018 AHA/ACC Multisociety Cholesterol Guideline.
  Circulation 2019;139(25):e1082-e1143.
  DOI: 10.1161/CIR.0000000000000625. Recommends the ACC/AHA Pooled Cohort
  Equations (PCE) for ASCVD risk; Framingham Hard CHD is retained as a
  historical comparator.
- 2019 ACC/AHA Primary Prevention Guideline.
  Circulation 2019;140(11):e596-e646.
  DOI: 10.1161/CIR.0000000000000678. PCE-based risk grouping.
- AHA PREVENT 2023 (Khan SS et al.) supersedes PCE for most US clinical
  uses going forward. Circulation 2024;149:e1-e98.
  DOI: 10.1161/CIRCULATIONAHA.123.067626.

## UK

- NICE CG181 *Cardiovascular disease: risk assessment and reduction,
  including lipid modification.*
  <https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/cg181> recommends QRISK3 as the primary
  10-year CVD risk tool in adults aged 25–84 years without prior CVD and
  without familial hypercholesterolaemia. QRISK3 is calibrated to the UK
  population and includes ethnicity, deprivation (Townsend), CKD, AF,
  migraine, severe mental illness, and corticosteroid use — predictors
  absent from Framingham.

## Europe

- 2021 ESC Guidelines on cardiovascular disease prevention in clinical
  practice. Eur Heart J 2021;42(34):3227-3337.
  DOI: 10.1093/eurheartj/ehab484. Recommends SCORE2 (Hageman et al. 2021)
  and SCORE2-OP (older persons) instead of Framingham.

## When Framingham Hard CHD is still appropriate

- Historical comparison in long-running cohort follow-up.
- Patient or clinician request for the calculator they have used
  previously.
- Settings without a population-specific recalibrated tool (Framingham has
  been recalibrated for many international populations).

## When to prefer a successor

- **UK** — use QRISK3 (NICE CG181).
- **US** — use PREVENT 2023 (or PCE 2013 if PREVENT unavailable).
- **Europe (general)** — use SCORE2 / SCORE2-OP (ESC 2021).
- **Europe + Type 2 diabetes** — use SCORE2-Diabetes (Hageman 2023).
- **Type 1 diabetes** — use the Steno T1 Risk Engine.
