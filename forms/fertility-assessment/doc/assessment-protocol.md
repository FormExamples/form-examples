# Assessment protocol

## Intended use

A 10-step questionnaire that captures reproductive, menstrual, lifestyle,
and partner information; records investigation results; and produces a
three-level concern band per NICE CG156 to support a referral decision.

## Intended users

- UK primary care: GP or practice nurse
- Secondary care: general gynaecology outpatient
- Tertiary care: assisted-conception unit (ACU) intake

## Setting

UK primary or secondary care. Where appropriate the form is completed
collaboratively by both partners, with partner-factor sections completed by
the male partner directly.

## Workflow

1. **Demographics** — names, DOBs, GPs, ethnicity (both partners where
   applicable).
2. **Reproductive history** — gravidity, parity, miscarriages, ectopic
   pregnancies, prior contraception, duration trying without success.
3. **Menstrual cycle history** — menarche, cycle length, regularity,
   bleeding pattern. Cycle 21-35 days with < 8 days variation defines a
   regular cycle per CG156.
4. **Medical and surgical history** — relevant conditions: thyroid disease,
   pituitary disease, eating disorders, sexually transmitted infections,
   PID, endometriosis, fibroids, prior pelvic surgery.
5. **Lifestyle factors** — BMI, smoking, alcohol (units/week), caffeine,
   recreational drugs, occupational exposures, stress.
6. **Current medications and supplements** — NSAID use timing, folic acid,
   chemotherapy or radiotherapy history, recent vaccinations.
7. **Partner factors and semen analysis** — partner's age, medical history,
   surgical history, lifestyle, semen analysis results per WHO 6th edition
   2021 reference limits.
8. **Hormone profile** — early follicular FSH, LH, AMH, oestradiol,
   prolactin, TSH; mid-luteal progesterone.
9. **Investigations** — pelvic ultrasound AFC, HSG/HyCoSy, hysteroscopy,
   chlamydia screen, rubella immunity.
10. **Clinical recommendation** — engine outputs concern band, recommended
    next step (continue trying, primary-care investigation, secondary
    referral, tertiary ART referral), and a structured PDF.

## Flagged issues

The form will flag:

- Age ≥ 36 with duration trying ≥ 6 months — early referral
- Anovulation (irregular cycles) with no investigation — refer for FSH,
  LH, prolactin, TSH
- AMH ≤ 5.4 pmol/L or AFC ≤ 4 — low ovarian reserve flag
- Semen analysis result below WHO 6th edition lower reference limits
- BMI < 19 or ≥ 30 — lifestyle optimisation flag
- Smoking — strong cessation recommendation
- Significant alcohol intake — reduction recommendation
- History of PID or chlamydia — consider tubal assessment
- Recurrent miscarriage — refer per RCOG Green-top 17

## Output

- Concern band (Low / Moderate / High)
- Investigation summary
- Lifestyle advice list aligned with CG156 §1.2
- Referral recommendation
- PDF report suitable for the GP referral letter

## Out of scope

- Detailed IVF/ICSI stimulation protocols
- Embryology decisions
- Donor gametes counselling
- Surrogacy pathway selection
- Fertility preservation for oncology patients (separate ESHRE pathway)
