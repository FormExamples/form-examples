# Clinical guideline alignment

## Primary instrument

- **NICE CG156** — *Fertility problems: assessment and treatment*. Published
  February 2013, last updated September 2017.
  <https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/cg156>

## Supporting NICE references

- **NICE CKS** — *Infertility*: <https://cks.nice.org.uk/topics/infertility/>
- **NICE Pathway** — Fertility (consult the NICE site for the current pathway).

## European guideline

- **ESHRE Guideline: Unexplained infertility (2023)** — European Society of
  Human Reproduction and Embryology:
  <https://www.eshre.eu/Guidelines-and-Legal/Guidelines/Unexplained-infertility>
- Romualdi D, et al. *ESHRE guideline: female fertility preservation.*
  Human Reproduction Open. 2020;2020(4):hoaa052. DOI:
  <https://doi.org/10.1093/hropen/hoaa052>

  <https://www.monash.edu/medicine/mchri/pcos>

## HFEA

The Human Fertilisation and Embryology Authority regulates UK fertility
treatment but does not publish clinical assessment criteria. Patient-facing
information for prospective service-users:

- HFEA: <https://www.hfea.gov.uk/>
