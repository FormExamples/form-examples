# Grading rules

The fertility-assessment form follows NICE Clinical Guideline 156 (CG156)
*Fertility problems: assessment and treatment* (published 20 February 2013,
last updated September 2017). The CG156 page is at
<https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/cg156>.

The form does not produce a probabilistic fertility prediction. It produces
a three-level "concern" band that drives a referral pathway recommendation.

## Concern bands

| Band | Criteria (any of) | Action |
| ---- | ----------------- | ------ |
| Low | Female partner < 36 and duration trying < 12 months; regular cycles; no known reproductive pathology; normal partner factors | Continue attempts; lifestyle optimisation; review at 12 months |
| Moderate | Duration trying 12–24 months in female < 36, OR > 6 months in female ≥ 36; one or two modifiable factors (BMI, smoking, alcohol, prolactin, TSH); cycle irregularity | Targeted investigation and treatment of identified factor; primary-care or community gynaecology |
| High | Known cause (tubal disease, severe male factor, anovulation refractory to lifestyle change), AMH < 5.4 pmol/L or AFC < 4, age ≥ 40, secondary subfertility with ≥ 2 abnormal investigations, recurrent miscarriage | Early specialist / ART referral |

These bands are presentational; CG156 itself does not codify a "low /
moderate / high" terminology.

## CG156 referral triggers

CG156 §1.3 — *When to offer further clinical assessment and investigation*
states (paraphrased):

- Offer further clinical assessment after one year of unprotected vaginal
  intercourse without conception (NICE CG156 §1.3.1).
- Offer earlier specialist consultation when the woman is aged 36 years or
  over, or where there is a known clinical cause of infertility or a history
  of predisposing factors (NICE CG156 §1.3.1.2).

The form's grading mirrors these triggers.

## Investigations referenced (CG156 §1.4)

- Semen analysis — performed and interpreted per WHO laboratory manual.
  Current edition is the WHO laboratory manual for the examination and
  processing of human semen, **6th edition (2021)**:
  <https://www.who.int/publications/i/item/9789240030787>
- Mid-luteal serum progesterone for ovulation confirmation.
- Serum gonadotrophins (FSH, LH) and oestradiol in women with irregular
  cycles.
- Anti-Müllerian Hormone (AMH) or antral follicle count (AFC) for ovarian
  reserve.
- TSH and prolactin if menstrual disturbance.
- Chlamydia screen.
- Tubal patency — hysterosalpingography (HSG), hystero-contrast
  sonography (HyCoSy), or laparoscopy.

## Ovarian reserve thresholds

CG156 §1.4.2 quotes the following AMH and AFC thresholds:

- **Low response prediction**: AMH ≤ 5.4 pmol/L or AFC ≤ 4
- **High response prediction**: AMH ≥ 25 pmol/L or AFC > 16

These are taken directly from the CG156 recommendations and are used in the
form's flagged-issues logic only — not for diagnosing infertility.

## Semen analysis reference values (WHO 6th edition 2021)

The WHO 6th edition lower reference limits (5th centile) are:

- Semen volume: 1.4 mL
- Sperm concentration: 16 million/mL
- Total sperm number: 39 million/ejaculate
- Total motility: 42%
- Progressive motility: 30%
- Vitality: 54%
- Normal forms (morphology): 4%

Source: WHO laboratory manual, 6th edition (2021). The form references the
official thresholds and does not substitute custom values.

## CG156 NHS-funded IVF criteria

The form records the patient's NHS-funded IVF eligibility as an information
item, citing CG156 §1.11. Funding decisions are made by ICBs (Integrated
Care Boards) in England and equivalent bodies in the devolved nations; the
form does not make a funding decision.

## Out of scope

- Detailed embryology stimulation protocols
- Pre-implantation genetic testing
- Donor gametes counselling
- Surrogacy pathway
- Recurrent miscarriage investigation (RCOG Green-top 17)
