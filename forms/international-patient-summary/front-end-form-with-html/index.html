Compile an International Patient Summary (IPS): the minimal, specialty-agnostic, cross-border-ready record of the patient's core clinical facts. The eight mandatory sections — problems, medications, allergies, immunisations, procedures, results, patient demographics, and the authoring clinician — must each be populated for a complete IPS. All entries are saved automatically in your browser as you go.

This form is for documentation only and does not replace clinical judgement; verify and reconcile records with the patient and with primary-source data before signoff.

The International Patient Summary (IPS) follows ISO 27269:2021 and the HL7 FHIR International Patient Summary Implementation Guide (R4 and R5). It is intended for cross-border and unplanned care and must be reconciled with primary-source clinical records by an authoring clinician.
