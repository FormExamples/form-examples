# Methodology reference — employee offboarding

The Employee Offboarding Checklist captures the steps an organisation
takes to transition a departing employee out of the organisation: from
resignation, dismissal, redundancy, retirement, or end of fixed-term
contract through final pay and post-employment obligations.

## CIPD framework

The **Chartered Institute of Personnel and Development (CIPD)** treats
offboarding as a structured process covering knowledge transfer,
revocation of access, return of property, exit interview, final pay,
and post-employment obligations.

## UK statutory baseline

The checklist must address, where applicable:

| Obligation | Authority |
| --- | --- |
| Statutory notice period (employer or employee) | Employment Rights Act 1996, s.86 |
| Final pay including accrued holiday | Employment Rights Act 1996, Part II; Working Time Regulations 1998 |
| Statutory redundancy payment (if applicable) | Employment Rights Act 1996, Part XI |
| P45 issued at termination | HMRC PAYE regulations |
| Return of company property | Common law / contract |
| Cessation of pension contributions | Pensions Act 2008 |
| References (if given) | common law duty of care: Spring v Guardian Assurance plc [1995] 2 AC 296 |
| Continued duties of confidentiality / restrictive covenants | contract; case law on restraint of trade |
| Data subject rights post-employment | UK GDPR; ICO guidance |

## Reason-for-leaving taxonomy

| Reason | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| Resignation | voluntary; notice per contract or s.86 ERA 1996 |
| Dismissal — conduct | ACAS Code of Practice 1 |
| Dismissal — capability | ACAS Code of Practice 1 |
| Dismissal — some other substantial reason (SOSR) | ERA 1996 s.98(1)(b) |
| Redundancy | ERA 1996 Part XI; consultation thresholds in Trade Union and Labour Relations (Consolidation) Act 1992 s.188 |
| End of fixed-term contract | Fixed-term Employees (Prevention of Less Favourable Treatment) Regulations 2002 |
| Retirement | retirement age is not in itself a fair reason post Equality Act 2010, save for employer-justified retirement age |
| Death in service | separate process; engage HR and benefits |
| Mutual termination / settlement agreement | ERA 1996 s.203(3); legal advice required for waiver of statutory claims |

## Exit interview methodology

Exit interviews capture the reason for leaving and feedback on the
employee experience. CIPD recommends:

- Structured questions, not free-form discussion.
- Conducted by HR (not the line manager) for honesty.
- Anonymised aggregation for trend reporting.
- Held in the final week, after notice tensions have settled.

CIPD — Exit interviews factsheet:
<https://www.cipd.org/uk/knowledge/factsheets/employee-turnover-retention-factsheet/>

## Knowledge transfer

ISO 30401:2018 "Knowledge management systems" defines knowledge
management requirements. The checklist captures handover artefacts
(documentation, work-in-progress, key contacts, access reassignments)
to operationalise the standard in the offboarding context.

- ISO 30401:2018. <https://www.iso.org/standard/68683.html>
