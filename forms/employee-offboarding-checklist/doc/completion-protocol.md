# Completion protocol — offboarding checklist

## Sections

The checklist is grouped by phase:

1. **Notification and notice**
   - Resignation letter / termination notice recorded.
   - Reason for leaving recorded (see taxonomy in methodology-reference).
   - Notice period calculated per s.86 ERA 1996 or contract.
   - Last working day set.
   - Where applicable: ACAS Code of Practice 1 procedure documented.

2. **Knowledge transfer and handover**
   - Active projects and responsibilities documented.
   - Successor / interim cover identified.
   - Customer/stakeholder contact list handed over.
   - Documentation up to date.
   - Open access requests, approvals, queues transferred.

3. **Property return**
   - Laptop, mobile, peripherals.
   - Building / vehicle access cards.
   - Keys, badges, identity documents (company-issued).
   - Company credit card.
   - Sample / loan equipment.

4. **Access revocation (cross-checked with IT and Security)**
   - Email, calendar, file shares.
   - SSO accounts.
   - SaaS application access.
   - VPN and remote access.
   - Code repositories, CI/CD systems, production access.
   - Physical access (building, parking, lab).
   - Mailing lists, distribution groups.
   - Customer / supplier portals.

5. **HR and payroll**
   - Final pay calculation (notice, holiday accrual, deductions).
   - P45 issued via HMRC PAYE.
   - Pension fund notified; auto-enrolment contributions ceased.
   - Benefits cessation (private medical, life cover, share plans).
   - Outstanding expenses settled.

6. **Statutory and contractual obligations**
   - Confidentiality, restrictive covenants, IP assignment reminders.
   - Return of confidential information.
   - GDPR data-subject information pack.
   - Reference policy clarified.
   - Settlement agreement, if any, signed and lodged.

7. **Exit interview and feedback**
   - Exit interview scheduled (preferably with HR, not line manager).
   - Standard exit questionnaire completed.
   - Themes captured for retention analysis.

## Statutory blocking items

The checklist will not allow completion if any of the following are
missing:

- **Final pay calculation** including accrued holiday under Working
  Time Regulations 1998 reg. 14.
- **P45** issued.
- **Pension** offboarding event recorded with the scheme administrator.
- **Access revocation** complete for all in-scope systems.
- **Property return** signed off or written-off with rationale.

## Reasonable adjustments

Where the departing employee has a disability under the Equality Act
2010, the offboarding process must continue to make reasonable
adjustments (e.g. accessible format for documentation, additional
time, alternative interview format).

## Data subject rights post-employment

The implementation surfaces the following standard messages to the
leaver, drawn from ICO employment guidance:

- Their personal data retention schedule and purposes.
- Their right to subject access (UK GDPR Art. 15).
- Their right to rectification (Art. 16) and erasure (Art. 17, subject
  to statutory retention).
- The contact details of the data protection officer.

## Sign-off

Completion requires:

1. All statutory blocking items completed with evidence.
2. Line manager attestation that handover is complete.
3. HR attestation that final pay and statutory documents are issued.
4. IT / Security attestation that access is revoked.
5. Leaver attestation that property is returned and final pay is
   correct.
