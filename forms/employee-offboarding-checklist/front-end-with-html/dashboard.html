The Employee Offboarding Checklist tracks completion of mandatory leaver requirements (exit interview, knowledge transfer, equipment return, access revocation, final payroll, references, NDA, forwarding details, sign-off). This dashboard is an HR / management review tool used to surface outstanding blockers that put patient safety, organisational data, or service continuity at risk.
