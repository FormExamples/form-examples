# Guideline alignment

How the HRT assessment maps to NICE, BMS, IMS, and ENDO guidance.

## Primary guidance

- NICE NG23 *Menopause: diagnosis and management.* National Institute for
  Health and Care Excellence; first published 2015, last updated 2024.
  <https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/ng23>
- British Menopause Society (BMS) consensus statements.
  <https://thebms.org.uk/publications/consensus-statements/>
- International Menopause Society (IMS) recommendations.
  <https://www.imsociety.org/>
- The 2022 Hormone Therapy Position Statement of The North American
  Menopause Society (NAMS). Menopause 2022;29(7):767-794.
  DOI: 10.1097/GME.0000000000002028.
- Endocrine Society. *Treatment of Symptoms of the Menopause: An Endocrine
  Society Clinical Practice Guideline.* J Clin Endocrinol Metab
  2015;100(11):3975-4011. DOI: 10.1210/jc.2015-2236.

## NICE NG23 essentials

- Vasomotor symptoms: offer HRT after discussion of benefits and risks
  (NG23 §1.3.1).
- Routes: oral or transdermal. Transdermal oestrogen avoids first-pass
  metabolism and is preferred where VTE or migraine is a concern (BMS
  consensus).
- Progestogen: women with a uterus require systemic or local progestogen
  to protect the endometrium.
- Premature ovarian insufficiency: offer HRT or combined oral
  contraceptive at least until average age of natural menopause (51 y) —
  NG23 §1.5.
- VTE risk: oral oestrogen approximately doubles risk; transdermal
  oestrogen does not increase risk in epidemiological studies.
- Breast cancer risk: NICE NG23 references the meta-analysis by the
  Collaborative Group on Hormonal Factors in Breast Cancer (Lancet
  2019;394(10204):1159-1168. DOI: 10.1016/S0140-6736(19)31709-X).
- Cardiovascular risk: HRT is **not** indicated for primary prevention of
  CVD. Discuss baseline risk per CG181 / QRISK3.

## Contraindications (NICE NG23 §1.4)

Absolute contraindications:

- Active or recent breast cancer.
- Active or recent VTE / pulmonary embolism without anticoagulation.
- Active liver disease with abnormal LFTs.
- Untreated endometrial hyperplasia.
- Undiagnosed vaginal bleeding.
- Active porphyria.

Relative (case-by-case):

- Strong family history of breast cancer; BRCA1/2 carriage.
- Migraine with aura → prefer transdermal.
- Previous VTE → prefer transdermal; consider thrombophilia screen.
- Active gallbladder disease.

## Onward referrals

- Premature ovarian insufficiency → specialist gynaecology / fertility.
- Breast lump or atypical bleeding → 2-week-wait cancer pathway.
- Strong family history of breast / ovarian cancer → cancer-genetics
  clinic (NICE NG241).
- Osteoporosis with previous fragility fracture → bone clinic.
