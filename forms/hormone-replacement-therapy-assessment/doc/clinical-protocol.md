# Clinical protocol

Operational protocol for the HRT assessment.

## Indications

- Vasomotor symptoms (hot flushes, night sweats) affecting quality of life.
- Genitourinary syndrome of menopause (vulvovaginal atrophy, dyspareunia,
  urinary symptoms).
- Premature ovarian insufficiency (< 40 y) — HRT until at least average
  age of natural menopause.
- Treatment-induced menopause (surgical, chemotherapy, GnRH agonists).

## Preconditions

- Recent (≤ 12 months) blood pressure measurement.
- BMI calculation.
- Personal and family history of breast, ovarian, endometrial cancer.
- Personal history of VTE and migraine.
- LFTs and U&E within 12 months for new HRT initiation.

## Step-by-step

1. **Demographics.** Patient identifiers; ethnicity (relevant for fracture
   risk and breast cancer baseline).
2. **Menopause Status.** Last menstrual period; surgical menopause;
   primary ovarian insufficiency.
3. **MRS Symptom Scale.** 11-item self-report (see
   `menopause-rating-scale.md`).
4. **Vasomotor Symptoms.** Frequency and severity; quality-of-life
   impact.
5. **Bone Health.** Previous fragility fracture; FRAX risk (≥ 50 y);
   parental hip fracture history.
6. **Cardiovascular Risk.** BP, lipids; QRISK3 / SCORE2 baseline; smoking
   status.
7. **Breast Health.** Last mammogram; breast symptoms; family history.
8. **Current Medications.** SSRIs / SNRIs (alternative to HRT for
   vasomotor symptoms), tamoxifen / aromatase inhibitors (HRT
   contraindicated), antihypertensives, anticoagulants.
9. **Contraindications Screen.** NICE NG23 §1.4 contraindications.
10. **Treatment Preferences.** Patient priorities; oral vs transdermal;
    cyclical vs continuous; combined vs oestrogen-only (uterus status).

## Treatment selection

- **Uterus present**: combined HRT (oestrogen + progestogen).
- **Hysterectomy**: oestrogen-only HRT.
- **Recent menstrual cycles (< 1 y since LMP)**: cyclical combined.
- **≥ 1 y postmenopausal**: continuous combined.
- **VTE concern**: transdermal oestrogen.
- **Migraine with aura**: transdermal oestrogen.
- **GSM only, no systemic symptoms**: vaginal oestrogen (NICE NG23 §1.3.3).

## Onward referrals

- Persistent symptoms despite optimized HRT → menopause specialist /
  British Menopause Society accredited practitioner.
- Unscheduled bleeding > 6 months on continuous HRT → 2-week-wait
  pathway.
- Breast lump or change → 2-week-wait pathway.
- BRCA carriage or strong cancer family history → cancer-genetics.

## Review schedule

- 3 months after HRT initiation: efficacy, side-effects, adherence.
- Annually thereafter: symptom control, side-effects, ongoing
  contraindications, BP, breast awareness.
