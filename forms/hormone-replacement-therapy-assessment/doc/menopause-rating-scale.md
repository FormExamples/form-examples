# Menopause Rating Scale (MRS)

The Menopause Rating Scale is an 11-item self-administered instrument
covering somatic, psychological, and urogenital symptoms of the
peri- and post-menopausal transition. Each item is scored 0 (no symptom)
to 4 (very severe symptoms).

## Origin and validation

- Heinemann LAJ, Potthoff P, Schneider HPG. *International versions of
  the Menopause Rating Scale (MRS).* Health Qual Life Outcomes
  2003;1:28. DOI: 10.1186/1477-7525-1-28. PMID: 12914663.
- Heinemann K, Ruebig A, Potthoff P, et al. *The Menopause Rating Scale
  (MRS) scale: a methodological review.* Health Qual Life Outcomes
  2004;2:45. DOI: 10.1186/1477-7525-2-45. PMID: 15345062.

The MRS is hosted and maintained by the Berlin Centre for Epidemiology and
Health Research and is available in > 30 languages at
<https://www.menopause-rating-scale.info/>.

## Subscales

The MRS has three subscales (Heinemann 2003):

### Somatic (items 1–4)

1. Hot flushes, sweating.
2. Heart discomfort (unusual awareness of heart beat, palpitations).
3. Sleep problems.
4. Joint and muscular discomfort.

Subscale score: 0–16.

### Psychological (items 5–8)

5. Depressive mood.
6. Irritability.
7. Anxiety.
8. Physical and mental exhaustion.

Subscale score: 0–16.

### Urogenital (items 9–11)

9. Sexual problems.
10. Bladder problems.
11. Dryness of vagina.

Subscale score: 0–12.

Total score: 0–44.

## Severity bands

Heinemann 2004 §Table 5 publishes the population-based severity bands:

| Severity | Total | Somatic | Psychological | Urogenital |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| None / minimal | 0–4 | 0–2 | 0–1 | 0 |
| Mild | 5–8 | 3–4 | 2–3 | 1 |
| Moderate | 9–16 | 5–8 | 4–6 | 2–3 |
| Severe | ≥ 17 | ≥ 9 | ≥ 7 | ≥ 4 |

## Use in this form

The MRS is used at Step 3 of the assessment as a self-rated symptom
inventory. The instrument is **not** a diagnostic device; it quantifies
symptom burden to support shared decision-making about HRT initiation,
dose adjustment, and ongoing review.
