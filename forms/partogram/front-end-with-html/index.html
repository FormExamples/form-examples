Record the progress of labour. Enter the labour context and the active-phase start time (dilatation 4 cm), the patient identification and admission findings, then add one row per timed set of observations in the Observation series step. The live indicator updates the labour-progress classification, the latest dilatation and elapsed hours, the alert / action line expectations, and the flagged issues as you go. Answers are saved automatically in your browser.

This is a monitoring aid that classifies progress and prompts review; it is not a validated numeric score, and it is not a diagnosis of obstructed labour or fetal compromise. The classification and flags support clinical judgement; they do not replace it.

The Partogram is an intrapartum monitoring instrument. The alert line (1 cm/hour from 4 cm) and the action line (four hours to its right), and the threshold flags, draw on the WHO Labour Care Guide (2020), WHO intrapartum care recommendations (2018), Philpott and Castle (1972), and NICE NG235. This tool is for monitoring and decision support only and is not a diagnosis; assessment and escalation decisions rest with the clinician.
