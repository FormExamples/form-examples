This dashboard is a clinical-review tool. The KDIGO G-stage, albuminuria stage, risk zone, and review completeness document the classification and whether the annual review is whole; they are not a diagnosis. A very-high risk zone or a referral flag (very-high risk, eGFR < 30, or ACR ≥ 70) prompts nephrology referral unless the patient is already under specialist care.
