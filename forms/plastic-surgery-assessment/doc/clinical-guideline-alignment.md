# Clinical Guideline Alignment — Plastic Surgery Assessment

## UK regulatory framework for cosmetic surgery

- Royal College of Surgeons of England *Cosmetic Surgery Standards*.
  https://www.rcseng.ac.uk/standards-and-research/standards-and-guidance/service-standards/cosmetic-surgery/
- Care Quality Commission *Regulation of cosmetic surgery*.
  https://www.cqc.org.uk/guidance-providers/registration-notifications/cosmetic-surgery
- General Medical Council *Guidance for doctors who offer cosmetic
  interventions* (2024).
  https://www.gmc-uk.org/professional-standards/professional-standards-for-doctors/cosmetic-interventions

These authorities define mandatory two-stage consent, cooling-off period
(typically at least two weeks for aesthetic procedures), and named-surgeon
follow-up, all reflected in step 10.

## Pre-operative tests

- NICE NG45 *Routine preoperative tests for elective surgery*.
  https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/ng45

Step 7 (anaesthetic risk assessment) maps tests to ASA × procedure-grade
exactly as NG45 specifies.

## CPOC preoperative assessment

- Centre for Perioperative Care. *Preoperative Assessment and Optimization
  for Adult Surgery* (June 2021). https://cpoc.org.uk/

## Wound assessment

- CDC *Surgical Site Infection (SSI) Event* (NHSN procedure-associated
  module). https://www.cdc.gov/nhsn/psc/ssi/index.html
- NICE NG125 *Surgical site infections: prevention and treatment.*
  https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/ng125

Step 5 (wound and tissue assessment) follows NHSN classification and
NICE NG125 antibiotic prophylaxis recommendations.

## Psychological assessment for aesthetic surgery

- Veale D, Ellison N, Werner TG, et al. *Development of a Cosmetic
  Procedure Screening Questionnaire (COPS) for Body Dysmorphic Disorder.*
  J Plast Reconstr Aesthet Surg. 2012;65(4):530-532. PMID: 21986288.
- Royal College of Psychiatrists *College Report CR196 — Cosmetic surgery:
  considerations for psychiatrists* (2016). Index:
  https://www.rcpsych.ac.uk/improving-care/campaigning-for-better-mental-health-policy/college-reports

## Reconstructive surgery — flaps, microsurgery

- BAPRAS guidance index: https://www.bapras.org.uk/professionals
- BSSH (British Society for Surgery of the Hand) guidance index:
  https://www.bssh.ac.uk/

## Compliance frameworks

- MDCG 2019-11 Rev.1 (EU MDR/IVDR software classification).
- UK Medical Devices Regulations 2002.
- UK MHRA *Software and AI as a Medical Device*.
- ISO/IEC/IEEE 26514:2022.
