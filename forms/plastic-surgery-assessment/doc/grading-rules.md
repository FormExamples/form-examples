# Grading Rules — Plastic Surgery Assessment

This form combines three established instruments to produce a composite
risk level (low / moderate / high / critical).

## ASA Physical Status Classification

- American Society of Anesthesiologists. *ASA Physical Status
  Classification System.*
  https://www.asahq.org/standards-and-practice-parameters/statement-on-asa-physical-status-classification-system

| Grade | Definition |
| --- | --- |
| I | A normal healthy patient |
| II | A patient with mild systemic disease |
| III | A patient with severe systemic disease |
| IV | A patient with severe systemic disease that is a constant threat to life |
| V | A moribund patient who is not expected to survive without the operation |
| VI | A declared brain-dead patient whose organs are being removed for donor purposes |
| E | Emergency modifier |

## Surgical Wound Classification

- Centers for Disease Control and Prevention. *Surgical Site Infection (SSI)
  Event* (NHSN procedure-associated module). National Healthcare Safety
  Network. https://www.cdc.gov/nhsn/psc/ssi/index.html
- Original definition: Berard F, Gandon J. *Postoperative wound infections:
  the influence of ultraviolet irradiation of the operating room and of
  various other factors.* Ann Surg. 1964;160(Suppl 2):1-192. PMID: 14195387.
  (CDC wound class definitions trace to this Ad Hoc Committee report.)

| Class | Definition |
| --- | --- |
| I — Clean | Uninfected operative wound in which no inflammation is encountered and the respiratory, alimentary, genital, or uninfected urinary tracts are not entered |
| II — Clean-Contaminated | Operative wound in which the respiratory, alimentary, genital, or urinary tract is entered under controlled conditions and without unusual contamination |
| III — Contaminated | Open, fresh, accidental wounds; or operations with major breaks in sterile technique; or gross spillage from the gastrointestinal tract |
| IV — Dirty/Infected | Old traumatic wounds with retained devitalized tissue; or existing clinical infection or perforated viscera |

## Surgical complexity / procedure grade

This form uses the four-band complexity scale (1–4) consistent with the
NHS Modernization Agency operative-procedure grading used by NICE NG45:

- Grade 1 (minor) — e.g., excision of skin lesion, simple repair
- Grade 2 (intermediate) — e.g., local flap, complex laceration repair
- Grade 3 (major) — e.g., free-tissue transfer, major reconstruction
- Grade 4 (major+ / complex) — e.g., multi-stage reconstruction, head-and-neck
  composite reconstruction

Source: NICE *Routine preoperative tests for elective surgery* (NG45).
https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/ng45

## Composite mapping

Composite risk is the max of:

- ASA-derived risk: I-II → low, III → moderate, IV-V → critical
- Wound class: I → low, II → moderate, III → high, IV → critical
- Procedure grade: 1 → low, 2 → moderate, 3 → high, 4 → critical

Any combination of "high" categories in two or more axes escalates one band.

## Photographic documentation

Photographic records for reconstructive and aesthetic planning follow the
British Association of Plastic, Reconstructive and Aesthetic Surgeons
(BAPRAS) and British Association of Aesthetic Plastic Surgeons (BAAPS)
guidance. Authority indices:

- BAPRAS: https://www.bapras.org.uk/
- BAAPS: https://baaps.org.uk/

Specific licensing requirements (patient consent for photography, secure
storage, retention) are recorded in step 8.

## Psychological screening

For aesthetic procedures, body dysmorphic disorder (BDD) screening is
recommended. The Cosmetic Procedure Screening Questionnaire (COPS) is
the canonical instrument:

- Veale D, Ellison N, Werner TG, et al. *Development of a Cosmetic
  Procedure Screening Questionnaire (COPS) for Body Dysmorphic Disorder.*
  J Plast Reconstr Aesthet Surg. 2012;65(4):530-532.
  DOI: 10.1016/j.bjps.2011.09.007. PMID: 21986288.

Step 6 (psychological assessment) records the chosen screening tool and
score; this form does not embed COPS verbatim due to licensing.
