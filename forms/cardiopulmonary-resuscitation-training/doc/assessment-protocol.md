# Assessment Protocol — BLS Skills Verification

This document describes how the examiner runs and scores each of the 8
form steps in a standard BLS Skills Verification session, using the AHA
*BLS Provider Manual* (2020 edition) and the Resuscitation Council UK
*Adult BLS Skills Test*.

## Examination setting

- Adult BLS manikin with feedback (rate, depth, recoil, ventilation
  volume), e.g. Laerdal Resusci Anne QCPR, Prestan Professional, or
  Brayden Pro.
- Training AED with shock-simulating cartridge.
- Stopwatch.
- BLS skill sheet.
- 1 examiner per candidate (with optional second observer).

## Step-by-step scoring

### Step 2 — Scene safety and initial assessment

- "Scene is safe" called aloud before approaching.
- Universal precautions (gloves) considered.

Critical action: failure to call scene safety → not yet a Fail by itself,
but adds to the non-critical deficiency count.

### Step 3 — Responsiveness and breathing check

- Tap shoulders and shout "Are you OK?".
- Look, listen, feel for breathing for **no more than 10 seconds**.
- Agonal gasps recognized as abnormal.

Critical action: spending more than 10 seconds on breathing check, or
failure to start CPR after detecting abnormal breathing.

### Step 4 — Activate emergency response

- Call out for help.
- Direct a specific bystander: "You — call 999/911 and get the AED."
- Confirm the bystander has understood and is leaving.

Critical action: failure to activate emergency response before starting
compressions (in adults) or after 2 minutes of CPR (in children).

### Step 5 — Chest compressions

**Required metrics on the manikin feedback device** (2-minute cycle):

- Rate: 100-120 compressions per minute.
- Depth: 5-6 cm; >80% of compressions within range.
- Recoil: >80% of compressions allow full recoil.
- Hand position: lower half of sternum.
- Compression fraction: >60% of the cycle is compressions.

Critical action: any one of rate, depth, or recoil fails on >50% of
compressions in any 2-minute cycle.

### Step 6 — Airway and rescue breaths

- Head-tilt chin-lift.
- 30:2 compression-to-ventilation ratio.
- Each breath delivered over ~1 second.
- Visible chest rise.
- Total ventilation time <10 seconds per pair.

Critical action: failure to produce visible chest rise on two of the four
attempted pairs in a 2-minute cycle.

### Step 7 — AED use and shock delivery

- Switches on AED.
- Attaches pads to dry, bare chest in correct positions.
- Calls out "Clear" and visually confirms no one is in contact during
  analysis and shock.
- Delivers shock when advised.
- Resumes compressions immediately after shock.

Critical action: any rescuer in contact with patient during shock; pads
applied to wet skin; pad applied directly over implanted device.

### Step 8 — Team dynamics, handover, and post-event feedback

For two-rescuer scenarios:

- Defined team roles.
- Closed-loop communication.
- Compressor rotation every 2 minutes.
- Structured handover using SBAR or ATMIST.
- Post-event debrief facilitated by team leader.

## Overall result

- **Pass**: all critical actions met; ≤2 non-critical deficiencies.
- **Fail — critical action**: any single critical-action failure.
- **Fail — cumulative**: more than 2 non-critical deficiencies.

## Remediation

Candidates who fail must:

1. Receive immediate feedback identifying each failed metric.
2. Practise on a feedback manikin for at least 20 minutes.
3. Re-test on the same day if practical, otherwise within 30 days.
4. Failure on the second attempt requires repeating the full BLS course.

## Documentation

The scoring engine records:

- Per-step metric values where measurable (compressions/min, depth in cm,
  recoil fraction, ventilation volume, hands-off time during AED).
- Per-step pass/fail flags.
- Critical-action failure list (if any).
- Cumulative non-critical deficiency count.
- Overall outcome (Pass / Fail — critical action / Fail — cumulative).
- Examiner name and signature.
- Date of certificate expiry (2 years from pass date, AHA convention;
  3 years for EFAW under HSE L74 in the UK).
