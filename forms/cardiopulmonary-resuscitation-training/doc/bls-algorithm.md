# Adult Basic Life Support Algorithm

This form verifies adult Basic Life Support (BLS) competency against the
American Heart Association (AHA) and Resuscitation Council UK (RCUK) /
European Resuscitation Council (ERC) 2020/2021 guidelines, which are now
harmonised on the major BLS metrics.

## Adult BLS sequence

1. **Scene safety** — ensure safety of rescuer and patient.
2. **Check responsiveness** — shout, gently shake shoulders.
3. **Shout for help** — activate emergency response and request an AED.
4. **Open airway** — head-tilt chin-lift (jaw thrust if trauma).
5. **Check breathing** — look, listen, feel for normal breathing for
   no more than 10 seconds. Agonal gasps count as abnormal breathing.
6. **Start CPR** if breathing is absent or abnormal.
7. **Apply AED** as soon as it arrives.
8. **Continue** in 2-minute cycles until ROSC, ALS team arrival, or
   rescuer exhaustion.

## Critical-action metrics

The five quantitative BLS metrics that determine pass/fail on automated
manikins (and that the assessor records in step 5):

| Metric | AHA 2020 | RCUK / ERC 2021 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Compression rate | 100-120/min | 100-120/min |
| Compression depth (adult) | At least 5 cm, no more than 6 cm | 5-6 cm |
| Compression depth (child) | Approximately 5 cm (≥1/3 AP chest depth) | 1/3 AP chest depth |
| Compression depth (infant) | Approximately 4 cm (≥1/3 AP chest depth) | 1/3 AP chest depth |
| Compression:ventilation ratio | 30:2 single rescuer (adult); 15:2 child/infant with two trained rescuers | Identical |
| Full chest recoil | Required; no leaning | Identical |
| Chest compression fraction | >60% (target >80%) | Identical |
| Tidal volume per ventilation | Approximately 500-600 mL (chest rise) | Identical |
| Ventilation duration | 1 second per breath | Identical |

## AED operation

1. Switch on the AED.
2. Attach pads to bare, dry chest:
   - Adult / child >8 y: right upper chest, left lateral chest.
   - Child 1-8 y or <25 kg: paediatric pads or attenuator if available;
     anterior-posterior placement preferred.
3. Stop CPR and ensure no one is in contact during analysis.
4. Deliver shock if advised; ensure clear "Stand clear" call before
   pressing shock button.
5. Resume compressions immediately after shock or "No shock advised".
6. Re-analyse every 2 minutes.

Universal AED safety:

- Remove patches (GTN, hormonal) before pad placement.
- Move pad at least 8 cm from implanted pacemaker / ICD.
- Wet patient — dry the chest; remove from standing water.
- Hairy chest — shave if pad adhesion is poor.

## Pass criteria for this form

- **Pass**: every critical action met; no critical-action failure;
  ≤2 non-critical deficiencies.
- **Fail (critical-action)**: any of the following triggers automatic
  Fail regardless of other performance:
  - Compression rate consistently outside 100-120/min
  - Compression depth consistently outside 5-6 cm (adult)
  - Failure to allow full recoil
  - Failure to deliver effective ventilations (chest rise)
  - Unsafe AED delivery (any rescuer in contact during shock, fail to
    clear the patient before analysis)
  - Failure to switch on or apply AED within 2 minutes of arrival
- **Fail (cumulative)**: more than 2 non-critical deficiencies (e.g.
  imperfect hand position, slow rhythm-check, slow handover).

## Compression-only CPR

For untrained or unwilling lay rescuers, AHA / RCUK / ERC all support
compression-only ("hands-only") CPR until an AED arrives. This form
records both modalities; the candidate must demonstrate the standard
30:2 sequence to pass at the healthcare-provider level.

## Special populations

- **Pregnancy** (>20 weeks gestation, or uterus palpable above the
  umbilicus): perform left-lateral uterine displacement throughout CPR.
- **Hypothermia**: continue prolonged resuscitation; do not declare death
  until the core temperature is >32 °C.
- **Drowning**: start with 5 rescue breaths before compressions; otherwise
  standard BLS.
- **Opioid overdose**: standard BLS; consider naloxone if available.

## Post-event team feedback

The 2020 AHA "high-performance team" framework is the basis for step 8:

- Defined roles (team leader, compressor, ventilator, AED operator,
  timer/recorder).
- Closed-loop communication.
- Constructive intervention.
- Knowledge sharing and reassessment.
- Mutual respect and clear summary at end of event.
