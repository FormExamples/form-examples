Pass criterion: all required skills demonstrated, no critical-action failure, and at most two non-critical deficiencies. Answers are saved automatically to your browser as you go.

The AHA BLS Skills Verification Checklist is a competency-based instrument used to certify rescuers on adult Basic Life Support and AED skills. This examiner tool records observed performance and applies AHA pass/fail criteria; it is not a substitute for the instructor's professional judgement.
