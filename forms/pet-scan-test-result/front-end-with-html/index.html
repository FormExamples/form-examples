Answers are saved automatically in your browser as you go.

The four-axis interpretation grade follows RCR actionable-reporting principles and the Deauville / PERCIST structured-reporting systems: distant metastasis or progressive metabolic disease is a critical finding that auto-escalates the follow-up urgency to a critical alert and must be communicated directly to the referrer.

This structured molecular-imaging report is aligned with the Royal College of Radiologists (RCR) standards for the interpretation and reporting of imaging investigations, the Lugano classification / 5-point Deauville score for lymphoma, the PERCIST metabolic-response criteria for solid tumours, EANM / SNMMI FDG-PET reporting guidance, and IR(ME)R 2017. It is a reporting aid, not a diagnosis; interpret and act in line with local critical-result policies and clinical judgement.
