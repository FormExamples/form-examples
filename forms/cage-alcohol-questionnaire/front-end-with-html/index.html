Complete the four lifetime CAGE questions below for an adult (16 years or over). Each question scores 1 point for a "yes", for a total of 0 to 4. A score of 2 or more is a positive screen and prompts a fuller assessment of drinking. Answers are saved automatically in your browser as you go.

CAGE is a screening prompt, not a diagnosis of an alcohol-use disorder. A score below 2 does not exclude hazardous or harmful drinking: a "yes" to the eye-opener question is a strong marker of physical dependence, and a consumption-focused tool such as AUDIT-C is more sensitive to earlier at-risk drinking.

The CAGE questionnaire was described by Ewing (JAMA 1984) and derived from earlier work by Mayfield et al. (Am J Psychiatry 1974). This screening tool is for information only and is not a diagnosis; a positive screen (CAGE >= 2) is a prompt for a fuller assessment of drinking and clinical judgement.
