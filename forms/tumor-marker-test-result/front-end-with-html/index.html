Tumour markers are poor screening tests: interpret every value in clinical context per ACB and RCPath guidance. A markedly elevated value, or a rising trend on treatment, is the signal that drives action. A very high AFP or beta-hCG suggesting a germ-cell tumour is a critical result and auto-escalates the follow-up urgency to a critical alert.

This structured tumour-marker report is aligned with ACB and RCPath laboratory-medicine reporting standards and ASCO germ-cell tumour-marker guidance, including the germ-cell critical thresholds (AFP ≥ 1000 ng/mL, beta-hCG ≥ 5000 IU/L) and minimum retesting intervals. It is a reporting aid, not a diagnosis; interpret and act in line with local oncology pathways and clinical judgement.
