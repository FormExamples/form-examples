# SNOT-22 scoring rules

## Instrument

The **Sino-Nasal Outcome Test 22 (SNOT-22)** is a 22-item disease-specific
patient-reported outcome measure for chronic rhinosinusitis (CRS) and other
sinonasal disorders. It was developed from the predecessor SNOT-20 by Hopkins
et al. and is the primary outcome measure of the UK National Comparative
Audit of Surgery for Nasal Polyposis and Chronic Rhinosinusitis.

- Hopkins C, Gillett S, Slack R, Lund VJ, Browne JP. *Psychometric validity of
  the 22-item Sinonasal Outcome Test*. Clinical Otolaryngology 2009;34:
  447-454. PMID: 19793277. DOI: 10.1111/j.1749-4486.2009.01995.x

## Items

Twenty-two items, each scored 0-5 on a Likert scale:

- 0 = No problem
- 1 = Very mild problem
- 2 = Mild or slight problem
- 3 = Moderate problem
- 4 = Severe problem
- 5 = Problem as bad as it can be

Total range **0-110**.

## Domains

The 22 items aggregate into five symptom domains (Khan A et al. 2019):

1. **Rhinologic** (items 1, 2, 3, 8, 21)
2. **Extra-nasal rhinologic** (items 4, 6, 22)
3. **Ear/facial** (items 5, 7, 9, 10, 19, 20)
4. **Psychological** (items 11, 12, 14, 15, 16, 17, 18)
5. **Sleep** (items 13)

(Items numbered as in the original Hopkins 2009 paper.)

## Category cut-points used in this form

The form classifies the total SNOT-22 into three operational categories
mirroring the EPOS 2020 stratification used in the EUFOREA care pathway:

| Total | Category | Action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0-7 | Mild | Reassurance, intranasal saline, recheck in 3 months |
| 8-19 | Moderate | Intranasal corticosteroid (mometasone, fluticasone) + saline irrigation; review at 8 weeks |
| ≥20 | Severe | ENT referral; consider CT sinuses, biologics for CRSwNP, FESS |

The 7- and 20-point cut-points correspond to the EPOS 2020 VAS-equivalent
banding for mild / moderate / severe disease impact.

## Minimal clinically important difference

- MCID ≈ **8.9 points** decrease after intervention (Hopkins C et al. 2009).
- The form flags any post-treatment improvement ≥9 points as "clinically
  meaningful response".

## Comparison instruments captured

- **TNSS (Total Nasal Symptom Score)** — used in allergic rhinitis trials,
  0-12 across nasal congestion, rhinorrhoea, sneezing, and itch.
- **Lund-Mackay CT score** — endoscopic / radiologic score recorded if a CT
  has been performed.
- **Lund-Kennedy endoscopy score** — for objective assessment in research
  settings.

## Flagged-issue triggers

- SNOT-22 ≥ 20 → ENT referral recommendation per EPOS 2020 / BSACI Scadding
  2017 rhinitis guideline.
- SNOT-22 ≥ 40 → severe CRS, consider biologics (dupilumab, mepolizumab,
  omalizumab) per NICE TA751, TA833, TA824.
- Unilateral nasal symptoms (Step 2 + endoscopic finding) + epistaxis →
  2-week-wait head & neck cancer referral per NICE NG12.
- Persistent unilateral nasal polyp → urgent ENT referral (rule out
  inverted papilloma).
