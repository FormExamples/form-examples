# Clinical guideline alignment

## EPOS 2020

- Fokkens WJ, Lund VJ, Hopkins C, et al. *European Position Paper on
  Rhinosinusitis and Nasal Polyps 2020*. Rhinology 2020 Suppl 29:1-464.
  PMID: 32077450. <https://epos2020.com/>
- EPOS 2020 is the definitive European consensus on diagnosis and management
  of acute and chronic rhinosinusitis. The form embeds the EPOS 2020 primary
  care care-pathway and the SNOT-22 mild/moderate/severe banding.

## BSACI

- Scadding GK, Kariyawasam HH, Scadding G, et al. *BSACI guideline for the
  diagnosis and management of allergic and non-allergic rhinitis (revised
  edition 2017)*. Clinical & Experimental Allergy 2017;47:856-889.
  PMID: 30239057
- Form coverage: cross-references the BSACI rhinitis pathway when SNOT-22 is
  rhinitis-dominant (rhinologic domain >> ear/facial domain).

## NICE

- NICE NG12 *Suspected cancer: recognition and referral*.
  <https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/ng12>
- NICE CKS *Chronic rhinosinusitis*. <https://cks.nice.org.uk/topics/sinusitis/>
- NICE TA751 *Dupilumab for treating severe chronic rhinosinusitis with
  nasal polyps*. <https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/ta751>
- NICE TA833 *Mepolizumab for treating chronic rhinosinusitis with nasal
  polyps*. <https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/ta833>
- NICE TA824 *Omalizumab for treating chronic rhinosinusitis with nasal
  polyps*. <https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/ta824>

## ENT-UK / British Rhinological Society

- ENT-UK / British Rhinological Society. *Position statement on the
  management of CRS in primary care*. London: ENT-UK.
  <https://www.entuk.org/>
- Royal College of Surgeons commissioning guide for rhinosinusitis.
