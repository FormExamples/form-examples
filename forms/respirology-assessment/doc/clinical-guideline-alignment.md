# Clinical guideline alignment

The respirology assessment is a general respiratory consultation form that
uses the MRC dyspnoea scale as its primary outcome instrument. It aligns
with NICE, BTS, ERS, and the GOLD report.

## NICE

- NICE NG115 *Chronic obstructive pulmonary disease in over 16s: diagnosis
  and management*. <https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/ng115>
- NICE NG80 *Asthma: diagnosis, monitoring and chronic asthma management*
  (withdrawn; superseded by NG245). <https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/ng245>
- NICE NG138 *Cough (acute): antimicrobial prescribing*.
  <https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/ng120>
- NICE NG12 *Suspected cancer: recognition and referral*.
  <https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/ng12>
- NICE NG191 *COVID-19 rapid guideline: managing the long-term effects of
  COVID-19*. <https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/ng188>

## BTS

- BTS Pulmonary Rehabilitation Guideline 2013. *Thorax* 2013;68 Suppl 2.
  PMID: 23880483
- BTS Guideline for the Investigation and Management of Pulmonary Nodules
  2015. *Thorax* 2015;70 Suppl 2:ii1-ii54. PMID: 26082159
- BTS Cough Guidelines — Royal College of Physicians and BTS recommend the
  Leicester Cough Questionnaire (LCQ) for chronic cough.
  <https://www.brit-thoracic.org.uk/quality-improvement/guidelines/>

## ERS

- Morice AH, Millqvist E, Bieksiene K, et al. *ERS guidelines on the
  diagnosis and treatment of chronic cough in adults and children*. Eur
  Respir J 2020;55:1901136. PMID: 31515408

## GOLD (cross-reference)

When the form identifies airflow obstruction it cross-references the
pulmonology-assessment form's GOLD staging.

## NHS Targeted Lung Health Check

- NHS England Targeted Lung Health Check programme — eligibility 55-74
  years, current or former smoker. Cross-referenced when smoking history is
  positive.
- <https://www.england.nhs.uk/cancer/early-diagnosis/targeted-lung-health-checks/>
