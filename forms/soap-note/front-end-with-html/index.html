Record a single patient encounter as a SOAP note — Subjective, Objective, Assessment, and Plan — plus encounter context, patient identification, and a free-text summary. Answers are saved automatically in your browser as you go.

This is a documentation and completeness instrument, not a numeric clinical score. It grades the note Complete, Partial, or Incomplete, computes a completeness percentage, and raises safety flags (for example: a missing Assessment or Plan, red-flag symptoms without a plan, or no safety-netting advice). A Complete grade means the note is well documented so that another clinician can safely continue care — it does not mean the clinical care was correct.

Submit the form to see the report.

The SOAP format was introduced as part of Lawrence Weed's problem-oriented medical record and is the most widely taught structure for clinical progress notes. This tool grades the completeness of the written record and prompts safety-netting; it is not a diagnosis, a risk score, or a substitute for clinical judgement.
