This structured fluoroscopy report is aligned with the Royal College of Radiologists (RCR) Standards for the interpretation and reporting of imaging investigations, the ACR practice parameter for contrast examinations, and IR(ME)R 2017 dose-audit requirements (screening-time recording). It is a reporting aid, not a diagnosis; interpret and act in line with local radiology pathways and clinical judgement.
