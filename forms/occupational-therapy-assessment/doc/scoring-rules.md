# Scoring Rules — Occupational Therapy Assessment (COPM)

## Canadian Occupational Performance Measure (COPM)

The primary instrument is the COPM, a client-centred outcome measure
developed by the Canadian Association of Occupational Therapists (CAOT)
in collaboration with researchers at the University of Toronto and McGill.

- Canonical reference: Law M, Baptiste S, McColl M, Opzoomer A, Polatajko
  H, Pollock N. *The Canadian Occupational Performance Measure: an outcome
  measure for occupational therapy.* Can J Occup Ther. 1990;57(2):82-87.
  DOI: 10.1177/000841749005700207. PMID: 10104738.
- Manual (most recent): *Canadian Occupational Performance Measure*,
  6th edition (CAOT Publications ACE, 2024).
- Official instrument site: https://www.thecopm.ca/

### Procedure

1. The therapist interviews the client (with proxy if needed) across
   three occupational performance areas:
   - Self-care (personal care, functional mobility, community management)
   - Productivity (paid / unpaid work, household management, play / school)
   - Leisure (quiet recreation, active recreation, socialisation)
2. The client identifies up to five problems and rates **importance**
   (1–10).
3. The five highest-importance problems are carried forward.
4. The client rates **performance** (1–10) and **satisfaction** (1–10)
   for each.
5. Performance and satisfaction scores are averaged.

### Score interpretation

| Score range | Interpretation |
| --- | --- |
| 1–4 | Significant performance / satisfaction issues |
| 5–7 | Moderate concerns |
| 8–10 | Good performance / satisfaction |

### Clinically important change

The published minimal clinically important difference (MCID) is a 2-point
change on performance or satisfaction.

- Carswell A, McColl MA, Baptiste S, et al. *The Canadian Occupational
  Performance Measure: a research and clinical literature review.* Can J
  Occup Ther. 2004;71(4):210-222. DOI: 10.1177/000841740407100406.
  PMID: 15586853.

### Licensing

COPM is a copyrighted instrument; the manual must be purchased from CAOT
Publications ACE (https://caot.ca/site/pd/copm). The form records the COPM
scores but does not embed the manual or the protocol verbatim.

## Productivity, self-care, and leisure framing

The COPM categories reflect the CAOT occupational performance framework:

- CAOT *Profile of Occupational Therapy Practice in Canada*.
  https://caot.ca/site/aboutot/profile

## Environmental factors — ICF

Step 8 (environmental factors) is structured to the WHO International
Classification of Functioning, Disability and Health (ICF) e-codes:

- WHO ICF: https://www.who.int/standards/classifications/international-classification-of-functioning-disability-and-health

## Physical & cognitive status

Step 9 captures contextual information that influences COPM
interpretation. Validated measures referenced (not embedded):

- Cognition: Montreal Cognitive Assessment (MoCA), 4AT
- Physical: Barthel Index, FIM, modified Rankin Scale
- Mood: PHQ-9, HADS

## Composite mapping

The form's category emits:

- < 5 → Significant issues
- 5–7 → Moderate concerns
- > 7 → Good performance

Per the COPM manual the score is averaged over the chosen problems;
individual problem scores are also retained in the export.
