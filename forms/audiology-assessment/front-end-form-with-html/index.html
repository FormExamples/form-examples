If you have sudden hearing loss, severe vertigo, or active ear discharge, please seek urgent care; sudden hearing loss warrants ENT review within 72 hours.

Hearing-loss grading uses the World Health Organization (WHO) classification by pure tone average (PTA): ≤25 dB normal, 26-40 mild, 41-60 moderate, 61-80 severe, >80 profound. This screening tool is for information only and is not a diagnosis; please discuss your results with your audiologist or clinician.
