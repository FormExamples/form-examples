Reasonable-adjustments request wizard for a neurodivergent worker. Record the worker and their role, the manager or HR contact handling the request, the neurodivergent profile (conditions, diagnosis status, disability self-assessment, and disclosure consent), the functional difficulties experienced across the ACAS functional areas, the specific adjustments requested across the ACAS adjustment categories, any supporting evidence, and the current impact and urgency. The engine computes a four-axis grade (Equality Act 2010 eligibility, impact / wellbeing, request completeness, and handling priority) plus a set of compliance and wellbeing flags.

A formal diagnosis is not required for the Equality Act 2010 duty to make reasonable adjustments to apply. The final decision on adjustments remains a matter for the worker and employer to agree together, without unreasonable delay.
