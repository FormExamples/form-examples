# Clinical guideline alignment

## Primary UK guidance

The gynecology-assessment form aggregates symptom-specific NICE guidance
rather than implementing a single composite score. Each section of the form
maps to a NICE guideline or RCOG Green-top guideline.

| Form domain | Primary guideline |
| ----------- | ----------------- |
| Heavy menstrual bleeding | NICE NG88 |
| Pelvic pain / endometriosis | NICE NG73 |
| Suspected gynaecological cancer | NICE NG12 |
| Urinary incontinence / prolapse | NICE NG123 |
| Menopause / HRT | NICE NG23 |
| Sexual health symptoms | BASHH guidelines |
| Cervical screening | NHS Cervical Screening Programme |

## RCOG Green-top guidelines

The RCOG Green-top portal is the canonical source:
<https://www.rcog.org.uk/guidance/browse-all-guidance/green-top-guidelines/>

Specific Green-top guidelines that the form's flagging logic references:

- GTG 17 — *Recurrent miscarriage* (when miscarriage history is entered)
- GTG 20a — *External cephalic version and reducing the incidence of term
  breech presentation* (handled in obstetrics form)
- GTG 24 — *Endometriosis* (now superseded by NICE NG73)
- GTG 41 — *The management of women with cervical cancer screening
  abnormalities*
- GTG 62 — *Management of the menopause* (now NICE NG23)
