# Assessment protocol

## Intended use

A 10-step intake questionnaire that captures the full gynaecological
history, applies symptom-specific NICE/RCOG triage rules, and surfaces NICE
NG12 red flags for suspected cancer.

## Intended users

- GPs and practice nurses
- Outpatient gynaecology clinic clinicians
- Sexual health clinic clinicians

## Setting

UK primary or secondary care.

## Workflow

1. **Demographics** — patient identifiers, GP, ethnicity, language,
   pregnancy status.
2. **Chief complaint** — single primary reason for attendance with
   structured options (HMB, IMB, pain, prolapse symptoms, dyspareunia,
   subfertility concern, lump).
3. **Menstrual history** — LMP, cycle, bleeding pattern, dysmenorrhoea NRS,
   HMB impact question (NG88), intermenstrual or postcoital bleeding.
4. **Gynaecological symptoms** — pelvic pain, prolapse symptoms, urinary
   incontinence, bowel symptoms, vaginal discharge, vulval symptoms.
5. **Cervical screening** — last screening date, result, recall status.
6. **Obstetric history** — gravidity, parity, mode of deliveries, miscarriage,
   termination, ectopic. (Detailed pregnancy assessment handled in the
   obstetrics-assessment form.)
7. **Sexual health** — current sexual activity, partners, contraception,
   STI screening history, dyspareunia, postcoital bleeding.
8. **Medical history** — comorbidities, surgical history, anticoagulation,
   relevant family history.
9. **Current medications** — including OTC and complementary, with focus
   on bleeding-relevant agents.
10. **Family history** — breast, ovarian, endometrial, colorectal cancer
    family history for hereditary cancer screening per NICE NG12.

## Output

- A per-domain severity band (mild/moderate/severe)
- A red-flag list aligned to NICE NG12 §1.5 (gynaecological cancers)
- A suggested referral pathway: continue GP management, routine gynae,
  urgent gynae (2-week-wait), urgent menopause, pelvic floor / urogyn,
  sexual health clinic
- A structured PDF report

## Safety-net behaviour

- Postmenopausal bleeding → automatic 2-week-wait recommendation
- IMB ≥ 4 weeks in women aged 45+ → 2-week-wait recommendation
- Suspected ovarian mass features (early satiety, abdominal distension >
  12 days/month in women 50+) → urgent investigation and CA125
- Vulval lesion with persistent itching → urgent vulvoscopy referral
- Pregnancy-related bleeding → redirected to prenatal-assessment pathway

## Out of scope

- Cervical examination findings (clinician-only)
- Colposcopy interpretation
- Hysteroscopy reporting
- POP-Q staging (clinician examination)
- Detailed obstetric care (see obstetrics-assessment)
- Detailed contraception eligibility (see contraception-assessment)
