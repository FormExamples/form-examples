Complete the nine HAS-BLED criteria below for an adult (18 years or over) with atrial fibrillation who is on, or being considered for, oral anticoagulation. Each criterion scores 1 point when present, for a total of 0 to 9. A score of 3 or more indicates a higher estimated risk of major bleeding. Answers are saved automatically in your browser as you go.

A high HAS-BLED score is not a contraindication to anticoagulation and must not by itself be used to withhold it. Its value is to highlight modifiable bleeding-risk factors that can be corrected. Use it alongside a stroke-risk score (CHA2DS2-VASc), not instead of it, and with clinical judgement.

The HAS-BLED score was derived and validated by Pisters et al. (Chest 2010) and is recommended for bleeding-risk assessment by the ESC and NICE AF guidelines. This screening tool is for information only and is not a diagnosis; it does not decide for or against anticoagulation. Correct modifiable factors, weigh against CHA2DS2-VASc stroke risk, and use clinical judgement.
