Record a single daily ward round review — the review header, patient identification, and the ten review components (overnight events, current problems and progress, examination and NEWS2, investigations, VTE assessment, medication changes, plan and jobs, escalation, and estimated discharge). Answers are saved automatically in your browser as you go.

This is a documentation and completeness instrument, not a numeric clinical score. It grades the entry Complete, Partial, or Incomplete, computes a completeness percentage over the eight required components, and raises safety flags (for example: a deteriorating NEWS2 needing escalation, a VTE assessment not done, or no plan documented). An explicit negative — "no changes", "none outstanding" — counts as documented. A Complete grade means the entry is well documented so another clinician can safely continue care between shifts; it does not mean the clinical care was correct.

The ward round note is a contemporaneous daily record supporting continuity of care between shifts and good medical record-keeping (GMC Good Medical Practice; AoMRC record standards; NEWS2; NICE NG89 VTE). This tool grades the completeness of the written record and prompts escalation and safety-netting; it is not a diagnosis, a risk score, or a substitute for clinical judgement.
