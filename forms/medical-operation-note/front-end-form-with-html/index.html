Operating-team data-entry wizard for the surgical operation note. Document objective intra-operative findings across the 12 sections below; the engine computes a composite operative risk grade (Routine / Complicated / High-risk / Critical) using the Clavien-Dindo classification, estimated blood loss, swab and instrument counts, never-event candidates, and anaesthetic incidents, then emits a list of safety flags.

This is the contemporaneous record written by the operating team immediately after the procedure, before they leave theatre. The final attestation is the responsibility of the signing lead surgeon.

Submit the form to see the report.

Composite operative risk grading uses a max-grade algorithm over Clavien-Dindo (Dindo & Clavien, Ann Surg 2004; 240:205), estimated blood loss bands, swab/needle/instrument counts, NHS England Never Events Policy, and anaesthetic incident criteria. This screening tool is for clinical decision support only; the final attestation, surgeon override, and dictated record remain the responsibility of the signing lead surgeon.
