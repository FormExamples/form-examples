# Grading Rules — Medical Operation Note

The operation note records the composite operative risk grade
(Routine / Complicated / High-risk / Critical) using a **max-band**
algorithm: the worst driver fires the grade. The contributing instruments
are listed here with citations.

## Clavien–Dindo classification

Used for any intra-operative or in-recovery complication.

- Canonical reference: Dindo D, Demartines N, Clavien P-A. *Classification
  of surgical complications: a new proposal with evaluation in a cohort of
  6336 patients and results of a survey.* Ann Surg. 2004;240(2):205-213.
  DOI: 10.1097/01.sla.0000133083.54934.ae. PMID: 15273542.
- Updated: Clavien PA, Barkun J, de Oliveira ML, et al. *The Clavien-Dindo
  classification of surgical complications: five-year experience.*
  Ann Surg. 2009;250(2):187-196. DOI: 10.1097/SLA.0b013e3181b13ca2.
- Reference site: https://www.assessurgery.com/clavien-dindo-classification/

| Grade | Description |
| --- | --- |
| I | Any deviation from the normal postoperative course not requiring pharmacological treatment or surgical, endoscopic or radiological intervention |
| II | Requiring pharmacological treatment with drugs other than those allowed for grade I (including blood transfusions and total parenteral nutrition) |
| IIIa | Requiring surgical, endoscopic or radiological intervention not under general anaesthesia |
| IIIb | Requiring surgical, endoscopic or radiological intervention under general anaesthesia |
| IVa | Life-threatening complication (single organ dysfunction) requiring IC/ICU management |
| IVb | Life-threatening complication with multiorgan dysfunction |
| V | Death of a patient |

## WHO Surgical Safety Checklist (sign-out)

- Reference: WHO Surgical Safety Checklist (2009 with subsequent updates).
  https://www.who.int/teams/integrated-health-services/patient-safety/research/safe-surgery
- Implementation manual: *Implementation manual: WHO Surgical Safety
  Checklist* (1st ed., 2008). Available from the WHO safe-surgery page.

Sign-out items recorded in this form:

- Name of the procedure recorded
- Instrument, sponge and needle counts are correct (or not applicable)
- How the specimen is labelled (including patient name)
- Whether there are any equipment problems to be addressed
- Key concerns for recovery and management of this patient

## ASA Physical Status (context)

ASA grade is carried forward from the pre-operative assessment and used as
a context modifier (ASA IV escalates to High; ASA V/VI to Critical).
Source: American Society of Anesthesiologists,
https://www.asahq.org/standards-and-practice-parameters/statement-on-asa-physical-status-classification-system

## Estimated blood loss thresholds

Thresholds in this form follow Royal College of Surgeons of England *Good
Surgical Practice* documentation conventions for op-note minimum data sets.

- Royal College of Surgeons of England. *Good Surgical Practice*. Index:
  https://www.rcseng.ac.uk/standards-and-research/standards-and-guidance/good-practice-guides/good-surgical-practice/

## Never events

A never-event suspicion is grounds for immediate escalation regardless of
all other findings. The form's never-event flag list is taken from the NHS
England *Never Events list* (most recent edition).

- NHS England *Never Events policy and framework*. Index:
  https://www.england.nhs.uk/patient-safety/patient-safety-insight/never-events/

## Composite mapping table

| Band | Drivers |
| --- | --- |
| Routine | EBL ≤ 500 mL, no complications, counts correct, no conversion, planned disposition, Clavien–Dindo 0, ASA I–II |
| Complicated | EBL 500–1500 mL, Clavien–Dindo I–II, minor anaesthetic event, planned step-up to enhanced care |
| High-risk | EBL 1500–3000 mL, Clavien–Dindo IIIa–IIIb, transfusion required, conversion to open, unplanned HDU/ICU admission, ASA IV |
| Critical | EBL > 3000 mL, Clavien–Dindo IVa–V, count discrepancy unresolved, retained foreign body suspected, never-event suspicion, intra-operative arrest, ASA V–VI |

The mapping is the form's editorial composite; the drivers (Clavien–Dindo,
EBL, ASA) are the cited instruments.
