Record the performed exam type and laterality, the examination adequacy and ACR breast density, the clinical history and comparison with previous imaging, the narrative and structured findings, the largest lesion measurement, and the summary impression with the ACR BI-RADS final assessment category. Answers are saved automatically in your browser as you go.

The BI-RADS final assessment is the key structured score: a BI-RADS 4 category raises an urgent two-week-wait biopsy referral, and a BI-RADS 4c or 5 result (≥ 50 % likelihood of malignancy) auto-escalates the follow-up urgency to a critical alert that must be communicated to the referrer.

This structured mammography report is aligned with the ACR BI-RADS atlas and RCR / NHSBSP breast-imaging reporting standards, including the BI-RADS final assessment categories, the ACR breast-composition (density) bands, and the two-week-wait suspected-cancer referral pathway for BI-RADS 4 and 5 results. It is a reporting aid, not a diagnosis; interpret and act in line with local breast-imaging pathways and clinical judgement.
