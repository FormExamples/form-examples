# Regulatory Framework

The Legal Requirements Privacy Notice covers patient personal data that an
NHS GP practice is **statutorily required** to share with named bodies
including NHS England, the Care Quality Commission, and the UK Health
Security Agency. Unlike the Care Privacy Notice (direct-care sharing under
implied authority), the legal-requirements flows are mandated by primary
or secondary legislation, and the patient cannot opt out.

## Primary instruments

- **UK GDPR** Article 6(1)(c) — processing necessary for compliance with a
  legal obligation to which the controller is subject. Indexed:
  <https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2016/679/contents>
- **UK GDPR** Article 9(2)(h) — processing for the provision of health or
  social care, authorized by UK law (DPA 2018 Schedule 1 Part 1 paragraph
  2). Article 9(2)(i) is the appropriate special-category basis where the
  recipient is the UK Health Security Agency for public-health purposes.
- **Data Protection Act 2018** (c. 12) —
  <https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2018/12/contents>

## Statutory recipients

### NHS England (formerly NHS Digital)

NHS England receives mandatory data under the Health and Social Care Act
2012 sections 254 and 259 (Directions issued by the Secretary of State).
Examples include GP Extraction Service flows, the General Practice
Extraction Service successor (GPES / DARS), and the National Diabetes
Audit.

- Health and Social Care Act 2012, s. 254 —
  <https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2012/7/section/254>
- Health and Social Care Act 2012, s. 259 —
  <https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2012/7/section/259>
- NHS England Data Access Request Service:
  <https://digital.nhs.uk/services/data-access-request-service-dars>

### Care Quality Commission

The CQC is empowered under the Health and Social Care Act 2008 (and
secondary regulations) to require providers to make information available
for inspection.

- Health and Social Care Act 2008 (c. 14) —
  <https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2008/14/contents>
- CQC — How we use information:
  <https://www.cqc.org.uk/about-us/our-policies/privacy-statement>

### UK Health Security Agency (UKHSA)

UKHSA receives notifiable-disease data under the Public Health (Control of
Disease) Act 1984 and the Health Protection (Notification) Regulations 2010
(SI 2010/659).

- Public Health (Control of Disease) Act 1984 —
  <https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1984/22/contents>
- Health Protection (Notification) Regulations 2010 —
  <https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2010/659/contents>
- UKHSA — About us:
  <https://www.gov.uk/government/organizations/uk-health-security-agency>

## Common-law duty interaction

Where data is shared with a statutory recipient under a legal duty, the
controller has a defence to the common-law duty of confidentiality. The
NHS Confidentiality Code of Practice 2003 articulates this defence at
section "Disclosures required by law".

NHS Confidentiality Code of Practice (archived):
<https://webarchive.nationalarchives.gov.uk/ukgwa/+/http://www.dh.gov.uk/en/Publicationsandstatistics/Publications/PublicationsPolicyAndGuidance/DH_4069253>

## Patient choice

The notice is read-and-acknowledge. The patient **cannot** opt out of
statutory disclosures because the controller is acting under a legal
obligation. The acknowledgment evidences that the patient was informed in
satisfaction of UK GDPR Article 13.

The National Data Opt-Out (which covers research and planning) does **not**
apply to legal-requirements flows; this is documented at:
<https://digital.nhs.uk/services/national-data-opt-out>
