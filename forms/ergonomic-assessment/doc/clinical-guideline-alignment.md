# Clinical Guideline Alignment — Ergonomic Assessment

## HSE — Health and Safety Executive (UK)

The UK HSE is the principal regulator for workplace ergonomics. Relevant
publications and tools:

- HSE Musculoskeletal disorders portal:
  https://www.hse.gov.uk/msd/
- HSE *Manual Handling: Manual Handling Operations Regulations 1992 (as
  amended)* (L23, 4th ed., 2016):
  https://www.hse.gov.uk/pubns/books/l23.htm
- HSE *Work with display screen equipment* (L26, 2003):
  https://www.hse.gov.uk/pubns/books/l26.htm
- HSE Management Standards for work-related stress:
  https://www.hse.gov.uk/stress/standards/
- HSE *Risk assessment: a brief guide to controlling risks in the
  workplace* (INDG163):
  https://www.hse.gov.uk/pubns/indg163.htm

## Regulations

- *Manual Handling Operations Regulations 1992* (MHOR):

## NICE guidance

- NICE NG13 *Workplace health: management practices*.
  https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/ng13
- NICE NG175 not applicable.

## Chartered Institute of Ergonomics & Human Factors (CIEHF)

- https://www.ergonomics.org.uk/

CIEHF publishes practitioner guidance on REBA, RULA, NIOSH lifting
equation, and human factors in healthcare.

## NIOSH

- *NIOSH Lifting Equation*: Waters TR, Putz-Anderson V, Garg A, Fine LJ.
  *Revised NIOSH equation for the design and evaluation of manual lifting
  tasks.* Ergonomics. 1993;36(7):749-776.
  DOI: 10.1080/00140139308967940. PMID: 8339717.
- *Applications Manual for the Revised NIOSH Lifting Equation* (1994):
  https://www.cdc.gov/niosh/docs/94-110/

## ISO ergonomics standards

- ISO 11228 series — Ergonomics: Manual handling:
  - Part 1: Lifting and carrying. https://www.iso.org/standard/76820.html
  - Part 2: Pushing and pulling. https://www.iso.org/standard/76882.html
  - Part 3: Handling of low loads at high frequency.
    https://www.iso.org/standard/76834.html
- ISO 11226 — Ergonomics: Evaluation of static working postures.
  https://www.iso.org/standard/25573.html
- ISO 9241 series — Ergonomics of human-system interaction (display
  screen and workstation design).
  https://www.iso.org/standard/63500.html
