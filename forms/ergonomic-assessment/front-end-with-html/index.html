REBA (Rapid Entire Body Assessment) is a validated postural risk assessment tool for whole-body musculoskeletal exposure (Hignett & McAtamney, 2000). Scores 1-15: 1 negligible risk; 2-3 low; 4-7 medium; 8-10 high; 11-15 very high. This screening tool is for information only and is not a diagnosis; please discuss your results with your clinician.
