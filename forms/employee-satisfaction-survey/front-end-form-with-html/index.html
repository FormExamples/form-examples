Employee Satisfaction Survey

Anonymous employee experience survey across ten organisational domains.

This survey measures your overall experience as an employee across ten short topics: workload, management, growth, compensation, culture, environment, recognition and overall retention intent. Most items are 1-5 Likert statements (Strongly disagree through Strongly agree); the closing step also asks an eNPS-style "would you recommend us as an employer" 0-10 question.

Your responses are anonymous. Please do not enter your name, your manager's name, or any details that could identify you or a colleague. Demographic questions in step 1 are deliberately broad (department, tenure band, hours band) so no individual can be singled out from the aggregate dashboard.

Answers are saved automatically in your browser as you go. Nothing leaves your device until you submit.

Submit the form to see the report.

This survey is a screening and engagement tool. The composite score and eNPS are designed to inform retention, engagement and culture programmes — they are not a clinical assessment. Aggregate results should be discussed with your HR partner. If you are personally in distress, please speak to your GP or a confidential support line such as Samaritans on 116 123 (UK).
