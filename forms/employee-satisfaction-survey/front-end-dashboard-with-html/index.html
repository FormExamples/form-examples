Likert-scale Employee Satisfaction Survey covering eight domains (Workload & Work-Life Balance, Management & Leadership, Growth & Development, Compensation & Benefits, Culture & Inclusion, Environment & Resources, Recognition & Engagement, Overall Experience). Item responses are aggregated into a normalised 0-100 composite and banded Excellent (85-100), Good (70-84), Satisfactory (50-69), Poor (25-49), Very Poor (0-24). Employee Net Promoter Score (eNPS) is calculated from the retention-intent and recommend-as-employer items, ranging from -100 to +100. This dashboard supports HR action planning; it is not a clinical instrument.
