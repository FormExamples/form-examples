# Clinical Guideline Alignment

The integumentary assessment combines a head-to-toe skin / hair / nail
inspection with the Braden Scale and TIME wound assessment. The
following authoritative sources informed which structured fields are
collected and which rules are applied.

## NICE guidance

| Code | Title | URL |
| --- | --- | --- |
| CG179 | Pressure ulcers: prevention and management | https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/cg179 |
| QS89 | Pressure ulcers quality standard | https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/qs89 |
| NG12 | Suspected cancer: recognition and referral (skin cancer section) | https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/ng12 |
| NG142 | End of life care for adults: service delivery (skin care in EoL) | https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/ng142 |
| NG10 | Atopic eczema in under 12s | https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/ng10 |

CG179 establishes the requirement for a validated pressure-ulcer
risk-assessment scale on every adult admission; this assessment uses the
Braden Scale to meet that requirement.

## EPUAP / NPIAP / PPPIA international guideline

- *Prevention and Treatment of Pressure Ulcers/Injuries: Clinical
  Practice Guideline.* 3rd edition, 2019. Published jointly by NPIAP,
  EPUAP and PPPIA. https://npiap.com and https://www.epuap.org

This is the canonical international source for pressure-injury staging
and is the basis of the staging fields on Step 6.

## NHS / Royal College sources

- NHS England *Pressure ulcers: revised definition and measurement*
  framework (2018). https://www.england.nhs.uk/publication/pressure-ulcers-revised-definition-and-measurement-framework/
- Royal College of Nursing *Pressure ulcer prevention*. https://www.rcn.org.uk
- Tissue Viability Society (UK). https://tvs.org.uk

## British Association of Dermatologists (BAD)

BAD clinical guidelines index for dermatological conditions:
https://www.bad.org.uk/healthcare-professionals/clinical-standards/clinical-guidelines

These are referenced for the hair, scalp, and nail examination
sub-sections (alopecia, onychomycosis, psoriatic nail changes).

## What this engine does NOT score

- We do not compute the Waterlow score or the Norton scale.
- We do not compute the PUSH (Pressure Ulcer Scale for Healing) score.
- We do not produce a wound prognosis.
- We do not recommend a specific dressing.

## Carer / patient self-assessment caveats

The Braden Scale was developed for clinician administration. If any
sub-scale is completed from patient self-report (e.g. nutrition,
moisture), the report should explicitly note the data source and the
clinician should re-score before clinical action.
