Integumentary Assessment

Skin, hair, and nail review with Braden Scale pressure-ulcer risk scoring.

This integumentary assessment is normally completed by a clinician with the patient. It records a head-to-toe skin inspection together with hair, scalp, and nail findings; characterises any wound using the TIME framework; and grades pressure-ulcer risk using the validated Braden Scale (range 6-23, lower = higher risk). Answers are saved automatically in your browser as you go.

If the patient has a wound that is bleeding heavily, has signs of sepsis (spreading erythema, fever, hypotension), or rapidly progressing necrosis, escalate to urgent clinical care immediately.

The Braden Scale for Predicting Pressure Sore Risk (Bergstrom, Braden, Laguzza & Holman, 1987) is a validated 6-subscale instrument scored 6-23. This screening tool supports clinical decision making but is not a substitute for individualised clinical judgement.
