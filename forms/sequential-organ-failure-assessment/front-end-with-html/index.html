Record the objective physiological and laboratory findings for each of the six organ systems below for a critically ill adult (16 years or over). Each system scores 0 to 4; the six sub-scores are summed to a total of 0 to 24. A live sub-score is shown as you enter each system, and the running total, mortality band, and delta-SOFA appear on the final step. Answers are saved automatically in your browser as you go.

SOFA describes and quantifies organ dysfunction; it is not by itself a diagnosis. Under Sepsis-3, an acute rise of 2 or more points in a patient with suspected infection identifies sepsis — escalate and treat in line with local policy and clinical judgement.

The SOFA (Sequential Organ Failure Assessment) score was developed by the Working Group on Sepsis-Related Problems of the ESICM (Vincent et al., Intensive Care Medicine 1996) and is used for Sepsis-3 screening (Singer et al., JAMA 2016). This tool is for information only and is not a diagnosis; escalate and treat in line with local policy and clinical judgement.
