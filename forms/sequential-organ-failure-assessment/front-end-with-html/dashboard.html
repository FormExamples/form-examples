The SOFA score is a validated organ-dysfunction instrument used for Sepsis-3 screening. This dashboard is a clinical-review tool and is not a diagnosis. A rise of 2 or more points in a patient with suspected infection meets the Sepsis-3 criterion and prompts escalation.
