Complete the eight CHA2DS2-VASc criteria below for an adult with non-valvular atrial fibrillation or flutter. Each present criterion adds its weighted points — congestive heart failure (1), hypertension (1), age ≥ 75 (2) or 65–74 (1), diabetes (1), prior stroke/TIA/thromboembolism (2), vascular disease (1), and female sex category (1) — for a total of 0 to 9. Answers are saved automatically in your browser as you go.

This tool informs, but does not replace, the anticoagulation decision. It is for non-valvular AF only (not for moderate-to-severe mitral stenosis or a mechanical valve, which are anticoagulated regardless of score), and it estimates stroke risk, not bleeding risk — pair it with HAS-BLED and clinical judgement.

The CHA2DS2-VASc score is drawn from Lip et al. (Chest 2010) and is recommended by ESC, NICE (NG196), and AHA/ACC/HRS guidelines. This tool is for information only and is not a diagnosis; the decision to anticoagulate weighs stroke risk against bleeding risk (HAS-BLED) and rests on clinical judgement and shared decision-making.
