The CHA2DS2-VASc score is a validated stroke-risk instrument for non-valvular atrial fibrillation (Lip et al., Chest 2010). This dashboard is a clinical-review tool and is not a diagnosis. A high risk band prompts consideration of oral anticoagulation, weighed against bleeding risk (HAS-BLED).
