# Agile Principles Assessment — Implementation Plan

## Goal

Deliver a 14-step single-page wizard that scores adoption of the 12 Agile
Manifesto principles, computes a composite maturity level, and generates a
report with a coaching action plan.

## Engine design

- One `PrincipleResponse` row per principle (12 total) with a 1–5 Likert
  score and optional comment.
- `calculateMaturity` computes mean over answered principles; thresholds
  defined in `index.md`.
- Each principle below 3 fires a coaching rule; any score of 1 raises a
  critical-gap flag.
- Operational flags (burnout, technical-debt, etc.) are derived
  per-principle and surfaced independently of the composite maturity level.

## Open questions

- Whether to support **multi-respondent aggregation** (mean of means) in
  the dashboard. Out of scope for v1; the dashboard lists individual
  submissions but aggregation can be added later.
- Whether weights per principle should be configurable. Out of scope for
  v1; unweighted mean keeps the scoring transparent.
