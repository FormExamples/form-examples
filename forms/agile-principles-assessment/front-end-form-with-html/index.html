Agile Principles Assessment

12 Agile Manifesto principles — composite maturity level and coaching action plan.

0 of 12 principles scored (0%)

Score your team or organisation on each of the 12 principles of the Agile Manifesto. Use the 1–5 Likert scale (1 = strongly disagree, 5 = strongly agree). Answer at least six principles for a composite-maturity result.

The tool computes a maturity level (Optimising, Mature, Developing, Initial, or Ad-hoc), surfaces operational flags (burnout risk, technical-debt risk, command-and-control, etc.), and produces a coaching action plan. Results are entirely client-side — nothing is sent to a server from this static page.

Based on the Manifesto for Agile Software Development (Beck et al., 2001) and the 12 principles behind the Agile Manifesto. This self-report is intended to seed coaching conversations and retrospectives, not to deliver a final verdict.
