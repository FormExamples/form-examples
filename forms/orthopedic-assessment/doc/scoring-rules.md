# Scoring Rules — Orthopaedic Assessment (DASH)

## DASH — Disabilities of the Arm, Shoulder and Hand

The primary instrument is the 30-item DASH outcome measure, developed by
the Institute for Work & Health (Toronto) and the American Academy of
Orthopaedic Surgeons (AAOS).

- Canonical publication: Hudak PL, Amadio PC, Bombardier C, and the Upper
  Extremity Collaborative Group (UECG). *Development of an upper extremity
  outcome measure: the DASH (Disabilities of the Arm, Shoulder and Hand)*.
  Am J Ind Med. 1996;29(6):602-608.
  DOI: 10.1002/(SICI)1097-0274(199606)29:6<602::AID-AJIM4>3.0.CO;2-L.
  PMID: 8773720.
- Official instrument site: https://dash.iwh.on.ca/
- AAOS overview:
  https://www.aaos.org/quality/research-resources/patient-reported-outcome-measures/

### Scoring formula

The 30-item disability/symptom scale is scored as:

DASH score = ([sum of n responses] / n − 1) × 25

…where n is the number of completed responses. At least 27 of 30 items
must be completed for a valid score. Result ranges from 0 (no disability)
to 100 (most severe disability).

- Reference for scoring rule (DASH Manual, 3rd ed., 2002, IWH):
  https://dash.iwh.on.ca/sites/dash/files/downloads/dash_manual_2002_2.pdf

### Optional modules

The 30-item disability/symptom scale is sometimes paired with two optional
modules (each 4 items):

- Work module
- Sports / performing arts module

This form records the disability/symptom scale plus the two optional
modules where indicated.

### QuickDASH

QuickDASH is an 11-item short form, validated against the full DASH:

- Beaton DE, Wright JG, Katz JN, UECG. *Development of the QuickDASH:
  comparison of three item-reduction approaches.* J Bone Joint Surg Am.
  2005;87(5):1038-1046. DOI: 10.2106/JBJS.D.02060. PMID: 15866967.

This form supports DASH or QuickDASH; the active instrument is recorded
in the data export.

### Licensing

The DASH instrument is free for clinical use but the Institute for Work &
Health requires a one-time registration for commercial use. Reference:
https://dash.iwh.on.ca/about-dash

## Range of motion (ROM)

Step 5 records goniometric ROM. Reference standards:

- American Medical Association *Guides to the Evaluation of Permanent
  Impairment* (6th ed.). https://www.ama-assn.org/practice-management/cpt/ama-guides
- AAOS *Joint Motion: Method of Measuring and Recording* (1965; reprinted).

## Strength testing (MRC scale)

Step 6 records manual muscle testing on the MRC 0–5 scale:

- Medical Research Council. *Aids to the examination of the peripheral
  nervous system* (Memorandum no. 45, 1976). Reissued 2010.
  https://mrc.ukri.org/

## Pain assessment

Step 3 uses the Numeric Rating Scale (0–10) and the McGill Pain
Questionnaire short-form (where used):

- Melzack R. *The short-form McGill Pain Questionnaire.* Pain.
  1987;30(2):191-197. DOI: 10.1016/0304-3959(87)91074-8.
  PMID: 3670870.

## Composite interpretation

Per the AAOS / IWH guidance:

| DASH range | Interpretation |
| --- | --- |
| 0–15 | Minimal disability |
| 16–40 | Moderate disability |
| 41–70 | Severe disability |
| 71–100 | Extreme disability |

The form's "category" emits these bands; the underlying DASH score is
always stored as the canonical numeric.
