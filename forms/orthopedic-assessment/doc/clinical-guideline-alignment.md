# Clinical Guideline Alignment — Orthopaedic Assessment

## British Orthopaedic Association (BOA) standards

- BOA Standards for Trauma and Orthopaedics (BOASTs) index:
  https://www.boa.ac.uk/standards-guidance/boasts.html

BOASTs cover specific upper-limb topics relevant to a DASH-driven
assessment, including:

- BOAST: *Open fractures*
- BOAST: *Peripheral nerve injury*
- BOAST: *Wrist fracture* (distal radial fractures)
- BOAST: *Diagnosis and management of distal biceps tendon rupture*

The form's step 8 (imaging history) and step 9 (current treatment) align
with BOAST documentation expectations.

## American Academy of Orthopaedic Surgeons (AAOS)

- AAOS Clinical Practice Guidelines index:
  https://www.aaos.org/quality/quality-programs/lower-extremity-programs/
- AAOS *Appropriate Use Criteria*:
  https://www.aaos.org/quality/quality-programs/auc-program/

AAOS publishes evidence-based guidance for shoulder, elbow, wrist and
hand conditions that contextualises DASH scores.

## British Society for Surgery of the Hand (BSSH)

- BSSH index: https://www.bssh.ac.uk/
- BSSH guidelines: https://www.bssh.ac.uk/professionals/bssh_guidelines.aspx

BSSH guidelines for specific conditions (Dupuytren's, carpal tunnel
syndrome, trigger digit) inform step 9 treatment options.

## NICE guidance

- NICE CG177 *Osteoarthritis: care and management* (now NG226).
  https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/ng226
- NICE NG100 *Rheumatoid arthritis in adults: management.*
  https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/ng100

DASH scores trajectory is part of the joint health assessment recommended
in these guidelines.

## Patient-reported outcomes (PROM) governance

- NHS England *PROMs programme* (orthopaedic surgery is a national PROMs
  domain, primarily hip/knee replacement; upper-limb PROMs use DASH /
  QuickDASH / Oxford shoulder score etc.).
  https://www.england.nhs.uk/statistics/statistical-work-areas/proms/

## Pain assessment

- British Pain Society resources:
  https://www.britishpainsociety.org/british-pain-society-publications/
- IASP pain definitions and outcome guidance:
  https://www.iasp-pain.org/
