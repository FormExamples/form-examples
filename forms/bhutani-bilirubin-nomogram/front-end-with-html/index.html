Enter a newborn infant's total serum bilirubin (TSB) and age in hours to place the point on the hour-specific Bhutani nomogram. The tool assigns a percentile risk zone — low, low-intermediate, high-intermediate, or high — that predicts the likelihood of subsequent significant hyperbilirubinaemia, and compares the same point with the age- and gestation-specific NICE phototherapy and exchange-transfusion thresholds. Answers are saved automatically in your browser as you go.

This is a classification and decision-support tool, not a diagnosis of the cause of jaundice. A low-risk zone does not remove the need for follow-up. Use clinical judgement alongside the local threshold charts, and treat according to the gestation-specific NICE graphs.

The Bhutani hour-specific bilirubin nomogram (Bhutani et al., Pediatrics 1999) predicts the risk of subsequent significant hyperbilirubinaemia. Treatment decisions follow the NICE CG98 gestation-specific phototherapy and exchange-transfusion threshold graphs. This tool is for information only and is not a diagnosis; interpret alongside clinical judgement and local policy.
