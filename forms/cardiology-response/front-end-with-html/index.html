Consult-reply wizard for a cardiology response. A cardiology clinician records the responding clinician and originating referral, the patient identification, the clinical assessment (clinical summary, examination, investigations), the structured cardiac findings (ischaemia / CAD, significant arrhythmia, reduced ejection fraction, significant valve disease, structural abnormality, uncontrolled hypertension, non-cardiac cause), the diagnosis and key LV ejection fraction, the management plan and recommended follow-up, and the critical-result communication and sign-off. The engine computes a four-axis interpretation grade (response classification, condition severity, response completeness, and follow-up urgency) plus a set of safety flags.

A critical result auto-escalates the follow-up urgency to a critical-alert and raises the critical-finding flag. This is clinical decision support; the final clinical judgement remains the responsibility of the responding cardiology clinician.

Submit the form to see the interpretation report.

Interpretation is aligned with NICE chronic heart failure (NG106), the 2021 ESC/EACTS valvular heart disease guidelines, and the NHS e-Referral advice-and-guidance / counter-referral model. This screening tool is for clinical decision support; the final clinical judgement remains the responsibility of the responding cardiology clinician.
