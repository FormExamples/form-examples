## Instrument licensing

| Instrument | Licence |
| --- | --- |
| MUST | Free for non-commercial use with attribution to BAPEN. Reproduce the tool unaltered and cite *The 'MUST' Explanatory Booklet*. |
| GLIM | Published consensus criteria, freely citable (Clinical Nutrition 2019;38(1):1–9). |
| NRS-2002 | Published in Clinical Nutrition 2003;22(3):321–36; freely usable with citation. |
| SARC-F | Published in JAMDA 2013;14(8):531–2; freely usable with citation. |
| Bristol Stool Form Scale | Published 1997; widely reproduced in clinical practice with citation. |
| IDDSI framework | Free for clinical, educational, and research use under the IDDSI licence, with attribution and no modification. |
| SCOFF | Published in BMJ 1999;319:1467–8; freely usable with citation. |

Confirm the licence position with the instrument owner before any commercial
distribution of a derived product.
