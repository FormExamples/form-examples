Clinician referral wizard for a blood cross-match / transfusion compatibility request. Record the requesting clinician, patient identification, the requested test and component, the clinical indication, the patient's blood group and antibody / transfusion history, pre-transfusion sample collection and two-sample (group-check) status, and the requested urgency. The engine computes a four-axis grade (appropriateness, identity / sample safety, request completeness, and triage priority) plus a set of safety flags.

This is clinical decision support for the transfusion-laboratory vetting desk. The final acceptance, query, and prioritisation decision remains the responsibility of the vetting biomedical scientist or haematologist.

Submit the form to see the vetting report.

Grading is aligned with NICE NG24 Blood transfusion, British Society for Haematology (BSH) pre-transfusion compatibility guidance, and the Serious Hazards of Transfusion (SHOT) recommendations on positive patient identification and the two-sample (group-check) rule. The 1–9 appropriateness axis has no single published transfusion-ordering score; it is anchored on NICE NG24 restrictive thresholds and indication appropriateness. This screening tool is for clinical decision support; the final acceptance and prioritisation decision remains the responsibility of the vetting clinician.
