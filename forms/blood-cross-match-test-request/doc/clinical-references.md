# Clinical references

Authoritative guidance underpinning the four-axis grading engine.

## Appropriateness — NICE NG24 restrictive thresholds

NICE NG24 *Blood transfusion* (2015, surveilled) recommends a **restrictive**
red-cell transfusion strategy. There is **no single published
transfusion-ordering score**, so the 1–9 appropriateness axis is anchored on
these thresholds and on the clinical indication.

| Context | Threshold | Post-transfusion target |
| --- | --- | --- |
| General (no acute coronary syndrome) | 70 g/L | 70–90 g/L |
| Acute coronary syndrome | 80 g/L | 80–100 g/L |
| Chronic / transfusion-dependent anaemia | individualised | individualised |

Bands: usually-appropriate (7–9), may-be-appropriate (4–6),
usually-not-appropriate (1–3). A request well above the restrictive threshold
without a strong indication scores low; a request meeting threshold for a clear
indication scores high.

## Identity / sample safety — BSH / SHOT two-sample rule

BSH pre-transfusion compatibility guidance and the *Administration of blood
components* guideline require **positive patient identification** and a
**two-sample (group-check) rule**: a patient's ABO/Rh group must be confirmed on
**two samples from two separate venepuncture events**, ideally taken by
different staff, before group-specific (non-O) red cells are issued. This guards
against **Wrong Blood in Tube (WBIT)** — a never event identified across SHOT
annual reports.

| Band | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| ok | positive patient ID confirmed; two-sample rule met or historical group on record |
| caution | single sample only / awaiting second group-check sample |
| reject-risk | identification or labelling discrepancy; mislabel risk |

## Triage tiers

| Tier | Target | Triggers |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Stat | minutes | declared major / massive haemorrhage; emergency O-negative |
| Emergency | same hour | haemodynamic instability, active uncontrolled bleeding |
| Urgent | same day / 24–72 h | symptomatic anaemia, known antibodies needing extra time |
| Routine | standard booking | group-and-save, elective pre-operative crossmatch |

Known red-cell alloantibodies extend crossmatch turnaround (antigen-negative
units must be sourced), so they raise the triage tier even when the request is
otherwise routine.
