# References

## Record standards

- Academy of Medical Royal Colleges and Health and Social Care Information
  Centre. *Standards for the Clinical Structure and Content of Patient Records.*
  July 2013.
- General Medical Council. *Good Medical Practice.* Effective 30 January 2024.
  Record-keeping duties at paragraph 20.
- NHS England. *Clinical record keeping standards.* Professional Record
  Standards Body (PRSB) core information standard.

## Early warning and deterioration

- Royal College of Physicians. *National Early Warning Score (NEWS) 2:
  Standardising the assessment of acute-illness severity in the NHS.* Updated
  report of a working party. London: RCP, December 2017.
- National Institute for Health and Care Excellence. NG51. *Sepsis: recognition,
  diagnosis and early management.* 2016, updated 2024.
- National Institute for Health and Care Excellence. NG191. *Acutely ill adults
  in hospital: recognizing and responding to deterioration.* 2007, updated 2025.
