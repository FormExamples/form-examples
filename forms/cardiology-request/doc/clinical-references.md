# Cardiology Request — clinical references

Reference notes grounding the four-axis vetting engine. These inform the
appropriateness band, the safety / red-flag screen, and the triage rules; they
are not patient-facing.

## Appropriateness (Axis A)

- **NICE CG95 — Chest pain of recent onset** — structures assessment of chest
  pain by anginal typicality (typical / atypical / non-anginal); rapid-access
  chest-pain clinic referral is appropriate for suspected stable angina.
  <https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/cg95>
- **NICE NG106 — Chronic heart failure** — suspected heart failure with raised
  NT-proBNP warrants specialist assessment and echocardiography; referral to a
  heart-failure service is appropriate.
  <https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/ng106>
- **NHS e-Referral advice and guidance** — some questions are better answered by
  advice and guidance than a routine clinic appointment; "usually-not-appropriate"
  covers referrals manageable in primary care.
  <https://digital.nhs.uk/services/e-referral-service>

## Safety / red-flag (Axis B)

- **Suspected acute coronary syndrome** — ongoing or crescendo cardiac-sounding
  chest pain, or an elevated troponin, indicates a possible ACS that belongs on
  the emergency pathway, not a routine cardiology referral.
- **Exertional syncope** — syncope on exertion raises concern for structural
  (e.g. severe aortic stenosis, hypertrophic cardiomyopathy) or arrhythmic
  causes and warrants urgent assessment (NICE CG109).
  <https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/cg109>
- **New-onset heart failure** — new clinical heart failure, especially with a
  raised BNP / NT-proBNP, warrants urgent assessment per NICE NG106.

## Completeness (Axis C)

- Mandatory fields: referral reason, specific clinical question, requested
  service, and triage urgency. The reason and clinical question are weighted
  highest because they are the most commonly omitted, highest-value fields.

## Triage (Axis D)

- Red flags (suspected ACS, exertional syncope, new-onset heart failure) and an
  emergency setting escalate the triage tier (routine → urgent → emergency) with
  an associated target timeframe.
