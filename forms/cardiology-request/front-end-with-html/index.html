Clinician referral wizard for a cardiology consult. Record the referring clinician, patient identification, the requested cardiology service and primary reason for referral, the specific clinical question, presenting symptoms and functional class, acute red flags (suspected acute coronary syndrome, exertional syncope, new-onset heart failure) and investigations already performed (resting ECG, troponin, BNP), the patient's cardiac history and risk factors, and the requested urgency. The engine computes a four-axis vetting grade (referral appropriateness, safety / red-flag, request completeness, and triage priority) plus a set of safety flags.

This is clinical decision support for the cardiology referral-vetting desk. The final triage and booking decision remains the responsibility of the vetting cardiologist or specialist nurse.

Vetting is aligned with NICE chest pain (CG95), chronic heart failure (NG106), transient loss of consciousness (CG109), and the NHS e-Referral advice-and-guidance / referral-vetting model.
