# Clinical guideline alignment

## NICE

- NICE NG202 *Obstructive sleep apnoea/hypopnoea syndrome and obesity
  hypoventilation syndrome in over 16s*. Published August 2021.
  <https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/ng202>
- NICE Clinical Knowledge Summary (CKS) *Insomnia*.
  <https://cks.nice.org.uk/topics/insomnia/>
- NICE TA77 *Guidance on the use of zaleplon, zolpidem and zopiclone for the
  short-term management of insomnia*. Withdrawn but historically referenced.
  <https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/ta77>

## AASM

- American Academy of Sleep Medicine. *International Classification of Sleep
  Disorders, 3rd ed., text revision (ICSD-3-TR)*. Darien, IL: AASM, 2023.
  <https://aasm.org/clinical-resources/international-classification-sleep-disorders/>
- Edinger JD, Arnedt JT, Bertisch SM, et al. *Behavioral and psychological
  treatments for chronic insomnia disorder in adults: an American Academy of
  Sleep Medicine clinical practice guideline*. J Clin Sleep Med 2021;17:255-262.
  PMID: 33164742
- Kapur VK, Auckley DH, Chowdhuri S, et al. *Clinical practice guideline for
  diagnostic testing for adult obstructive sleep apnea: an AASM clinical
  practice guideline*. J Clin Sleep Med 2017;13:479-504. PMID: 28162150

## ESRS / ERS

- Fietze I, Laharnar N, Bargiotas P, et al. *Management of obstructive sleep
  apnea in Europe — a 10-year follow-up*. Sleep Med 2022;97:64-72.
  PMID: 35752119
- European Respiratory Society guidelines on long-term management of OSA:
  Randerath WJ et al. *Eur Respir J* 2017;50:1700961. PMID: 28798080

## STOP-BANG

- Chung F, Yegneswaran B, Liao P, et al. *STOP questionnaire: a tool to
  screen patients for obstructive sleep apnea*. Anesthesiology 2008;108:812-21.
  PMID: 18431116
- STOP-BANG validated cut-points: ≥3 = intermediate risk, ≥5 = high risk OSA.

## Resuscitation Council UK / DVLA cross-references

- UK DVLA *Assessing fitness to drive: a guide for medical professionals*.
  Excessive sleepiness and OSA disclosure requirements.
  <https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/assessing-fitness-to-drive-a-guide-for-medical-professionals>

## Cognitive behavioural therapy for insomnia (CBT-I)

- Trauer JM, Qian MY, Doyle JS, Rajaratnam SMW, Cunnington D. *Cognitive
  Behavioral Therapy for Chronic Insomnia: A Systematic Review and
  Meta-analysis*. Ann Intern Med 2015;163:191-204. PMID: 26054060
- NHS-recommended digital CBT-I platforms (Sleepio is recommended by NICE in
  MTG70). NICE Medical technologies guidance MTG70.
  <https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/mtg70>
