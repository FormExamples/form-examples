Clinician referral wizard for a breast mammography examination. Record the requesting clinician, patient identification, the requested examination, the clinical indication and specific question, breast symptoms, breast history and risk factors, and the requested urgency. The engine computes a four-axis grade (appropriateness, cancer-pathway urgency, request completeness, and clinical priority) plus a set of safety flags.

This is clinical decision support for the imaging vetting desk. The final triage and booking decision remains the responsibility of the vetting radiologist or radiographer.
