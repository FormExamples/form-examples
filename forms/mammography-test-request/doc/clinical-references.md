# Clinical references

Authoritative guidance underpinning the four-axis grading engine.

## Appropriateness (ACR Appropriateness Criteria — breast)

Ordinal 1–9 scale in three bands: usually-appropriate (7–9),
may-be-appropriate (4–6), usually-not-appropriate (1–3). The recommended
initial imaging depends on age and degree of suspicion:

| Patient age | Usually-appropriate initial imaging |
| --- | --- |
| Under 30 | Targeted breast ultrasound |
| 30–39 | Diagnostic mammography / tomosynthesis and / or ultrasound |
| 40 and over | Diagnostic mammography / tomosynthesis |

Mapped per (indication × exam type × age band).

## Cancer-pathway urgency (NICE NG12)

| Tier | Target | Triggers |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Emergency | same day | acute inflammatory presentation requiring same-day assessment |
| Two-week-wait | within 2 weeks | unexplained breast lump aged ≥ 30; unexplained axillary lump aged ≥ 30; nipple discharge / retraction / other concerning change in one nipple aged ≥ 50; skin changes suggestive of breast cancer |
| Urgent | within ~1 week | clinician-flagged urgent symptomatic work-up not meeting 2ww |
| Routine | standard booking | routine screening; asymptomatic surveillance; benign-pattern symptoms |

Non-urgent referral is appropriate for an unexplained breast lump aged under 30.

## Clinical priority

Symptom and risk escalation: a lump, bloody nipple discharge, or skin change
raises the priority band toward high; family history and abnormal previous
mammogram raise it toward moderate; routine asymptomatic screening stays low.
