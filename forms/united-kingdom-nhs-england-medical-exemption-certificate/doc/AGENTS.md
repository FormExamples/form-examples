## Submission rules

- Must be the **original paper FP92A** posted to:
  NHS Business Services Authority, Medical Exemption Certificates,
  Bridge House, 152 Pilgrim Street, Newcastle upon Tyne, NE1 6SN.
- NHSBSA **rejects** photocopies, email attachments, downloaded copies, and
  printed copies. This digital form is a practitioner-side staging tool only.

## Ordering blank FP92A forms

- **Integrated care boards (ICBs) and Sub-ICB Locations (SICBLs)** —

## The ten qualifying conditions

| # | Condition | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Permanent fistula | Caecostomy, colostomy, laryngostomy, ileostomy, etc., requiring continuous surgical dressing or appliance. |
| 2 | Hypoadrenalism (Addison's etc.) | Substitution therapy must be essential. |
| 3 | Diabetes insipidus / hypopituitarism | Includes other forms of hypopituitarism. |
| 4 | Diabetes mellitus | **Excludes** treatment by diet alone. |
| 5 | Hypoparathyroidism | |
| 6 | Myasthenia gravis | |
| 7 | Myxoedema | Hypothyroidism requiring thyroid hormone replacement. |
| 8 | Epilepsy | Requires continuous anticonvulsant therapy. |
| 9 | Continuing physical disability | Cannot go out without the help of another person. Excludes temporary disability (broken leg, etc.). |
| 10 | Cancer | Active treatment, effects of cancer, or effects of cancer treatment. |

## Validity

- Certificates are valid for **5 years** from the date of issue.
- A practitioner may apply for renewal as the expiry approaches.
- A separate **replacement** application is used if the certificate is lost.

## Related exemptions (out of scope for FP92A)

- **FW8** — Maternity exemption (pregnant, or within 12 months of giving
  birth).
- **Age** — Free prescriptions under 16, 16-18 in full-time education, and
  60+. No FP92A required.
- **HC1 / HC2 / HC3** — Low-income scheme.
