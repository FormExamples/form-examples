# Regulatory framework — NHS Medical Exemption Certificate (FP92A)

The Medical Exemption Certificate (MedEx, form **FP92A**) entitles
holders in England to free NHS prescriptions when their medical condition
falls within the statutory list of exempt conditions. The certificate is
issued by the **NHS Business Services Authority (NHSBSA)** following
application by a registered medical practitioner.

(Wales, Scotland, and Northern Ireland have universally free NHS
prescriptions and do not operate FP92A. The FP92A is England-only.)

## Primary statute

- **National Health Service Act 2006** (c. 41), section 172 and Schedule
  12 — charges and exemptions.
  <https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2006/41/contents>
- **NHS (Charges for Drugs and Appliances) Regulations 2015** (SI 2015/
  570), regulation 4 and Schedule 1 (exempt conditions list).
  <https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2015/570/contents>

## Statutory list of exempt medical conditions (SI 2015/570 Sch. 1)

A person holding a MedEx is exempt from NHS prescription charges if they
have any of the following conditions:

1. A permanent fistula (including caecostomy, colostomy, laryngostomy,
   or ileostomy) requiring continuous surgical dressing or an appliance.
2. A form of **hypoadrenalism** (for example Addison's disease) for
   which specific substitution therapy is essential.
3. **Diabetes insipidus** and other forms of hypopituitarism.
4. **Diabetes mellitus**, except where treatment is by diet alone.
5. **Hypoparathyroidism**.
6. **Myasthenia gravis**.
7. **Myxoedema** (i.e. hypothyroidism requiring thyroid hormone
   replacement).
8. **Epilepsy** requiring continuous anticonvulsive therapy.
9. A **continuing physical disability** which means the person cannot
   go out without the help of another person (this exemption does **not**
   include a temporary disability even if it is expected to last for
   some months).
10. **Cancer**: treatment for cancer, the effects of cancer, or the
    effects of current or previous cancer treatment.

The list is exhaustive — there are no "other" exemptions on medical
grounds under the regulations.

## Issuer

The FP92A is **applied for by the patient** but the medical condition
must be **certified by a registered medical practitioner** (GMC
register). The application is then sent to the NHSBSA, which issues the
exemption certificate. The certificate is valid for **five years** and
must be renewed.

## Penalty for misuse

Claiming an exemption to which one is not entitled is recoverable plus a
penalty charge of up to **£100** under the NHS (Penalty Charge)
Regulations 1999 (SI 1999/2794).
<https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1999/2794/contents>
