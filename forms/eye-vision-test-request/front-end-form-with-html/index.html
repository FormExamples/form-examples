Clinician referral wizard for an ophthalmic eye vision test. Record the requesting clinician, the patient, the requested examination and the clinical indication and specific question, relevant history, symptoms and red flags, risk factors, and the requested urgency. The engine computes a four-axis grade (appropriateness, urgency / triage, request completeness, and clinical priority) plus a set of safety flags.

This is clinical decision support for the eye-care vetting desk. The final triage and booking decision remains the responsibility of the vetting clinician.

Submit the form to see the vetting report.

Grading is aligned with Royal College of Ophthalmologists (RCOphth) referral and acute-eye guidance, NICE NG81 glaucoma, NICE QS180 serious eye disorders, and the NHS Diabetic Eye Screening Programme. Red flags (sudden visual loss, retinal-detachment symptoms, acute painful red eye, suspected giant cell arteritis) auto-escalate the triage tier to emergency. This screening tool is for clinical decision support; the final triage and booking decision remains the responsibility of the vetting clinician.
