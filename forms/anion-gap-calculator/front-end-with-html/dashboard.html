The anion gap is a decision-support prompt, not a diagnosis. Values at or above 20 mmol/L are very high and prompt urgent review; a raised gap should trigger a structured HAGMA differential (GOLDMARK / MUDPILES). Interpret in the clinical context and use local laboratory reference ranges where they differ.
