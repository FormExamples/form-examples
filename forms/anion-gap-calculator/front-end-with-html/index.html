Enter a routine serum electrolyte panel for an adult. Sodium, chloride, and bicarbonate are required; potassium is optional and selects the potassium-inclusive formula and reference range. The calculator computes the anion gap, an albumin-corrected gap when a serum albumin is entered, and classifies the result against the reference range. Answers are saved automatically in your browser as you go.

The anion gap is a decision-support prompt, not a diagnosis. A high gap is the laboratory signature of a high anion gap metabolic acidosis (HAGMA) and should trigger a structured search for the cause (GOLDMARK / MUDPILES). A normal gap does not exclude a normal-gap (hyperchloraemic) acidosis. Values at or above 20 mmol/L are flagged for urgent action.

The anion-gap formula and albumin correction are drawn from Kraut & Madias (CJASN 2007) and Figge et al. (Crit Care Med 1998). This calculator is an interpretation aid for information only and is not a diagnosis; interpret in the clinical context and use local laboratory reference ranges where they differ.
