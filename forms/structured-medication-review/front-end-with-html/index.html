The Structured Medication Review is an NHS England PCN service. Scoring draws on the STOPP/START v3 criteria (O'Mahony et al., 2023), the Anticholinergic Cognitive Burden (ACB) scale (Boustani et al.), NICE NG5 and NG197, and the PrescQIPP / NHS Polypharmacy guidance. This tool is for documentation and decision support only and is not a diagnosis; prescribing decisions rest with the clinician and the patient.
