Complete the seven TIMI criteria below for an adult with a working diagnosis of unstable angina (UA) or non-ST-elevation myocardial infarction (NSTEMI). Each criterion scores 1 point when present, for a total of 0 to 7. The total maps to a risk band (0-1 low, 2-4 intermediate, 5-7 high) and the 14-day risk of death, myocardial infarction, or urgent revascularisation. Answers are saved automatically in your browser as you go.

This is the UA/NSTEMI version of the TIMI score and must not be used to score a STEMI presentation (use the separate TIMI STEMI score). It is a prognostic and therapeutic-decision aid, not a diagnostic test: a low score does not exclude an acute coronary syndrome. Use clinical judgement alongside serial troponin testing and your local ACS pathway.

The TIMI risk score for UA/NSTEMI is derived from Antman et al. (JAMA 2000) from the TIMI 11B and ESSENCE cohorts, and is referenced in NICE NG185 and the 2020 ESC ACS guidelines. This tool is for information only and is not a diagnosis; treat and escalate in line with local ACS policy and clinical judgement.
