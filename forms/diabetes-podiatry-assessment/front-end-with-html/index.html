Complete the diabetes-podiatry-assessment record below. For each foot, record the sensory neuropathy status, pedal pulses, structural deformity, callus, skin breakdown, active ulceration (and its severity), ulceration and amputation history, and a suspected-Charcot marker, plus the patient-wide risk factors (renal replacement therapy, visual acuity impairment, self-care ability, footwear). The engine counts each foot's risk factors, applies the active-urgent and history overrides, takes the worse of the two feet, applies the patient-wide overrides, and classifies the overall risk category, review pathway, referral, and review interval — and raises safety flags. This is a risk-stratification record, not a wound-management or treatment tool. It records the findings an assessor has made on examination and applies the programme's deterministic risk-classification rules to produce the recommended review pathway; it does not diagnose, debride, dress, or otherwise manage a wound.

This record mirrors the NICE NG19 (Diabetic foot problems: prevention and management) risk-stratification categories (low, moderate, high, active/urgent) and the IWGDF guidelines and NHS England diabetic foot care pathway. It is for information only and is not a diagnosis; manage and refer in line with local policy and clinical judgement.
