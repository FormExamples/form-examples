This questionnaire records an acute stroke assessment based on the 15-item National Institutes of Health Stroke Scale (NIHSS), symptom onset timing, vascular risk factors, and current medications. Total NIHSS score range is 0-42. All answers are saved automatically in your browser as you go.

If a patient is having an acute stroke — sudden weakness or numbness of the face, arm, or leg (especially on one side), sudden confusion, trouble speaking or seeing, or sudden severe headache — call your local emergency number immediately. Time is brain.

The NIHSS is a validated clinical tool for measuring stroke severity. This screening form is for information only and is not a diagnosis; an acute neurological deficit requires immediate clinical evaluation and imaging. Patients within 4.5 hours of symptom onset may be candidates for thrombolysis — discuss with the stroke team urgently.
