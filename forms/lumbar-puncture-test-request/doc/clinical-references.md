# Clinical references

Authoritative guidance underpinning the four-axis grading engine.

## Appropriateness (indication match, 1–9 ordinal)

Ordinal 1–9 scale in three bands: usually-appropriate (7–9),
may-be-appropriate (4–6), usually-not-appropriate (1–3). Mapped per indication
and procedure intent (diagnostic vs therapeutic). Core diagnostic indications —
suspected meningitis, suspected subarachnoid haemorrhage (CT-negative),
suspected multiple sclerosis, suspected Guillain–Barré, idiopathic intracranial
hypertension (with manometry), suspected CNS malignancy, and CNS infection — are
usually-appropriate when the clinical question is well-formed.

## Safety / contraindication band

| Concern | Threshold / rule | Band impact |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Raised intracranial pressure | Suspected raised ICP, new focal neurological signs, or reduced consciousness (GCS ≤ 9) without prior CT head | caution → contraindicated until imaged and stabilized |
| INR | > 1.5 | caution / contraindicated |
| Platelet count | < 40–50 ×10⁹/L | caution / contraindicated |
| Anticoagulant therapy | active anticoagulation | caution; hold / reverse and discuss with haematology |
| Antiplatelet therapy | dual / potent agents | caution |
| Local skin / soft-tissue infection at site | present | contraindicated |

NICE NG240: do **not** routinely neuroimage before LP, but image (and treat /
stabilize) first when there are risk factors for an evolving space-occupying
lesion or signs of raised ICP — new focal neurological features, abnormal
pupillary reactions, or GCS ≤ 9. Antibiotics and blood cultures should not be
delayed for imaging in suspected bacterial meningitis.

## Triage tiers

| Tier | Target | Triggers |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Emergency | same hour / same day | suspected bacterial meningitis, suspected subarachnoid haemorrhage |
| Urgent | 24–72 h | suspected Guillain–Barré with progression, symptomatic IIH, suspected CNS infection |
| Routine | standard booking | diagnostic work-up of MS, stable IIH monitoring, elective CSF analysis |

For suspected subarachnoid haemorrhage with a negative CT, perform LP at ≥ 12 h
after headache onset to allow CSF xanthochromia (bilirubin) to develop for
spectrophotometry.
