Complete the seven sections below to document a will for England and Wales. The engine does not give legal advice and does not make the will for you. It checks that the record is complete (an executor, beneficiaries whose shares total 100%, a residuary clause, a trustee for any child beneficiary) and that the Wills Act 1837 formalities are planned or met (the testator is 18 or over, the will is in writing and signed in front of two independent adult witnesses present together, and no witness is a beneficiary). It classifies the will as valid (ready to execute), incomplete, or formality risk and raises flags. Answers are saved automatically in your browser as you go.

Disclaimer: this tool is documentation support, not legal advice. A classification of valid does not confirm the will is legally valid. Take advice from a solicitor or a regulated will writer if the will is not straightforward, for example a large estate, property abroad, a blended family, or doubt about capacity.
