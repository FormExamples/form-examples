# Endocrine axes

Reference for the six axes that the endocrinology assessment encodes.

## 1. Thyroid axis

Hypothalamic-pituitary-thyroid axis. Primary disorders are
hypothyroidism (auto-immune Hashimoto's most common) and hyperthyroidism
(Graves' disease, toxic multinodular goitre, toxic adenoma).

- **Tests**: TSH (primary screen), free T4, free T3, anti-TPO antibodies,
  TSH-receptor antibodies (TRAb) for Graves' disease.
- **Reference intervals (typical adult UK ranges)**: TSH 0.3–4.5 mIU/L,
  free T4 12–22 pmol/L, free T3 3.1–6.8 pmol/L. Pregnancy-specific
  intervals apply.
- **Guidance**: NICE NG145 *Thyroid disease: assessment and management.*
  <https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/ng145>

## 2. Adrenal axis

Hypothalamic-pituitary-adrenal axis. Primary disorders are Cushing's
syndrome (cortisol excess), Addison's disease (primary adrenal
insufficiency), Conn's syndrome (primary aldosteronism), and
phaeochromocytoma.

- **Tests**: 9 am cortisol; short Synacthen test; overnight 1 mg
  dexamethasone suppression test or 24-hour urinary free cortisol; renin /
  aldosterone ratio; plasma or 24-hour urinary metanephrines.
- **Guidance**: Endocrine Society Clinical Practice Guidelines.
  - Nieman LK et al. *Cushing's Syndrome.* J Clin Endocrinol Metab
    2008;93(5):1526-1540. DOI: 10.1210/jc.2008-0125.
  - Bornstein SR et al. *Diagnosis and Treatment of Primary Adrenal
    Insufficiency.* J Clin Endocrinol Metab 2016;101(2):364-389.
    DOI: 10.1210/jc.2015-1710.
  - Funder JW et al. *Primary Aldosteronism Endocrine Society Guideline.*
    J Clin Endocrinol Metab 2016;101(5):1889-1916.
    DOI: 10.1210/jc.2015-4061.

## 3. Glucose metabolism

Insulin / glucagon axis. Captured separately as the diabetes-assessment
form but included in the general endocrine review for completeness.

- **Tests**: HbA1c, fasting glucose, oral glucose tolerance test (OGTT),
  C-peptide for type-distinction.
- **Diagnostic thresholds (WHO/ADA)**: HbA1c ≥ 48 mmol/mol (6.5 %);
  fasting glucose ≥ 7.0 mmol/L; 2-hr OGTT ≥ 11.1 mmol/L.

## 4. Reproductive axis

Hypothalamic-pituitary-gonadal axis. Disorders include hypogonadism,
polycystic ovary syndrome (PCOS), hyperprolactinaemia, premature ovarian
insufficiency, and androgen-excess syndromes.

- **Tests**: FSH, LH, oestradiol or testosterone, SHBG, prolactin,
  17-hydroxyprogesterone (for CAH screening), DHEAS.
- **Guidance**:
  - Teede HJ et al. *International evidence-based guideline for the
    assessment and management of polycystic ovary syndrome 2023.* Fertil
    Steril 2023;120(4):767-793.
    DOI: 10.1016/j.fertnstert.2023.07.025.
  - Bhasin S et al. *Testosterone Therapy in Men With Hypogonadism: An
    Endocrine Society Clinical Practice Guideline.* J Clin Endocrinol
    Metab 2018;103(5):1715-1744. DOI: 10.1210/jc.2018-00229.

## 5. Pituitary function

Anterior pituitary disorders include acromegaly (GH excess), prolactinoma,
ACTH-secreting tumours (Cushing's disease), TSHomas, gonadotropinomas,
non-functioning adenomas, and hypopituitarism. Posterior pituitary
disorders include diabetes insipidus and SIADH.

- **Tests**: prolactin, IGF-1 (with GH suppression test for acromegaly),
  pituitary MRI, dynamic function tests.
- **Guidance**:
  - Melmed S et al. *Pituitary Adenoma Society Statement on Acromegaly
    Diagnosis and Management.* J Clin Endocrinol Metab 2018;103(11):
    3933-3951. DOI: 10.1210/jc.2018-00229.
  - Casanueva FF et al. *Pituitary Society International Consensus on
    Diagnosis and Management of Prolactinomas.* Pituitary
    2025;28(1):1-49. (Latest international consensus.)

## 6. Bone & calcium

Parathyroid, vitamin D, and calcium homeostasis. Disorders include primary
hyperparathyroidism, hypoparathyroidism, vitamin D deficiency, and
osteoporosis (NICE NG157 / FRAX).

- **Tests**: corrected calcium, phosphate, PTH, 25-OH vitamin D, ALP, eGFR.
- **Guidance**:
  - Bilezikian JP et al. *Evaluation and Management of Primary
    Hyperparathyroidism: Summary Statement and Guidelines from the Fifth
    International Workshop.* J Bone Miner Res 2022;37(11):2293-2314.
    DOI: 10.1002/jbmr.4677.
  - NICE NG157 *Osteoporosis: assessing the risk of fragility fracture.*
    <https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/cg146> (originally CG146; risk
    assessment integrated with NG157).
  - National Osteoporosis Guideline Group (NOGG) UK guideline 2022.
    <https://www.nogg.org.uk/>

## Cross-axis interactions

- Hypothyroidism elevates LDL-C and may cause secondary hyperprolactinaemia.
- Cushing's syndrome causes hypertension, glucose intolerance, and
  osteoporosis.
- Primary aldosteronism causes hypertension and hypokalaemia.
- Chronic kidney disease alters PTH, vitamin D, and bone metabolism.
