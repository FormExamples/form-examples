# WHO Emergency Unit Standardized Clinical Form: General

This form implements the WHO standard clinical-encounter form for
non-trauma adult and paediatric presentations to a hospital emergency
unit (EU). It is part of the WHO *Emergency Care Toolkit* family of
documentation tools developed by the WHO Department of Integrated Health
Services, Clinical Services and Systems unit.

## Position in the WHO Emergency Care Toolkit

The Emergency Care Toolkit is WHO's published bundle of tools for
strengthening emergency unit services in low- and middle-income
settings. It includes:

1. **WHO Emergency Unit Form: General** (this form).
2. **WHO Emergency Unit Form: Trauma** (paired trauma variant).
3. **WHO Prehospital Form** (prehospital encounter).
4. **WHO Emergency First Aid Form** (community first aid).
5. **WHO Acute Referral Form** and **Counter-Referral Form**.
6. **WHO Trauma Care Checklist** (Surgery & Critical Care).
7. **Hospital Emergency Response Checklist**.

All sit under the WHO Emergency, Trauma and Acute Care programme launched
by World Health Assembly Resolution **WHA60.22 (2007)** and refined by
**WHA72.16 (2019)** *Emergency care systems for universal health
coverage*. See:
<https://apps.who.int/gb/ebwha/pdf_files/WHA72/A72_R16-en.pdf>

## ABCD primary survey

The general form uses the ABCD survey (no F for FAST as in the trauma
form) but applies the same high-risk-signs panel before primary survey.

| Letter | Section in the form |
| --- | --- |
| A — Airway | Step 4 |
| B — Breathing | Step 5 |
| C — Circulation | Step 6 |
| D — Disability | Step 7 |

The CABCDE catastrophic-haemorrhage-first variant used in the WHO First
Aid Form is not needed here because the patient has already reached a
facility with surgical and blood-product capability.

## High-risk signs panel

Eight high-risk signs (Step 3) are drawn directly from the WHO BEC
*Approach* algorithm:

- Abnormal AVPU (V, P, or U)
- Heart rate <55 or >130 in an adult
- Stridor, voice change, or unable to swallow
- Poor perfusion, weak pulse, capillary refill >3 seconds
- Temperature >39 °C or <36 °C
- SpO2 <90% on room air
- Respiratory distress (grunting in child, retractions, cyanosis)
- "Vomits everything" or unable to drink / feed (paediatric red flag
  mirroring IMCI)

Reference: WHO. *Integrated Management of Childhood Illness chart
booklet*. Geneva: WHO; 2014.
<https://www.who.int/publications/i/item/9789241506823>

## Review of Systems — 14 systems

The form's 14 ROS rows match the WHO general-EU reference card and serve
as a structured prompt for the clinician to ask focused screening
questions in each system. The rows are aligned with the *Bates' Guide
to Physical Examination* general medical history:

- Bickley LS, Szilagyi PG, Hoffman RM, Soriano RP. *Bates' Guide to
  Physical Examination and History Taking*, 13th edition. Wolters Kluwer;
  2020. ISBN 978-1496398178.

## Diagnostics layout

The labs section follows the visual diagram of the printed WHO form to
reduce transcription errors at point of care:

- **CBC** — WBC, Hgb, Plt, Hct
- **Lytes / Cr / Glucose** — Na, Cl, BUN, K, HCO3, Cr, Glucose
- **UPT** — urine pregnancy test
- **Malaria** — rapid diagnostic test
- **HIV** — rapid test
- **Blood type**
- **Urine dip** — glucose, nitrites, ketones, leukocytes, blood, protein

This layout mirrors the WHO essential laboratory list for emergency
units. Reference: WHO. *Model List of Essential In Vitro Diagnostics
(EDL)*, 4th edition, 2023.
<https://www.who.int/publications/i/item/9789240081758>

## ECG interpretation

The ECG fields (rate, sinus rhythm, ischaemia, interpretation) reflect
WHO's expectation that any emergency unit with ECG capability records
both a numerical rate and a clinical interpretation. WHO BEC chapter 4
provides the underlying rhythm-strip teaching.

## Disposition codes

Six discrete disposition outcomes are captured:

- Admit to Ward
- Admit to ICU
- Admit to Operating Theatre (OT)
- Transfer to other facility (free text destination)
- Discharge (with plan discussed)
- Died (cause specified; cardiopulmonary arrest excluded as a cause —
  the underlying disease must be named)
- Left without being seen / before treatment complete

These map onto the WHO emergency-unit indicator framework for reporting
on case-mix and case-fatality.
