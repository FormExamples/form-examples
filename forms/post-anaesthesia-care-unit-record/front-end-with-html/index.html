Complete the recovery observations below for an adult emerging from anaesthesia or sedation. The five Modified Aldrete parameters each score 0, 1, or 2, for a total of 0 to 10. A total of 9 or more with the oxygen-saturation parameter scoring 2 is the conventional threshold for discharge from PACU. Answers are saved automatically in your browser as you go.

A passing score confirms that documented discharge criteria are met; it is not an instruction to discharge. The supervising anaesthetist retains responsibility for the discharge decision. For day-surgery cases, the optional PADSS assessment covers street fitness for discharge home.

The Modified Aldrete Score (Aldrete & Kroulik, Anesth Analg 1970; Aldrete, J Clin Anesth 1995) and the Post-Anaesthesia Discharge Scoring System (Chung, Can J Anaesth 1995) are validated recovery instruments. This tool documents that discharge criteria are met and is not a discharge order; discharge from PACU remains subject to the supervising anaesthetist's judgement and local policy.
