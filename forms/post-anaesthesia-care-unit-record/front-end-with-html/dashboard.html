The Modified Aldrete Score is a validated post-anaesthesia recovery instrument. This dashboard is a clinical-review tool and is not a discharge order. A total of 9 or more with the oxygen-saturation parameter satisfied is the conventional threshold for discharge from PACU, subject to the supervising anaesthetist's judgement.
