Clinician referral wizard for a lower-GI endoscopy examination. Record the requesting clinician and patient, the requested procedure, the clinical indication and specific question, lower-GI red flags and triage labs (FIT and haemoglobin), anticoagulant / antiplatelet medication, bowel-preparation fitness and renal function, ASA physical status, and the requested urgency. The engine computes a four-axis grade (appropriateness, cancer-pathway urgency, request completeness, and pre-procedure risk) plus a set of safety flags.

This is clinical decision support for the endoscopy vetting desk. The final triage and booking decision remains the responsibility of the vetting clinician.
