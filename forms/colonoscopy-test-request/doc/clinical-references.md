# Clinical references

Authoritative guidance underpinning the four-axis grading engine.

## Cancer-pathway urgency (NICE NG12 / DG56)

- **NICE DG56** — refer adults on the suspected colorectal cancer pathway if
  the FIT result is **≥10 µg Hb/g faeces**. FIT applies to abdominal mass,
  change in bowel habit, iron-deficiency anaemia, and age-stratified rectal
  bleeding / weight loss / abdominal pain combinations.
- **NICE NG12** — lower-GI two-week-wait referral for suspected colorectal
  cancer (abdominal / rectal mass, unexplained rectal bleeding, change in bowel
  habit, iron-deficiency anaemia by age threshold).

## Appropriateness (ASGE Appropriate Use Criteria / EPAGE / NICE)

Ordinal 1–9 scale in three bands: usually-appropriate (7–9),
may-be-appropriate (4–6), usually-not-appropriate (1–3). Mapped per
(indication × procedure).

## Triage tiers

| Tier | Target | Triggers |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Emergency | same day | acute lower-GI haemorrhage with instability (emergency setting) |
| Two-week-wait | ≤ 14 days | FIT ≥10 µg/g, NICE NG12 lower-GI red-flag combination |
| Urgent | ~2–4 weeks | symptomatic but below 2WW threshold, IBD flare assessment |
| Routine | standard booking | surveillance, screening, low-risk reassurance |

## Pre-procedure risk (BSG / ESGE)

- **Periprocedural anticoagulation** — colonoscopy with anticipated
  polypectomy is a high-bleeding-risk procedure. Continue low-dose aspirin;
  consider interrupting clopidogrel / DOAC / warfarin per BSG / ESGE, balancing
  thrombotic risk; bridge high-thrombotic-risk warfarin patients.
- **Bowel preparation** — split-dose regimens improve mucosal views. Caution
  with reduced eGFR / chronic kidney disease and electrolyte disturbance when
  selecting the prep agent. Confirm the patient is fit to take prep.
- **ASA grade** — ASA IV/V flags significant comorbidity affecting sedation and
  setting; may warrant anaesthetist-supported lists or CT colonography.
