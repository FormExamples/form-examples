Complete the seven report sections below after a cytology examination has been performed. Record the examined specimen type and its adequacy, the clinical history and any comparison with previous cytology or histology, the cytology result category and HPV status, the headline findings and microscopic description, the diagnosis and structured-reporting category, and the impression and recommended follow-up. Answers are saved automatically in your browser as you go.

The result category should use the recognised grading system for the specimen type (NHS cervical-screening dyskaryosis grades, RCPath Thy1–Thy5 for thyroid FNA, C1–C5 for breast FNA). Malignant cells or a high-grade category auto-escalate the follow-up urgency to a critical alert.

This structured cytology report is aligned with RCPath cytopathology reporting standards and NHS cervical-screening guidance, including the dyskaryosis grading bands, the RCPath Thy (thyroid FNA) and C (breast FNA) categories, and the requirement to communicate a critical result directly to the referrer. It is a reporting aid, not a diagnosis; interpret and act in line with local screening pathways and clinical judgement.
