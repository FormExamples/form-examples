Record the outcome of a home faecal immunochemical test (FIT) kit for an adult in the NHS Bowel Cancer Screening Programme. Enter the kit return and sample adequacy and, when a valid sample is available, the measured faecal haemoglobin concentration (µg Hb/g). The engine classifies the result against the programme threshold and reports the management action. Answers are saved automatically in your browser as you go.

This is a screening result-documentation and classification tool, not a diagnosis. A positive screen (faecal Hb at or above the threshold) prompts colonoscopy referral; a negative screen does not exclude cancer in a symptomatic person, who is routed to the urgent suspected-cancer pathway regardless of the FIT result.

Submit the form to see the report.

The programme (screening) threshold is ≥ 120 µg Hb/g; the symptomatic threshold under NICE DG56 is ≥ 10 µg Hb/g. This form documents and classifies a screening result and is an aid for information only; it is not a diagnosis. Interpret in the clinical context and follow local NHS Bowel Cancer Screening Programme operational standards.
