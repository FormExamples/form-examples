Record the report identification, the clinical history, the manometry opening pressure and macroscopic CSF appearance, the cell counts and biochemistry (protein, glucose, CSF:serum glucose ratio, lactate), the microbiology (Gram stain, culture, PCR) and specialist tests (oligoclonal bands, xanthochromia), the structured interpretive findings, and the impression. A critical CSF result — a bacterial meningitis pattern, a suggested subarachnoid haemorrhage, or a positive culture — auto-escalates the follow-up urgency to a critical alert. Interpret the CSF profile against standard reference patterns per NICE NG240 and the UK NEQAS guidelines, and do not delay antibiotics for investigations.

This structured CSF analysis report is aligned with NICE NG240 (bacterial meningitis / meningococcal disease), the UK NEQAS national guidelines for CSF bilirubin (xanthochromia) analysis in suspected subarachnoid haemorrhage, and standard CSF interpretation thresholds. It is a reporting aid, not a diagnosis; interpret and act in line with local neurology, microbiology, and acute-medicine pathways and clinical judgement.
