Enter the standardised serum creatinine (µmol/L), age, and sex for an adult. The calculator applies the CKD-EPI 2021 creatinine equation (race-free) and reports an eGFR in mL/min/1.73 m², banded into a KDIGO 2012 CKD G-stage (G1–G5). Answers are saved automatically in your browser as you go.

The eGFR is a staged estimate that supports monitoring, medication-dose review, and referral decisions; it assumes a steady-state creatinine and is not a diagnosis of chronic kidney disease, which also requires albuminuria and chronicity over ≥ 3 months. A non-steady-state creatinine (possible acute kidney injury) and stages G4/G5 are flagged for action.

Submit the form to see the report.

The CKD-EPI 2021 creatinine equation is drawn from Inker et al. (N Engl J Med 2021) and staged per the KDIGO 2012 CKD guideline and NICE NG203. This calculator is an interpretation aid for information only and is not a diagnosis; interpret in the clinical context, confirm chronicity, and use local laboratory reference ranges where they differ.
