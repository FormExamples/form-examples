  dementia and their carers*. NICE guideline NG97.
  https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/ng97

## Classification systems

- American Psychiatric Association (2022). *Diagnostic and Statistical
  Manual of Mental Disorders, Fifth Edition, Text Revision (DSM-5-TR)*.
  Codes 331.83 / G31.84 (mild NCD) and disorder-specific codes for
  major NCD.

- World Health Organization (2024). *ICD-11 for Mortality and Morbidity
  Statistics*. Codes 6D70 (mild NCD) and 6D80–6D8Z (dementia subtypes).
  https://icd.who.int/browse11/l-m/en
