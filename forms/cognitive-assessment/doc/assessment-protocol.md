# Cognitive assessment protocol

This document describes the administration procedure, environmental
controls, and corroborative-history sources required alongside the MMSE.

## Administration procedure

The MMSE must be administered by a trained clinician in a structured,
standardized way. Departures from the standard wording or order can
invalidate the score.

| Step                       | Standard procedure                                       |
| -------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------- |
| Orientation (Time)         | Ask year → season → month → day-of-week → date           |
| Orientation (Place)        | Ask country → county/state → city → building → floor     |
| Registration               | Read 3 unrelated words clearly, 1 sec apart, score first trial |
| Attention                  | Serial 7s from 100 (5 subtractions) OR spell WORLD backwards |
| Recall                     | Ask for the 3 words from Registration                    |
| Naming                     | Show wristwatch, show pencil                             |
| Repetition                 | "No ifs, ands, or buts"                                  |
| 3-stage command            | Verbal instruction; do not demonstrate                   |
| Read and obey              | Present "CLOSE YOUR EYES" written in block capitals      |
| Write a sentence           | Patient writes a spontaneous sentence                    |
| Visuospatial copy          | Patient copies intersecting pentagons                    |

## Environmental controls

- **Sensory aids**: ensure the patient is wearing their hearing aids and
  glasses if normally worn. Hearing or visual impairment without aids can
  invalidate scores.
- **Language**: administer in the patient's first language whenever
  possible. If interpreting is required, document the interpreter and
  note that some items (e.g. "No ifs, ands, or buts" repetition) do not
  translate cleanly.
- **Quiet room**: minimize external distractions for the attention and
  recall items.
- **Time of day**: cognition fluctuates in delirium and Lewy body
  dementia. Document the time of administration.

## Clinician behavioural observations

The clinician should record:

- **Level of consciousness** — alert / drowsy / fluctuating.
- **Speech and language** — fluency, naming difficulties, paraphasias.
- **Mood and affect** — depression can present as pseudo-dementia.
- **Motor signs** — tremor, bradykinesia, gait, frontal release signs.
- **Behaviour** — disinhibition, perseveration, confabulation.

## Corroborative history

NICE NG97 §1.2.2 requires collateral history from a person who knows the
patient well. The Functional History step captures:

- Activities of daily living (ADLs) — washing, dressing, eating,
  continence.
- Instrumental activities of daily living (IADLs) — shopping, cooking,
  finances, medication management, driving.
- Behavioural and psychological symptoms — apathy, agitation,
  hallucinations, delusions, sleep disturbance.
- Onset and trajectory — sudden vs gradual; stepwise vs progressive vs
  fluctuating.

## Differential diagnosis prompts

NICE NG97 §1.3 requires consideration of:

- **Delirium** (ICD-11 6D70) — acute, fluctuating; must be excluded
  before diagnosing dementia. The 4AT screen is recommended.
- **Depressive pseudodementia** — depression can produce cognitive
  symptoms; treat depression and re-assess.
- **Medication-induced cognitive impairment** — anticholinergics,
  benzodiazepines, opioids, antiepileptics, sedating antihistamines.
- **Reversible causes** — B12 / folate / TSH / electrolytes; structural
  brain lesions (CT/MRI); normal pressure hydrocephalus.
- **Specific dementia subtypes** — Alzheimer (ICD-11 6D80), vascular
  (6D81), Lewy body (6D82), frontotemporal (6D83), mixed (6D85).

## Outcome recording

At the end of the assessment, the clinician records:

- MMSE total (0–30) and per-domain sub-scores.
- Severity band: `normal` / `mild` / `severe`.
- Provisional ICD-11 / DSM-5-TR impression or "further investigation".
- Reversible-cause workup ordered (FBC, U&E, LFTs, TSH, B12, folate,
  calcium, glucose, ± CT/MRI).
- Onward referral (Memory Assessment Service / Old Age Psychiatry).
- Functional impact summary.
