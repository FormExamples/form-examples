# UK NHS England Medical Exemption Certificate 

entitles you to free UK NHS prescriptions.
 
Form Name: FP92A application form.

How to Get It: You must collect this form from your GP surgery or a healthcare professional. You cannot usually download a printable version yourself because a doctor must sign it to confirm your eligibility.Eligible Conditions: Includes cancer, diabetes, epilepsy, and permanent physical disabilities that prevent you from leaving home without help.
