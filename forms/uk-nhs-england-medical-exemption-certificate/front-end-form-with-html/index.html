FP92A — Medical Exemption Certificate

UK NHS Business Services Authority. Completed by a doctor or health professional with access to the patient's medical records.

Step 1 of 10

The FP92A entitles a patient to a five-year NHS Medical Exemption Certificate (MedEx) granting free NHS prescriptions. Only the closed list of qualifying medical conditions defined by the NHSBSA confers eligibility. NHSBSA only accepts the original paper FP92A posted to Bridge House — this form is a practitioner-side staging tool.

Step 4 of 10 — Age-based exclusion check

Patients aged under 16, 16-18 in full-time education, or 60+ are automatically entitled to free prescriptions on age grounds — no FP92A required.

Step 5 of 10 — Pregnancy / maternity check

Pregnant patients (or those within 12 months of giving birth) qualify for the FW8 maternity exemption instead of FP92A.
