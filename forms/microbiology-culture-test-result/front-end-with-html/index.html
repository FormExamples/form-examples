A critical organism or result — a positive blood culture, a CSF isolate, a carbapenemase-producing Enterobacterales (CPE), or any flagged critical organism — auto-escalates the follow-up urgency to a critical alert that must be telephoned to the requesting or infection team per RCPath critical-result communication standards.

This structured microbiology report is aligned with RCPath and UK Standards for Microbiology Investigations (SMI) reporting guidance, including the requirement for critical-result communication of alert organisms and the distinction between significant growth, contaminant / mixed growth, and no growth. It is a reporting aid, not a diagnosis; interpret and act in line with local infection-prevention-and-control pathways and clinical judgement.
