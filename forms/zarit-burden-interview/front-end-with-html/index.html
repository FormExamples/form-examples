Zarit Burden Interview (ZBI)

A caregiver self-report questionnaire measuring carer burden. The full form rates 22 items (0-4) for a total of 0-88; a 12-item short form (0-48) is also supported.

These questions ask how you feel when caring for your relative or friend. For each item, choose the answer that comes closest to how you feel: never, rarely, sometimes, quite frequently, or nearly always. There are no right or wrong answers. Each option shows the points it contributes so the scoring is fully transparent, and a live total and burden band update as you answer. Answers are saved automatically in your browser as you go.

The ZBI measures your own perceived burden as a carer. It is a screen, not a diagnosis, and it does not assess the condition of the person you care for. A higher total, and a high rating on the final global item, prompt carer support, respite, and screening for depression and anxiety. If you are struggling to cope, speak to your GP, social worker, or carer-support service.

The Zarit Burden Interview (Zarit, Reeves & Bach-Peterson, The Gerontologist 1980; short form Bédard et al. 2001) is a validated measure of caregiver burden. This tool is for information only and is not a diagnosis; assess, support, and refer in line with NICE NG97, local carer-support and safeguarding policy, and clinical judgement. A low score does not mean no support is needed.
