The NREMT Psychomotor Skills Examination is a competency assessment used by approved evaluators to verify scene size-up, primary survey, history taking, secondary assessment, and reassessment skills, with explicit critical-criteria overrides (PPE, scene safety, oxygen, airway/breathing/shock, transport urgency, dangerous intervention, spinal protection, 15-minute transport call). This dashboard is a training-administration tool and is not a clinical record.
