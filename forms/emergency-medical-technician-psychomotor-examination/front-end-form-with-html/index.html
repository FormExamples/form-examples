Use this checklist while observing a candidate perform the NREMT Psychomotor Skills Examination on a medical patient. For each scored item, mark whether the candidate performed the action correctly, did not perform it, or whether the item was not applicable to this scenario. Critical criteria are highlighted in red; any critical-criterion failure results in an automatic Fail, regardless of point total.

Pass criterion: at least 80% of available points and no critical-criterion failure. Answers are saved to your browser as you go, so a partial fill survives a page reload.

The NREMT Psychomotor Skills Examination is a competency-based instrument used to certify Emergency Medical Technicians on scene size-up, primary survey, history-taking, secondary assessment, and reassessment. This examiner tool records observed performance and applies NREMT pass/fail criteria; it is not a substitute for the lead evaluator's professional judgement.
