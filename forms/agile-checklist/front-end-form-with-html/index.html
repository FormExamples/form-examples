Audit your team or organisation against 57 concrete agile behaviours grouped in three sections — Teams, Stakeholders, and Practices. Answer each item Yes, No, or N/A. Answer at least 30 items for a composite-maturity result.

The tool computes a per-section percentage of "yes" answers (N/A items are excluded from the denominator), an overall maturity level (Optimising, Mature, Developing, Initial, or Ad-hoc), per-section coaching rules, and operational flags such as finished-work risk, experimentation-blocked, and psychological-safety risk. Everything runs client-side; nothing is sent to a server from this static page.

Companion form to the Agile Principles Assessment. This checklist measures observable behaviour; the principles assessment measures intent and belief. Treat both as inputs to coaching conversations rather than a final verdict.
