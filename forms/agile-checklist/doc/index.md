# Agile Checklist — Documentation

Background reference notes for the agile-checklist form.

The 57 items in `../seed.md` are organised in three sections covering
Teams (25 items), Stakeholders (14 items), and Practices (18 items).
Each item is a concrete observable behaviour rather than a Likert-scaled
belief.

## Contents

- [`scoring-algorithm.md`](scoring-algorithm.md) — per-section
  percentage, composite mean, maturity thresholds, worked example,
  determinism guarantees.
- [`coaching-rules.md`](coaching-rules.md) — the 12 fired-rule strings
  (3 sections × 4 bands) with stable rule IDs and coaching philosophy.
- [`flag-rules.md`](flag-rules.md) — the 9 operational flags with
  predicates, priorities, and the reasoning behind each threshold.
- [`sister-form-comparison.md`](sister-form-comparison.md) — how this
  form pairs with `agile-principles-assessment`: the four
  intent-vs-behaviour quadrants and a recommended cadence.

## Companion form

[`agile-principles-assessment`](../../agile-principles-assessment) covers the
twelve Agile Manifesto principles on a 1–5 Likert scale. The two forms
are designed to be complementary — see
[`sister-form-comparison.md`](sister-form-comparison.md) for the full
mapping.

- **Principles assessment** — measures intent and belief.
- **Behaviour checklist** — measures observable practice.

A team can score high on principles but low on behaviour (aspirational
gap), or high on behaviour but low on principles (cargo-cult gap).
