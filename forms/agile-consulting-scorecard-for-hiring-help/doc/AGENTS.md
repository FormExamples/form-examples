# Agile Consulting Scorecard for Hiring Help — Documentation

Reference materials for the agile-consulting scorecard. The primary
source is the form's own `seed.md`, plus the published authorities
behind each cluster of items:

- **Manifesto items 1–4** — Beck, K. et al. *Manifesto for Agile
  Software Development*, 2001. <https://agilemanifesto.org/>
- **Principles items 5–16** — *Principles behind the Agile Manifesto*.
  <https://agilemanifesto.org/principles.html>
- **NPS (principle 1)** — Reichheld, F. *The One Number You Need to
  Grow*, Harvard Business Review, 2003.
- **3-amigos MVP (principle 5)** — Dinwiddie, G. *The Three Amigos
  Strategy of Developing User Stories*, Solutions IQ, 2009.
- **Process-improvement skills (principle 10)** — Womack, J.P. & Jones,
  D.T. *Lean Thinking*, 1996; Ohno, T. *Toyota Production System*,
  1988; Goldratt, E.M. *Theory of Constraints*; Six Sigma DMAIC.
- **Self-organization (principle 11)** — Seddon, J. *The Vanguard
  Method*; Likert, R. *A Technique for the Measurement of Attitudes*,
  Archives of Psychology, 1932 (origin of the 5-point scale).
- **Senior-leader change reading (manifesto 4)** — Kotter, J.P.
  *Leading Change*, 1996, as a representative agile-change guide.
- **Reflection / retrospectives (principle 12)** — Derby, E. & Larsen,
  D. *Agile Retrospectives: Making Good Teams Great*, 2006.

Authoritative source PDFs live in `seeds/` (added when the form is
promoted from design-spec to fully implemented).
