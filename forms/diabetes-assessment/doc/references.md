# References

Annotated bibliography for the diabetes assessment form.

## Guidelines

- NICE NG28 *Type 2 diabetes in adults: management.* National Institute
  for Health and Care Excellence; 2015, last updated 2022.
  <https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/ng28>
- NICE NG17 *Type 1 diabetes in adults: diagnosis and management.* NICE;
  2015, updated 2022. <https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/ng17>
- NICE NG19 *Diabetic foot problems: prevention and management.* NICE;
  2015, updated 2019. <https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/ng19>
- NICE NG203 *Chronic kidney disease: assessment and management.* NICE;
  2021. <https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/ng203>
- NICE QS6 *Diabetes in adults quality standard.*
  <https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/qs6>
- NICE CG181 *Cardiovascular disease: risk assessment and reduction.*
  <https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/cg181>
- Marx N et al. *2023 ESC Guidelines for the management of cardiovascular
  disease in patients with diabetes.* Eur Heart J 2023;44(39):4043-4140.
  DOI: 10.1093/eurheartj/ehad192.
- American Diabetes Association. *Standards of Care in Diabetes — 2025.*
  Diabetes Care 2025;48(Suppl 1).
  <https://diabetesjournals.org/care/issue/48/Supplement_1>
