Ten-step wizard for the UK Statement of Fitness for Work (commonly called the fit note or Med 3). Complete each section, then click Submit and view report to render the fit note and the computed grade.

Per DWP policy 3.2 a fit note cannot certify someone as fit for work — only not fit for work or may be fit for work taking account of advice. The form enforces this.

Source: DWP Fit note: guidance for patients and employees (August 2023). This is a reference implementation and is not a production-issued fit note.
