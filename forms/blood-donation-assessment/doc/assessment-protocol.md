# Assessment protocol

## Intended use

A donor-screening questionnaire that captures health, medication, illness,
travel, lifestyle, and sexual-behaviour history (per the IRA), records
vital signs and Hb, and applies JPAC DSG rules to produce an eligibility
decision.

## Intended users

- NHSBT / SNBTS / Welsh Blood Service / NIBTS donor session staff
- Donor session nurses and doctors
- Donor carer / Donor Health Check personnel

## Setting

UK blood donation session (fixed centre or mobile session).

## Workflow

1. **Donor demographics** — name, DOB, donor number, weight, height,
   prior donation history.
2. **General health and wellbeing** — feeling well today, energy levels,
   sleep, recent symptoms.
3. **Medical history** — chronic conditions list aligned to DSG: cardiac,
   respiratory, neurological, autoimmune, malignancy, diabetes, mental
   health.
4. **Recent illness and infections** — fever, GI illness, respiratory
   illness, dental infection, urinary infection.
5. **Travel history** — countries visited within past 12 months;
   geographic-risk matrix for malaria, WNV, Chagas, vCJD, dengue, Zika,
   chikungunya.
6. **Lifestyle and risk behaviours** — IV drug use, body modification
   (tattoo, piercing, acupuncture), recent endoscopy or surgery, sexual
   behaviour (IRA question set).
7. **Pregnancy and transfusion history** — pregnancy status; lactation;
   past transfusion in UK or abroad.
8. **Vital signs** — Hb (HemoCue or equivalent), blood pressure, pulse,
   temperature.
9. **Informed consent** — donor declaration; data-protection statement;
   testing-consent statement.
10. **Donation plan** — component (whole blood, platelets, plasma);
    deferral note if applicable.

## Output

- Eligibility decision (Eligible / Temporary / Permanent)
- Reason codes (DSG section)
- Return date for temporary deferral
- Donor advice and signposting
- Structured PDF donor record

## Safety-net behaviour

- Hb below threshold → defer and provide dietary advice; signpost GP if
  persistent
- BP outside DSG range → defer and signpost GP
- Persistent symptoms during pre-donation review → defer and refer
- Vasovagal history or recent fainting → consult session clinician
- Pregnant or recently pregnant → defer per DSG postpartum schedule
- Active medication on a deferral list → consult session clinician

## Out of scope

- Apheresis component-specific risk assessment beyond DSG
- Donor-typing decisions
- Recipient compatibility testing
- Component manufacturing decisions
- Bone marrow / stem cell donation (see bone-marrow-donation-assessment)
- Tissue donation (cornea, heart valves) — separate selection guidance
