Complete the three AUDIT-C consumption questions below for an adult (16 years or over). Each question scores the chosen response 0 to 4, for a total of 0 to 12. A score of 5 or more is a positive screen and prompts the full 10-item AUDIT and a brief intervention. A unit is 8 g / 10 mL of pure alcohol (UK definition). Answers are saved automatically in your browser as you go.

AUDIT-C is the consumption subset (questions 1-3) of the full AUDIT. It is a screening prompt, not a diagnosis of an alcohol-use disorder. A raised score is a prompt to assess further and to offer advice or referral; a low score does not exclude an alcohol problem where other concerns exist.

AUDIT-C is the consumption subset of the AUDIT (Saunders et al., Addiction 1993), validated as a standalone screen by Bush et al. (Arch Intern Med 1998). This screening tool is for information only and is not a diagnosis; a positive screen (AUDIT-C >= 5) is a prompt for the full 10-item AUDIT, a brief intervention, and clinical judgement.
