# Regulatory Framework

The Screening Program Privacy Notice covers patient personal data used in
NHS screening programmes (cervical, breast, bowel, abdominal aortic
aneurysm, diabetic eye, antenatal and newborn, and the national clinical
audits that read from screening outcomes). The notice satisfies UK GDPR
Article 13 transparency and records the patient's National Data Opt-Out
preference where applicable.

## Primary instruments

- **UK GDPR** Articles 6, 9, 13, 21, 89 —
  <https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2016/679/contents>
- **Data Protection Act 2018** (c. 12) —
  <https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2018/12/contents>
- **NHS Act 2006** s.251 — statutory mechanism for setting aside the
  common-law duty of confidentiality for defined screening / public-health
  purposes —
  <https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2006/41/section/251>
- **Health and Social Care Act 2012** ss.254, 259 — Secretary of State
  Directions for data flows to NHS England —
  <https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2012/7/contents>

## Lawful basis

For NHS national screening programmes the lawful bases are:

- UK GDPR Article 6(1)(e) — task in the public interest.
- UK GDPR Article 9(2)(h) — provision of health care, including
  preventive medicine and assessment of working capacity.
- UK GDPR Article 9(2)(i) — public-interest reasons in the area of public
  health (used by UKHSA for screening surveillance).
- UK GDPR Article 9(2)(j) — scientific research (where the screening
  cohort feeds an approved research project).

Underlying DPA 2018 conditions: Schedule 1 Part 1 paragraphs 2, 3, and 4.

## UK National Screening Committee

The UK National Screening Committee (UK NSC) sets the criteria for which
screening programmes the NHS runs.

- UK NSC — overview and recommendations:
  <https://www.gov.uk/government/groups/uk-national-screening-committee-uk-nsc>
- NHS — Screening:
  <https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/nhs-screening/>

## Common-law duty and s.251

Screening flows are typically supported by an NHS Act 2006 s.251 approval
from the Confidentiality Advisory Group, which sets aside the common-law
duty of confidentiality for the defined purpose. The CAG approval
register is public:

<https://www.hra.nhs.uk/approvals-amendments/what-approvals-do-i-need/confidentiality-advisory-group/>

## National Data Opt-Out

The National Data Opt-Out applies to screening **planning and audit** flows
but **not** to the operational screening invitation, attendance, and
result-return flow itself (which is direct care).

- NHS England — National Data Opt-Out:
  <https://digital.nhs.uk/services/national-data-opt-out>

## Recipients

Typical recipients named in the notice include:

| Recipient | Statutory link |
| --- | --- |
| NHS England Screening Service | Health and Social Care Act 2012 s.254 / s.259 |
| UK Health Security Agency | Public Health (Control of Disease) Act 1984; Health Protection (Notification) Regulations 2010 |
| Public Health regional teams (ICBs) | NHS Act 2006 commissioning structure |
| Approved research consortia (e.g. NIHR) | HRA + CAG approval |
