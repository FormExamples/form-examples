# Clinical Guideline Alignment — Fall Risk Assessment

## NICE CG161 — Falls in older people: assessing risk and prevention

- https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/cg161

CG161 recommends:

- Older people in contact with healthcare professionals should be asked
  routinely whether they have fallen in the past year (step 2 fall history)
- Multifactorial falls risk assessment for those at risk (step 3 MFS + steps 4–9)
- Multifactorial intervention (step 10 prevention plan)

## NICE QS86 — Falls in older people quality standard

- https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/qs86

QS86 sets specific quality statements aligned to CG161.

## NICE NG161 — Suspected sepsis

Not directly applicable; included only to disambiguate from CG161. Step
indicators for sepsis are out of scope for this form.

## Royal College of Physicians — National Audit of Inpatient Falls (NAIF)

- https://www.rcp.ac.uk/improving-care/programmes/national-audit-inpatient-falls/

The NAIF dataset informs the inpatient mode of this form (step 7
environmental assessment, step 5 medication review).

## NICE NG124 — Mental capacity (relevant where cognitive impairment found)

- NICE NG108 *Decision-making and mental capacity*.
  https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/ng108

Step 8 cognitive assessment uses validated screens (AMT-4, 4AT, MMSE)
rather than embedded versions, due to licensing on some instruments.

## Clinical Frailty Scale (Rockwood)

Where the patient is older or has multiple comorbidities, the form
captures the Clinical Frailty Scale:

- Rockwood K, Song X, MacKnight C, et al. *A global clinical measure of
  fitness and frailty in elderly people.* CMAJ. 2005;173(5):489-495.
  DOI: 10.1503/cmaj.050051. PMID: 16129869.
- CFS official site (Dalhousie University):
  https://www.dal.ca/sites/gmr/our-tools/clinical-frailty-scale.html

## Medication review — STOPP/START criteria

For step 5 (medication review):

- O'Mahony D, O'Sullivan D, Byrne S, et al. *STOPP/START criteria for
  potentially inappropriate prescribing in older people: version 2.*
  Age Ageing. 2015;44(2):213-218. DOI: 10.1093/ageing/afu145.
- STOPPFall (most recent specific to fall risk):
  Seppala LJ, Petrovic M, Ryg J, et al. *STOPPFall (Screening Tool of
  Older Persons Prescriptions in older adults with high fall risk): a
  Delphi study by the EuGMS Task and Finish Group on Fall-Risk-Increasing
  Drugs.* Age Ageing. 2021;50(4):1189-1199. DOI: 10.1093/ageing/afaa249.
