# Scoring Rules — Fall Risk Assessment (Morse Fall Scale)

## Morse Fall Scale (MFS)

The primary instrument is the Morse Fall Scale, a six-item scale developed
by Janice Morse in 1989 to assess the likelihood of falls.

- Canonical reference: Morse JM, Morse RM, Tylko SJ. *Development of a
  scale to identify the fall-prone patient.* Can J Aging.
  1989;8(4):366-377. DOI: 10.1017/S0714980800008576.
- Follow-up validation: Morse JM. *Preventing Patient Falls* (2nd ed.,
  Springer, 2009). https://www.springerpub.com/preventing-patient-falls-9780826103895.html
- Public domain article describing the scale: Morse JM. *A prospective
  study to identify the fall-prone patient.* Soc Sci Med.
  1989;28(1):81-86. DOI: 10.1016/0277-9536(89)90309-2. PMID: 2916167.

### Six items and scoring

| Item | Options and points |
| --- | --- |
| History of falling | No = 0, Yes = 25 |
| Secondary diagnosis | No = 0, Yes = 15 |
| Ambulatory aid | None / bed rest / nurse assist = 0, crutches / cane / walker = 15, furniture = 30 |
| IV / heparin lock | No = 0, Yes = 20 |
| Gait / transferring | Normal / bed rest / immobile = 0, weak = 10, impaired = 20 |
| Mental status | Orientated to own ability = 0, forgets limitations = 15 |

Maximum total = 125.

### Risk bands

| Total | Risk band |
| --- | --- |
| 0–24 | Low risk |
| 25–44 | Moderate risk |
| ≥ 45 | High risk |

Cut-points used in the form match the bands published in Morse (1989) and
re-stated in Preventing Patient Falls (2009). Individual institutions
sometimes re-calibrate cut-points to local incidence; the form documents
the chosen cut-points in its export.

## NICE CG161 — Falls in older people

- NICE CG161 *Falls in older people: assessing risk and prevention.*
  https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/cg161
- NICE quality standard QS86 *Falls in older people.*
  https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/qs86

CG161 specifies multifactorial assessment when MFS is moderate or high.
Step 4 onwards captures the CG161 multifactorial domains:

- Falls history
- Gait, balance and mobility (Tinetti / TUG)
- Muscle weakness
- Osteoporosis risk
- Older person's perceived functional ability and fear relating to falling
- Visual impairment
- Cognitive impairment and neurological examination
- Urinary incontinence
- Home hazards
- Cardiovascular examination and medication review

## Composite mapping

The form's composite "severity" is the worst-band of:

- MFS total
- Critical modifiers (recurrent falls with injury, anticoagulation
  (NICE NG196 *atrial fibrillation*), MFS ≥ 75)
- CG161 multifactorial findings (≥ 3 positive multifactorial domains
  escalates one band)
