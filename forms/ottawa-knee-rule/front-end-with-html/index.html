Complete the five Ottawa Knee Rule criteria below for an adult with an acute knee injury. This is a decision rule, not a score: a knee X-ray is indicated when at least one criterion is present, and not indicated when all five are absent. The criteria are neither summed nor weighted — one positive criterion is sufficient. Answers are saved automatically in your browser as you go.

The rule directs whether to image; it does not diagnose or exclude a fracture on its own. It is validated in adults with acute injury and is not for children, isolated superficial skin injuries, reassessment of a known fracture, or patients in whom examination is unreliable — use the appropriate pathway and clinical judgement.

The Ottawa Knee Rule was derived and validated by Stiell et al. (Ann Emerg Med 1995; JAMA 1996; JAMA 1997) and is highly sensitive for clinically significant knee fractures. This tool is for information only and is not a diagnosis; image, treat, and escalate in line with local policy and clinical judgement.
