# Ottawa Knee Rule

A validated clinical decision rule that decides whether a **knee radiograph (X-ray)** is needed after an acute knee injury. It records five objective bedside criteria and applies simple **ANY-of** logic: a knee X-ray is indicated when **at least one** criterion is present, and is **not** indicated when all five are absent. The rule is highly sensitive for clinically significant knee fractures, so a negative result safely rules out the need for imaging and reduces unnecessary radiography.
