# UK LPA for Health and Care Decisions — reference material

Reference documentation for this form: statutory citations, the rule
catalogue, OPG guidance summaries, the certificate-provider eligibility
flow, the section-by-section mapping to the **Mental Capacity Act 2005**,
and the cross-form check against the ADRT record.

Planned files:

- `rule-catalogue.md` — every validity rule with predicate, severity, and
  source statute or regulation
- `mca-2005-mapping.md` — donor / attorney / certificate-provider rules
  cross-walked to MCA 2005 sections
- `opg-lp12-alignment.md` — Office of the Public Guardian practice-note
  alignment (LP12 *Decisions*, LP13 *Life-sustaining treatment*)
- `certificate-provider-decision-tree.md` — skill-based / knowledge-based
  eligibility flowchart
- `adrt-cross-check.md` — how this form interacts with the *Advance
  Decision to Refuse Treatment* form
- `safety-case-notes.md` — DCB0129 / DCB0160 placeholders for deployment
  inside an NHS trust

See the parent [`../AGENTS.md`](../AGENTS.md) for design context.
