# UK LPA for Health and Care Decisions — reference material

Free-form documentation directory. Place reference material — statutory
citations, the rule catalogue, OPG practice-note summaries, MCA 2005
section maps, certificate-provider eligibility flowcharts, ADRT cross-form
notes, safety-case placeholders — here as markdown files.

Filenames are descriptive: `rule-catalogue.md`, `mca-2005-mapping.md`,
`opg-lp12-alignment.md`, `certificate-provider-decision-tree.md`,
`adrt-cross-check.md`, `safety-case-notes.md`.

Contents are read by humans and agents alike.
