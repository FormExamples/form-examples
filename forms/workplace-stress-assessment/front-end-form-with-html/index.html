This survey uses the UK Health and Safety Executive (HSE) Management Standards Indicator Tool. There are 35 short statements grouped into seven workplace topics: demands, control, manager support, peer support, relationships, role clarity and change. For each statement, choose the response that best reflects your experience over the past six months.

Your responses are anonymous. Please do not enter your name, your manager's name, or any details that could identify you or a colleague. Demographic questions in step 1 are deliberately broad (department, tenure band, hours band) so that no individual can be singled out from the aggregate dashboard.

Answers are saved automatically in your browser as you go. Nothing leaves your device until you submit.

The HSE Management Standards Indicator Tool is a 35-item validated instrument for surveying perceived work-related stress. This screening tool produces a benchmarked indication of risk; it is not a clinical diagnosis. Aggregate results should be discussed with your occupational health team or HR partner. If you are personally in distress, please speak to your GP or contact a confidential support line such as Samaritans on 116 123 (UK).
