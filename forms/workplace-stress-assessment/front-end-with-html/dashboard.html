Based on the UK HSE Management Standards Indicator Tool (35 items across seven domains: Demands, Control, Manager Support, Peer Support, Relationships, Role, Change). Domain means use the standard 1-5 Likert scale (higher = better, except Demands and Change where the items are reverse-scored). Overall risk band is benchmarked against HSE percentile thresholds (20th, 50th, 80th). This dashboard supports management action planning; it is not a clinical diagnosis.
