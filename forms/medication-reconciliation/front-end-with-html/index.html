Complete the reconciliation below at a transition of care. Add each information source (two or more independent sources are required), record the allergy status and any drug allergies, add one row per medicine on the best-possible medication history (BPMH) or the inpatient list, then classify every discrepancy and document its intended action and rationale. The live indicator updates the status and counts as you go. Answers are saved automatically in your browser.

This is a documentation-and-completeness instrument, not a numeric score and not a definitive interaction checker. The status and flags support, but do not replace, the pharmacist's and prescriber's clinical judgement.

Medication reconciliation is a WHO High 5s patient-safety intervention and a NICE NG5 and Royal Pharmaceutical Society expectation at every transfer of care. This tool documents the reconciliation and surfaces completeness and safety prompts (insufficient sources, allergy conflict, interaction, unresolved discrepancies); it is not a diagnosis and does not replace clinical decision support. Prescribing decisions rest with the clinician.
