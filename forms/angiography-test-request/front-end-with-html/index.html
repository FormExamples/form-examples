Clinician referral wizard for a vascular angiography examination. Record the requesting clinician, patient identification, the requested modality and body region, the clinical indication and specific question, contrast and renal safety, bleeding and anticoagulation status, pregnancy and radiation considerations, and the requested urgency. The engine computes a four-axis grade (appropriateness, contrast / radiation safety, request completeness, and triage priority) plus a set of safety flags.

This is clinical decision support for the imaging vetting desk. The final triage and booking decision remains the responsibility of the vetting radiologist or interventional radiologist.

Grading is aligned with the ACR Appropriateness Criteria, RCR iRefer, the ESUR Contrast Media Safety guidance, the ACR–NKF consensus on iodinated contrast, and the UK IR(ME)R radiation regulations.
