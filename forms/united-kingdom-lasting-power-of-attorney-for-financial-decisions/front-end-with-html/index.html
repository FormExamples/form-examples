The donor (or a friend or relative helping the donor while the donor still has mental capacity) completes this LP1F deed: the donor (section 1), the attorneys (section 2), how attorneys make decisions (section 3), replacement attorneys (section 4), when attorneys can act (section 5), people to notify (section 6), preferences and instructions (section 7), legal rights (section 8), the donor / certificate-provider / attorney signatures (sections 9–11), and the OPG registration application (sections 12–15). The engine computes a validity band and a composite-risk grade and lists every statutory blocker and additional flag.

This is decision support for preparing the deed; it is not legal advice. The completed LP1F must be signed on paper in the fixed order and registered by the Office of the Public Guardian before it can be used.
