Please complete the renal (kidney) assessment below. The scoring engine applies the KDIGO 2012/2024 chronic kidney disease (CKD) classification, combining GFR category (G1-G5) with albuminuria category (A1-A3) into a composite risk level. All answers are saved automatically in your browser as you go.

If the patient has acute symptoms suggestive of severe kidney injury - anuria, severe hyperkalemia, fluid overload, or uremic encephalopathy - seek urgent nephrology / emergency review immediately.

The KDIGO CKD classification is a validated framework for staging chronic kidney disease using estimated GFR (eGFR) and the urine albumin-to-creatinine ratio (ACR). This screening tool is for information only and is not a diagnosis; please discuss results with a nephrologist.
