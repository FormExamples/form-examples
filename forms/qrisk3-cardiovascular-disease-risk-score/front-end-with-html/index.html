Complete the sections below to estimate an adult's 10-year risk of a first cardiovascular disease (CVD) event. QRISK3 is intended for primary prevention in adults aged 25 to 84 without established cardiovascular disease. A result of 10% or more meets the NICE threshold at which a statin (atorvastatin 20 mg) should be offered alongside lifestyle advice. Answers are saved automatically in your browser as you go.

This tool uses a documented representative weighted model in the shape of QRISK3 for demonstration — it is not the official QRISK3-2017 algorithm and must not drive real prescribing decisions. QRISK3 estimates risk; it is not a diagnosis and does not prescribe.

QRISK3 (Hippisley-Cox et al., BMJ 2017; 357:j2099) is the calculator recommended by NICE (NG238 / CG181) for CVD risk assessment in England and Wales. This implementation is a representative approximation for information only; it is not a diagnosis. Discuss results with a clinician and use validated tooling for real clinical decisions.
