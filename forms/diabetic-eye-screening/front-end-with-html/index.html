Complete the diabetic-eye-screening record below. For each eye, record the retinopathy (R) grade, maculopathy (M) grade, photocoagulation (P) marker, and ungradable (U) marker assigned to the retinal photographs. The engine ranks retinopathy severity R0 < R1 < R2 < R3S < R3A, takes the worst grade across both eyes, and classifies the recall or referral outcome — urgent ophthalmology, hospital eye service referral, slit-lamp re-screen, 6-monthly surveillance, or routine 12- / 24-monthly recall — and raises safety flags. Answers are saved automatically in your browser as you go.

This is a documentation and result-classification record, not an interpretation of raw images. It records the grade a trained screener/grader has assigned and applies the programme's deterministic outcome rules; the output prompts recall or referral rather than determining treatment.

This record mirrors the NHS Diabetic Eye Screening Programme (DESP) grading feature classification (R0–R3, M0–M1, P, U) and pathway standards (PHE / NHS England), and the Royal College of Ophthalmologists Diabetic Retinopathy Guidelines. It is for information only and is not a diagnosis; manage and refer in line with local policy and clinical judgement.
