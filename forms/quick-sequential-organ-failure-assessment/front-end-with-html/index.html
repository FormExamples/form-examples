Complete the three qSOFA criteria below for an adult (16 years or over) with suspected or confirmed infection. Each criterion scores 1 point when present, for a total of 0 to 3. A score of 2 or more is a positive screen and prompts escalation. Answers are saved automatically in your browser as you go.

qSOFA is a screening prompt, not a diagnosis of sepsis. A score below 2 does not exclude sepsis: re-score if the patient deteriorates, and use clinical judgement alongside your national early-warning score (NEWS2).

The qSOFA (quick Sequential Organ Failure Assessment) score is drawn from the Sepsis-3 consensus definitions (Singer et al., JAMA 2016; Seymour et al., JAMA 2016) and NICE NG51. This screening tool is for information only and is not a diagnosis; escalate and treat in line with local sepsis policy and clinical judgement.
