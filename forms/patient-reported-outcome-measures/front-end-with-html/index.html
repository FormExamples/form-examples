This form captures a battery of 4 independent, validated patient-reported outcome instruments, commonly administered together in spine-surgery outcomes research: the SF-36v2 Health Survey (36 items, 8 domains), the Neck Disability Index (10 sections), the modified Japanese Orthopedic Association score (6 subscales), and EQ-5D-3L (5 dimensions plus a visual analogue scale). Each instrument is scored independently — there is no cross-instrument composite score.

Everything runs client-side; nothing is sent to a server from this static page. Your progress is saved to this browser as you go.
