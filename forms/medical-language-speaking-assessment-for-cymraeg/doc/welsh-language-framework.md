# Welsh language framework — More Than Just Words

The Welsh Government's *More Than Just Words* strategic framework sets
the duty on NHS Wales and partner social-care organizations to provide
Welsh-language services on an *active offer* basis — services
should be available in Welsh without the patient having to ask.

- Welsh Government. *More than just words — strategic framework for
  Welsh language services in health, social services and social
  care.* Original strategy 2012; refreshed 2016 and 2022.
  Index: <https://www.gov.wales/welsh-language-health-social-services-and-social-care>
- Welsh Government. *More than just words — follow-on strategic
  framework 2016–2019.*
  <https://www.gov.wales/sites/default/files/publications/2019-05/follow-on-strategic-framework-for-welsh-language-services-in-health-social-services-and-social-care.pdf>

## Welsh Language Standards

- *Welsh Language (Wales) Measure 2011.* The statute that established
  the Welsh Language Commissioner and the legal framework for the
  Welsh Language Standards.
  <https://www.legislation.gov.uk/mwa/2011/1/contents>
- Welsh Language Commissioner. *Welsh Language Standards regulations
  applicable to NHS Wales bodies.*
  <https://www.welshlanguagecommissioner.wales/welsh-language-standards>

NHS Wales bodies are subject to the *Health Bodies* standards under
the *Welsh Language Standards (No. 7) Regulations 2018* (SI
2018/441).
<https://www.legislation.gov.uk/wsi/2018/441/contents/made>

## Workforce planning — *Cymraeg 2050*

The Welsh Government strategy *Cymraeg 2050: A million Welsh speakers*
targets one million Welsh speakers by 2050 and underpins workforce
language-skills planning across the public sector.

- *Cymraeg 2050: A million Welsh speakers.* 2017.
  <https://www.gov.wales/cymraeg-2050-welsh-language-strategy>

## Linguistic mapping to CEFR

The form maps Welsh-language proficiency onto the Common European
Framework of Reference for Languages (CEFR). The CEFR descriptors and
the *Companion volume* are the authoritative source.

- Council of Europe. *Common European Framework of Reference for
  Languages — Learning, teaching, assessment.* 2001.
  <https://www.coe.int/en/web/common-european-framework-reference-languages>
- Council of Europe. *CEFR Companion Volume.* 2020.
  <https://www.coe.int/en/web/common-european-framework-reference-languages/the-cefr-companion-volume>

Welsh-language-specific CEFR alignment is published by:

- *CEFR-aligned Welsh-for-adults framework.* National Centre for
  Learning Welsh.
  <https://learnwelsh.cymru/>
