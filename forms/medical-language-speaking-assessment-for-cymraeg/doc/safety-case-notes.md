# Safety case notes — Welsh clinical speaking assessment

## Patient-safety considerations of language matching

Mismatched clinician–patient language is an established source of
clinical risk. Evidence is summarised in:

- Flores G. *Language Barriers to Health Care in the United States.*
  N Engl J Med 2006; 355: 229–231. PMID: 16855260.
  <https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMp058316>
- de Moissac D, Bowen S. *Impact of Language Barriers on Quality of
  Care and Patient Safety for Official Language Minority Francophones
  in Canada.* J Patient Exp 2019; 6(1): 24–32. PMID: 31236448.
  <https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/2374373518769008>
- Hughes E et al. *Why does language matter in healthcare?* British
  Journal of Healthcare Management 2020.

The active-offer principle in *More Than Just Words* recognises that
some Welsh-speaking patients (particularly older patients living with
dementia, very young children, and patients in acute distress) revert
to first-language Welsh and cannot reliably switch to English. The
implication for safety is that the assessment must avoid
*over-attesting* a clinician's competence.

## Sources of false-positive scoring

The form output is used for workforce deployment. The principal
sources of false-positive scoring (clinician scored above true level)
are:

- Examiner overestimation due to social rapport (mitigation: two
  examiners on Severe-cohort cases, or recording for later
  moderation).
- Role-play content too easy for clinical context (mitigation: rotate
  role-play scenarios per the Welsh Government CPD library).
- Test-day language familiarity bias (the candidate happens to know
  the scenario vocabulary) — mitigation: blind selection from a
  scenario bank.

## Workforce-planning use

The assessment outcome SHOULD drive the *Cymraeg in the Workplace*
deployment label (None / Greeting / Conversational / Service /
Specialist / Native, broadly aligning with CEFR A0 / A1 / A2 / B1 / B2
/ C1+).

- ESCO. *Welsh language skill descriptors.*
  <https://esco.ec.europa.eu/en>
- *NHS Wales staff record (ESR) Welsh-language field guidance.*

## Privacy

- Lawful basis (UK GDPR): Article 6(1)(b) performance of an
  employment contract.
- The candidate's assessment record is *occupational data*. Retention
  per NHS Wales workforce record-retention policy.
- The recorded role-play (if used for moderation) MUST be deleted
  within the published moderation window unless retained on
  documented training-evidence grounds with candidate consent.
