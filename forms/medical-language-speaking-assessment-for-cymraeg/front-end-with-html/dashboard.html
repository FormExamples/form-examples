The Medical Language Speaking Assessment For Cymraeg is a Welsh-language clinical communication assessment for healthcare professionals working with Welsh-speaking patients in NHS Wales settings (Bangor, Aberystwyth, Caernarfon, Carmarthen and beyond). It is aligned to the NHS Wales Welsh Language Standards and the Iaith Gwaith framework. Most NHS Wales Welsh-essential posts require Grade B (350+). This dashboard is a training and exam admin tool and is not a regulatory determination.
