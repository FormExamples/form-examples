This examiner rating form supports clinical Welsh-language (Cymraeg) speaking assessment for healthcare professionals working with Welsh-speaking patients. It is aligned to the NHS Wales "More Than Just Words" (Mwy na Geiriau) framework and the Welsh Language Standards under the Welsh Language Measure 2011, and is mapped to CEFR levels A1 to C2. The candidate completes two role-played clinical scenarios in Welsh; the examiner rates the candidate against four linguistic criteria and five clinical communication indicators, producing a scaled score (0-500) and an overall grade (A, B, C+, C, D, or E).

All ratings are saved automatically in the browser as you go. This is a training and assessment tool only and not a substitute for an accredited Welsh-language proficiency examination (e.g. WJEC / Coleg Cymraeg Cenedlaethol Use of Welsh).

Modelled on the OET Speaking Sub-test framework and adapted for clinical Welsh-language communication under the NHS Wales Welsh Language Standards and "More Than Just Words" / "Mwy na Geiriau" strategic framework. Linguistic criteria are rated 0-6; clinical communication indicators are rated 0-3. Combined raw scores are scaled to 0-500 and mapped to grades A-E. This tool supports examiner training and self-assessment; an official Welsh-language clinical proficiency grade can only be issued by an accredited Welsh-language assessment body.
