# Clinical guideline alignment — psychiatry assessment

## Comprehensive psychiatric assessment standards

This form supports a comprehensive psychiatric assessment for a new
patient. The required elements are drawn from the Royal College of
Psychiatrists curriculum, NICE clinical guidelines, and the Mental
Health Act 1983 Code of Practice.

## Royal College of Psychiatrists — core curriculum

Source: Royal College of Psychiatrists. *Core Psychiatry Curriculum*.
URL: https://www.rcpsych.ac.uk/training/curricula-and-guidance

The RCPsych curriculum identifies the components of a comprehensive
psychiatric history and mental state examination. The form's steps
align with each domain:

| Domain                       | Step                            |
| ---------------------------- | ------------------------------- |
| Identification               | Demographics                    |
| Presenting complaint         | Presenting Complaint            |
| History of presenting illness | Presenting Complaint           |
| Past psychiatric history     | Psychiatric History             |
| Mental status examination    | Mental Status Exam              |
| Risk to self / others        | Risk Assessment                 |
| Depression / anxiety         | Mood & Anxiety                  |
| Substance use                | Substance Use                   |
| Current medications          | Current Medications             |
| Past medical history         | Medical History                 |
| Social and developmental     | Social History                  |
| Capacity and consent         | Capacity & Consent              |

## NICE guidelines referenced by the form

The Mood & Anxiety, Risk, and Substance Use steps depend on the relevant
NICE clinical guidelines. The most-commonly-applied are:

- **NG222** — Depression in adults: treatment and management (2022).
  https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/ng222
- **CG113** — Generalised anxiety disorder and panic disorder in adults:
  management (2011, updated 2019).
  https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/cg113
- **NG225** — Self-harm: assessment, management and preventing
  recurrence (2022). https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/ng225
- **CG178** — Psychosis and schizophrenia in adults: prevention and
  management (2014, updated 2014).
  https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/cg178
- **CG185** — Bipolar disorder: assessment and management (2014, updated
  2023). https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/cg185
- **NG215** — Stroke (cross-reference) and **NG116** — PTSD (2018).
- **CG72** — Attention deficit hyperactivity disorder (superseded by
  NG87). https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/ng87
- **CG142** — Autism in adults (2012, updated 2021).
- **CG90 / NG134** — Depression in adults with a chronic physical
  health problem.
- **NG116** — Post-traumatic stress disorder (2018).

## DSM-5-TR

American Psychiatric Association (2022). *Diagnostic and Statistical
Manual of Mental Disorders, Fifth Edition, Text Revision (DSM-5-TR)*.
The clinician records DSM-5-TR diagnostic codes where this classification
is used locally.

## ICD-11

World Health Organization. ICD-11 MMS — Chapter 06: Mental, behavioural
and neurodevelopmental disorders. URL: https://icd.who.int/browse11/l-m/en

Key chapter blocks:

- **6A00–6A0Z** — Neurodevelopmental disorders.
- **6A20–6A2Z** — Schizophrenia or other primary psychotic disorders.
- **6A60–6A6Z** — Bipolar or related disorders.
- **6A70–6A7Z** — Depressive disorders.
- **6B00–6B0Z** — Anxiety or fear-related disorders.
- **6B20–6B2Z** — Obsessive-compulsive or related disorders.
- **6B40–6B4Z** — Disorders specifically associated with stress (PTSD,
  CPTSD, adjustment disorder).
- **6B60–6B6Z** — Dissociative disorders.
- **6B80–6B8Z** — Feeding and eating disorders.
- **6C40–6C5Z** — Disorders due to substance use or addictive behaviours.
- **6D10–6D1Z** — Personality disorders and related traits.

## UK Mental Health Act 1983 Code of Practice

Source: UK Department of Health and Social Care (2015). *Mental Health
Act 1983 Code of Practice*.
URL: https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/code-of-practice-mental-health-act-1983

The Capacity & Consent step records:

- Capacity for the relevant decision (Mental Capacity Act 2005).
- Whether detention under the Mental Health Act 1983 is indicated.
- Whether a Lasting Power of Attorney for Health and Care Decisions
  (LP1H) is in place.
- Best-interests considerations and consultees.
