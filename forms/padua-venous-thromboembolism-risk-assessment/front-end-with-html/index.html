Complete the eleven Padua risk factors below for a hospitalised medical (non-surgical) adult. Each factor contributes its weighted points when present, for a total of 0 to 20. A score of 4 or more is high risk and prompts consideration of pharmacological thromboprophylaxis, subject to a bleeding-risk check. Answers are saved automatically in your browser as you go.

The Padua Prediction Score is a decision-support tool, not a diagnostic test. A low score does not exclude VTE and does not replace a bleeding- risk assessment or clinical judgement. It is not for surgical, obstetric, or paediatric patients.

Submit the form to see the report.

The Padua Prediction Score was derived and validated by Barbar et al. (J Thromb Haemost 2010) and is recommended for VTE risk assessment in medical inpatients by the ACCP (9th ed.) and NICE NG89. This tool is for information only and is not a diagnosis; assess bleeding risk and use clinical judgement before starting thromboprophylaxis.
