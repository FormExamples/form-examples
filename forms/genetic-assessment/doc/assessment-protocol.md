# Assessment protocol

## Intended use

A referral-triage questionnaire that captures the patient's personal and
family history across cancer, cardiovascular, neurological, and
reproductive genetics; applies NICE / NCCN-aligned referral criteria; and
produces a recommended referral pathway.

## Intended users

- GPs and practice nurses (primary care referral triage)
- Cancer-genetics MDT clinicians (intake)
- Cardiology, neurology, obstetrics teams seeking genetics input

## Setting

UK primary or secondary care.

## Workflow

1. **Demographics** — name, DOB, GP, contact details, language interpreter
   need.
2. **Referral information** — referring clinician, presenting concern,
   patient's understanding of why they are being seen.
3. **Personal medical history** — own cancers, congenital anomalies,
   neurological conditions, cardiomyopathy, sudden collapse, lipid disorder.
4. **Cancer history** — types, ages at diagnosis, treatment history,
   prior genetic results.
5. **Family pedigree** — three-generation pedigree fields: relationship,
   age, current health status, diagnoses, age at diagnosis, age at death,
   cause of death.
6. **Cardiovascular genetics** — premature CAD < 55 (M) / < 65 (F),
   cardiomyopathy, sudden cardiac death, channelopathy, familial
   hypercholesterolaemia.
7. **Neurogenetics** — early-onset dementia, movement disorder,
   neuropathy, ataxia, epilepsy syndrome, mitochondrial disease.
8. **Reproductive genetics** — recurrent miscarriage, fetal anomaly,
   consanguinity, carrier status.
9. **Ethnic background and consanguinity** — ancestry contributing to
   carrier-frequency considerations; consanguinity grade.
10. **Genetic testing history** — prior NHS testing, prior commercial
    testing (23andMe etc.), known familial variants.

## Output

- Composite weight and risk band (Low/Moderate/High)
- Per-domain finding
- Recommended referral pathway:
  - Cancer-genetics: regional MDT or community service per CG164 thresholds
  - Cardiac-genetics: regional inherited cardiac conditions service
  - Neuro-genetics: regional Clinical Genetics service
  - Reproductive: maternal-fetal medicine and Clinical Genetics
- Structured PDF referral letter

## Safety-net behaviour

- Suspected hereditary cancer with high-risk pedigree → urgent referral
- Known pathogenic familial variant → direct to predictive-testing pathway
- Suspected high-risk inherited cardiomyopathy → cardiology and inherited
  cardiac conditions service
- Carrier status for severe recessive condition with planned pregnancy →
  reproductive genetics referral within 4 weeks

## Out of scope

- Variant classification (handled by clinical genetics laboratory)
- Pre- and post-test counselling (clinical genetics service)
- Result disclosure
- Embryo selection / pre-implantation genetic testing decisions (HFEA
  pathway)
- Direct interpretation of patient-supplied commercial genetic test reports
