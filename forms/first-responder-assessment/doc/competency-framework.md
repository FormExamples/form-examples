# First Responder Competency Framework

This form implements a fitness-and-competency evaluation for ambulance
service first responders, including paramedics, EMTs, ambulance
technicians, community first responders, and workplace first aiders. It
draws on the published competency frameworks of the UK College of
Paramedics, the UK HCPC standards for paramedics, the JRCALC clinical
guidelines, and the NHS Knowledge & Skills Framework.

## Authoritative sources

### UK clinical competency

- **Health and Care Professions Council (HCPC)** — *Standards of
  proficiency: Paramedics*. HCPC; 2014 (revised). The statutory
  competency standard for paramedic practice in the UK.
  <https://www.hcpc-uk.org/standards/standards-of-proficiency/paramedics/>
- **College of Paramedics** — *Paramedic Career Framework*, 5th edition
  (2024). The professional body's career-stage competency framework.
  <https://collegeofparamedics.co.uk/COP/Professional-development/Career-Framework.aspx>
- **JRCALC / AACE** — *UK Ambulance Services Clinical Practice
  Guidelines*. Joint Royal Colleges Ambulance Liaison Committee and the
  Association of Ambulance Chief Executives. Class Publishing; updated
  annually.
  <https://aace.org.uk/clinical-practice-guidelines/>

### US clinical competency

- **National Highway Traffic Safety Administration (NHTSA)** —
  *National EMS Education Standards*. DOT HS 811 077A, January 2009.
  <https://www.ems.gov/projects/education-standards.html>
- **NHTSA** — *National EMS Scope of Practice Model 2019*. DOT HS 812 666.
  <https://www.ems.gov/projects/scope-of-practice.html>
- **NREMT** — National Registry of Emergency Medical Technicians.
  <https://www.nremt.org/>

### Occupational fitness

- **NHS Employers / Skills for Health** — *Health and Safety at Work
  etc Act 1974 (c. 37)* employer fitness-for-work duties.
  <https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1974/37/contents>
- **Faculty of Occupational Medicine (FOM)** — *Guidance on
  occupational aspects of the management of UK ambulance personnel*.
  <https://www.fom.ac.uk/>
- **NFPA 1582 (US)** — *Standard on Comprehensive Occupational Medical
  Program for Fire Departments* — frequently used by US EMS agencies
  alongside fire-service medical evaluations.
  <https://www.nfpa.org/codes-and-standards/1582>

## Competency levels

The four-level scale used in this form (Not Competent / Developing /
Competent / Expert) maps to the descriptors used in:

- College of Paramedics *Career Framework* levels 5 (Newly Qualified) →
  level 8 (Consultant Paramedic).
- NHS Knowledge & Skills Framework (KSF) levels 1-4 for the cluster
  competencies relevant to clinical practice.
  <https://www.nhsemployers.org/publications/simplified-ksf>
- *Benner novice-to-expert* model widely used in nursing and AHP
  competency assessment.
  Benner P. *From Novice to Expert: Excellence and Power in Clinical
  Nursing Practice.* Addison-Wesley; 1984. ISBN 978-0201002997.

## Driving qualification reference

UK ambulance service drivers require:

- Standard category B licence plus
- Category C1 entitlement (vehicles 3.5–7.5 t) — most NHS ambulances
- DSA-approved emergency response driving (high-speed driver training,
  often "blue light" course aligned with the *Roadcraft* standard) —
  Police Driver Manual *Roadcraft* (TSO; latest edition 2020).

References:

- DVLA driver licensing categories:
  <https://www.gov.uk/driving-licence-categories>
- *Roadcraft: The Police Driver's Handbook*. The Stationery Office; 2020.
  ISBN 978-0117083219.
- Driver and Vehicle Standards Agency (DVSA) emergency response driving
  standards. <https://www.gov.uk/government/organizations/driver-and-vehicle-standards-agency>

## Psychological readiness reference

- *Trauma Risk Management (TRiM)* — peer-support model developed by the
  UK Defence Medical Services and adopted by NHS ambulance trusts.
  <https://march-on-stress.com/trim/>
- *NICE NG116* — *Post-traumatic stress disorder*. NICE guideline NG116,
  2018. <https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/ng116>
- *College of Paramedics — Mental Health and Wellbeing Resource*.
  <https://collegeofparamedics.co.uk/COP/Mental_Health.aspx>

## Versioning note

The JRCALC guidelines are updated annually, the HCPC SOPs are periodically
reviewed (last major revision 2014; refreshed 2023), and the College of
Paramedics Career Framework was reissued in 5th edition in 2024.
Implementations should record the version of each framework that was in
force at the time of assessment.
