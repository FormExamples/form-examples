A strongly positive test, exertional hypotension, ischaemia induced at a low workload, or a high-risk Duke treadmill score (≤ −11) is a critical result: it auto-escalates the follow-up urgency to a critical alert and must be communicated directly to the referrer.

This structured cardiac stress report is aligned with ACC/AHA exercise-testing reporting standards, including the Duke treadmill score risk bands (≥ +5 low risk, −10 to +4 intermediate, ≤ −11 high risk), the ≥85% age-predicted heart-rate adequacy threshold, and the critical-result triggers (strongly positive test, exertional hypotension, ischaemia at low workload, high-risk Duke score). It is a reporting aid, not a diagnosis; interpret and act in line with local cardiology pathways and clinical judgement.
