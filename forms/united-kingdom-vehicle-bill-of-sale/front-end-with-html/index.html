Complete the ten sections below to record a private vehicle sale. The engine does not give legal advice. It checks that the bill of sale is complete — who sold what to whom, for how much, and on what terms — and looks for risk: outstanding finance, a missing or mismatched V5C, a seller who is not the registered keeper, or a vehicle that does not match its description. It classifies the record as complete, incomplete, or risk flagged and raises flags such as no VIN, no mileage, payment risk, and missing signatures. Answers are saved automatically in your browser as you go.

This form is documentation support, not legal advice. A classification of complete does not prove the seller owns the vehicle, that finance is settled, or that the vehicle matches its description. Related forms: the DVLA forms in this collection and United Kingdom Vehicle Registration Application.
