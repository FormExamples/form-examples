# Clinical references

Verify each reference before relying on it clinically; they are anchors for the engine's rules.

- European Federation for Immunogenetics (EFI). Standards for Histocompatibility and Immunogenetics Testing (current version).
- British Society for Histocompatibility and Immunogenetics (BSHI) and British Transplantation Society (BTS). Guidelines on detection and characterization of clinically relevant antibodies in allotransplantation; guidelines on donor-recipient histocompatibility testing for solid-organ transplantation.
- KDIGO. Clinical Practice Guideline on the Evaluation and Management of Candidates for Kidney Transplantation (2020).
- KDIGO. Clinical Practice Guideline for the Care of Kidney Transplant Recipients (2009).
- ISO 15189: Medical laboratories, requirements for quality and competence (request form content, sample identification, rejection criteria).
- IATA Dangerous Goods Regulations (packaging and carriage of diagnostic specimens).
- MDCG 2019-11 Rev.1: EU MDR/IVDR software classification.
- Haematopoietic stem cell transplantation donor selection guidance, for example the EBMT Handbook and WMDA donor-selection standards (verify current versions).
