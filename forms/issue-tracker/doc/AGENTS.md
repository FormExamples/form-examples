# Issue Tracker — Documentation

Reference materials for the issue-tracker form. The primary source is the
form's own `seed.md`, plus the published authorities behind each of the
seven scoring scales:

- **Priority rank** — to-do list practice, no formal authority.
- **Severity of impact** — Saffir, H.S. & Simpson, R.H. *The Saffir-Simpson
  Hurricane Wind Scale*, US National Hurricane Center.
- **Magnitude of damage** — Richter, C.F. *An Instrumental Earthquake
  Magnitude Scale*, Bulletin of the Seismological Society of America, 1935.
- **Harm grade** — NHS Learn from Patient Safety Events (LFPSE) harm-grade
  rubric, NHS England.
- **Failure condition** — US FAA Advisory Circular 25.1309-1A *System Design
  and Analysis*; EASA CS-25 §1309 mirror.
- **MoSCoW requirement** — Clegg, D. & Barker, R. *Case Method Fast-Track:
  A RAD Approach*, Addison-Wesley, 1994.
- **Frequency percent** — epidemiological / quality-management practice.

Authoritative source PDFs live in `seeds/` (added when the form is
promoted from design-spec to fully implemented).
