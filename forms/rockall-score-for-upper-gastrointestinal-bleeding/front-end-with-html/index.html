Answers are saved automatically in your browser as you go.

The Rockall score is a risk estimate and an escalation prompt, not a diagnosis or a treatment decision. A low score does not exclude significant bleeding: reassess if the patient deteriorates, and use clinical judgement. NICE recommends the Glasgow-Blatchford score for the first (pre-endoscopy) decision.

The Rockall score was derived and validated by Rockall et al. (Gut 1996) from a UK national audit of acute upper GI haemorrhage; NICE CG141 recommends the full Rockall score after endoscopy for risk of rebleeding and death. This scoring tool is for information only and is not a diagnosis; escalate and treat in line with local policy and clinical judgement.
