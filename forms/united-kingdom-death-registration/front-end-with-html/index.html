Complete the seven sections below to check whether a death in England and Wales is ready to be registered. The engine does not compute a clinical score. It checks that the registration is complete and valid — that the medical examiner has confirmed the death can be registered, that any coroner referral has been resolved, that the informant is qualified, and how many of the 5 days allowed have passed — and it classifies the registration as ready to register, incomplete, referred to coroner - pending, or registered. Answers are saved automatically in your browser as you go.

This tool is not legal advice. The registrar’s own checks remain definitive. The medical certificate itself is completed by a doctor; see the separate medical certificate of cause of death form.
