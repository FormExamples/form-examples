# Regulatory framework — DVLA V1 (visual)

The V1 questionnaire collects visual function information used by the
Driver & Vehicle Licensing Agency (DVLA, Swansea) to assess fitness to
drive on visual grounds for Group 1 (cars and motorcycles) and Group 2
(LGV / PCV) licences.

## Primary statute

- **Road Traffic Act 1988**, sections 92–96. Section 96 sets the basic
  eyesight requirement (reading a number plate at the prescribed distance).
  <https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1988/52/contents>
- **Motor Vehicles (Driving Licences) Regulations 1999** (SI 1999/2864),
  regulation 72 and Schedule 8.
  <https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1999/2864/contents>
- EU Directive **2006/126/EC**, Annex III §6 (vision).

## Statutory eyesight standard (Group 1)

A driver must be able to:

- Read a current-format vehicle number plate from 20 metres (or, for
  pre-2001 plates, 20.5 metres), with corrective lenses if needed.
- Have visual acuity of at least Snellen **6/12** (binocular).
- Have an adequate field of vision (see DVLA guide chapter).

Source: Road Traffic Act 1988 s.96; SI 1999/2864 reg. 72; DVLA "Assessing
fitness to drive" Visual disorders chapter.

## Statutory eyesight standard (Group 2)

- Binocular visual acuity at least **6/7.5** in the better eye and **6/60**
  in the worse eye (with corrective lenses if used).
- Horizontal field of vision at least 160°, with extensions at least 70°
  left and right, and at least 30° up and down; no defect within the
  central 30°.
- Source: SI 1999/2864 Schedule 8; Directive 2006/126/EC Annex III.

## DVLA medical standards

The clinical thresholds are published in **"Assessing fitness to drive: a
guide for medical professionals"** — DVLA Drivers Medical Group, current
edition:
<https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/assessing-fitness-to-drive-a-guide-for-medical-professionals>

The Visual disorders chapter covers:

1. Visual acuity standards.
2. Visual field defects (including the **Esterman binocular field test**
   for borderline cases).
3. Monocular vision.
4. Diplopia.
5. Night blindness, glare.
6. Specific conditions: glaucoma, diabetic retinopathy, retinitis
   pigmentosa, age-related macular degeneration, cataract, stroke /
   homonymous hemianopia, optic neuritis, nystagmus.

## Esterman binocular field test

DVLA's preferred field assessment for borderline applicants. Performed on
a Humphrey Field Analyzer using the Esterman binocular pattern.

- Reference: Esterman B. (1982) "Functional scoring of the binocular
  field." *Ophthalmology* 89(11): 1226–34. PMID 7155532.

## Driver duty to notify

Drivers must notify DVLA of any visual condition that affects, or may
affect, safe driving (Road Traffic Act 1988 s.94). Failure to do so is
an offence punishable by a fine up to £1,000.

- Driver-facing: <https://www.gov.uk/driving-eyesight-rules>
- Online notification: <https://www.gov.uk/health-conditions-and-driving>
