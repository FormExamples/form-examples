# Assessment protocol — DVLA V1 visual screening

The V1 questionnaire is completed by, or with, an ophthalmic clinician
(optometrist, ophthalmologist, or appropriately trained orthoptist) and
returned to the DVLA. This document maps each section to the relevant
chapter of the "Assessing fitness to drive" guide and notes the standards.

Authoritative source: **"Assessing fitness to drive: a guide for medical
professionals"** — DVLA Drivers Medical Group, current edition:
<https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/assessing-fitness-to-drive-a-guide-for-medical-professionals>

## Sections and corresponding standards

### 1. Visual acuity

- **Group 1**: at least Snellen 6/12 (binocular), corrected if necessary;
  plus number-plate test at 20 m (Road Traffic Act 1988 s.96).
- **Group 2**: at least 6/7.5 in better eye and 6/60 in worse eye, with
  correction up to +8 dioptres permitted.

Record uncorrected and corrected acuity for each eye and binocular.

### 2. Visual field

Standards drawn from Directive 2006/126/EC Annex III §6 and SI 1999/2864
Schedule 8:

- **Group 1**: horizontal field at least 120°, extensions at least 50°
  left and right, no significant defect within the central 20°.
- **Group 2**: horizontal field at least 160°, extensions at least 70°
  left and right, ≥30° up and down, no defect within central 30°.

Borderline cases are assessed by the **Esterman binocular field test**
(Humphrey Field Analyzer, Esterman programme).

### 3. Monocular vision

Group 1 driving permitted if the applicant has adapted to the condition
and meets the standard with the remaining eye. Group 2 driving usually
not permitted.

### 4. Diplopia (double vision)

- **Insuperable diplopia**: must not drive.
- **Diplopia controlled by spectacles, prism, or eye patch acceptable to
  DVLA**: Group 1 driving may be permitted.
- **Group 2**: generally not permitted with diplopia.

### 5. Specific conditions

- **Glaucoma**: Group 1 may continue to drive if both eyes meet field and
  acuity standards.
- **Diabetic retinopathy** with significant macular oedema or
  proliferative changes: assessed individually; field test usually
  required.
- **Stroke / homonymous hemianopia**: Group 1 driving generally not
  permitted unless full field re-established.
- **Cataract**: drive if visual standards met; not while pupils dilated.
- **Retinitis pigmentosa**: field defect typically disqualifies.
- **Age-related macular degeneration**: standards-based.
- **Night blindness / glare sensitivity**: declarable; assessed
  individually.

## Clinician sign-off

V1 is typically completed by a registered optometrist (GOC register) or a
consultant ophthalmologist (GMC register). Where field testing is needed,
the Esterman binocular plot is attached.

## Reporting outcome

The DVLA Medical Adviser issues one of:

- Licence retained / re-issued, possibly with code restrictions (e.g.
  code 01 — corrective lenses required).
- Short-period licence.
- Refusal / revocation under Road Traffic Act 1988 s.93.

## Appeal

Magistrates' court appeal under Road Traffic Act 1988 s.100; 6-month
time limit.
