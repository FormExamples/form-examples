The engine does not compute a clinical score and does not decide whether to refer — it checks whether the referral is complete enough to send, reports a completeness percentage, echoes the urgency (routine, urgent, two-week-wait suspected cancer, or emergency), and raises flags for the referrer to resolve before sending. The urgency is shown even while the referral is incomplete so the correct pathway is never hidden. Answers are saved automatically in your browser as you go.

This tool structures and grades a primary-care referral letter under the NHS e-Referral Service standards and NICE NG12 (suspected cancer). It is an administrative documentation and completeness-checking aid; it does not diagnose or treat, and it does not decide whether to refer or replace local Directory-of-Services pathway rules. Consent follows Montgomery v Lanarkshire Health Board (2015).
