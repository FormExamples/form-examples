# Clinical guideline alignment

This form implements UK contraceptive eligibility screening using guidance
from the Faculty of Sexual & Reproductive Healthcare (FSRH), supplemented by
WHO and NICE references where the UK guidance defers to international
consensus.

## Primary guidance

### FSRH UK MEC 2016 (amended September 2019)

The principal instrument. Defines the four-category framework, condition list,
and method list used by the scoring engine.

- Landing page: <https://www.fsrh.org/standards-and-guidance/uk-medical-eligibility-criteria/>
- Publisher: Faculty of Sexual & Reproductive Healthcare of the RCOG

### WHO Medical Eligibility Criteria, 5th edition (2015)

UK MEC is derived from WHO MEC and aligns category numbering. Where UK MEC is
silent on a niche scenario, the form points clinicians to WHO MEC as the
upstream reference.

- WHO publication page: <https://www.who.int/publications/i/item/9789241549158>
- Title: *Medical eligibility criteria for contraceptive use*, fifth edition

### FSRH CEU clinical guidance documents (referenced for method-specific rules)

The form's flagging rules for each method follow the relevant CEU document.
All are catalogued at <https://www.fsrh.org/standards-and-guidance/>.

- FSRH Guideline: *Combined Hormonal Contraception* (most recent edition)
- FSRH Guideline: *Progestogen-only Pills*
- FSRH Guideline: *Progestogen-only Implant*
- FSRH Guideline: *Progestogen-only Injectable*
- FSRH Guideline: *Intrauterine Contraception*
- FSRH Guideline: *Emergency Contraception*
- FSRH Guideline: *Contraception After Pregnancy*
- FSRH Guideline: *Drug Interactions with Hormonal Contraception*
- FSRH Guideline: *Overweight, Obesity and Contraception*

Specific versions and publication dates are intentionally not duplicated here —
the FSRH portal is the canonical source of truth and is updated as guidance
versions are superseded.

## Supporting NICE guidance

NICE does not publish a contraception-specific guideline that supersedes
UKMEC. The relevant NICE documents that intersect with this form are:

- NICE CKS *Contraception assessment* —
  <https://cks.nice.org.uk/topics/contraception-assessment/>
- NICE CKS *Contraception combined hormonal methods* —
  <https://cks.nice.org.uk/topics/contraception-combined-hormonal-methods/>
- NICE CKS *Contraception progestogen-only methods* —
  <https://cks.nice.org.uk/topics/contraception-progestogen-only-methods/>
- NICE CKS *Contraception IUS/IUD* —
  <https://cks.nice.org.uk/topics/contraception-iud-ius/>
- NICE NG3 *Diabetes in pregnancy* — referenced for prepregnancy planning in
  diabetic women: <https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/ng3>

## Cardiovascular risk references

- ESC *Guidelines on cardiovascular disease prevention in clinical practice*
  (most recent edition): <https://www.escardio.org/Guidelines/Clinical-Practice-Guidelines>
- QRISK calculator (used by UK primary care for 10-year CVD risk):
  <https://qrisk.org/>

## Migraine with aura

UKMEC categorizes migraine with aura as Category 4 for combined hormonal
contraception. The diagnostic criteria for migraine with aura follow ICHD-3:

- International Classification of Headache Disorders, 3rd edition (ICHD-3):
  <https://ichd-3.org/>
