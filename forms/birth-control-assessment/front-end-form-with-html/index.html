Please answer the questions below about your medical and contraceptive history. A clinician will use your answers, together with the UK Medical Eligibility Criteria (UK MEC), to assess which contraceptive methods are safe and suitable for you. All answers are saved automatically in your browser as you go.

This screening tool is for information only and is not a clinical diagnosis or prescription. Please discuss your results with your clinician before starting, stopping, or changing contraception.

Risk classification follows the UK Medical Eligibility Criteria for Contraceptive Use (UK MEC). MEC 1 = no restriction; MEC 2 = advantages outweigh risks; MEC 3 = risks usually outweigh advantages; MEC 4 = unacceptable health risk. Always discuss results with a qualified clinician.
