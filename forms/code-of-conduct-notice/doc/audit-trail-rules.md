# Audit Trail Rules

The Code of Conduct Notice produces an acknowledgment record per staff
member. This document specifies retention, re-issue, and access rules.

## Retention

- The acknowledgment record forms part of the staff member's HR file.
- Retention follows the NHS Records Management Code of Practice 2021 for
  HR records: typically 6 years after the staff member leaves the
  employer, longer for specific schedules (e.g. occupational health,
  pension scheme).

NHS England — Records Management Code of Practice:
<https://www.nhsx.nhs.uk/information-governance/guidance/records-management-code/>

## Re-issue triggers

| Trigger | Why |
| --- | --- |
| Code of Conduct is amended (new principle, change to wording) | Staff member must re-acknowledge the current version |
| Employer changes (e.g. provider merger) | Acknowledgment is bound to the employer; new acknowledgment required |
| Anniversary review (annual) | Recommended re-acknowledgment for audit comfort |
| Staff member changes role | Recommended re-acknowledgment for role-specific principles |

The dashboard emits `flag_acknowledgment_outdated` where the staff
member's acknowledgment version differs from the current notice version.

## Demonstrating accountability

The employer demonstrates compliance with HR / governance requirements by
retaining:

- The versioned notice text (each version archived).
- The per-staff-member acknowledgment record.
- An export of the dashboard showing coverage across the workforce.

This export contributes to:

- NHS Data Security and Protection Toolkit returns:
  <https://www.dsptoolkit.nhs.uk/>
- CQC inspection evidence.
- Internal audit and Caldicott Guardian governance.

## Subject access

Under UK GDPR Article 15, the staff member is entitled to access their
own acknowledgment record. The dashboard supports a per-employee export of:

- Notice versions acknowledged.
- Dates of acknowledgment.
- Any role-specific addenda.

ICO subject access guidance:
<https://ico.org.uk/for-organizations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/individual-rights/right-of-access/>

## Disciplinary use

The acknowledgment record may be used as evidence in a Fitness to
Practise referral, an HR disciplinary process, or a CQC investigation.
The record must be:

- Retained in unmodified form (versioned notice text alongside
  signature).
- Available to authorized investigators on request.
- Disclosed to the staff member as the subject of any process.

Where the record is referenced in a Fitness to Practise hearing, GMC
*Good Medical Practice* (2024) and the relevant Fitness to Practise rules
apply.

## Access controls

Read access is restricted to:

- The staff member themselves.
- The staff member's line manager.
- The HR / People function.
- The compliance / governance / Caldicott Guardian function.
- Authorized investigators under a Fitness to Practise or HR process.

The acknowledgment record is **not** routinely visible to clinical
colleagues, and is not part of the patient record.
