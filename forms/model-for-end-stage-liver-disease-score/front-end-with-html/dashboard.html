The MELD score is a decision-support aid, not a diagnosis. Scores at or above 15 prompt transplant discussion and at or above 30 prompt urgent review; the dialysis flag marks records where creatinine was set to 4.0 mg/dL. Interpret in the clinical context and use local allocation policy where it differs.
