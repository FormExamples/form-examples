# Scoring Rules — Mobility Assessment (Tinetti POMA)

## Tinetti Performance-Oriented Mobility Assessment (POMA)

The primary instrument is the Tinetti POMA (also called the Tinetti
Assessment Tool, balance + gait subscales).

- Canonical reference: Tinetti ME. *Performance-oriented assessment of
  mobility problems in elderly patients.* J Am Geriatr Soc.
  1986;34(2):119-126. DOI: 10.1111/j.1532-5415.1986.tb05480.x. PMID: 3944402.
- Validation: Tinetti ME, Williams TF, Mayewski R. *Fall risk index for
  elderly patients based on number of chronic disabilities.* Am J Med.
  1986;80(3):429-434. DOI: 10.1016/0002-9343(86)90717-5. PMID: 3953620.

### Subscales

- Balance: 9 items, total 0–16
- Gait: 7–10 items (varies by version), total 0–12
- Combined POMA: 0–28

### Risk bands (Tinetti 1986)

| Combined score | Risk band |
| --- | --- |
| 25–28 | Low fall risk |
| 19–24 | Moderate fall risk |
| < 19 | High fall risk |

## Timed Up and Go (TUG)

Captured in step 6 alongside POMA:

- Podsiadlo D, Richardson S. *The Timed "Up & Go": a test of basic
  functional mobility for frail elderly persons.* J Am Geriatr Soc.
  1991;39(2):142-148. DOI: 10.1111/j.1532-5415.1991.tb01616.x. PMID: 1991946.

### TUG interpretation

| Time (seconds) | Interpretation |
| --- | --- |
| < 10 | Normal mobility |
| 10–20 | Within normal limits for frail elderly / disabled patients |
| 20–29 | Variable mobility |
| ≥ 30 | Impaired mobility, increased fall risk |

CDC STEADI recommends ≥ 12 s as a cut-point for older adults at increased
fall risk; the form's flag is set at this lower threshold.

- CDC STEADI Algorithm and TUG protocol:
  https://www.cdc.gov/falls/steadi/index.html

## Range of motion

Step 7 records lower-limb goniometric ROM:

- AAOS *Joint Motion: Method of Measuring and Recording* (1965; reissued).
- AMA *Guides to the Evaluation of Permanent Impairment* (6th ed.).
  https://www.ama-assn.org/practice-management/cpt/ama-guides

## Functional independence

Step 10 references the Functional Independence Measure (FIM) or Barthel
Index where used:

- Mahoney FI, Barthel DW. *Functional evaluation: the Barthel Index.*
  Md State Med J. 1965;14:61-65. PMID: 14258950.
- The original Barthel paper is the source; the form uses Barthel's 10
  items, scored 0–100.

## Composite mapping

The form's composite "category" is taken from POMA combined score:

- < 19 → High fall risk
- 19–24 → Moderate fall risk
- 25–28 → Low fall risk

The TUG and Barthel results are recorded alongside the POMA and inform
the clinical narrative; they do not change the categorical mapping.
