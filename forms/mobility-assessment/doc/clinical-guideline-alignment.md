# Clinical Guideline Alignment — Mobility Assessment

## NICE CG161 — Falls in older people

- https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/cg161

POMA is one of the recommended balance / gait instruments under the
multifactorial assessment. This form is the mobility-domain implementation
of CG161; results feed into a separate fall-risk-assessment form for the
multifactorial bundle.

## NICE NG83 — Rehabilitation after critical illness

- https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/cg83 (CG83 *Rehabilitation after
  critical illness in adults*)

Step 10 functional-independence assessment references CG83 for goal
setting in patients post critical illness.

## CDC STEADI

- https://www.cdc.gov/falls/steadi/index.html

The STEADI algorithm uses TUG (≥ 12 s flag), 30-second chair stand, and
4-stage balance as a paired triad. Step 5 (gait), step 6 (TUG), and step
4 (balance) operationalize the STEADI battery.

## Royal College of Physicians — National Audit of Inpatient Falls

- https://www.rcp.ac.uk/improving-care/programmes/national-audit-inpatient-falls/

The NAIF inpatient mobility section informs step 8 (assistive devices)
and step 9 (medications affecting mobility).

## Chartered Society of Physiotherapy (CSP) — falls / mobility resources

- https://www.csp.org.uk/

The Allied Health Professions Federation publishes falls-prevention
guidance for community settings.

## Older People's Specific Surgery (POPS) framework

- British Geriatrics Society *Comprehensive Geriatric Assessment Toolkit*:
  https://www.bgs.org.uk/cgatoolkit

CGA includes the mobility domain; the form's outputs are CGA-compatible.
