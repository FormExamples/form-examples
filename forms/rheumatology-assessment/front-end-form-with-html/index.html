If you are experiencing severe joint swelling, fever, sudden loss of function, or chest pain, contact your clinician or local emergency service immediately.

The DAS28 (Disease Activity Score in 28 joints) is a validated composite measure of rheumatoid arthritis activity. This screening tool is for information only and is not a diagnosis; please discuss your results with your clinician.
